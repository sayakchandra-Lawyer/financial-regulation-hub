Applies to
Every listed entity with specified securities (equity) listed on the main board, SME exchange or Innovators Growth Platform; Entities with listed non-convertible securities (Chapter V); A high value debt listed entity (listed non-convertible debt of Rs 5,000 crore or more outstanding): regulations 15 to 27 apply under regulation 15(1A), and an entity with only listed debt follows Chapter VA (regulations 62B onwards)

Key concepts

  • Board composition and independent directors (regulation 17)
  • Audit, nomination and remuneration, stakeholders relationship and risk management committees (regulations 18 to 21)
  • Related party transactions and materiality thresholds in Schedule XII (regulation 23)
  • Secretarial audit (regulation 24A)
  • Disclosure of material events (regulation 30 and Schedule III)
  • Shareholding pattern (regulation 31)
  • Financial results (regulation 33)
  • Obligations for listed non-convertible securities (Chapter V)

Key obligations

  • regulation 13(1): redress investor grievances within 21 calendar days of receipt, and stay registered on SCORES
  • regulation 17(2): the board meets at least four times a year, with no more than 120 days between meetings
  • regulation 21: the Risk Management Committee has at least three members and meets at least twice a year
  • regulation 23(1): a related party transaction is material if it crosses the Schedule XII thresholds; material transactions need prior shareholder approval
  • regulation 24A: the listed entity and its material unlisted Indian subsidiaries undergo secretarial audit by a peer reviewed company secretary
  • regulation 27(2): quarterly compliance report on corporate governance, signed by the compliance officer or CEO, including cyber security incidents
  • regulation 30: disclose material events; Para A of Schedule III events are deemed material, Para B events are tested against the materiality criteria
  • regulation 46: maintain a functional website with the required disclosures

Deadlines and cycles

  • regulation 30(6): board meeting outcomes within 30 minutes of the meeting closing (three hours if the meeting closes after market hours and more than three hours before the next session); events within the entity within 12 hours; other events within 24 hours, unless Schedule III sets another time
  • regulation 29: prior intimation of board meetings on specified matters at least two working days in advance
  • regulation 31(1)(b): shareholding pattern within 21 days of the end of each quarter (half-yearly for SME exchange)
  • regulation 33(3): quarterly results within 45 days of quarter end; annual audited results within 60 days of year end
  • regulation 52(1): debt listed entities file quarterly results within 45 days of quarter end (other than the last quarter)
  • regulation 57(1): certificate on payment of interest or principal on non-convertible securities within one working day of it falling due
  • regulation 15(1A) and 62C: an entity that crosses the Rs 5,000 crore high value debt threshold during the year complies within six months of the trigger

If you breach it

  • Fines and freezing of promoter holdings by stock exchanges under the standard operating procedure in the LODR Master Circular
  • SEBI Act s. 15A(b), 15C and 15HB; SCRA s. 23E

General consequences under the statute. SEBI Act, 1992: SEBI may issue directions, including disgorgement and debarment (s. 11, 11B), pass cease and desist orders (s. 11D) and suspend or cancel registration (s. 12(3)). Adjudicating officers impose penalties under ss. 15A to 15HB: for example, failure to file returns or furnish information carries at least Rs 1 lakh, and up to Rs 1 lakh a day while it continues, capped at Rs 1 crore (s. 15A); insider trading and fraudulent or unfair trade practices carry Rs 10 lakh (s. 15G) or Rs 5 lakh (s. 15HA) up to Rs 25 crore or three times the profit, whichever is higher; any breach with no specific penalty carries Rs 1 lakh up to Rs 1 crore (s. 15HB). Contraventions are also offences punishable with imprisonment up to 10 years, a fine up to Rs 25 crore, or both (s. 24). Proceedings may be settled under s. 15JB. Appeals lie to the Securities Appellate Tribunal within 45 days (s. 15T).
