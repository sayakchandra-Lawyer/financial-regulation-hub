Applies to
Mutual funds, their sponsors, trustee companies and asset management companies; Specialized Investment Funds (Chapter IX) and Mutual Fund Lite (Chapter X)

Key concepts

  • Sponsor eligibility and AMC net worth (regulation 5)
  • Trustee company and its duties (regulations 11, 12)
  • AMC eligibility and obligations (regulations 16, 17, 22)
  • Permitted business of an AMC (regulation 21)
  • Scheme launch, winding up and investment restrictions
  • Total expense ratio (regulations 66, 67)
  • Specialized Investment Fund and MF Lite

Key obligations

  • regulation 5(1)(e): the sponsor keeps the AMC's net worth at the required level at all times: Rs 50 crore under the profitability route; Rs 150 crore at registration under the alternative route, then at least Rs 100 crore
  • regulation 16(2)(c): at least half of the AMC board are not associates of the sponsor, its subsidiaries or the trustees
  • regulation 17(a): no person may be a director of more than one AMC, except an independent director with board approval; AMC directors need prior trustee approval
  • regulation 21: an AMC may not act as trustee and may run only management and advisory services to pooled assets, with segregation and no material conflict of interest
  • regulation 42(1): a scheme may borrow only for temporary liquidity needs, up to 20% of net assets and for up to six months
  • regulation 43: NAV of each scheme is computed every day
  • regulation 44(4): exit load on an open-ended scheme may not exceed 3% of NAV
  • regulation 67(2): no charges other than the base expense ratio, brokerage, transaction cost, statutory levies and exit load may be charged to investors

Deadlines and cycles

  • regulation 22(2)(ii): bio-data of key personnel to the trustees within 15 calendar days of appointment
  • regulation 36(3): trustees give notice of winding up of a scheme within one day
  • regulation 70(1)(a): annual report or abridged summary to unitholders within four months of year end
  • regulation 70(2)(a): half-yearly unaudited results on the website within one month of 31 March and 30 September
  • regulation 12: trustees review AMC reports and net worth quarterly and report to SEBI half-yearly

If you breach it

  • regulation 46(2): delay in paying redemption, IDCW or refunds carries interest at 15% a year for the period of delay
  • regulation 81: AMC, mutual fund, trustee or sponsor in default is liable to action under the SEBI Act and the Intermediaries Regulations
  • SEBI Act s. 15D and 15E

General consequences under the statute. SEBI Act, 1992: SEBI may issue directions, including disgorgement and debarment (s. 11, 11B), pass cease and desist orders (s. 11D) and suspend or cancel registration (s. 12(3)). Adjudicating officers impose penalties under ss. 15A to 15HB: for example, failure to file returns or furnish information carries at least Rs 1 lakh, and up to Rs 1 lakh a day while it continues, capped at Rs 1 crore (s. 15A); insider trading and fraudulent or unfair trade practices carry Rs 10 lakh (s. 15G) or Rs 5 lakh (s. 15HA) up to Rs 25 crore or three times the profit, whichever is higher; any breach with no specific penalty carries Rs 1 lakh up to Rs 1 crore (s. 15HB). Contraventions are also offences punishable with imprisonment up to 10 years, a fine up to Rs 25 crore, or both (s. 24). Proceedings may be settled under s. 15JB. Appeals lie to the Securities Appellate Tribunal within 45 days (s. 15T).
