Applies to
Acquirers of shares, voting rights or control in a listed company, and persons acting in concert; Target companies and their promoters

Key concepts

  • Open offer on reaching 25% (regulation 3(1))
  • Creeping acquisition of up to 5% a year (regulation 3(2))
  • Acquisition of control (regulation 4)
  • Offer size of at least 26% (regulation 7)
  • Disclosure of acquisitions and disposals (regulation 29)
  • Encumbrance disclosure by promoters (regulation 31)

Key obligations

  • regulation 3(1): no acquirer may acquire shares or voting rights which, with those of persons acting in concert, entitle them to 25% or more of the voting rights without a public announcement of an open offer
  • regulation 3(2): a holder of 25% or more but below the maximum non-public shareholding may not acquire more than 5% in a financial year without an open offer
  • regulation 4: acquiring control requires an open offer, whatever the shareholding
  • regulation 7(1): the open offer is for at least 26% of total shares
  • regulation 29(1) and (2): disclose holdings (with persons acting in concert) on reaching 5%, and every later acquisition or disposal that changes the holding by more than 2%

Deadlines and cycles

  • regulation 29(3): disclosures within two working days of the acquisition or disposal

If you breach it

  • SEBI Act s. 15H: Rs 10 lakh up to Rs 25 crore or three times the profit, whichever is higher
  • regulation 32: directions, including divestment and making an open offer with interest

General consequences under the statute. Securities and Exchange Board of India Act, 1992: SEBI may issue directions, including disgorgement and debarment (s. 11, 11B), pass cease and desist orders (s. 11D) and suspend or cancel registration (s. 12(3)). Adjudicating officers impose penalties under ss. 15A to 15HB: for example, failure to file returns or furnish information carries at least Rs 1 lakh, and up to Rs 1 lakh a day while it continues, capped at Rs 1 crore (s. 15A); insider trading and fraudulent or unfair trade practices carry Rs 10 lakh (s. 15G) or Rs 5 lakh (s. 15HA) up to Rs 25 crore or three times the profit, whichever is higher; any breach with no specific penalty carries Rs 1 lakh up to Rs 1 crore (s. 15HB). Contraventions are also offences punishable with imprisonment up to 10 years, a fine up to Rs 25 crore, or both (s. 24). Proceedings may be settled under s. 15JB. Appeals lie to the Securities Appellate Tribunal within 45 days (s. 15T).
