Applies to
Every intermediary associated with the securities market, listed companies, issuers and any person dealing in securities

Key concepts

  • Functions and powers of SEBI (s. 11)
  • Interim and final directions, including disgorgement (s. 11B)
  • Cease and desist orders (s. 11D)
  • Registration of intermediaries (s. 12)
  • Prohibition of manipulative and deceptive devices, insider trading and substantial acquisition (s. 12A)
  • Adjudication of penalties (ss. 15A to 15-I)
  • Settlement of administrative and civil proceedings (s. 15JB)
  • Appeal to the Securities Appellate Tribunal (s. 15T)

Key obligations

  • s. 12(1): no stock broker, merchant banker, portfolio manager, investment adviser or other intermediary may buy, sell or deal in securities except under a certificate of registration from SEBI
  • s. 12A: no person may use manipulative or deceptive devices, deal on unpublished price sensitive information, or acquire control of a company except in line with SEBI regulations
  • s. 11(2A) and 11C: SEBI may call for information, inspect books and records, and investigate

Deadlines and cycles

  • s. 15T(3): appeal to the Securities Appellate Tribunal within 45 days of receiving the order

If you breach it

  • s. 15A: failure to furnish information, returns or books, or furnishing false information: at least Rs 1 lakh, up to Rs 1 lakh a day while it continues, capped at Rs 1 crore
  • s. 15C: listed company or intermediary failing to redress investor grievances when called upon by SEBI: penalty under s. 15C
  • s. 15E: AMC breaching restrictions on its activities: at least Rs 1 lakh, up to Rs 1 lakh a day while it continues, capped at Rs 1 crore
  • s. 15EA: breach of the AIF, InvIT or REIT regulations or of SEBI directions: at least Rs 1 lakh, up to Rs 1 lakh a day while it continues, capped at Rs 1 crore or three times the gains made, whichever is higher
  • s. 15F: stock broker failing to issue contract notes or charging excess brokerage: penalty under s. 15F
  • s. 15G: insider trading: Rs 10 lakh up to Rs 25 crore or three times the profit, whichever is higher
  • s. 15H: failure to make takeover disclosures, a required public announcement or open offer, or to pay the offer consideration: Rs 10 lakh up to Rs 25 crore or three times the profit, whichever is higher
  • s. 15HA: fraudulent and unfair trade practices: Rs 5 lakh up to Rs 25 crore or three times the profit, whichever is higher
  • s. 15HB: breach with no separate penalty: Rs 1 lakh up to Rs 1 crore
  • s. 24(1): offence punishable with imprisonment up to 10 years, fine up to Rs 25 crore, or both

General consequences under the statute. SEBI Act, 1992: SEBI may issue directions, including disgorgement and debarment (s. 11, 11B), pass cease and desist orders (s. 11D) and suspend or cancel registration (s. 12(3)). Adjudicating officers impose penalties under ss. 15A to 15HB: for example, failure to file returns or furnish information carries at least Rs 1 lakh, and up to Rs 1 lakh a day while it continues, capped at Rs 1 crore (s. 15A); insider trading and fraudulent or unfair trade practices carry Rs 10 lakh (s. 15G) or Rs 5 lakh (s. 15HA) up to Rs 25 crore or three times the profit, whichever is higher; any breach with no specific penalty carries Rs 1 lakh up to Rs 1 crore (s. 15HB). Contraventions are also offences punishable with imprisonment up to 10 years, a fine up to Rs 25 crore, or both (s. 24). Proceedings may be settled under s. 15JB. Appeals lie to the Securities Appellate Tribunal within 45 days (s. 15T).
