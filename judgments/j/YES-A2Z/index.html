Yes Bank Ltd v. A2Z Maintenance & Engineering Services Ltd

Exclusive charge holder outside CDR

Open in Key judgmentsJudgment text ↗

Case details

Court
Delhi High Court
Date
30 Jul 2014
Case no.
CS(OS) No. 217 of 2014
Bench
Manmohan Singh J.
Statutes
Indian Contract Act, 1872; Transfer of Property Act, 1882, s. 48; Code of Civil Procedure, 1908, Order XXXIX
Status
CDR has been replaced by RBI's June 2019 framework, under which lenders holding 75% by value and 60% by number bind those who sign the inter-creditor agreement.

The facts

Yes Bank held an exclusive charge over certain assets of a borrower. The borrower went into corporate debt restructuring, which Yes Bank did not join, and the CDR package proposed sharing that security with the other lenders.

The question

Can a lender with an exclusive charge be made to share its security under a restructuring it did not join?

What the court held

  • No. A CDR package binds only lenders who are party to it. Yes Bank was entitled to protect its exclusive charge, and the borrower was restrained from dealing with the charged assets.

What it means in practice

  • Lenders that do not sign an inter-creditor agreement are not bound by the majority's decisions, but the resolution plan must give dissenting lenders at least their liquidation value.
