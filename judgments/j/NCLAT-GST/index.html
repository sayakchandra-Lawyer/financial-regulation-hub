Assistant Commissioner, CGST and Central Excise v. Pradeep Kabra (Cengres Tiles Ltd)

Excise and GST dues in a resolution plan

Open in Key judgmentsJudgment text ↗

Case details

Court
National Company Law Appellate Tribunal
Date
23 Jan 2025
Case no.
Company Appeal (AT) (Insolvency) No. 844 of 2024
Bench
Ashok Bhushan J. (Chairperson), Barun Mitra and Arun Baroka
Statutes
Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016, ss. 5(21), 30(2), 53; Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017

The facts

The tax department's admitted claim of about Rs 11.77 crore for excise and GST dues was given Rs 1 lakh under the approved resolution plan. It appealed, relying on Rainbow Papers to argue it was a secured creditor.

The question

Are central excise and GST dues secured debt, entitling the department to more than the plan offered?

What the tribunal held

  • No. These dues are operational debts. Rainbow Papers turned on the statutory first charge under the Gujarat VAT Act and did not apply. The appeal was dismissed.

What it means in practice

  • Central tax departments rank as operational creditors. After the IBC (Amendment) Act, 2026, government dues have a fixed place in section 53 regardless of any statutory charge.
