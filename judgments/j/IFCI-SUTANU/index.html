IFCI Ltd v. Sutanu Sinha

Compulsorily convertible debentures are equity

The facts

IFCI had subscribed to compulsorily convertible debentures (CCDs) of a toll-road project company. In the company's insolvency it claimed as a financial creditor; the resolution professional rejected the claim because the project documents, the concessioning authority and the lenders had treated the CCDs as equity.

The question

Are compulsorily convertible debentures debt or equity for a claim in insolvency?

What the court held

  • The CCDs were equity. The project documents treated them as equity, they were never recategorised with the concessioning authority's approval, and repayment obligations lay with the sponsor, not the company.
  • Instruments compulsorily convertible into shares are generally equity, not debt. The holder was not a financial creditor.

What it means in practice

  • On these project documents, CCD holders ranked as shareholders. Whether an instrument is debt or equity turns on its terms and how the parties treated it; a redemption obligation points towards debt.
