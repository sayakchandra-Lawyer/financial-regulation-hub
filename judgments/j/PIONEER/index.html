The question

Is the 2018 amendment to the IBC that treats allottees of real estate projects as financial creditors constitutional?

What the court held

  • The amendment is valid. Allottees are financial creditors who can start insolvency under section 7 and sit on the Committee of Creditors through authorised representatives.
  • RERA must be read harmoniously with the Code. Where the two conflict, the Code prevails.

What it means in practice

  • In a builder's insolvency, homebuyers can hold a large share of the votes in the Committee of Creditors. This can dilute the voting power of lenders to builders, including HFCs and NBFCs, depending on the amounts admitted for each class.
  • Construction finance needs to price the risk that homebuyers will drive the resolution plan.
