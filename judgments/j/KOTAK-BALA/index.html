Kotak Mahindra Bank Ltd v. A. Balakrishnan

Recovery certificates and decrees are financial debt

The facts

Kotak Mahindra Bank had obtained a recovery certificate from the Debts Recovery Tribunal for a loan, so the certificate arose from a financial debt. It then filed a section 7 application relying on the certificate. The appellate tribunal held that a recovery certificate holder was not a financial creditor.

The question

Is a liability under a DRT recovery certificate a financial debt, and does the certificate give a fresh starting point for limitation?

What the court held

  • A liability under a recovery certificate is a financial debt, and its holder is a financial creditor who may file under section 7.
  • A recovery certificate gives a fresh cause of action, so a section 7 application filed within three years of the certificate is in time.

What it means in practice

  • Lenders that have won at the DRT can still use the IBC, but should file within three years of the recovery certificate.
