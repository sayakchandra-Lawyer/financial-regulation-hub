Mahakali Sujatha v. Branch Manager, Future Generali India Life Insurance Co. Ltd

Insurer's burden to prove suppression

The question

Who must prove that the insured suppressed other policies, and with what evidence?

What the court held

  • Where the insurer repudiates on the ground of suppression, the burden is on the insurer to prove that the fact suppressed was material and that the insured knew of it.
  • The insurer had produced no documents to show the other policies. The repudiation could not stand.

What it means in practice

  • Claims teams must keep documentary proof, such as other insurers' records, before repudiating for non-disclosure. Allegations alone will fail.
