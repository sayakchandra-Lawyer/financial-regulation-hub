ICICI Bank Ltd v. SIDCO Leathers Ltd

First and second charge holders in liquidation

Statutes
Companies Act, 1956, ss. 529, 529A; Transfer of Property Act, 1882, s. 48
Status
Decided under the Companies Act, 1956. Under the IBC, priority among secured creditors in liquidation is pending in the Supreme Court.

The facts

ICICI Bank held a first charge over a company's assets and another lender held a second charge. When the company was wound up, the second charge holder argued that section 529A of the Companies Act, which ranks secured creditors pari passu with workmen, also wiped out priority among secured creditors.

The question

In a winding up, does a first charge holder keep its priority over a second charge holder?

What the court held

  • Yes. Section 529A puts workmen's dues and secured creditors' dues on an equal footing, but it does not abolish the priority among secured creditors that comes from section 48 of the Transfer of Property Act and their contracts.

What it means in practice

  • Lenders rely on this principle when arguing that charge priority should be respected in an IBC liquidation. The IBC's section 53 treats secured creditors who relinquish security as one class, and the NCLAT's rulings on pro-rata distribution (see Technology Development Board and later cases) are being tested in the Supreme Court.
