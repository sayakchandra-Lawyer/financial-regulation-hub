Wave Megacity Centre Pvt Ltd v. Rakesh Taneja

Fraudulent self-filing for insolvency

Open in Key judgmentsJudgment text ↗

Case details

Court
National Company Law Appellate Tribunal
Date
5 Jan 2023
Case no.
Company Appeal (AT) (Insolvency) No. 918 of 2022
Bench
Ashok Bhushan J. (Chairperson) and Barun Mitra
Statutes
Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016, ss. 10, 10A, 65

The facts

A real estate developer filed for its own insolvency under section 10, largely to avoid dues to the development authority. The authority argued the filing was fraudulent.

The question

Can a company's own insolvency application be rejected as filed with fraudulent or malicious intent?

What the tribunal held

  • Yes. The application was rejected under section 65 as filed to defeat a creditor rather than to resolve insolvency.
  • The section 10A bar on filings for defaults during the COVID-19 period does not cover defaults before 25 March 2020.

What it means in practice

  • Lenders and authorities can resist a borrower's tactical self-filing by showing malicious intent.
