Ajoy Khanderia v. Barclays Bank

Pledgor's liability is limited to the pledge

Open in Key judgmentsJudgment text ↗

Case details

Court
Delhi High Court
Date
20 Jul 2021
Case no.
W.P.(C) No. 4030 of 2020
Bench
Rajiv Sahai Endlaw and Asha Menon JJ.
Statutes
Indian Contract Act, 1872, ss. 172, 176

The facts

A person pledged securities to a bank for a loan to someone else but was neither the borrower nor a guarantor. After selling the pledged securities, the bank sought to recover the shortfall from him.

The question

Is a third-party pledgor liable for the shortfall after the pledged goods are sold?

What the court held

  • No. Under section 176 of the Contract Act, the pawnee can sue the debtor for the shortfall, but a pledgor who is not the debtor or a guarantor is liable only up to the value of the pledged goods.

What it means in practice

  • Lenders who want recourse beyond the pledged assets must take a guarantee from the third-party pledgor.
