Winding up: unitholders' consent

The question

When trustees wind up a scheme, what does 'consent of the unitholders' under Regulation 18(15)(c) mean?

What the court held

  • Consent means consent of a majority of the unitholders who take part in the poll, not a majority of all unitholders of the scheme.
  • Consent of the majority binds all unitholders, including those who did not agree. The Court accepted the e-voting results for the six schemes.

What it means in practice

  • Trustees seeking unitholders' consent to wind up need a simple majority of those voting. Regulation 36(4) of the SEBI (Mutual Funds) Regulations, 2026 now writes this in: votes are counted at one vote per unit, and results must be published within 45 calendar days of the winding-up notice.
