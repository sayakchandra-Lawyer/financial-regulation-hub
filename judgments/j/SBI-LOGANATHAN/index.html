Assistant General Manager, State Bank of India v. S. Loganathan

DRT recovery certificate: property outside the tribunal's area

Pamidighantam Sri Narasimha and Alok Aradhe JJ.
Statutes
Recovery of Debts and Bankruptcy Act, 1993, s. 19(1), s. 19(23), Chapter V

The facts

A bank's case was transferred to and decided by the tribunal at Chennai, which issued a recovery certificate. The mortgaged properties were in Tiruchirappalli. The High Court held that only the tribunal at Madurai could execute the certificate.

The question

Can the Debts Recovery Tribunal that issued a recovery certificate execute it against property outside its territorial limits?

What the court held

  • Yes. The tribunal that issued the certificate can execute it, through its Recovery Officer, against property outside its local limits.
  • Territorial jurisdiction to decide the case (section 19(1)) is different from the reach of the Recovery Officer in execution.
  • "May" in section 19(23) gives the tribunal a judicial discretion to send the certificate to another tribunal; it is not an option for the bank or the Recovery Officer.
  • The Code of Civil Procedure does not restrict the tribunal's execution powers. Contrary High Court views (Delhi, Punjab and Haryana, Calcutta) are overruled.

What it means in practice

  • Lenders need not start again before a second tribunal merely because the mortgaged property lies elsewhere.
  • Where transmission is sought, tribunals apply the test and safeguards in paragraphs 50 and 51 of the judgment.
