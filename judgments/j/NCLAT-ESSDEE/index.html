State Bank of India v. IDBI Bank Ltd

Liquidation proceeds shared by admitted claims

Open in Key judgmentsJudgment text ↗

Case details

Court
National Company Law Appellate Tribunal
Date
28 Jan 2025
Case no.
Company Appeal (AT) (Insolvency) Nos. 321 and 335 of 2024
Bench
Ashok Bhushan J. (Chairperson) and Barun Mitra
Statutes
Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016, s. 53
Status
Subject to the Supreme Court's decision on the Technology Development Board appeal.

The facts

Secured lenders in a liquidation disagreed on how the sale proceeds should be shared: by the value of each lender's security or in proportion to admitted claims.

The question

How are liquidation proceeds shared among secured creditors who relinquished their security?

What the tribunal held

  • In proportion to admitted claims, following the Supreme Court in India Resurgence ARC v. Amit Metaliks (2021), subject to the outcome of the pending Supreme Court appeal against the Technology Development Board ruling.

What it means in practice

  • Until the Supreme Court decides, liquidators distribute pro rata to admitted claims among secured creditors in the same class.
