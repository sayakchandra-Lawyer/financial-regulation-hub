Manmohan Nanda v. United India Assurance Co. Ltd

Pre-existing conditions disclosed at proposal

The question

Can a health insurer reject a claim by citing a medical condition that the insured disclosed, and that the insurer assessed, before issuing the policy?

What the court held

  • The insurer must assess the risks from the health condition disclosed in the proposal form before it issues the policy.
  • Once the policy is issued after that assessment, the insurer cannot reject a claim by citing a disclosed existing condition that led to the risk claimed for.

What it means in practice

  • Underwriting must be done before issue. A health insurer that conducted a medical examination and accepted disclosed conditions cannot later rely on them to repudiate.
