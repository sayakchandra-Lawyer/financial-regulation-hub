SEBI v. Rakhi Trading Pvt Ltd

Synchronised and reversal trades

The question

Can pre-arranged, reversed trades in the futures and options segment be manipulative, even though they are cash-settled?

What the court held

  • Yes. Trading is meant to make profit. Where one party consistently loses in pre-planned, rapid reversal trades, the trades are not genuine and amount to an unfair trade practice.
  • The Securities Appellate Tribunal was wrong to hold that such trades in the F&O segment cannot manipulate the market because there is no change of beneficial ownership.
  • SEBI's appeals against the brokers were dismissed. Merely facilitating a trade is not a violation, and SEBI had shown no negligence or connivance by the brokers.

What it means in practice

  • Clients who trade in pre-arranged reversals in derivatives are exposed to action even though the trades are cash-settled. A broker is liable only on proof of its own negligence or connivance, so surveillance alerts and records of client instructions matter.
