Vistra ITCL (India) Ltd v. Torrent Investments Pvt Ltd

Creditors can keep negotiating after the challenge round

Open in Key judgmentsJudgment text ↗

Case details

Court
National Company Law Appellate Tribunal, New Delhi
Date
2 Mar 2023
Case no.
Company Appeal (AT) (Insolvency) Nos. 132-134 and 139 of 2023
Bench
Ashok Bhushan J. (Chairperson) and Barun Mitra (Member, Technical)
Statutes
Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016, ss. 30, 227; IBBI (Insolvency Resolution Process for Corporate Persons) Regulations, 2016, reg. 39(1A)
Status
The highest bidder in the first round appealed to the Supreme Court. In March 2023 the Court let the further round go ahead without prejudice to the parties' rights; the appeal has not been finally decided.

The facts

The Reserve Bank superseded the board of a large NBFC in November 2021 and its insolvency was started with an Administrator in charge. Bidders took part in a challenge mechanism (an auction among resolution applicants) on 21 December 2022, which ended with a highest bid of Rs 8,640 crore in net present value. Another bidder then sent a higher offer. The committee of creditors decided to hold an extended challenge round. On the highest bidder's application the NCLT held that the committee could not negotiate further once the challenge mechanism had concluded.

The question

After a challenge mechanism under regulation 39(1A) has concluded, can the committee of creditors negotiate further or hold another round?

What the tribunal held

  • Yes. Regulation 39(1A) lets the committee use a challenge mechanism to improve plans. It does not take away the committee's power to negotiate with one or more resolution applicants afterwards, or to annul the process and invite fresh plans, where the request for resolution plans reserves those rights.
  • The highest bidder in the challenge round gets no vested right to have its plan approved. Maximising the value of the corporate debtor's assets is the committee's commercial decision.
  • The NCLT's order was set aside and the committee was allowed to hold a further round.

What it means in practice

  • Lenders on a committee of creditors can seek better offers after an auction round, provided the bid documents keep that right open. Bid documents should say so clearly.
  • Bidders should treat the result of a challenge round as provisional until the committee votes on a plan.
