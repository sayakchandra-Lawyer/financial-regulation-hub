Oriental Bank of Commerce v. Anil Anchalia (Liquidator of Bala Techno Industries Ltd)

Exclusive charge holder in liquidation distribution

Open in Key judgmentsJudgment text ↗

Case details

Court
National Company Law Appellate Tribunal
Date
26 May 2022
Case no.
Company Appeal (AT) (Insolvency) No. 547 of 2022
Bench
Ashok Bhushan J. (Chairperson) and Shreesha Merla
Statutes
Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016, ss. 52, 53
Status
The related question of inter se priority among secured creditors is pending in the Supreme Court.

The facts

A bank with an exclusive charge over certain assets of a company in liquidation relinquished its security and asked to be paid the full sale value of those assets.

The question

Is a secured creditor that relinquishes an exclusive charge paid from the value of that asset, or pro rata with other secured creditors?

What the tribunal held

  • Pro rata. After relinquishment, all secured creditors share in proportion to their claims under section 53. The tribunal relied on the Supreme Court's ruling in India Resurgence ARC v. Amit Metaliks (2021).

What it means in practice

  • A lender with an exclusive charge that wants to keep the benefit of it should consider realising the security itself under section 52 rather than relinquishing it.
