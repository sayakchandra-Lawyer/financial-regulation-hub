Sucheta Dalal v. Union of India

Unclaimed deposits, shares and insurance money

The facts

A public interest petition raised the large sums lying unclaimed with banks and other financial institutions and transferred to statutory funds, and the difficulty heirs face in tracing them.

The question

Should the Court direct a central mechanism for citizens to trace and recover unclaimed deposits, investments and insurance money?

What the court held

  • The Court closed the petition after noting the steps taken by the regulators and the Government during the case to help claimants find and recover unclaimed assets.
  • It recorded the authorities' assurances and expects them to keep improving access, speed and transparency.
  • A person with a specific grievance may approach the competent authority.

What it means in practice

  • No new court-made obligation, but regulators' measures on unclaimed assets remain under watch.
  • Keep unclaimed-asset search tools, nominee processes and claim settlement in good order.
