Who may bid: section 29A

The facts

In the insolvency of Essar Steel, the resolution professional found both bidders, ArcelorMittal and Numetal, ineligible under section 29A: ArcelorMittal because of its link to a defaulting company, Numetal because of its link to the Ruia promoters.

The question

How is section 29A applied to resolution applicants, and what are the roles of the committee of creditors and the tribunals?

What the court held

  • Section 29A is read by looking at the substance: 'control', 'management' and 'connected person' cover persons who act jointly or in concert, and the corporate veil may be lifted to see who really stands behind a bid.
  • Both bidders were ineligible when they bid, but were given a chance to become eligible by paying the overdue debts of the connected defaulting companies within two weeks.
  • The feasibility and commercial merits of a plan are for the committee of creditors. The tribunals review only legality. Time lost in litigation may be excluded from the CIRP timeline.

What it means in practice

  • Lenders on a committee of creditors must test eligibility under section 29A on substance and cannot leave it to the resolution professional alone.
