Group credit life claim rejected for non-disclosure: premium refunded under section 45

Early death claim under loan-linked group life cover

Case details

Court
Insurance Ombudsman, Pune
Date
28 Aug 2017
Case no.
Award No. IO/PUN/A/LI/0104/2017-2018 (Complaint No. PUN-L-006-1718-0301)
Statutes
Insurance Act, 1938, s. 45 (as substituted in 2015); Insurance Ombudsman Rules, 2017
Status
An Ombudsman award binds the insurer in that complaint only. It is not a precedent, but it shows how such complaints are decided. The source is the summary of the award published by the Council for Insurance Ombudsmen.

The facts

A borrower was covered under a group term life policy taken with a loan from an NBFC. She died 362 days into the cover. The insurer rejected the death claim because she had not disclosed treatment for cancer in the proposal, and the cause of death was related to it.

The question

What is payable where a life insurer proves suppression of a material fact but does not allege fraud?

What the Ombudsman decided

  • The insurer proved the non-disclosure with cogent evidence, so the rejection of the death claim stood.
  • Because the insurer had not alleged fraud, the amended section 45 of the Insurance Act required it to refund the premium paid. It was directed to do so.

What it means in practice

  • For loan-linked group life cover sold through lenders, the health declaration taken at the time of the loan decides whether an early claim is paid. Lenders acting as master policyholders should make sure borrowers complete it themselves.
  • Where a claim within three years is rejected for misstatement not amounting to fraud, the insurer has to refund the premiums.
