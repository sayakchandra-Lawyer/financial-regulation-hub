                                  Consultation Paper

Review of Regulation 38 (2) of the SECC Regulations, 2018 and Regulation 7(c)
(i) of the D&P Regulations, 2018 for providing an automatic approval route for
investment in related activities by the MIIs

1. Background:

    1.1. Regulation 38 of the Securities Contracts (Regulation) (Stock Exchanges and
         Clearing Corporations) Regulations, 2018 (SECC Regulations) and Regulation
         7(c)(i) of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Depositories and
         Participants) Regulations, 2018 (D&P Regulations) govern the utilization of
         profits and investments by recognized Stock Exchanges and Clearing
         Corporations; and Depositories respectively (collectively called as ‘Market
         Infrastructure Institutions' or 'MIIs').

    1.2. The relevant provisions of the SECC Regulations and the D&P Regulations
         have been reproduced at Annexure – 1.

    1.3. Under Regulation 38(2) and 7(c) (i), an MII is prohibited from carrying on any
         activity whether involving deployment of funds or otherwise without prior
         approval of the SEBI.

    1.4. Two provisos currently exist under each of the said provisions, as follows:
         1.4.1. First Proviso: Treasury investments made in accordance with the MII
                 Board-approved investment policy are exempt from prior approval.
         1.4.2. Second Proviso: Activities unrelated or not incidental to the MII's core
                 function may be undertaken through a separate legal entity or Strategic
                 Business Unit specific to each activity subject to the SEBI approval.

    1.5. Therefore, as observed, only single carve-out exists i.e. treasury investments
         under a Board-approved policy. Every other investment or activity, regardless
         of size or nature, requires approval of the SEBI.

    1.6. Certain MIIs have submitted proposals suggesting amendments to Regulation
         38 and Regulation 7(c)(i), primarily to reduce the regulatory burden of seeking
         prior approval for certain investments or activities.

2. Deliberations of the Secondary Market Advisory Committee:

    2.1. The proposals of the MIIs were examined and an agenda in this regards was
         taken to Secondary Market Advisory Committee (SMAC) for its deliberations
         and recommendation.

    2.2. SMAC agreed with the overall approach whereby a framework for automatic
         approval route for deployment of funds and undertaking of new related
         activities is being proposed based on certain parameters. SMAC left the final
         discretion on the parameters, including the various financial parameters being
         proposed for eligibility of the automatic approval route, to SEBI.

                                                      Page 1 of 12
3. Objective:

    3.1. To review and rationalize the current requirement of prior SEBI approval for all
         activities and fund deployments by MIIs other than recognized clearing
         corporations under Regulation 38(2) of the SECC Regulations and Regulation
         7(c)(i) of the D&P Regulations

    3.2. The existing framework, which mandates prior approval irrespective of the
         size, nature or regulatory mandate of the activity, has led to an increased
         approval burden on MIIs.

    3.3. The proposed framework seeks to distinguish between activities that are
         related to an MII's core function and those that are unrelated and introduce a
         proportionate governance safeguards and retain mandatory prior approval for
         activities that are unrelated or not incidental to the MII's core function, thereby
         aligning regulatory oversight with actual risk.

4. Rationale for Revisiting the Current Approach:
    4.1. Increased burden for otherwise low-risk activities: The current framework
         treats all non-treasury activities identically, e.g. a minor investment of a few
         lakhs in a technology platform directly supporting market operations attracts
         the same prior approval requirement as a large investment in an entirely
         unrelated business. This may not be in line to the risk.
    4.2. Redundancy where independent oversight already exists: Certain
         activities are directed by SEBI through circulars or involve entities regulated by
         other statutory financial sector regulators (such as IFSCA, IRDAI, RBI,
         PFRDA, etc.). Requiring a separate Reg 38(2) or Reg 7(c)(i) approval in such
         cases creates an avoidable duplication of regulatory scrutiny.
    4.3. Governance-based oversight may achieve the regulatory objective more
         effectively: A framework built on MII Governing Board oversight, Governing
         Board-approved Standard Operating Procedures and periodic reporting to the
         regulator may provide equivalent oversight as compared to case-by-case
         approval by SEBI, while allowing MIIs operational flexibility within clearly
         defined guardrails and promote Ease of Doing Business.

5. Based on the proposals taken to SMAC, recommendations of the SMAC and
    further internal deliberations, following is proposed:

    5.1. The MIIs (excluding the recognized clearing corporations) may engage in
         activities whether involving deployment of funds or otherwise, without prior
         approval of SEBI (hereinafter referred to as the "automatic route"), in the
         following routes and subject to the financial limits specified at paragraph 5.1.3
         and the governance conditions specified at paragraph 5.1.4 below:

         5.1.1. Route A: activities related or incidental to the activity of the MII in the
                 securities market where

         (a) the activity is undertaken pursuant to directions of SEBI; or

                                                      Page 2 of 12
(b) the activity involves a transfer of shares of a company held by the MII or by
a subsidiary of the MII, to the MII or to another subsidiary of the MII; or
(c) the activity involves deployment of funds by an operating subsidiary of the
MII and such deployment is made exclusively from funds generated from the
subsidiary's own operations.

5.1.2. Route B: An activity that is similar in function to an activity carried on by
        the MII and is undertaken through an investee company regulated by
        another financial sector regulator in India.

Provided, SEBI has previously approved such activity and the activity remains
the same, notwithstanding that such activity is not an activity in the securities
market.

5.1.3. Financial limits
    5.1.3.1. The aggregate deployment of funds under the automatic route in a
               financial year shall not exceed the lower of
         5.1.3.1.1. five percent of the net worth of the MII as on 31 March of the
                       financial year immediately preceding the financial year in
                       which the deployment is made; and
         5.1.3.1.2. Regulatory minimum net worth.

               For this purpose, deployment of funds by the MII and by any of its
               subsidiaries shall be aggregated and reckoned against the said
               limit, irrespective of the entity making such deployment and
               irrespective of the source of the funds so deployed.

    5.1.3.2. The net worth of the MII, post the proposed deployment of funds,
               shall not fall below 1.5 times the minimum net worth prescribed by
               the Board.

               Provided that where the audited financial statements of the MII for
               the immediately preceding financial year have not been adopted as
               on the date of the proposed deployment of funds, the net worth as
               on the last day of the financial year preceding that year shall apply,
               and any deployment of funds made on that basis shall be reckoned
               against the limit computed on the basis of the audited financial
               statements of the immediately preceding financial year upon their
               adoption.

5.1.4. Governance conditions relating to the above automatic route are
      as follows:

    5.1.4.1. The sharing of logo, brand, if any, with the entity in which the
               activity is being carried out shall be at arm’s length pricing basis or
               otherwise as specifically approved by Board.

5.1.4.2.  If there is sharing of resources with the entity in which the activity
          is being carried out, MII shall, prior to sharing resources, execute
5.1.4.3.  an appropriate resource sharing agreement which shall ensure
5.1.4.4.  that such sharing is ring fenced and does not adversely impact the
5.1.4.5.  operations, financial health and other interest of the MII.
5.1.4.6.  There shall not be any conflict of interest of the new activity with
          the core functions of the MII or with the role as first level regulator.
5.1.4.7.  The MIIs shall ensure that the investee companies, their directors,
5.1.4.8.  and shareholders comply with “fit and proper criteria” at all times
          Each new activity, or each instance of deployment of funds, shall
          require prior approval of the Governing Board of the MII.
          Further, before giving the approval the governing board shall inter
          alia ensure that there is no conflict of interest and adequate
          resources including liquid net worth is maintained for technological
          upgradations, meeting regulatory obligations such as IPF, SGF,
          piloting innovative solutions in securities market, Investor
          Awareness etc.
          Every MII shall formulate Standard Operating Procedures, duly
          approved by its Governing Board, incorporating appropriate
          guidelines governing such activities and investments.
          Every MII shall submit to the Board a half-yearly report setting out
          particulars of all new activities undertaken through the automatic
          routes during the said period, including those involving deployment
          of funds, together with the basis on which each such activity has
          been treated as related or incidental to the activity of the MII, within
          thirty days from the end of the half year, in such format as may be
          specified.

6. Delegation:

    Considering that the conditions specified above are operational and detailed in
    nature, it is proposed that Regulation 38(2) of the SECC Regulations and
    Regulation 7(c)(i) of the D&P Regulations be amended to provide an enabling
    delegation to SEBI to specify such conditions by way of a circular. The proposed
    amendments to the said regulations are placed at Annexure-2 and the draft
    consolidated circular setting out the detailed conditions is placed at Annexure-3.

A. Regulation 38 of the SECC Regulations, 2018

    Utilization of profits and investments

  38. (1) The utilization of profits and investments by recognised clearing
  corporations shall be in accordance with the norms specified by the Board.
  (2) The recognized stock exchange or recognized clearing corporation shall not
  carry on any activity whether involving deployment of funds or otherwise without
  prior approval of the Board:
  Provided that prior approval of the Board shall not be required in case of treasury
  investments if such investments are as per the investment policy approved by the
  governing board of recognized stock exchange or recognized clearing corporation;
  Provided further, that the recognised stock exchange and recognised clearing
  corporation may engage in activities whether involving deployment of funds or
  otherwise that are unrelated or not incidental to its activity as a stock exchange or
  clearing corporation, as the case may be, through a separate legal entity and
  subject to approval of the Board.
  (3) An employee of a recognised stock exchange or recognised clearing
  corporation shall not simultaneously be an employee of any other company where
  the recognised stock exchange or recognised clearing corporation has invested.
  (4) A director, committee member or employee of a recognised stock exchange or
  a recognised clearing corporation shall not receive any compensation or any other
  financial benefit from the companies where the recognised stock exchange or
  recognised clearing corporation has invested, other than fees and expenses
  related to the governing board and committee meetings.

Subject: Framework for undertaking of related activities and deployment of
funds by Market Infrastructure Institutions

1. Background
    1.1. Regulation 38(2) of the Securities Contracts (Regulation) (Stock Exchanges
         and Clearing Corporations) Regulations, 2018 (SECC Regulations) and
         Regulation 7(c)(i) of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Depositories
         and Participants) Regulations, 2018 (D&P Regulations) provide that a Market
         Infrastructure Institution (MII) shall not carry on any activity whether involving
         deployment of funds or otherwise without prior approval of the Board.
    1.2. In terms of the third proviso to Regulation 38(2) of the SECC Regulations and
         the third proviso to Regulation 7(c)(i) of the D&P Regulations, a recognised
         stock exchange and a depository may engage in activities that are related or
         incidental to its activity as an MII without prior approval of the Board, subject
         to such conditions as may be specified by the Board.
    1.3. Accordingly, the conditions subject to which such activities may be undertaken
         are specified hereunder.

2. Applicability
    2.1. The provisions of this circular shall apply to recognised stock exchanges and
         depositories.

3. Activities that may be undertaken without prior approval of the Board
    3.1. The MIIs (excluding the recognized clearing corporations) may engage in
         activities whether involving deployment of funds or otherwise, without prior
         approval of SEBI (hereinafter referred to as the "automatic route"), in the
         following routes and subject to the financial limits specified at paragraph 3.2
         and the governance conditions specified at paragraph 3.3 below:

         3.1.1. Route A: activities related or incidental to the activity of the MII in the
               securities market where

         (a) the activity is undertaken pursuant to directions of SEBI; or

                                                      Page 9 of 12
     (b) the activity involves a transfer of shares of a company held by the MII or by
     a subsidiary of the MII, to the MII or to another subsidiary of the MII; or
     (c) the activity involves deployment of funds by an operating subsidiary of the
     MII and such deployment is made exclusively from funds generated from the
     subsidiary's own operations.

    3.1.2. Route B: An activity that is similar in function to an activity carried on by
          the MII and is undertaken through an investee company regulated by
          another financial sector regulator in India.

     Provided, SEBI has previously approved such activity and the activity remains
     the same, notwithstanding that such activity is not an activity in the securities
     market.

3.2. Financial limits
    3.2.1. The aggregate deployment of funds under the automatic route in a
          financial year shall not exceed the lower of
                      (a) five percent of the net worth of the MII as on 31 March of the
                      financial year immediately preceding the financial year in which
                      the deployment is made; and
                      (b) regulatory minimum net worth.

             For this purpose, deployment of funds by the MII and by any of its
             subsidiaries shall be aggregated and reckoned against the said limit,
             irrespective of the entity making such deployment.

    3.2.2. The net worth of the MII, post the proposed deployment of funds, shall
             not fall below 1.5 times the minimum net worth prescribed by the Board.

    3.2.3. For the purposes of this circular, ‘net worth’ shall have the meaning
             assigned to it in Explanation I to Regulation 14 of the SECC Regulations
             or the Explanation to Regulation 15(1) of the D&P Regulations, as the
             case may be, as on the last day of the immediately preceding financial
             year as per the audited financial statements of the MII.

             Provided that where the audited financial statements of the MII for the
             immediately preceding financial year have not been adopted as on the
             date of the proposed deployment of funds, the net worth as on the last
             day of the financial year preceding that year shall apply and any
             deployment of funds made on that basis shall be reckoned against the
             limit computed on the basis of the audited financial statements of the
             immediately preceding financial year upon their adoption.

3.3. Governance conditions relating to the above automatic route are as
     follows:

                                                Page 10 of 12
         3.3.1. The sharing of logo, brand, if any, with the entity in which the activity is
               being carried out shall be at arm’s length pricing basis or otherwise as
               specifically approved by the Board.

         3.3.2. If there is sharing of resources with the entity in which the activity is being
                 carried out, MII shall, prior to sharing resources, execute an appropriate
                 resource sharing agreement which shall ensure that such sharing is ring
                 fenced and does not adversely impact the operations, financial health
                 and other interest of the MII.

         3.3.3. There shall not be any conflict of interest of the new activity with the core
                 functions of the MII or with the role as first level regulator

         3.3.4. The MIIs shall ensure that the investee companies, their directors, and
                 shareholders comply with “fit and proper criteria” at all times

         3.3.5. Each new activity, or each instance of deployment of funds, shall require
                 prior approval of the Governing Board of the MII. Further, before giving
                 approval the governing board shall inter alia ensure that there is no
                 conflict of interest and adequate resources including liquid net worth is
                 maintained for technological upgradations, meeting regulatory
                 obligations such as IPF, SGF, piloting innovative solutions in securities
                 market, investor awareness, etc.

         3.3.6. Every MII shall formulate Standard Operating Procedures, duly approved
                 by its Governing Board, incorporating appropriate guidelines governing
                 such activities and investments.

         3.3.7. Every MII shall submit to the Board a half-yearly report setting out
                 particulars of all new activities undertaken during the said period,
                 including those involving deployment of funds through the automatic
                 route, together with the basis on which each such activity has been
                 treated as related or incidental to the activity of the MII, within thirty days
                 from the end of the half year, in such format as may be specified.

4. Any activity that does not satisfy the conditions specified in this circular shall
    require prior approval of the Board.
