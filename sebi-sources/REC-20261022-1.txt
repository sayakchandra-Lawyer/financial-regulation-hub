Subject: Margin Waiver on Subsequent Buy/Sell Transactions Post Early Pay-In
(EPI) in Cash Segment

1. Exchanges/Clearing Corporations (CCs) in consultation with SEBI, have issued
     various circulars/ guidelines on Margin collection and Reporting for the cases
     where the client has completed Early Pay-in of securities.

2. The said circulars/ guidelines inter-alia stipulate that in respect of sale of shares
     by a client for which EPI request via Block mechanism has been accepted, the
     same may be considered as margin collected towards peak margin for the said
     sale transaction. Further, in respect of sale of shares by a client for which EPI
     request via Block mechanism has been accepted and credit entry is posted of the
     sale value of the shares in the ledger account of the client, EPI value may be
     considered as margin collected towards subsequent margin requirement of the
     client. The sale value up to 100% of such securities (EPI value) shall be available
     as Margin for other positions across all the segments.

3. In this context, taking into account the proposals received from the Broker ISF,
     recommendations of Risk Management Review Committee (RMRC) of SEBI and
     internal deliberations, it is decided that where a client has a net sell obligation in a
     settlement; and the EPI request for the sold securities has been successfully
     accepted by the CC, the CC shall, on a real-time basis, not collect margin from the
     Trading Member (TM)/ Clearing Member (CM) on the subsequent buy/sell
     positions of the client, up to the amount of such accepted EPI.

4. The aforesaid facility shall be implemented in the following manner:

       4.1. Upon EPI accepted, CC on trade date shall compute the available value of
              EPI which shall be lower of the value of EPI done or value of securities sold.

      4.2. Upon the client taking position basis the EPI on same trade date in cash
             segment, CC shall check the availability of client margin after reducing the
             available value of EPI.

      4.3. If client squares off the sale position in securities for which client has availed
             EPI benefit, the value of EPI shall be removed and any shortfall in margins
             at client level due to such removal of EPI value, shall be blocked from the
             proprietary collateral of TM/CM by the CC. This condition will not be
             applicable to Trade for trade (TFT) securities as such transactions are
             settled on gross basis.

5. Additionally, TMs shall be directed to ensure that their risk management allows
    the client to square off the position for which client has given EPI or take any new
    position, only if the requisite margins of the client are available with the TM.

6. Further, CCs are directed to:

      6.1. Make amendments to the relevant bye-laws, rules and regulations for the
             implementation of the above framework, as may be applicable/necessary;

      6.2. Carry out system changes, if any, to implement the above framework;

      6.3. Disseminate the provisions of this circular on their website;

7. The provisions of this circular shall come into force with effect from four months
    from the date of issuance of this circular.

8. This circular is being issued in exercise of powers conferred under Section 11(1)
    of the Securities and Exchange Board of India Act, 1992 read with Regulation 51
    of the Securities Contracts (Regulation) (Stock Exchanges and Clearing
    Corporations) Regulations, 2018 to protect the interest of investors in securities
    and to promote development of, and to regulate securities market.

9. This circular is available on SEBI website at www.sebi.gov.in at “Legal Framework
    - Circulars.”

10. The circular has been issued with the approval of the competent authority.
