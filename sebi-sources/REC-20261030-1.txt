Subject: Extension of timeline with respect to compliance of Digital
Accessibility Circulars

Dear Sir/ Madam,

1. SEBI has issued circulars on ‘Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016 and
    rules made thereunder for mandatory compliance by all Regulated Entities’.

2. SEBI is in receipt of representations from Regulated Entities (REs) requesting an
    extension of timeline to submit their compliance of provisions of the aforesaid
    circulars.

         Page 1 of 2
3. Based on the representations received and review of the status of compliance of
    REs, the extension is granted for “Appointment of IAAP Auditor, Conduct of
    Accessibility Audit for the digital platforms and Remediation of findings from the
    Audit” by January 31, 2027 in order to ensure compliance.

4. All other provisions of the aforementioned circulars shall remain unchanged and
    shall be complied by REs.

5. This Circular is being issued in exercise of powers conferred under Section 11 (1)
    of the Securities and Exchange Board of India Act, 1992, to protect the interest of
    investors in securities and to promote the development of, and to regulate the
    securities market.

6. This Circular is available on SEBI website at www.sebi.gov.in under the category:
    “Legal” and drop “Circulars”.

7. This Circular is issued with the approval of Competent Authority.
