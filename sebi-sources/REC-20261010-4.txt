की पजष्ट करिे िाली एिंेसी” (पास्ट्ट ररस्ट्क एंड ररटिड िेररदफकेिि एिेंसी) द्वारा दी गई रेटटंग या रंैककंग का ही इस्ट्तेमाल करिे
की अिमजत होगी, ककंत इस संबंध मंे िीचे दी हुई ितों का पालि तो करिा ही होगा-

(क) दी गई रेटटंग या रंैककंग का आजखर मतलब क्या है और िह दकस तरह से दी गई है, इसके बारे में या तो जिज्ञापि में ही
साफ-साफ जिक्र कर ददया िाए या दफर उस जिज्ञापि में “पास्ट्ट ररस्ट्क एंड ररटिड िेररदफकेिि एिेंसी” की िेबसाइट या
जिजियामक (रेग्यूलेटरी) दायरे में आिे िाली एंरटटी की िेबसाइट के बारे मंे बता ददया िाए;

(ख) जिज्ञापि में यह भी जिक्र होिा चाजहए दक जिजियामक (रेग्यूलेटरी) दायरे मंे आिे िाली दकसी एंरटटी जििेष की सेिाएँ
लेिे या ि लेिे का फैसला केिल रेटटंग या रंैककंग के आधार पर ही ि जलया िाए;

(ग) ऐसी रेटटंग या रंैककंग “पास्ट्ट ररस्ट्क एंड ररटिड िेररदफकेिि एिंेसी” द्वारा करिाए गए अध्ययि या सिेक्षण (सिे) पर
आधाररत होिी चाजहए, जिसमंे संबंजधत श्रेणी के सभी माकेट पार्टडजसपंेर्टस (बािार से िडे लोग) िाजमल हों, तादक तलिा
की िा सके और जिष्पक्षता भी सजिजित की िा सके;

(घ) उि दूसरी ितों का भी पालि करिा होगा, िो बोडड द्वारा समय-समय पर जिधाडररत की िाएँ ।

           Definitions
           16G. For the purposes of this Chapter, unless the context otherwise requires: —

           (f) who has been a winner or runners-up in any prominent or popular competitive program aired on TV or
           OTT platform or any prominent personality who has gone through a series of qualifying rounds (for
           elimination of competitors) which may be known as qualifying round, quarter finals, semi-finals and finals or
           by any other name or;
           (g) who is a virtual influencer (fictional computer generated ‘people’ or avatars who have the realistic
           characteristics, features and personalities of humans) that bears influence on their audience or followers or;
           (h) who in the view of the Board or the supervisory body recognised by the Board for the regulated entity is
           capable of influencing the opinion of viewers of the advertisement:

           Provided that the term “celebrity” shall not include any founder, promoter, director, key managerial personnel

Applicability
16H. The provisions of this chapter shall be applicable for all advertisements issued by the regulated entity in

16I. All advertisements shall contain the following information:
(a) basic details such as name of the regulated entity, SEBI registration number and logo of the regulated

(b) disclaimers, as specified by the Board from time to time shall be displayed or communicated in the

Communications not considered as advertisement
16J. (1) Subject to sub-regulation (2), the following communications shall not be considered as
'advertisement':
(a) communication that is educational or informational in nature and is meant to impart financial knowledge

       or information or training. For such communication, the regulated entity shall comply with the following:
        (i) branding of the entity in such communications shall be minimal and incidental;
        (ii) such communication shall not contain any promotional call-to-action or attempt to influence or

            induce a purchase decision for a specific product or service provided by the regulated entity;

    (c) list of available products or services, including lists provided on regulated entity's website or app, which

    (e) communication in response to a request by a client or prospective client or investor for information about

    (f) communication providing basic factual information about regulated entity (such as name, brand logo,

    (h) greetings, condolences or congratulatory messages;

    (i) public communications inviting applications for recruitment or availing third party services provided it
           does not target clients or prospective clients or investors;

    (j) sponsorship for TV shows or talk shows and events communicating generic information about the

    (k) surveys issued by the regulated entities to their registered clients or investors to take feedback; and

    (2) Any communication which promotes products or services or solicits interest to avail any product or service

    Celebrity Endorsements

    16K. (1) The celebrities shall be allowed to endorse the regulated entity at the brand level or the entity level.

    (2) Such endorsements may include the name of products or services offered by a regulated entity but may not

    (3) Any advertisement featuring celebrity shall be issued only with the prior approval from supervisory
    body or the Board (if no such body has been specified by the Board for regulated entity).

    (4) Expenses incurred towards celebrity endorsements shall not be passed on to the clients or charged to
    the scheme of the mutual fund.

    (5) Such advertisements shall include the relevant disclaimers as may be specified.

    General Obligations

    16L. (1)  The regulated entity shall ensure compliance with the following:

    (a) advertisements shall be true, fair, accurate, complete and unambiguous;

    (b) advertisements shall not be so designed as likely to be misunderstood or likely to disguise the
    significance of any statement;

    (c) advertisements shall not be so framed as to exploit the lack of experience or knowledge of the

Prohibition on use of dark patterns
16M. The regulated entity shall refrain from engaging in a dark pattern as specified in Annexure I of the
Guidelines for Prevention and Regulation of Dark Patterns, 2023 issued by the Central Consumer Protection
Authority.

Use of ratings or rankings in advertisements
16N. The regulated entity shall be permitted to use only the ratings or rankings assigned by a Past Risk and
Return Verification Agency in the advertisement which subject to the following conditions -
(a) the ratings or rankings shall be properly explained either in the advertisement itself or by including details
of the Past Risk and Return Verification Agency website or regulated entity’s website where an investor will
be able to obtain further information about the meaning and methodology of such ratings or rankings;
(b) the advertisement should also state that ratings or rankings are only one of the factors to be taken into
account when deciding whether to avail the services of a specific regulated entity or not;
(c) such ratings or rankings shall emanate from a study or survey by Past Risk and Return Verification Agency
covering all the relevant market participants within the concerned category to ensure comparability and
objectivity;
(d) any other conditions which may be specified by the Board from time to time.

Prohibitions in the advertisement
16O. An advertisement shall not contain:
(a) any content, which is false, misleading, luring, biased, deceptive, exaggerated, ambiguous or based on

      assumptions or projections or includes any testimonials;
14     THE GAZETTE OF INDIA : EXTRAORDINARY  [PART III—SEC.4]

       (b) anything that, directly or indirectly, promises or gives assurance of any guaranteed or risk free or
             indicative returns to prospective or existing clients;

       (c) any direct or indirect promise or assurance of any fixed returns (except in case of Online Bond Platform
             Providers or any other entity in a form and manner as may be specified by the Board);

       (d) any content that, directly or indirectly, disparages or discredits other regulated entities, their products or
             services or makes unfair comparisons with such entities or imitates the advertisement content of other
             regulated entities;

       (e) any content that compares one product or asset class with other product(s) or asset class(es) of a different
             nature giving a misleading impression;

       (f) any content designed to exploit the lack of experience or knowledge of the investors or any slogan that
             is exaggerated or unwarranted or inconsistent with or unrelated to the nature and risk and return profile
             of the products or services being advertised;

       (g) logos, images (e.g. building imagery) or symbols of the Board or Market Infrastructure Institutions (MIIs)
             or any other organisation of such repute, unless specifically permitted;

       (h) any content that implies that past performance may recur or makes any unwarranted claim;

       (i) any reference to past performance in respect of the products or services of regulated entities unless the
             past performance is communicated in the manner as specified by Board from time to time;

       (j) any content which is indecent, vulgar or offensive;

       (k) adjectives such as high yield, high rated, high returns and synonyms of such words, which are vague and
             general without any basis;

       (l) anything, which is prohibited for publication or communication under the law;

       (m) any other communication as may be specified by the Board or supervisory body in consultation with the
             Board, from time to time.

       Reporting requirements

       16P. The reporting requirements for an advertisement issued by a regulated entity shall be applicable as
       specified by the Board from time to time.

       Action for non-compliance

       16Q. (1) The Board may, in case of violation of the provisions of this chapter or any circular issued thereunder,
       take such action as it may deem fit including action under Chapter V of these regulations or any applicable
       provisions of the relevant Act or any other regulations issued by the Board.

       (2) The Board may also direct the supervisory bodies to take appropriate action for violations specified under
       sub-regulation (1).
