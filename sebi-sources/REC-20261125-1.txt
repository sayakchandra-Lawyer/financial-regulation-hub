Subject: Half-yearly review of ‘Significant Indices’ under SEBI (Index Providers)
Regulations, 2024

    1. SEBI, vide Circular No. HO/47/17/12(8)2025-MRD-POD2 dated May 05, 2026
         (hereinafter “the said circular”), specified that a Benchmark or Index shall be
         considered as ‘Significant Indices’ if the daily average cumulative Asset Under
         Management (AUM) tracking the Benchmark or Index across schemes of
         Mutual Fund(s) exceeds ₹20,000 Crore for each of the past six months, ending
         on 30th June and 31st December each year.

2. In terms of the aforesaid provisions, the half-yearly review of the list of
    ‘Significant Indices’ has been undertaken for the period from January 01, 2026
    to June 30, 2026. Accordingly, the list of Significant Indices specified in the
    Annexure-A of the said circular is unchanged.

3. In the half-yearly review for the period from January 01, 2026 to June 30, 2026,
    the indices at Sr. No. 2 viz. BSE 200 and Sr. No. 33 viz. Nifty CPSE of
    Annexure-A of the said circular, did not satisfy the threshold limit for ‘Significant
    Indices’ in terms of Para 4 of the said circular. However, these indices are being
    retained in the list in terms of Para 5 of the said circular.
