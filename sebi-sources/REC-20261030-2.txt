Subject: Rationalisation of periodicity of KYC review for FPIs
1. Para 5 of Part B of the Master Circular for FPIs, DDPs and Eligible Foreign

    Investors dated May 30, 2024, as amended from time to time (“Master Circular”),
    provides for KYC review of FPIs annually or every three years, depending upon
    the jurisdiction, category and regulated status of the FPI. Separately, Para 4 of Part
    A of the Master Circular requires an FPI (other than FPIs investing only in
    Government Securities, hereinafter referred as ‘GS-FPIs’) to intimate changes in
    previously furnished information or submit a no-change declaration at the time of
    continuance of registration.

2. With a view to rationalise these requirements, reduce compliance burden and
    promote Ease of Doing Business, it has been decided:
    2.1. To increase the periodicity of KYC review of FPIs. The provisions related to
         periodicity of KYC review applicable to GS-FPIs and SWAGAT-FI FPIs to
         remain unchanged.
    2.2. To dispense with the requirements relating to intimation of change in
         information at the time of continuance of registration.

3. Accordingly, the Master Circular shall stand modified as under:
    3.1. Para 4 of Part A shall be modified as follows:

                  (a) Sub-paragraph (i) shall be substituted as under:

                          “i. FPI who wish to continue with their registration for the
                          subsequent block of three years (10 years in case of SWAGAT-
                          FI) shall pay the fees to their DDP.”

                 (b) Sub-paragraph (ii) shall be omitted.

4. Depositories, Custodians and DDPs shall make necessary changes in their
    systems and processes to give effect to the provisions of this circular, and shall
    bring the same to the notice of FPIs concerned.

5. The provisions of this circular shall come into force with effect from December 01,
    2026.

6. This Circular is issued in exercise of the powers conferred under Section 11(1) of
    the Securities and Exchange Board of India Act, 1992 read with Regulation 44 of
    SEBI (Foreign Portfolio Investors) Regulations, 2019 to protect the interest of
    investors in securities and to promote the development of, and to regulate the
    securities market.
