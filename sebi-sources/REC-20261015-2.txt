        SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE BOARD OF INDIA (ALTERNATIVE INVESTMENT FUNDS)
                                        (THIRD AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 2026

No. SEBI/LAD-NRO/GN/2026/326.— In exercise of the powers conferred by Section 30(1) read with Section 11(1),
Section 11(2)(ba), Section 11(2)(c), Section12(1) and Section12(1B) of the Securities and Exchange Board of India
Act, 1992 (15 of 1992), the Board hereby makes the following regulations to further amend the Securities and
Exchange Board of India (Alternative Investment Funds) Regulations, 2012, namely, –

     1. These Regulations may be called the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Alternative Investment Funds)
          (Third Amendment) Regulations, 2026.

     2. They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette.

     3. In the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Alternative Investment Funds) Regulations, 2012, –

              I. In regulation 2(1)(ab),

                                       i. the word and symbol “who,” appearing after the words “certificate of
                                           accreditation by an accreditation agency” shall be omitted;

                                      ii. after the words “certificate of accreditation by an accreditation agency”, the words
                                           and symbols “or who is accredited by a specified SEBI registered/regulated entity
                                           as an accredited investor, as per the following eligibility conditions, in a manner
                                           as may be specified by the Board from time to time, -” shall be inserted;

                                     iii. in sub-clause (i), in item (C), the symbol “.” shall be substituted with the word and
                                           symbol “; or”;

                                     iv. in sub-clause (i), after item (C), a new item (D) shall be inserted, namely, -

                                           “(D) securities market exposure of at least five crore rupees, as specified by the
                                           Board.”;

                                      v. sub-clause (ii) shall be substituted with the following, namely, -

                                           “(ii) in case of a limited liability partnership set up under the Limited Liability
                                           Partnership Act, 2008, has net worth of at least fifty crore rupees or securities
                                           market exposure of at least twenty crore rupees, as specified by the Board, or each
                                           partner independently meets the eligibility criteria for accreditation under these
                                           regulations;”;

                                     vi. the existing sub-clauses (iii) and (iv) shall be renumbered as (iv) and (v)
                                           respectively;

                                     vii. after sub-clause (ii), a new sub-clause (iii) shall be inserted, namely, -

                                           “(iii) in case of a body corporate other than a limited liability partnership, has net
                                           worth of at least fifty crore rupees or securities market exposure of at least twenty
                                           crore rupees, as specified by the Board;”

                                    viii. in sub-clause (iv) so renumbered, after the words “net worth of at least fifty crore
                                           rupees”, the words “or securities market exposure of at least twenty crore rupees,
                                           as specified by the Board” shall be inserted;

                                     ix. in sub-clause (v) so renumbered, after the words “eligibility criteria for
                                           accreditation”, the words “under these regulations” shall be inserted;

                                      x. in the proviso, the words and symbol “Category I foreign portfolio investors,
                                           sovereign wealth funds and” shall be omitted;

                                     xi. in the proviso, after the words “multilateral agencies” the words, symbols and
                                           numbers “, Persons Resident Outside India as defined under Foreign Exchange
                                           Management Act, 1999” shall be inserted;

                                     xii. in the proviso, the words “and may not be required to obtain a certificate of
                                           accreditation” shall be omitted.

II. regulation 25 shall be substituted with the following, namely, -

        “25. (1) All claims, differences or disputes between investors and the Alternative Investment Fund
        or the Manager arising out of or in relation to the activities of the Alternative Investment Fund or the
        Manager in the securities market shall be submitted to a dispute resolution mechanism that includes
        mediation and/or conciliation and/or arbitration, in accordance with the procedure specified by the
        Board.

        (2) Where claims, differences or disputes between investors and the Alternative Investment Fund or
        the Manager arise under sub-regulation (1), then any loss or damage or expenses incurred by the
        Manager or officers of the Manager, including those in relation to resolution of claims or disputes of
        investors, shall not be met out of property of the Alternative Investment Fund.”
