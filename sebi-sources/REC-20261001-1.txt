Subject: Display of “investor awareness message(s)” by stock brokers on their
             trading apps and websites, under Project Jagrook

1. SEBI vide circular no. SEBI/HO/MIRSD/MIRSD-PoD-1/P/CIR/2023/73 dated May
    19, 2023, inter-alia, mandated all stock brokers to display ‘Risk disclosures’ (given
    at Annexure-I of the said circular) to all their clients in the manner as specified
    therein (herein after referred to as ‘Risk disclosures’).

2. In continuation to the aforementioned circular, as part of Project Jagrook- SEBI's
    nationwide investor awareness initiative and the upcoming World Investor Week
    2026, SEBI has decided to introduce display of investor-centric messages by stock
    brokers, for the benefit, safety, and education of investors.

3. Accordingly, all stock brokers shall be required to prominently display the “investor
    awareness message(s)”, as shared by SEBI, on their websites and trading apps,
    in the manner prescribed.

4. The display of such messages shall be undertaken by stock brokers, as given
    below:

                  Page 1 of 3
    4.1 Websites of Stock Brokers:
         a. During October 5, 2026 to October 31, 2026, all stock brokers shall display
             the “investor awareness message(s)” shared by SEBI and the ‘Risk
             disclosures’ on their websites.

         b. From November 1, 2026 onwards, the stock brokers shall display the
             specified “investor awareness message(s)” on the landing page of their
             websites.

    4.2 Trading apps of Stock Brokers:
         a. During October 5, 2026 to October 31, 2026, all stock brokers may display
             the “investor awareness message(s)” shared by SEBI, on their trading apps,
             if any, on a voluntary basis. During the said time period, if on any day, the
             stock broker chooses to display the specified “investor awareness
             message(s)” on its trading app, display of the ‘Risk disclosures’ shall be
             optional.

         b. From November 1, 2026 onwards, the stock brokers shall mandatorily
             display the specified “investor awareness message(s)” and ‘Risk
             disclosures’, on alternate days, on their trading apps, if any. The display of
             such “investor awareness message(s)” should be on the landing page of
             such trading app of the stock broker.

    4.3 The “investor awareness message(s)” shared by SEBI may be modified from
         time to time.

5. Stock Exchanges and Depositories are directed to:

    5.1 . bring the provisions of this circular to the notice of their members/ participants,
          as applicable, and disseminate the same on their websites.

    5.2 . display the “investor awareness message(s)” shared by SEBI, as it is, on their

    5.3 . make necessary amendments to the relevant bye-laws, rules and regulations

          for the implementation of this circular; and
6. All other provisions of SEBI Circular SEBI/HO/MIRSD/MIRSD-PoD-

     1/P/CIR/2023/73 dated May 19, 2023, shall remain unchanged.
