Subject: Framework for net settlement of funds for transactions undertaken by
mutual fund schemes in cash market

1. SEBI Master Circular for Stock Exchanges and Clearing Corporations dated December
     30, 2024, inter-alia, stipulates that no institutional investor shall be allowed to do day
     trading, i.e., square off their transactions intra-day. Accordingly, transactions
     undertaken by institutional investors in the cash market are required to be delivery-
     backed and settled on gross basis at the custodian or client level.

2. SEBI, vide Circular dated April 24, 2026, permitted net settlement of funds for
     outright transactions undertaken by Foreign Portfolio Investors in the cash market
     while retaining gross settlement of securities, to be implemented by December 31,
     2026.

3. With a view to enhance operational efficiency, reduce temporary funding
     requirements and improving settlement efficiency for mutual fund schemes, it has
     been decided to permit net settlement of funds, at the level of an individual mutual
     fund scheme, for outright transactions undertaken in the cash segment of stock
     exchanges, subject to the conditions specified in this circular. Settlement of securities
     shall continue to be on gross basis.

4. For the purpose of this circular, “outright transaction” shall mean either a purchase
     transaction or a sale transaction, but not both, in a security in the same settlement
     cycle undertaken by a mutual fund scheme.

5. Accordingly, where a mutual fund scheme has both purchase and sale transactions in
     the same security within the same settlement cycle, such transactions in that security
     shall be treated as non-outright transactions for that settlement cycle and shall not
     be eligible for net settlement of funds under this circular.

6. The net settlement framework shall not apply to transactions such as derivative
     transactions, securities lending and borrowing transactions, short sale-related
     transactions and any other transaction not expressly covered under this circular.

Framework for net settlement of funds

7. Scheme-level netting:

   7.1. Netting of funds shall be permitted only within the same mutual fund scheme and
          within the same settlement cycle.

   7.2. No netting shall be permitted across different schemes of the same mutual fund.

   7.3. No adjustment of obligations shall be permitted between two or more schemes
          or portfolios.

   7.4. The AMC and custodian shall ensure that the netting mechanism does not affect
          scheme-wise accounting, NAV computation, asset allocation, investment limits
          or unit-holder interest.

8. Treatment of residual obligations:

   8.1. In case the value of outright sale transactions is less than the value of outright
          purchase transactions, the residual fund obligation shall be funded by the
          concerned mutual fund scheme.

   8.2. In case the value of outright sale transactions exceeds the value of outright
          purchase transactions, such excess outright sale proceeds shall not be adjusted
          against non-outright purchase obligations.

   8.3. Non-outright purchase obligations shall continue to be funded separately on
          gross basis.

   8.4. An illustration of obligations of mutual fund scheme under current practice and
          under net settlement framework is placed at Annexure.

9. Securities settlement, STT and stamp duty

   9.1. Settlement of securities shall continue to be carried out on gross basis between
          the mutual fund scheme and the custodian.

   9.2. Securities Transaction Tax and stamp duty shall continue to be charged on
          delivery basis, as applicable.

   9.3. The proposed netting shall not alter the delivery-backed nature of institutional
          trades in the cash market.

10. Operational safeguards

   10.1. AMCs and custodians shall ensure that adequate systems and controls are put in
          place for scheme-wise identification of outright purchase and sale transactions,
          non-outright transactions, gross fund obligations, net fund obligations and
          securities pay-in and pay-out obligations.

        10.2. The custodian shall maintain a clear audit trail of gross obligations, netted fund
                obligations and excluded non-outright transactions.

        10.3. The AMC and custodian shall ensure that no cross-scheme adjustment or inter-
                scheme funding takes place under this framework.

        10.4. Trustees shall review the implementation of this framework and ensure that the

11. Implementation standards

 11.1. Implementation standards for this framework shall be formulated by AMFI in
        consultation with recognised Clearing Corporations, recognised Stock Exchanges,
        custodians and other relevant stakeholders.

 11.2. The implementation standards shall, inter-alia, specify:

          11.2.1. file formats and reporting requirements;

          11.2.2. timelines for confirmation and settlement;

          11.2.3. reconciliation mechanism;

          11.2.4. audit trail requirements;

          11.2.5. treatment of rejected, unconfirmed or modified trades, including the
                    consequent treatment of fund obligations and shortfalls, if any;

          11.2.6. cut-off timelines, finalisation of eligible net obligations and exception
                    handling process;

          11.2.7. methodology for determination of the same settlement cycle, including
                    treatment of variations in settlement schedules, where applicable.

12. Role of recognised Clearing Corporations and Stock Exchanges

 12.1. Recognised Clearing Corporations and recognised Stock Exchanges shall make
        necessary changes in their systems, processes, files and reporting formats to facilitate
        the implementation of this framework.

 12.2. The manner of settlement between custodians and Clearing Corporations shall remain
        unchanged.

 12.3. Clearing Corporations shall continue to settle obligations at the clearing
        member/custodian level in accordance with the extant settlement framework.

13. System readiness

 13.1. AMCs, custodians, stock brokers, recognised Stock Exchanges and recognised Clearing
        Corporations are advised to make necessary changes in their systems and processes
        to give effect to the provisions of this circular.

 13.2. AMCs and custodians shall jointly confirm system readiness before implementation.

Illustration of obligations of mutual fund scheme under current practice and under
net settlement framework

1. Consider a mutual fund scheme which, on a particular day, has undertaken the
    following trades in the cash market:

SECURITY  BUY VALUE (₹)                   SELL VALUE (₹)           TREATMENT
      A         1,000                             Nil            Outright purchase
      B         1,000
      C           Nil                            2,000              Non-outright
                                                 2,000              Outright sale

2. Current practice — gross settlement: Under the current practice, all fund
    obligations of the mutual fund scheme are settled on gross basis with the custodian.

PARTICULARS                               PAY-IN (₹)             PAY-OUT (₹)
     Funds                                   2,000                    4,000

3. Net settlement of funds: Under the net settlement framework,

3.1. Security A is an outright purchase.

3.2. Security C is an outright sale.

3.3. Security B has both buy and sell transactions and shall be excluded from netting.

3.4. Outright purchase in A and outright sale in C shall be netted for fund settlement.

3.5. Security settlement shall continue on gross basis.

PARTICULARS                               PAY-IN ₹               PAY-OUT ₹
     Funds                                  1,000                   3,000

4. The Mutual Fund scheme’s funding requirement is reduced to the extent of netting
    permitted for outright transactions. However, obligations relating to Security B
    continue to be settled on gross basis as it is a non-outright transaction.
