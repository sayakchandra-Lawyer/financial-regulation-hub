OBJECTIVE
1. The objective of this consultation paper is to seek comments / views / suggestions

    from the public and other stakeholders on the recommendations of the Working
    Group (“Working Group”) constituted for review of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and
    Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“LODR Regulations”).

BACKGROUND AND CONSTITUTION OF THE WORKING GROUP

2. LODR Regulations, were published in the Gazette of India on September 2, 2015.
    The objective of bringing LODR Regulations, was to consolidate, streamline, and
    harmonize the various existing listing agreements into a single, unified framework
    and strengthen corporate governance, transparency, and protection of the interests
    of investors.

3. Since 2015, LODR Regulations have been periodically amended, which may have
    led to various interpretation issues arising out of multiple amendments.

4. Accordingly, the Working Group, chaired by Shri V S Sundaresan, Former Executive
    Director, SEBI, on the review of LODR Regulations was constituted with an aim to
    streamline the regulations, simplify the structure and language, remove ambiguities
    and redundancy, etc.

5. The Working Group has submitted its first report. The report consists of three
    chapters:

    5.1. Chapter I of the report deals with recommendations relating to monitoring and
         disclosure of utilization of issue proceeds;

    5.2. Chapter II of the report deals with recommendations relating to review of penal
         provisions and actions by stock exchanges (SEs) for non-compliance with SEBI
         regulations by listed companies including standard operating procedure (SOP)
         for action by stock exchange.

    5.3. Chapter III of the report deals with recommendations relating to clarifications on
         Related Party Transaction (RPT) Provisions under the LODR Regulations.
6. Subsequently, the first report of the Working group was placed for consideration
    before the Advisory Committee on Listing Obligations and Disclosures (“ACLOD”)
    of SEBI. The proposals of the Working Group were also deliberated internally by
    SEBI.

7. The proposals in this Consultation Paper pertain to the Chapter I of the report on
    review of the framework for monitoring and disclosure of utilization of issue proceeds.

RECOMMENDATIONS OF THE WORKING GROUP

8. The recommendations of the Working Group in Chapter I recommendations relating
    to monitoring and disclosure of utilization of issue proceeds, can be accessed at

     https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/commondocs/oct-
     2026/working_group_report_lodr_p.pdf

9. The recommendations of the Working Group in Chapter I includes the following:

    8.1 Empowering monitoring agency to directly submit its report to Stock Exchanges
           within prescribed timelines.

    8.2 In the event of non-cooperation by listed entity, during the process of monitoring,
           the report directly submitted by monitoring agency to Stock Exchanges shall be
           based on best available information and accompanied by "Statement of non-
           cooperation”.

    8.3 Levy of fine by stock exchanges, in case of non-cooperation by listed entity
           reported by monitoring agency.

    8.4 In case of termination of agreement before hundred percent utilization of the
           issue proceeds, both the listed entity and existing monitoring agency to ensure
           timely submission of monitoring agency report to Stock Exchanges during the
           transition period.
    8.5 Harmonization between the provisions of SEBI (Issue of Capital and Disclosure
           Requirements) Regulation, 2018 (“ICDR Regulations”) and LODR Regulations
           with respect to Monitoring and Disclosure of Utilization of issue proceeds, and
           certain other consequential amendments.

    8.6 Mandatory appointment of monitoring agency, for all issuances irrespective of
           the size of the issue (excluding size of offer for sale).

    8.7 Clarification with respect to monitoring of gross issue proceeds.

    8.8 Modification(s) in the existing format of report of monitoring agency.

    8.9 Deletion of sub-regulation (5) of Regulation 262 of the ICDR Regulations and
           Modification in Regulation 262(6) of the ICDR Regulations.

    8.10 Alignment of terminologies under Regulation 32 of the LODR Regulations and
           other consequential amendments.

    8.11 Regulation 32 of the LODR Regulations to deal with ‘Statement of utilization
           including variation(s)’, if any and to be made applicable to listed entities which
           are exempt from appointment of Monitoring Agency under the relevant
           provisions of the ICDR Regulations.

    8.12 Insertion of separate Regulation 32A under LODR Regulations, dealing with
           monitoring agency report related aspects.

    8.13 Transitory Provisions for implementation of the amended provisions under
           ICDR Regulations and LODR Regulations.
