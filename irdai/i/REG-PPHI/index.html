Key concepts

  • Customer Information Sheet
  • Electronic policies by default
  • Free look period
  • Advertisements (Part B)
  • Places of business
  • Outsourcing (material if over 5% of outsourcing spend)

Key obligations

  • Board policy on protecting policyholders' interests, reviewed annually
  • give the prospectus, benefit illustration and Customer Information Sheet; issue policies electronically by default
  • regulation 17: refunds and claims paid only to the policyholder's bank account
  • settle claims within the Board-approved turnaround times and ask for all documents at one go
  • regulations 27 to 29: distributors' advertisements need the insurer's prior written approval
  • an Outsourcing Committee of the CRO, CFO and Chief of Operations oversees outsourcing; records stay in India

Deadlines and cycles

  • free look period of 30 days for life policies and for new individual health policies of a year or more
  • nomination fee and assignment acknowledgement fee at most Rs 100

General consequences under the statute. Insurance Act, 1938: IRDAI may issue directions, including disgorgement of wrongful gains (s. 34). An insurer or intermediary that fails to file a return, comply with IRDAI directions or maintain solvency is liable to up to Rs 1 lakh a day, capped at Rs 10 crore (s. 102). Registration can be suspended or cancelled (s. 3 for insurers, s. 42D for intermediaries). Penalty cases follow the IRDAI Penalties Regulations 2026 and orders are published within 30 days (s. 105E).
