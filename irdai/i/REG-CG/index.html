Key concepts

  • At least three independent directors
  • CEO is a whole-time director
  • Prior IRDAI approval of the Board Chairperson (private insurers)
  • Board committees: Audit, Nomination and Remuneration, Risk Management, Policyholder Protection and Claims Monitoring, Investment; CSR where the Companies Act requires it; With Profits for life insurers with participating business
  • Stewardship policy
  • ESG and climate risk framework

Key obligations

  • regulation 4(2): tell IRDAI when independent directors fall below the minimum and fill the vacancy by the next Board meeting or within three months, whichever is later
  • regulation 5(7): KMPs may not combine a business and a control function, or two control functions
  • regulation 5(8): Board policy on related party transactions, reviewed annually
  • regulation 5(11): succession plan for directors and KMPs, reviewed annually
  • regulation 7: Board-approved remuneration policy for the Chairperson, non-executive directors and KMPs
  • regulation 8: at least two joint statutory auditors
  • regulation 10: annual compliance report and CCO certificate

Deadlines and cycles

  • regulation 4(2): reasons for an independent director's exit sent to IRDAI within 30 days
  • regulation 4(2): independent director vacancy filled by the next Board meeting or three months
  • regulation 10(2): compliance report filed annually

General consequences under the statute. Insurance Act, 1938: IRDAI may issue directions, including disgorgement of wrongful gains (s. 34). An insurer or intermediary that fails to file a return, comply with IRDAI directions or maintain solvency is liable to up to Rs 1 lakh a day, capped at Rs 10 crore (s. 102). Registration can be suspended or cancelled (s. 3 for insurers, s. 42D for intermediaries). Penalty cases follow the IRDAI Penalties Regulations 2026 and orders are published within 30 days (s. 105E).
