Key concepts

  • Appointed Actuary
  • Solvency: control level 1.50
  • Investment of policyholder and shareholder funds
  • Financial statements under Ind AS (Schedule IIA, from 01-04-2026)
  • Actuary Report

Key obligations

  • regulation 6A: an insurer not ready for Ind AS could seek one year's forbearance; requests were due by 30-04-2026 with a Board-approved action plan
  • regulation 6: prepare financial statements in the prescribed form
  • Schedule I: appoint an Appointed Actuary under s. 12A of the Insurance Act

Deadlines and cycles

  • regulation 6A: forbearance request by 30-04-2026; forbearance lasts one year

General consequences under the statute. Insurance Act, 1938: IRDAI may issue directions, including disgorgement of wrongful gains (s. 34). An insurer or intermediary that fails to file a return, comply with IRDAI directions or maintain solvency is liable to up to Rs 1 lakh a day, capped at Rs 10 crore (s. 102). Registration can be suspended or cancelled (s. 3 for insurers, s. 42D for intermediaries). Penalty cases follow the IRDAI Penalties Regulations 2026 and orders are published within 30 days (s. 105E).
