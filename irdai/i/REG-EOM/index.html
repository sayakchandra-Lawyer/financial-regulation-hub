Key obligations

  • Board-approved policies on expenses of management and on commission
  • regulation 5: annual business plan with projected expense ratios
  • regulation 8: life insurers stay within the segment-wise limits
  • regulation 10: extra allowance of up to 5% of the allowable expenses of management (not of premium) for insurtech and insurance awareness

Deadlines and cycles

  • limits measured each financial year

If you breach it

  • Insurance Act s. 40(3): commission paid outside the rules: penalty up to Rs 1 lakh

General consequences under the statute. Insurance Act, 1938: IRDAI may issue directions, including disgorgement of wrongful gains (s. 34). An insurer or intermediary that fails to file a return, comply with IRDAI directions or maintain solvency is liable to up to Rs 1 lakh a day, capped at Rs 10 crore (s. 102). Registration can be suspended or cancelled (s. 3 for insurers, s. 42D for intermediaries). Penalty cases follow the IRDAI Penalties Regulations 2026 and orders are published within 30 days (s. 105E).
