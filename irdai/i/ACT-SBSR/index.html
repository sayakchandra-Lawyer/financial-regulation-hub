Applies to
Insurers and insurance intermediaries (amends the Insurance Act, 1938 and the IRDA Act, 1999)

Key concepts

  • Foreign investment in Indian insurers up to 100% (new s. 3AA)
  • Intermediary registration no longer renewed every three years; continues subject to annual fee (s. 42D)
  • IRDAI power to direct disgorgement (s. 34)
  • New daily penalty for insurers and intermediaries (s. 102)
  • Penalty for unregistered intermediaries and those dealing with them (s. 105BA)
  • Factors for deciding penalties and publication of orders (s. 105E)

Key obligations

  • s. 42D: registered intermediaries, including corporate agents, keep their registration in force by paying the annual fee; non-payment is a ground for suspension
  • s. 40(2A): IRDAI may specify limits on and disclosure of commission to agents and intermediaries

Deadlines and cycles

  • In force from 05-02-2026

If you breach it

  • s. 102, s. 105BA and s. 105E as inserted in the Insurance Act, 1938

General consequences under the statute. Insurance Act, 1938: IRDAI may issue directions, including disgorgement of wrongful gains (s. 34). An insurer or intermediary that fails to file a return, comply with IRDAI directions or maintain solvency is liable to up to Rs 1 lakh a day, capped at Rs 10 crore (s. 102). Registration can be suspended or cancelled (s. 3 for insurers, s. 42D for intermediaries). Penalty cases follow the IRDAI Penalties Regulations 2026 and orders are published within 30 days (s. 105E).
