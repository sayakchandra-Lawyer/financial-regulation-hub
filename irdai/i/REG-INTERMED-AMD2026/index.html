Key concepts

  • Registration continues while the annual fee is paid
  • Fresh certificate for existing corporate agents
  • Functional ID of the seller on policy documents
  • Annual disclosure by large or foreign-majority corporate agents

Key obligations

  • Chapter I: existing corporate agents apply for a fresh certificate in Form A through the Agency Portal
  • Chapter I: no solicitation from a branch without an exclusive Specified Person
  • Chapter I: policy, proposal and certificate of insurance show the seller's name, functional ID and branch contact; online sales show the Principal Officer's contact
  • Chapter I: Principal Officer and Specified Persons complete 25 hours of training every three years
  • Chapter I: a corporate agent with foreign majority ownership or commission above Rs 10 crore publishes an annual disclosure on its website
  • Chapter I: annual fee is the higher of Rs 10,000 or 1/25 of 1% of commission and other receipts

Deadlines and cycles

  • Chapter I: existing corporate agents apply by 31-01-2027; late applications up to 31-03-2027 with Rs 750 extra, after which registration ceases
  • Chapter I: policy tagging with functional ID from 01-01-2027
  • Chapter I: annual fee paid before 31 January for the next year; 2% extra if paid within 30 days late, 10% after
  • Chapter I: audited financial statements to IRDAI by 30 September
  • Chapter I: NOC for a Specified Person to switch is deemed granted after 30 days

If you breach it

  • Chapter I: unpaid fee means suspension from 1 April; revoked if paid with 20% extra within 3 months, else cancelled
  • Chapter I: suspension without notice for fraud or conviction
  • Chapter I: a rejected applicant cannot reapply for one year

General consequences under the statute. Insurance Act, 1938: IRDAI may issue directions, including disgorgement of wrongful gains (s. 34). An insurer or intermediary that fails to file a return, comply with IRDAI directions or maintain solvency is liable to up to Rs 1 lakh a day, capped at Rs 10 crore (s. 102). Registration can be suspended or cancelled (s. 3 for insurers, s. 42D for intermediaries). Penalty cases follow the IRDAI Penalties Regulations 2026 and orders are published within 30 days (s. 105E).
