                      Modification in RFQ T+0 Market Timings for Corporate Bonds

This is in continuation to Exchange circular NSE/DS/43346 dated January 28,2020 and
NSE/DS/55230 dated January 13, 2023. The market timings for all types of Corporate
Bonds (Non-Convertible Securities, Securitised Debt Instruments, Security Receipts and
Municipal Debt Securities) executed on RFQ Platform for T+0 settlement from the existing
4:00 PM to 5:00 PM. There are no changes in the market timings for other instruments.
The revised timing for various instruments on RFQ Platform shall be effective from
October 19,2026:

  Market Hours
  09:00 AM to 05:00 PM for T+0 Settlement (Excluding G-sec, SDL and T-bills)
  09:00 AM to 05:00 PM for T+1 Settlement (For All Eligible Securities)

All other operational guidelines, settlement obligations and applicable terms and conditions
shall remain unchanged.

Participants are requested to take note of the above and make necessary arrangements
accordingly.
