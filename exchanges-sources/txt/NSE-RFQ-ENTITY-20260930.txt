All Participants,

Sub: Half-yearly confirmation of entity type by RFQ participants

This is with reference to Exchange circular reference no. NSE/COMP/73454 dated March 24, 2026, wherein all RFQ
participants were advised to confirm their entity type with the Exchange.

In order to ensure continued and accurate validation of the entity type of RFQ participants available with the
Exchange, all the existing RFQ participants are required to confirm their entity type with the Exchange for the half-
year ending on September 30, 2026, in accordance with the process outlined in Annexure A.

All existing RFQ participants are advised to provide this confirmation starting from October 01, 2026, till October
31, 2026, to ensure continued access to the RFQ platform

For any support, please reach out to the helpdesk on 1800 266 0050 (Select IVR option 3) or email at
memcompliance_support@nse.co.in
