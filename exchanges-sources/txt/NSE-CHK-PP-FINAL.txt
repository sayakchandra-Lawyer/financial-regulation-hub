No.

         the date of opening of the issue) along with Credit Rating Letters.

13. Listing Fees, if applicable

14. SEBI Fees of INR 5000/- per KID

Additional documents applicable for issuers seeking listing of Non-Convertible Securities instruments for the
first time on the Exchange

 15. Uniform Listing Agreement

16. SCORES ID from SEBI (To be entered in NEAPS on the following path: NEAPS > Debt Application >
         Masters > Industry Classification)

17. Initial Listing Fees of INR 7500/-

Additional documents

18. Information about the Company and Securities' forming part of Uniform Listing Agreement along with
         Covering Letter $

# GID shall contain Audited financials/Unaudited financials with Limited Review, as applicable, which are not older
than 6 months from the date of the GID or the issue opening date, as applicable, including for stub period. In case of
GID, the financial statements shall be audited and certified by the statutory auditor(s) who holds a valid certificate issued
by the Peer Review Board of the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India ("ICAI")

issued are secured or unsecured

preference shares'/'Perpetual debt instrument'/ `Perpetual non-cumulative preference Shares', as the case may be, in
