B. Submissions and Processing of applicaons

    a) Submission of Applicaon:

          Whenever a Company wants to file Scheme of Arrangement w.r.t Regulaon 37 and 59A of SEBI (LODR) Regulaons, 2015 with Exchange, it must
         submit the applicaon via NSE's Electronic Applicaon Processing System Portal ("NEAPS Portal"),BSE Lisng Centre and MSEI MyLisng Portal. At this
         stage, the Company can login into the system using login ID and password to access

          NEAPS Portal link and path - htps://neaps.nseindia.com/NEWLISTINGCORP/ > Issue > Scheme of Arrangements > Regulaon 37/59A of SEBI LODR
         2015 > Seeking Observaon leter

          BSE link and path for filing applicaon under Regulaon 37 - htps://lisng.bseindia.com/LoginAuth.aspx. >Lisng Operaons>Lisng
          Module>Issue Entry> Select issue type as `Scheme' and sub process as `Regulaon 37'

    b) BSE link and path for filing applicaon under Regulaon 59A- Please mail the documents at bse.schemes@bseindia.com. The maximum atachment
         size for the mails is 13 mbs.

    c) MSE link and path for filing applicaon under Regulaon 37 - htps://mylisng.msei.in/SitePages/UploadApplicaons/UploadApplicaons.aspx.
         Further Company is requested to mail the documents at lisng@msei.in.

    d) Steps for processing of Scheme of Arrangement under Regulaon 37 and 59A of SEBI (LODR) Regulaons, 2015.

         Once company submits the complete set of documents as per Exchange checklist available on website, the Exchanges shall review the same and
         accordingly process the applicaon within 7 working days.

C. Forward NOC to SEBI:
The Designated Stock Exchange shall forward the Scheme documents to SEBI for their Observaon Leter. Simultaneously, once all the queries raised by
the Exchange are resolved, the Exchanges in co-ordinaon provides NOC to SEBI.

D. Issuance of NOC along with Observaon Leter to Company (if any):

Once Observaon Leter is received from SEBI, Exchanges in co-ordinaon grants NOC to the company along with the Observaon leter for its further
acon such as filing such scheme with any Court or Tribunal.
