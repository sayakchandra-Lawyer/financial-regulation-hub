All Participants,

Muhurat Trading session on account of Diwali - Request for Quote (RFQ) platform

In continuation to Exchange circular No. NSE/DS/65902 dated December 23,2025, the Exchange
has proposed a special live trading session in RFQ which shall be held on Sunday, November 08,
2026, on account of Muhurat trading on Diwali as per the following schedule.

         Instruments       Market Timing                 Activities allowed
Only Corporate Bonds*
                       14:05 hrs to 15:00 hrs Trading, Confirmation, Modification, Cancellation

(T+1 settlement)       15:00 hrs to 15:30 hrs Confirmation, Modification, Cancellation

*Corporate Bonds as defined under SEBI - Issue and Listing of Non-Convertible Securities

Regulations.

Important instructions for Market Participants:

    � All trades executed during the Diwali Muhurat trading session shall result in settlement
         obligations.

    � T+1 deals can be cancelled /Modified till 5 PM on settlement day (as per current Trade
         Cancellation and Modification policy)

    � CBRICS Platform shall not be available during the Diwali Muhurat trading session.
