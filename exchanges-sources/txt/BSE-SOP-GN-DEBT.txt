C. Points to be noted:

1. ISIN level penalty shall be levied for Non-compliance of applicable regulations on ISIN which

    are matured/redeemed.

2. In case where all ISIN's are matured/redeemed penalty shall not be levied, only if the Company

    has defaulted the payment of interest/maturity penalty shall be levied as per the applicable
    clauses.

3. Once intimation provided to Electronic Book Provider informing non-compliance, then

    compliance will be informed only if entity is compliant with all applicable provisions
    mentioned in the circular along with payment of fine.

4. SEBI(LODR) Regulations, 2015 are not applicable to Maharaja Bonds.
5. Fine shall be levied on non-submissions of Regulation of chapter III, V, VI of SEBI (LODR)

    Regulations, 2015/Commercial Paper circular.
