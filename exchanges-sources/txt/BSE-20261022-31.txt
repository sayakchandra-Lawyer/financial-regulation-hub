This is in partial modification to Exchange Notice No - 20251223-12 dated December 23, 2025,
regarding Trading Holidays for the Calendar Year 2026 for NDS-RST participants are requested to note
that special trading session is introduced for Request for Quote (RFQ) on NDS-RST (RFQ) platform on
Sunday, November 8, 2026, on account of Diwali Laxmi Pujan is as follows-

Instruments            Market Timing                        Settlement Cycle

Only Corporate Bonds*  02.05 pm to 03.00 pm  T+1 Settlement
                       03.00 pm to 03.30 pm  Confirmation, Modification, Cancellation

*Corporate Bonds as defined under SEBI-Issue and Listing of Non-Convertible Securities Regulations.

Important instructions for Market Participants:
All trades executed during the Diwali Muhurat trading session shall result in settlement obligations.
NDS-RST other than RFQ Platform shall not be available during the Diwali Muhurat trading session.
For further queries participants are requested to contact their Relationship Manager
or debtinfo@bseindia.com.
