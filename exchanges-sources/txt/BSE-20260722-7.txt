The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued notification No. SEBI/LAD-NRO/GN/2026/312
dated July 10, 2026, regarding further amendment to the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing
Obligations and Disclosure requirements) Regulations, 2015.

These regulations may be called the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure
Requirements) (Second Amendment) Regulations, 2026.

They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette.

The aforesaid notification is attached for reference.

All Listed companies are requested to take note and comply accordingly.
