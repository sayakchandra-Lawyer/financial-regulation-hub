To,
All Insurers and Reinsurers
Subject: Update in the formats for publishing financial results as required under the SEBI (Listing
Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 and Filing of Financial Results in XBRL Mode
by all Insurers and Reinsurers
This is in reference to the IRDAI Circular IRDAI/F&I/CIR/MISC/92/7/2026 dated July 08, 2026 read with IRDAI
(Actuarial, Finance and Investment Functions of Insurers), (Amendment) Regulations, 2026 as may be amended
from time to time and SEBI Master Circular no. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated January 30,
2026. Please note that, the formats for publishing Unaudited/Audited quarterly financial results by listed Insurers
to Stock Exchanges for all Life Insurers, General Insurers, Health Insurers and Reinsurers have been revised.
In this regard, we wish to inform you that the issuers can download excel utility of Integrated Filling Financials-
Insurance IND AS, as per the said revised format, from the BSE Listing Centre and can upload the same on the
below path:
BSE listing centre path for downloading XBRL excel utility:
Listing Centre Menu button > Listing compliance > XBRL > XBRL > Excel utility > Integrated Filing
(Finance)_Insurance Companies Ind AS
BSE listing centre path for submitting XML file:
Listing Centre Menu button > Listing compliance > XBRL > E-filing > Integrated Financial Result > Integrated
Financial Result IND AS
Further, the listed Insurers, who were granted forbearance for the financial year 2026-27 for Ind AS
implementation and are required to prepare their financial statements under Schedule II of the Insurance
Regulatory and Development Authority of India (Actuarial, Finance and Investment Functions of Insurers)
Regulations, 2024 shall continue publishing financial results as per the existing circulars given below:
(a) Circular no. IRDA/F&I/REG/CIR/208/10/2016 dated October 25, 2016
(b) Circular no. IRDA/F&A/CIR/LFTD/027/01/2017 dated January 30, 2017
In case of any queries, concerning the captioned subject, you may contact the respective team members on the
contact details available in the BSE Listing Centre at path bseindia.com > Contact Us or send an email to
bse.xbrl@bseindia.com.

Amish Shah                         Shyam Bhagirath
