             Revision of RFQ Trading Hours for Corporate Bonds
             No Attachment
All Participants,
This is in continuation to BSE Notice No. 20200127-41 dated January 27, 2020, regarding the launch of
the Request for Quote (RFQ) trading mechanism in the NDS-RST system.
The market timings for all types of Corporate Bonds executed on the RFQ Platform for T+0 settlement
are revised from the existing 4:00 PM to 5:00 PM.
The applicable RFQ market hours, effective 21 October 2026, are set out below:

        Market Hours

        09:00 AM to 05:00 PM for T+0 settlement (excluding G-Sec, SDL and T-Bills)

        09:00 AM to 05:00 PM for T+1 settlement (all eligible securities)

All other RFQ procedures, settlement requirements and existing terms and conditions will continue to
apply.

Participants are requested to take note of this revision and make the necessary operational
arrangements.
