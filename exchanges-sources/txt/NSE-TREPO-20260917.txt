 All Members,

Revision in auction market timings for ARCL Triparty Repo-Market platform on the Exchange

This is in partial modification of Exchange circular no. NSE/DS/57672 dated July 21, 2023
regarding the launch of ARCL Triparty Repo-Market platform.

The Exchange vide its circular had updated the applicable trading hours for the TRM platform.
Further to the same, there shall be a revision in auction market timings. Accordingly, please note
that the auction market timings shall be revised w.e.f September 18, 2026 as below:

Market                Current Trading hours  Revised Trading hours
Repo Auction Market   03.00 PM to 03.30PM    03.30 PM to 04.00PM

Members and market participants are requested to take note of the same.
