Key concepts

  • Reasonable security practices (s. 43A)
  • Sensitive personal data or information
  • CERT-In directions (s. 70B(6))

Key obligations

  • s. 43A: a body corporate that is negligent in maintaining reasonable security practices for sensitive personal data and causes wrongful loss or gain must pay compensation; the section is omitted by s. 44(2)(a) of the DPDP Act from 13-05-2027
  • s. 70B(6): CERT-In may call for information and give directions to service providers, intermediaries, data centres, body corporates and others
  • From 13-05-2027 s. 44(2) of the DPDP Act omits s. 43A and s. 87(2)(ob), the power under which the 2011 sensitive personal data rules were made; the DPDP Act and Rules then govern, while liabilities that arose before that date are dealt with under the general law on omitted provisions

Deadlines and cycles

  • 13-05-2027: s. 43A and s. 87(2)(ob) omitted

If you breach it

  • s. 70B(7): failure to provide information or comply with a CERT-In direction, imprisonment up to one year, or fine up to Rs 1 crore, or both
  • s. 43A: compensation for negligent security of sensitive personal data, until 13-05-2027

General consequences under the statute. Information Technology Act, 2000: compensation under s. 43A until it is omitted, and the offence under s. 70B(7).
