Significant Data Fiduciaries and transfers abroad

Additional duties of Significant Data Fiduciaries, and transfer of personal data outside India (ss. 10 and 16; rules 13 and 15)

Key concepts

  • Significant Data Fiduciary (s. 2(z))
  • Data Protection Officer based in India (s. 10(2)(a))
  • Independent data auditor (s. 10(2)(b))
  • Data Protection Impact Assessment (s. 10(2)(c))
  • Algorithmic due diligence (rule 13(3))
  • Data localisation for specified data (rule 13(4))
  • Negative-list approach to transfers (s. 16(1))

Key obligations

  • s. 10(1): the Central Government may notify a Data Fiduciary or class as significant, considering the volume and sensitivity of data, risk to Data Principals' rights, sovereignty and integrity, electoral democracy, security of the State and public order; large banks, insurers and market infrastructure handle data at the scale these factors look at
  • s. 10(2)(a): appoint a Data Protection Officer who represents the Significant Data Fiduciary, is based in India, answers to its board of directors or similar governing body and is the point of contact for grievances
  • s. 10(2)(b) and (c): appoint an independent data auditor, and carry out periodic Data Protection Impact Assessments and periodic data audits
  • rule 13(1) and (2): carry out the Data Protection Impact Assessment and audit every twelve months from notification, and have the person who carried them out report significant observations to the Board
  • rule 13(3): check with due diligence that technical measures, including algorithmic software for handling personal data, are not likely to pose a risk to Data Principals' rights
  • rule 13(4): ensure that personal data specified by the Central Government on a committee's recommendation, and the traffic data about its flow, is not transferred outside India
  • s. 16(1): personal data may be transferred abroad except to a country or territory the Central Government restricts by notification
  • s. 16(2): a law giving higher protection or stricter limits on transfer abroad continues to apply, such as RBI's requirement that digital lending data be stored in India
  • rule 15: a transfer abroad must meet any requirements the Central Government sets by general or special order on making personal data available to a foreign State or its agencies

Deadlines and cycles

  • rule 13(1): Data Protection Impact Assessment and audit every twelve months from the date of notification as a Significant Data Fiduciary
  • Applies from 13-05-2027

If you breach it

  • Breach of the additional obligations of a Significant Data Fiduciary (s. 10): up to Rs 150 crore (Schedule item 4)
  • Breach of a transfer restriction: up to Rs 50 crore (Schedule item 7)

General consequences under the statute. Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023: if the Data Protection Board finds, on concluding an inquiry, that a breach of the Act or the Rules is significant, it may impose a monetary penalty under s. 33 and the Schedule: up to Rs 250 crore for failing to take reasonable security safeguards (s. 8(5)); up to Rs 200 crore for failing to notify a personal data breach (s. 8(6)) or for breaching the obligations on children's data (s. 9); up to Rs 150 crore for breaching the additional obligations of a Significant Data Fiduciary (s. 10); up to Rs 10,000 for a Data Principal who breaches her duties (s. 15); and up to Rs 50 crore for any other breach. The Board weighs the nature, gravity and duration of the breach, the data affected, repetition, any gain made or loss avoided, mitigation, proportionality and the likely impact of the penalty (s. 33(2)). It may accept a voluntary undertaking, which bars proceedings under the Act on its contents unless the undertaking is breached (s. 32(4) and (5)). An appeal lies to the Telecom Disputes Settlement and Appellate Tribunal within 60 days (s. 29). Where penalties have been imposed on a Data Fiduciary in two or more instances, the Central Government may, on the Board's reference, order public access to its service to be blocked (s. 37). These provisions apply from 13-05-2027.
