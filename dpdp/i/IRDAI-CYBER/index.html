Key concepts

Key obligations

  • Replaces the 2023 IRDAI guidelines on information and cyber security and sets minimum standards and governance mechanisms for all regulated entities
  • With the DPDP Act: these controls support the reasonable security safeguards required by s. 8(5) and rule 6, and incident handling should cover rule 7 intimations to the Board and Data Principals

General consequences under the statute. Insurance Act, 1938: IRDAI may impose penalties and give directions for failure to comply with its guidelines. The DPDP Act applies alongside (s. 38).
