Credit Information Companies (Regulation) Act, 2005

Credit Information Companies (Regulation) Act, 2005: privacy and accuracy provisions

Applies to
Credit information companies, credit institutions (banks, NBFCs, HFCs) and specified users

Key concepts

  • Accuracy and security of credit information (s. 19)
  • Privacy principles (s. 20)
  • Correction within 30 days (s. 21(3))
  • Unauthorised access (s. 22)

Key obligations

  • s. 19: credit information companies, credit institutions and specified users must keep credit information accurate, complete and protected against loss or unauthorised access, use or disclosure
  • s. 20: they must follow the privacy principles on collecting, processing, keeping, sharing and using credit information, including purpose limits and deletion
  • s. 21(1) and (2): a loan applicant may ask the credit institution for a copy of the credit information it obtained
  • s. 21(3): on a borrower's request, update or correct credit information within 30 days, after the credit institution certifies the change
  • s. 22: no access to credit information without authority of law or a court or tribunal direction
  • With the DPDP Act: correction and access rights under ss. 11 and 12 of the DPDP Act sit alongside these; the DPDP Act prevails only where the two conflict (s. 38(2))

Deadlines and cycles

  • s. 21(3): correct or update credit information within 30 days of the request

If you breach it

  • s. 22(2): unauthorised access, fine up to Rs 1 lakh per offence and Rs 10,000 a day while it continues
  • s. 23(2): wilful breach of the privacy principles, fine up to Rs 1 crore
  • s. 23(3): wilfully providing false credit information or omitting a material statement, fine up to Rs 1 crore
  • s. 23(4): any other contravention, fine up to Rs 1 lakh, and up to Rs 5,000 a day while it continues

General consequences under the statute. Credit Information Companies (Regulation) Act, 2005: fines imposed by a court under ss. 22 and 23 (up to Rs 1 crore for wilful breach of the privacy principles or wilfully false credit information; otherwise up to Rs 1 lakh, plus Rs 5,000 a day while it continues), and penalties imposed by RBI under s. 25. The DPDP Act applies alongside it (s. 38).
