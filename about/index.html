About

Financial regulation, in one place

Financial Regulation Hub brings together the rules that finance professionals work with every day: what each rule requires, who it applies to, when things are due and what happens on a breach, with a link to the official text for every point.

What it covers

Each regulator or law has its own section. Inside a section you can browse by subject, see a calendar of deadlines, read definitions and penalties, and open the full text of each instrument.

Who it is for

  • Compliance, legal and risk teams at NBFCs, housing finance companies, banks, insurers, brokers, asset managers and other regulated entities.
  • Company secretaries, auditors and consultants who advise them.
  • Students and anyone who wants a plain-English starting point before reading the official text.

How to use it

  • Search from the home page across every section, or inside one section.
  • What changed this month on the home page lists new and amended rules, each linked to the regulator's own notice.
  • Ask Not So Intelligent AI (bottom right on the home and section pages) answers questions from the texts on this site and cites the provision. Treat its answers as a pointer, not an opinion.

Independent and unofficial

This site is not run by, linked to or endorsed by any regulator, court or government body. It summarises public documents for general information. It is not legal advice, and it may be incomplete or out of date. Always check the official text, and take professional advice for a specific situation. See the disclaimer.

Help us improve it

If a summary is wrong, a rule is missing or something is hard to find, please send feedback.
