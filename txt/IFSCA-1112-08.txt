     CIRCULAR

F. No. IFSCA-Insu/CIRC/1/2021-Insurance  November 12, 2026

To,

All International Financial Services Centre Insurance Intermediary Offices (IIIOs)

Subject: Clarification for removing ambiguity in the International Financial
Services Centres Authority (Insurance Intermediary) Regulations, 2021 (‘IIIO
Regulations’), pursuant to the Sabka Bima Sabki Raksha (Amendment of
Insurance Laws) Act, 2025.

1. Reference may be drawn to the Insurance Act, 1938 and the amendments
         effected thereto vide the Sabka Bima Sabki Raksha (Amendment of Insurance
         Laws) Act, 2025 (“SBSR Act”), in particular sub-section (4A) of section 42D,
         which, inter- alia, provides that the validity of the registration granted to an
         Insurance Intermediary shall remain in force subject to payment of annual fee,
         unless suspended or cancelled by the Authority.

2. Clarification on the validity and renewal of the Certificate of Registration
         granted to IFSC Insurance Intermediaries (IIIOs)

         (a) In terms of IFSCA (Insurance Intermediary) Regulations, 2021 the
                  validity of Certificate of Registration (CoR) was three (03) years.

         (b) Therefore, the provisions related to the validity of CoRs and fees payable
                  by the IIIOs under the IFSCA (Insurance Intermediary) Regulations,
                  2021, need to be revised to align with the SBSR Act. Accordingly, it has
                  been decided that till the revised/ amended regulations are notified-

                  (i) All the CoRs shall be issued to the IIIOs with continued validity
                           subject to the compliance with the IIIO Regulations, other
                           applicable directions of the Authority and payment of annual fee,

                  (ii) An IIIO whose CoR is due for renewal shall file an application to
                           the Authority, as and when due, and the Authority may renew the
                           CoR with continued validity subject to applicant’s compliance with
