Subject: Revision of limits relating to requirement of underlying exposure for

currency derivatives contracts

    1. This is further to Clause 5.3.2.3 and Clause 5.3.3 of Master circular for Stock
         Exchanges and Clearing Corporations dated December 30, 2024, regarding the
         limits for currency derivatives contracts beyond which market participants are
         required to establish the existence of underlying exposure.

    2. The Reserve Bank of India (RBI), vide A.P. (DIR Series) Circular No. 25 dated
         October 10, 2026, on Risk Management and Inter-Bank Dealings, has revised
         the threshold limits for taking positions in foreign exchange derivatives involving
         the Indian Rupee (INR) without establishing the existence of underlying
         exposure. RBI has also prescribed additional requirements relating to
         cancellation and rebooking of foreign exchange derivative contracts and
         documentation for hedging contracted exposures. A copy of RBI’s circular is
         enclosed for reference and necessary compliance.

    3. Accordingly, the provisions of Clause 5.3.3 of SEBI Master Circular for Stock
         Exchanges and Clearing Corporations, related to threshold limits for taking
         unhedged positions in foreign exchange, dated December 30, 2024 are
         modified as under:

              “5.3.3.1 Domestic clients and Foreign Portfolio Investors (FPIs) may take
              long or short positions in currency derivatives contracts involving INR,
              without having to establish the existence of underlying exposure, up to a

         single limit of USD 5 million equivalent of notional value (outstanding at any
         point), across all currency pairs involving INR, put together, and combined
         across all recognised stock exchanges.

         5.3.3.2 FPIs shall ensure that their short positions at all stock exchanges
         across all contracts in FCY-INR pairs do not exceed USD 5 million.

         5.3.3.3 ….

         5.3.3.4 To take long positions in excess of USD 5 million in all contracts in
         FCY-INR pairs, FPIs shall be required to have an underlying exposure in
         Indian debt or equity securities, including units of equity/debt mutual funds.

         5.3.3.5 Domestic clients may take positions in excess of USD 5 million in all
         contracts in FCY-INR pairs, subject to the conditions specified in the
         RBI’s Master Direction - Risk Management and Inter-Bank Dealings dated
         July 05, 2016, as amended from time to time, and RBI A.P. (DIR Series)
         Circular No. 25 dated October 10, 2026.

         5.3.3.6 ….

         5.3.3.7 ….”

4. All other requirements, terms and conditions specified in SEBI Master Circular
    dated December 30, 2024, shall remain unchanged, except to the extent
    modified by this circular or otherwise directed by SEBI.

5. Stock exchanges and clearing corporations are directed to:

     a. Take necessary steps to put in place systems for implementation of this
         circular, including consequential amendments to the relevant bye-laws,
         rules and regulations;

     b. Bring the provisions of this circular to the notice of stock brokers and
         clearing members and disseminate the same on their websites; and

     c. Communicate to SEBI the status of implementation of the provisions of this
         circular.

6. This circular is being issued in exercise of the powers conferred under Section
    11(1) of the Securities and Exchange Board of India Act, 1992, to protect the
