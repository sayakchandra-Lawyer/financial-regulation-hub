New Delhi, the 4th September 2009
No. NHB.HFC.DIR.27/CMD/2009
Whereas, having considered it necessary in the public interest, the National Housing Bank had
issued Housing Finance Companies (NHB) Directions, 2001 in exercise of the powers conferred
on it under the National HousingBank Act, 1987 (53 of 1987);
And Whereas the said Directions have been amended further by Direction No. NHB.HFC.
DIR.24/ CMD/2008 dated December 02, 2008 and Direction No. NHB.HFC.DIR.25/CMD/2008
dated December 02, 2008 whereby changes were made with regard to categorisation of housing
loans, the extent of risk weights to be assigned after such categorisastion as also risk weights to
be assigned to fund based and non fund based exposures to commercial real estates necessitating
corresponding changes in half yearly return specified under paragraph 39 of the Directions;
Now therefore, the National Housing Bank having considered it necessary in the public interest
and being satisfied that, for the purpose of enabling the National Housing Bank to regulate the
housing finance system in the country to its advantage, it is necessary to do so, hereby in
exercise of the powers conferred by section 30A and 31 of the National Housing Bank Act, 1987
(53 of 1987) and all the powers enabling it in this behalf, directs that the Housing Finance
Companies Directions, 2001, shall with immediate effect, be further amended in the following
