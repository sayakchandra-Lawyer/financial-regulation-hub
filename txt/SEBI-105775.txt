PR No.72/2026

        Inauguration of SEBI’s Pavilion at the IITF, 2026 by Chairman, SEBI

The 45th India International Trade Fair (IITF), the annual multi-product exhibition
organised by the India Trade Promotion Organisation (ITPO) under the Ministry of
Commerce and Industry, Government of India, is being held at Bharat Mandapam,
New Delhi, from November 14–27, 2026.

As part of its continued efforts to showcase India’s well-regulated securities market
and to promote investor awareness and education, the Securities and Exchange
Board of India (SEBI), in association with NSE, BSE, MCX, MSEI, NCDEX, CDSL,
NSDL, KFin Technologies, CAMS, OBPP Association, CPAI, IRA, BIA, AMFI and
NISM, has set up a pavilion titled “Bharat Ka Share Bazaar” in Hall No. 4 at the fair.

In keeping with the overall IITF theme “Vikas aur Virasat”, this year’s Pavilion
presents India’s securities market, whose roots go back over 150 years, as a key
enabler of the nation’s journey towards Viksit Bharat @2047.

The Pavilion was inaugurated on November 14, 2026, by Shri Tuhin Kanta Pandey,
Chairman, SEBI, in the presence of SEBI officials, dignitaries from the participating
institutions and participants from across the securities market ecosystem.

In his address, the Chairman appreciated the joint effort of SEBI and the participating
institutions to promote investor awareness and education and expressed happiness
that the Pavilion has expanded since last year, with new participants. Chairman urged
the institutions to pay close attention to the discussions with the visitors to identify
processes that can be simplified and services that can be improved. He also
highlighted SEBI’s campaign “Samajh Se Investing Simple” under Project Jagrook
and the “Check, Validate and Verify” safety message, and advised investors to

The Pavilion showcases SEBI’s continuous efforts toward investor protection and
market integrity while making financial education interactive and experiential. It
features an AI Zone and an Investor Facilitation Camp (IFC), where visitors can
interact directly with officials and learn about safe investment practices.
Quizzes and games with exciting prizes will be held throughout the fair. These
initiatives aim to make investor education engaging, experiential and inclusive, while
also cautioning the public against illegal money mobilisation schemes, unregistered
entities and fraudulent investment offers, including those on social media and
messaging platforms.
The “Bharat Ka Share Bazaar” Pavilion truly embodies the spirit of “Vikas aur
Virasat”, bringing together the legacy of India’s securities market and its modern,
technology-driven present. It invites every citizen, from students and first-time
investors to senior citizens, to participate in the nation’s growth journey as informed
and protected investors.
Mumbai
