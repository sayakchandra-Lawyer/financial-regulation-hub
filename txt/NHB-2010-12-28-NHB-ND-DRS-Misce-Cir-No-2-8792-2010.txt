Submission of data to Credit Information Companies
In terms of Section 2(f) (v) of the Credit Information Companies (Regulation)Act, 2005
(herein after referred to as 'the said Act') a housing finance institution referred to in
Clause (d) of Section 2 of the National Housing Bank Act, 1987 (53 of 1987)has also
been included as "credit institution". Further, the said Act provides that every credit
institution in existence shall become a member of at least one credit information
company as per the statute.
2. In terms of Section 2 (e) of the said Act a credit information company means a
company formed and registered under the Companies Act, 1956 and which has been
granted a certificate of registration under sub-section (2) of Section 5 of the said Act by
the Reserve Bank of India. In this connection, we advise that following Credit
Information Companies have been granted certificate of registration by the Reserve

3. In terms of sub-sections (1) and (2) of Section 17 of the said Act, a credit information
company may require its members to furnish credit information as it may deem
necessary in accordance with the provisions of the said Act and every such credit
institution has to provide the required information to that credit information company.
Further, in terms of Regulation 10 (a) (ii) of the Credit Information Companies
Regulations, every credit institution shall:
(a) keep the credit information maintained by it, updated regularly on a monthly basis
or at such shorter intervals as may be mutually agreed upon between the credit
institution and the creditinformation company ; and
(b) take allsuch steps which may be necessary to ensure that the credit information
furnished by it, is update, accurate and complete.
4. It is therefore, advised that housing finance companies should become member of at
least one credit information company and provide them the current data in accordance
with the provisions of the said Act and the Rules/Regulations framed thereunder. Such
housing finance companies may also provide historical data in order to enable the new
credit information companies to validate their software and develop a robust database.
Care should also be taken to ensure that no wrong data/ history regarding borrowers
are given to Credit Information Companies.
