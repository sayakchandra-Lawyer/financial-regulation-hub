                                                     NOTIFICATION

                                           New Delhi, the 28th October, 2026
         No. 30/03/2016-Insolvency.— In accordance with the provisions of clause (b) of sub-section (1) of
section 189 of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016 (31 of 2016), the Central Government hereby
appoints Smt. Tanvi Sundriyal, Joint Secretary, Ministry of Corporate Affairs as ex-officio member in the
Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India to represent the said Ministry in said Board.
