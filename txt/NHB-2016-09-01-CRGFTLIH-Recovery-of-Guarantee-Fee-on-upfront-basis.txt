Recovery of Guarantee Fee on upfront basis by the Member Lending Institutions
from the borrowers under CRGFS
As you are aware that Credit Risk Guarantee Fund Scheme (CRGFS) has been notified
by the Government of India for the purpose of providing guarantee in respect of
Housing loans to EWS/LIG households in urban areas. In terms of sub paragraph (2)
of paragraph 11 (Chapter III) of the notified CRGF Scheme for low Income Housing,
"The Guarantee Fee shall not be charged from the beneficiary as an upfront processing
Fee. However, banks may alter the interest rate to cover up to 50% of the Guarantee
Fee. The premium charged should be clearly disclosed by the lending institution."
In the aforesaid context, we are pleased to inform that Member Lending Institutions
are now allowed to recover Guarantee Fee from the borrowers on upfront basis
instead of loading upon the housing loan interest rate".
The above amendment is effective from November 17, 2015. Accordingly, the member
lending institutions may now recover 50% of the Guarantee from the borrowers on an
upfront basis. The premium charged should be clearly disclosed by the lending
