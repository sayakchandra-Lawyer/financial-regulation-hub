Purpose of Loan- Advisory
It has been observed that in cases of Loan Against Property/Other Non-Housing loans
given to borrowers, the purpose of the loan sometimes is not being explicitly
mentioned/captured in the sanction letter/loan agreement/MITC or the purpose is being
mentioned only as 'Non-Housing'/'Loan Against Property'.
2.  The HFCs are therefore advised that the purpose of the loan has to be clearly
mentioned in the sanction letter, loan agreement and MITC executed with the borrowers
i.e whether the purpose of loan is for business or for non-business like medical expenses
etc.
3.  Further, HFCs are also encouraged to obtain a declaration/undertaking from
the customers at the time of Loan Application which may include clauses such as loan
will be utilised only for the purpose for which it is being sanctioned, purpose of use of
funds under the loan will not be changed in any manner during the tenor of loan, loan
shall not be used for any illegal and/or antisocial and/or speculative purposes in any
form.
All HFCs are advised to take note of the above and ensure compliance.
