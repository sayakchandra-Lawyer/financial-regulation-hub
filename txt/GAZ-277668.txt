रूप में संदर्भित) की अिुसूची VI [ताधिका: क्र.सं. 11] के साथ पठित िारा 400(1) के तहत प्रदत्त शधियों का प्रयोग
करते हुए, एतद्द्वारा धिर्दिष्ट करती है कक दकसी ऐसी कम्पनी (एतजममन पश्चात भुगतान प्राप्तकत्ताड के रूप मंे सन्िर्भडत) िो
दक दकसी अंतराडष्ट्रीय जित्तीय सेिा कंेद्र की एक इकाई हो और मुख्य रूप से एअर क्राफ्ट्स के पट्टे पर िेने के व्यिसाय मंे संलग्न
है, को दकसी अंतराषड ्ट्रीय जित्तीय सेिा कंेद्र की दकसी इकाई (एतजममन पश्चात भुगतान प्राप्तकत्ताड के रूप मंे सन्िर्भडत), िो दक
मुख्यतः एअर क्राफ्ट्स को पट्टे पर िेने के व्यिसाय मंे संलग्न है, के द्वारा लाभांि के रूप में दकए गए भुगतान से होने िाली आय
पर जनम्नजलजखत ितों के अधीन रहते हुए (इस अजधजनयम की धारा 393 (1) [ताधिका: क्र.सं. 7] के तहत दकसी प्रकार का
कर कटौती नहीं की िाएगी यथाः-

                                                              INCOME TAX

          S.O. 6016(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by section 400(1) read with Schedule VI [Table: S.No.
11] of the Income-tax Act, 2025 (30 of 2025) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government hereby
specifies that no deduction of tax shall be made under section 393(1)[Table: S.No. 7] of the said Act from any income
in the nature of dividend paid by any unit of an International Financial Services Centre, primarily engaged in the business
of leasing of an aircraft (hereinafter referred to as the payer) to a company, being a Unit of an International Financial
Services Centre primarily engaged in the business of leasing of an aircraft (hereinafter referred to as the payee) subject
to the following, namely:-

1. (1) The payee shall,—

    (a) furnish a statement-cum-declaration in the Form No.1(N) annexed to this notification (hereinafter
              referred to as the said Form) to the payer giving details of the tax year in which the dividend income
              eligible for exemption under Schedule VI [Table: S.No. 11] of the said Act is payable; and

    (b) such statement-cum-declaration shall be furnished and verified in the manner specified in the said
              Form for the tax year in which the dividend income eligible for exemption under Schedule VI [Table:
              S.No. 11] of the said Act is payable;

    (2) The payer shall,—

    (a) not deduct tax on payment made or credited to the recipient of such dividend (payee) after the date of
            receipt of copy of statement-cum-declaration in the said Form from payee; and

    (b) furnish the particulars of all the payments made to the recipient of such dividend on which tax has not
            been deducted in view of this Notification in the statement of deduction of tax referred to in section
            397(3)(b) of the said Act, read with the rule 219 of the Income-tax Rules, 2026.

2.  The Principal Director General of Income-tax (Systems) or the Director General of Income-tax (Systems), as

the case may be, shall lay down procedures, formats and standards for ensuring secure capture and transmission of data

and uploading of documents and they shall also be responsible for evolving and implementing appropriate security,

archival and retrieval policies.

Explanation.— For the purpose of this notification,—

    (a) "aircraft" shall have the same meaning as assigned to it in Note 3 of Schedule VI to the said Act;
    (b) "International Financial Services Centre" shall have the same meaning as assigned to it in clause (q)

              of section 2 of the Special Economic Zones Act, 2005 (28 of 2005); and
    (c) "Unit" shall have the same meaning as assigned to it in clause 2(zc) of the Special Economic Zones

              Act, 2005 (28 of 2005).
