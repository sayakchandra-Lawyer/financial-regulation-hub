Sub: - Migration of Non-Settlement Related Payments from Custodians to Trading Members in the Cash Market Segment
from T+2 to T+1 Settlement Cycle

Ref: Circular Ref. No. NCL/CMPT/40991 dated May 14, 2019

This is in reference to the above-mentioned circular regarding the facility for non-settlement related payments from Custodians to Trading
Members in the Cash Market segment.

In line with the migration of the settlement cycle from T+2 to T+1, the process for non-settlement related payments is revised as detailed
below:

1. Custodians shall upload a file with the details as provided in Annexure I by 4:00 PM on the settlement day.
2. NCL shall initiate the respective debit of amount from the Custodian clearing bank account and credit the amount into the Clearing

     Member’s (linked to the Trading Member) clearing bank account on the same day.
3. NCL shall generate and download a report to the Trading Members containing the details of the non-settlement related payments

     from the Custodians. The report format is provided in Annexure II.

The payments made under the aforementioned payment facility shall not be commingled with the settlement obligations of the
Custodians/Members and will not be guaranteed by NCL. The payments to Trading Members shall be made subject to realization from
Custodians. NCL shall not have any responsibility whatsoever in respect of these payments or any disputes related thereto.

The revised process shall be operational with effect from 16th November 2026(i.e. T+1 settlement cycle for trade date 13th November
2026).

                                                                             Annexure I

         Naming Convention:
         <Custodian Code>_NSP_DDMMYYYY.csv
         The file for a Custodian shall contain the following header
         Settlement Type
         Settlement Number
         TM Code
         CP Code
         Amount
         Exchange code
         The custodian shall provide the information pertaining to the headers mentioned above with respect to trading
         members (TM) and custodial participants (CPs) for which non settlement transactions need to be created.
