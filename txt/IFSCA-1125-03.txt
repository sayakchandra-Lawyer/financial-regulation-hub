    CIRCULAR

IFSCA-MIIT0IINX/1/2021-Capital Markets  November 25, 2026

To

All the recognised Stock Exchanges in the International Financial Services Centres
(IFSC)
All the recognised Clearing Corporations in the IFSC
All the recognised Depositories in the IFSC
All Broker Dealers and Clearing Members in the IFSC

Madam/Sir

Sub: Amendment to the “Master Circular for recognised Stock Exchanges and
recognised Clearing Corporations”

Reference may be drawn to the Circular dated June 05, 2026, titled “Master Circular for
recognised Stock Exchanges and recognised Clearing Corporations” (hereinafter referred
to as the “Master Circular”) issued under International Financial Services Centres
Authority (Market Infrastructure Institutions) Regulations, 2021 (the “MII Regulations”).

2. The Authority has received representations from stakeholders highlighting that
    providing additional time period to market makers to fulfil their delivery obligations
    would encourage greater participation by market makers in the secondary market and
    incentivise them to increase their quoting activities, thereby enhancing liquidity in the
    order book.

3. Based on the representations received and consultations held with stakeholders, the
    Authority has decided to amend the Master Circular to permit market makers for
    specified products to fulfil their delivery obligations within an additional period of up
    to two working days after expiry of the applicable settlement cycle, subject to such
    conditions as may be specified.

                                        Page 1 of 2
4. To operationalize the aforementioned, the Authority hereby amends the Master
    Circular, as under:

         a) under clause 27.1.1 of Chapter - IV of the Master Circular, the following
             proviso shall be added, namely: -

             “Provided that in case of specified products, market maker(s) may fulfil their
             delivery obligations within an additional period of up to two working days after
             the expiry of the applicable settlement cycle, subject to such conditions as may
             be specified.
             Explanation.- For the purpose of this proviso, the term ‘specified’ means
             specified by the recognised Clearing Corporation in consultation with the
             Authority.”

5. This Circular is being issued in exercise of powers conferred by Sections 12 and 13 of
    the International Financial Services Centres Authority Act, 2019, read with regulation
    72 of the MII Regulations and regulation 45 of the IFSCA (Capital Market
    Intermediaries) Regulations, 2025, and shall come into force with immediate effect.

A copy of this Circular is available on the website of International Financial Services
Centres Authority at www.ifsca.gov.in at “Legal Framework → Circulars”.
