                                                                    Circular

               NHB(ND)/DoS/ Sup. Circular No. 3/2020-21
               October 26,2020

               All Registered Housing Finance Companies (HFCs)

               Madam / Sir,

               Scheme for grant of ex-gratia payment of difference                  between compound interest
               and simple interest for six months to borrowers                      in specified loan accounts
               (March I, 2020 to August 31, 2020)

               In view of the unprecedented and extreme COVID-19 situation, the Central
               Government has approved "Scheme for grant of ex-gratia payment of difference
               between compound interest and simple interest by way of relief for six months i.e.
               March 1,2020 to August 31,2020 to borrowers in specified loan accounts."

               2. The benchmarks and modalities for ex-gratin payment of difference between
               compound interest and simple interest under the scheme is detailed out in the
               enclosed operational guidelines.

               3. Accordingly, the Housing Finance Companies (HFCs) are directed to
               implement the scheme in terms of the enclosed instruction and guidelines issued
               by the Central Government in this regard.

               4. HFCs are also advised to ensure that the payable ex-gratin amount under the
               scheme shall have to be credited to the account of the eligible borrower on or before
               5th November, 2020 post which HFCs can lodge their claim for reimbursement latest
               by 15th December, 2020 to the designated officer(s)/ cell at the State Bank of India
               (SBI) as per the details notified by SBI in this regard. Grievances of the HFCs shall
               be resolved through the designated cell at SBI in consultation with the Ministry of
               Finance, Government of India.

               5. Further, all HFCs shall put in place a grievance redressal mechanism for the
               eligible borrowers, as prescribed, for redressal of their grievances arising out of the
               present scheme within one week from the date of issuance of these scheme
               guidelines, at appropriate level(s).
