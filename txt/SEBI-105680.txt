PR No.70/2026

Commencement of SEBI (Settlement of Administrative and Civil Proceedings)
                                           Regulations, 2026

1. SEBI (Settlement of Administrative and Civil Proceedings) Regulations, 2026
    (“Settlement Regulations, 2026”) has been notified on 08.10.2026. Settlement
    Regulations, 2026 shall come into force on 07.11.2026. These regulations can be
    accessed here:
    https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/regulations/oct-2026/securities-and-exchange-board-
    of-india-settlement-of-administrative-and-civil-proceedings-regulations-
    2026_105126.html

2. For ease of understanding and filing of settlement applications, -
    (i) FAQs on Settlement Regulations, 2026 have been placed on the SEBI
             website www. sebi.gov.in which can be accessed here:
             https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/faqfiles/nov-2026/1793959419974.pdf
    (ii) A facility to help the applicants to calculate settlement amount has been
             provided on the SEBI website which can be accessed here:
             https://www.sebi.gov.in/set-cal-v2/set-cal.html
    (iii) Online settlement application form has been provided on SEBI website which
             can be accessed here:
             https://siportal.sebi.gov.in/intermediary/settlementApplication.html
    (iv) A Help desk has been set up, framework for which can be accessed here:
                                                                                                 Page 1 of 2
                  https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/faqfiles/jun-2026/1782726476068.pdf
        (v) An explainer video about how to use facility to calculate settlement amount

                  has been provided on SEBI website which can be accessed here.
                  https://www.sebi.gov.in/set-cal-v2/set-cal.html
    3. Eligible persons may apply for settlement in accordance with Settlement
        Regulations, 2026.
Mumbai
