National Stock Exchange of India

Circular

Department: Listing

Download Ref No: NSE/CML/77377                            Date: November 09, 2026

Circular Ref. No: 2038/2026

To All Members,

Subject: Reversal of action initiated against SPEL Semiconductor Limited (Symbol -SPELS) on
account of payment of Annual Listing Fees (“ALF”) for the Financial Year ended 2025-26.

This is in continuation to the Exchange Circular No. (Download Ref. No. NSE/CML/76264) dated September
09, 2026 notifying restriction in trading in securities of SPEL Semiconductor Limited (“the Company”) on
account of non-payment of ALF for the Financial Year ended 2025-26.

Members of the Exchange are hereby informed that the Company has cleared the pending ALF dues at the
other Exchange and hence it is decided to reverse the action initiated against the company.

Further, members are requested to note that the trading in the securities of the Company will be moved from
Trade for trade category (BE) to Rolling Settlement (EQ) w.e.f. November 11, 2026.

The details are as under:

Sr. No Company Name                                       Series

1  SPEL Semiconductor Limited                             EQ

This circular shall be effective from November 11, 2026.

For and on behalf of
National Stock Exchange of India Limited

Mandar Desai
Senior Manager
