          Requirement of Prior Consent for Appointment or Continuation of
 MD/CEO/Head of the Institution in cases of Promoter Shareholding Dilution by

                               HFCs under Refinance Arrangements

It has been decided that all Housing finance companies (HFCs) must obtain prior
written consent from the National Housing Bank (NHB) for the appointment or
continuation of the Managing Director (MD) and/or Chief Executive Officer (CEO) /
Head of the Institution in cases where the single largest promoter's shareholding of
the HFC is less than 26 per cent or is going to be less than 26 per cent.

2. This measure is a refinance arrangement covenant in the nature of a lender-to-
borrower obligation and shall be deemed applicable to all existing and prospective
refinance arrangements with HFCs.
