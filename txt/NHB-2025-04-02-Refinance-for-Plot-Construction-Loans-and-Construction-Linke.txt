Refinance for 'Plot + Construction' Loans and 'Construction Linked' Loans
National Housing Bank (NHB) extends refinance to Housing Finance Companies
(HFCs) in respect of their housing loans extended to individuals.
In this context, it may be noted that in cases where HFCs extend loans to individuals
for ongoing construction, refinance from NHB will be available only in respect of loans
where not more than one-half of the construction has been completed at the time of
disbursement of first tranche of the loan by the HFC.
The stage of construction should be clearly evidenced by the technical evaluation report
at the time of disbursement of the first tranche. This condition would be applicable for
loans extended for 'plot + construction' as well as construction made on self-owned
land.
All other terms and conditions of NHB refinance as communicated from time to time
remain unchanged.
The HFCs are accordingly advised to ensure flagging of only eligible loan accounts for
NHB refinance.
