This is with reference to Exchange Notice No. 20261001-37 dated October 01, 2026, and 20261016-18 dated
October 16, 2026, requiring all trading members to carry out a complete internal audit for the half year ended
September 30, 2026.

In view of the above, Members are reminded to submit the Internal Audit Report on or before November 30,
2026, through the BEFS (BSE Electronic Filing System) in the prescribed format and comply with the regulatory
requirements to avoid any penal charges/disciplinary action for the non-submission or delayed submission of
the Internal Audit Report in accordance with Exchange Notice No. 20260417-45 dated April 17, 2026.

Members are advised to take note of the above and comply.
