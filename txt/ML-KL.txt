       8. Power to deposit in Court money due on loan.--(1) Where a money-lender refuses to

loan, as the case may be.

          (2) Where any such application is made, the Court shall, after due inquiry, pass
orders recording full or part-satisfaction of the loan as the case may be.

          (3) The procedure laid down in the Code of Civil Procedure, 1908 (Central Act V of

          (4) An appeal shall lie from an order passed by a Court under sub-section (2) within
thirty days excluding the time for obtaining a certified copy of the order as if such an order

of the Code of Civil Procedure, 1908 (Central Act V of 1908).
