               Timely and hassle-free loans to beneficiaries of PMAY-U/PMAY-U 2.0

In order to facilitate the availability of housing loans under the Economically Weaker Section
(EWS) category and to ensure timely and hassle-free access to institutional finance by
beneficiaries under Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana - Urban (PMAY-U) / PMAY-U 2.0, the

Department of Financial Services (DFS) has undertaken the following initiatives:

2. A list of approved beneficiaries under the Beneficiary Led Construction (BLC) and
Affordable Housing in Partnership (AHP) verticals is made available at PMAY 2.0 Unified

Web Portal which can be used to connect with the beneficiaries and to assess their

creditworthiness. Interest Subsidy Scheme (ISS) is also one of the verticals of PMAY 2.0

wherein the eligible clients get interest subsidy from the Government of India.

3. To mitigate the potential credit risk, a dedicated Credit Risk Guarantee Fund Scheme for

Low Income Housing (CRGFTLIH) is also operational through National Credit Guarantee
Trust Company (NCGTC).

In this regard, all HFCs shall ensure timely and hassle-free sanction and disbursement of loans
to eligible PMAY-U / PMAY-U 2.0 beneficiaries by leveraging the aforesaid initiatives.
