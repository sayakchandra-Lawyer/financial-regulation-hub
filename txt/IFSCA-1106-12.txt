                                       PRESS RELEASE

    IFSCA notifies leasing of GPUs and connected data centre equipment as a
                                 financial product in GIFT IFSC

The International Financial Services Centres Authority (IFSCA) has notified Graphics Processing
Units (GPUs) and connected data centre equipment as specified equipment, thereby recognising
operating lease, including any hybrid of operating and financial lease of such equipment as a
financial product in GIFT IFSC.

IFSCA has issued Notification No. IFSCA/GN/2026/017 dated October 22, 2026, under Section
12 of the IFSCA Act, 2019, read with Government of India Notification No. S.O. 5199(E) dated
December 14, 2021, following its consultation paper of August 18, 2026. The notification was
published in the Gazette of India on October 29, 2026, and has come into effect from that date.

The notification covers GPUs, including General Purpose GPUs (GPGPUs), Artificial Intelligence
(AI) accelerators, Tensor Processing Units (TPUs), Neural Processing Units (NPUs), and other
comparable accelerated computing technologies, as well as rack-scale AI systems. It also covers
connected servers, storage, networking, interconnect and power equipment, along with their
parts and accessories, where such equipment is dedicated to and directly associated with GPUs.

India is currently fully import-dependent for such equipment. Enablement of leasing through
IFSC is aligned with the Government of India’s policy to ensure affordable and reliable access to
computing resources as an enabler of India’s AI ecosystem. Leasing provides access to
computing infrastructure without requiring ownership and would complement, rather than
compete with, public compute infrastructure. As with aircraft and ship leasing, enabling GPU
equipment leasing at this early stage of the global AI infrastructure expansion would help attract
leasing activity to the IFSC, broaden its leasing ecosystem and position India as an early
participant in this emerging market. Leasing of such equipment will make advanced computing
infrastructure accessible without large upfront investment and create opportunities for lessors
in the growing AI infrastructure sector. Framework for Leasing Activity in IFSC, to be issued
shortly, shall lay down the rules for finance companies in GIFT IFSC can undertake leasing
activities.

The notification is available at: Notification No. IFSCA/GN/2026/017
