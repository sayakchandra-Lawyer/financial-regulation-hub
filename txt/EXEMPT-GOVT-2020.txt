                                  MINISTRY OF CORPORATE AFFAIRS

                                                  NOTIFICATION

                                          New Delhi, the 2nd March, 2020

         G.S.R. 151(E).--In exercise of the powers conferred by clauses (a) and (b) of sub-section (1)
and in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 462 of the Companies Act, 2013 (18 of 2013), the
Central Government, in the public interest, hereby makes the following further amendments in the
notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Corporate Affairs, number G.S.R. 463(E),
dated the 5th June, 2015, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part-II, Section 3, Sub-
section (i), dated the 5th June 2015, namely:-

     In the said notification, in the Table:-
