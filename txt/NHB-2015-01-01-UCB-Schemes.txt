Refinance Schemes applicable to Scheduled Urban Cooperative Banks

NHB currently has the following refinance schemes in operation for UCBs :

                                                         Scheme    Short
No.                                                               Name

                                             Full Name           RS

    1. Regular Refinance Scheme *

2. Rural Housing Fund                                            RHF

3. Urban Housing Fund                                            UHF

4. Special Urban Housing Refinance Scheme for Low Income         U-LIH
     Households

* Concession in interest rates is offered for refinance availed in respect of the following loans :

 o Loans upto 10 lakhs
 o Loans in rural areas (as per 2011 Census)
 o Loans to women (where the woman is owner / co-owner of financed property)
 o Loans to persons of 3rd gender (where the beneficiary is owner / co-owner of financed property)
 o Loans to disabled or differently abled persons (where the beneficiary is owner / co-owner of financed

      property)

 o Loans to persons belonging to Scheduled Castes / Scheduled Tribes (where the beneficiary is owner /

      co-owner of financed property)

 o Loans for green housing (concession will be available where the loan is for domestic solar equipment, water

      harvesting, or energy efficient housing certified to save at least 18% under the NHB-KfW assessment model)
