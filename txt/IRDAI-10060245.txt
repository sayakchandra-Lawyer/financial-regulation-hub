On 23 September 2026, IRDAI issued a public consultation paper titled “Recalibrating
Economics of Insurance Distribution”. The paper sets out a comprehensive framework
of reforms covering the structure of insurance distribution, expenses, commissions,
market conduct, transparency and the use of digital infrastructure.

The public consultation received an overwhelming response. By the extended
deadline of 31 October 2026, about 2.18 lakh responses to the 32 questions had been
received from over 13,000 persons. These responses reflect the breadth of the
insurance ecosystem and the seriousness with which participants have engaged with
the proposals.

The objectives of the reforms remain to lower the cost of insurance, improve
policyholder outcomes, widen coverage, and support a sustainable and professional
distribution ecosystem. Insurance should be accessible, affordable and fairly sold, and
distribution should reward quality advice, service and long-term customer
relationships. These goals are central to IRDAI's mandate of protecting policyholders'
interests and promoting the orderly growth of the sector, in line with the national vision
of insurance for all.

IRDAI, today, constituted an Expert Committee of independent economists and
insurance professionals to suggest on measures to strengthen the design and
implementation of its distribution reforms. Mr. Ajay Seth, Chairman, IRDAI, said, “The
Committee's role is to help make the framework as robust, clear and effective as
possible. Its work will complement the ongoing engagement IRDAI maintains with all
segments of the industry.” Drawing on the depth of expertise available across the
sector and beyond, the Committee is expected to advise on how the proposals can be
further refined so that they are implemented smoothly and deliver their intended
benefits.

The Committee will be chaired by Dr. Indu Bhushan, IAS (Retd.), former CEO,
Ayushman Bharat Mission. Its members are Shri Suresh Goyal, Director General,
National Council of Applied Economic Research (NCAER); Shri A.V. Girija Kumar,
former CMD, The Oriental Insurance Co. Ltd.; Shri Praveen Vashishta, former CEO
and Chairman, Howden Insurance Brokers India; and Shri Mahesh Kumar Sharma,
former MD & CEO, SBI Life Insurance. Dr. Ayyaj Tamboli, IAS, Executive Director,
IRDAI, will be the Convenor. The Committee has been given time until 31 December
2026 to submit its report.
