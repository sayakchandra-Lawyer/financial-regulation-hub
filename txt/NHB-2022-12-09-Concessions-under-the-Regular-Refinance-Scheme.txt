Concessions under the Regular Refinance Scheme
As part of continued celebration of "Azadi ka Amrit Mahotsav", NHB has decided to extend
the concessions offered earlier for the selected categories on fresh disbursements till
September 30, 2023. However, considering the importance of credit flow to certain focus
segments an increased concession of 100 bps is being offered to the Primary Lending
Institutions till the above-mentioned period as under:

Notes:
Only one concession at a time will be available
In case of loans to women, concession will be available where the woman is owner/ co-owner
of financed property
In case of loans to 3rd gender, disabled or differently abled persons, concession will be available
where the beneficiary is owner / co-owner of financed property
In case of loans for green housing, concession will be available for loans certified by green
rating agencies viz. GRIHA, IGBC etc. (4 or above star/Platinum or Gold star)
This special discount in refinance rates is available for one year period and the resets under
Category A would be made at 10/ 15 bps and under Categories B & C at 25 bps
