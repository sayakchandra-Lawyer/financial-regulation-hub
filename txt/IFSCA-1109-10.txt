    Consultation Paper on Offering ‘Model Portfolio’ by Research Entities in the
                             International Financial Services Centre

1. Objective

    The objective of this consultation paper is to seek comments from the public and
    stakeholders on the draft circular for providing clarification that Research Entities
    may offer model portfolios and the guidelines to be followed by them while offering
    Model Portfolio.

2. Background and Proposal

    2.1 The International Financial Services Centres Authority (“IFSCA”) (Capital
            Market Intermediaries) Regulations, 2025 (“CMI Regulations”) provide for the
            regulatory framework for Research Entities operating within the International
            Financial Services Centre (“IFSC”). Currently, Research entities primarily
            provide research reports on specific securities, including "buy/sell/hold"
            recommendations, price targets etc.

    2.2 With the growth of the IFSC as a global financial hub, there is an increasing
            demand for sophisticated research products that go beyond individual security
            analysis. Market participants have expressed a need to offer ‘Model Portfolios’
            as curated baskets of securities with specific weightages based on defined
            investment themes or strategies.

    2.3 Given that IFSC is a jurisdiction for international financial services, these model
            portfolios are largely expected to be global in nature, often focusing on global
            markets and utilizing multi-asset strategies including equities, fixed income
            ETFs, commodity ETFs, etc.

    2.4 Currently, the CMI Regulations do not explicitly provide for research entities to
            offer Model Portfolios. Accordingly, with a view to provide a structured
            mechanism for research entities to offer diversified investment strategies, it is
            proposed to clarify that a research recommendation under a research report
            includes offering a Model Portfolio.

    2.5 Accordingly, any entity, including a Broker Dealer, shall be required to seek
            registration as Research Entity in order to offer Model Portfolio.

3. Key Proposals

    The major safeguards for research entities offering model portfolio are as under:

    (a) Weightages:

         Distinguishing a ‘Model Portfolio’ from a summary of recommendations by
         requiring the assignment of specific weightages to constituents.

    (b) Global & Multi-Asset Scope:

         Explicitly requiring Research Entities to define the geographical universe (e.g., US
         Markets, Emerging Markets, or other markets) and asset classes (e.g., multi-asset)
         to ensure investors understand the jurisdictional and market risks.

    (c) Performance & Benchmarking:

         Ensuring that performance claims are consistent and comparable against relevant,
         predefined indices.

    (d) Transparency in Rebalancing:

         The rebalancing of portfolios shall be transparently disclosed. The rebalancing of
         portfolios shall generally be at pre-defined intervals. However, in case of any
         rebalancing of portfolios other than at pre-defined intervals, the rationale for such
         rebalancing shall be disclosed.

    (e) Disclosures:

         Mandating disclosures for every portfolio including details of the selection
         methodology, rationale and investment horizon.

Subject: Offering Model portfolio by Research Entities

1. Reference may be drawn to the International Financial Services Centres Authority
     (Capital Market Intermediaries) Regulations, 2025 (“CMI Regulations”) which inter-
     alia specify the regulatory framework for capital market intermediaries, including
     research entities in the International Financial Services Centre (“IFSC”).

2. CMI Regulations allow a research entity to publish or provide research report with
     respect to securities. Research report is defined therein as any written or electronic
     communication that includes research analysis or research recommendation, or an
     opinion concerning securities or public offer, providing a basis for investment
     decision.

3. International Financial Services Centres Authority (IFSCA”) is in receipt of
     representations from stakeholders to allow to offer recommendations in the form of
     a basket of multiple securities based on specific investment strategies or themes,
     commonly referred to as ‘model portfolio’.

4. In order to provide a structured mechanism for research entities to offer diversified
     investment strategies, it is hereby clarified that providing research recommendation
     by the research entities includes offering model portfolio.

5. Operational Guidelines for offering ‘model portfolio’

5.1. Definitions

For the purpose of this circular, the following terms shall have the meaning as
mentioned below:

(a) “Model Portfolio” shall mean a basket of securities for which a research report
    is issued by a research entity recommending the relevant weightages for one
    or more securities mentioned therein.

Explanation- If the research report does not ascribe weightages to the
components of basket of securities, then merely a summary or consolidated
presentation of securities recommended shall not be regarded as a ‘model
portfolio’.

(b) “Disclosures” shall mean the set of disclosures as specified in this Circular to
    be mandatorily included in a model portfolio, to ensure that all the relevant
    facts and information which could impact the investment decision of a
    potential investor are adequately made known to the investors.

(c) “Launch Date” shall mean the date on which model portfolio was issued by
    the research entity.

(d) “Update Date” shall mean the date of issuance of each updated model portfolio
    following its review and update by the research entity at predefined intervals.

5.2. Obligations and Responsibilities

A research entity offering model portfolios shall ensure compliance with the
following obligations and responsibilities:

5.2.1.  Model Portfolio Report: A model portfolio shall be issued only through a
        formal research report. The opinion expressed on any constituent security
        within the portfolio shall not be contrary to the research entity’s
        individual research opinion on that security. Each report shall contain a
        comprehensive ‘factsheet’ setting out basic information, rationale, and
        underlying methodology.

5.2.2. Methodology: The report shall define the framework for selection of
          securities and shall indicate the following factors:

        a) Geographical Universe: The report shall specify the geographical
              or jurisdictional universe.

                  Page 5 of 6
        b) Asset Class/Classification: It shall be labelled to indicate the type
              of securities included.

        c) Underlying theme: If applicable, the report shall indicate an
              underlying theme (such as Tech Momentum, Space Tech, Artificial
              Intelligence, Fintech Innovation, or Sustainable Finance, etc.)

5.2.3.  Parameters: The selection parameters, such as fundamental analysis,
        technical analysis, or quantitative factor-based modelling, shall be
        discussed in detail in the report.

5.2.4.  Labelling: Model portfolios shall be ‘true to label’, the nomenclature shall
        clearly state the portfolio type and provide a description of the investment
        objective for ease of understanding.

5.2.5.  Investment Horizon: Every model portfolio report shall specify the
        recommended investment horizon to allow investors to align the strategy
        with their own investment period.

5.2.6.  Frequency of update of Model Portfolio: Whether the model portfolio
        would be updated and at what intervals shall be predefined in the report.
        The rebalancing, if any, of the constituent securities in the model portfolio
        shall generally be done within the overall framework of the model
        portfolio and shall be communicated to the clients. However, in case of any
        rebalancing of portfolios other than at pre-defined intervals, the rationale
        for such rebalancing shall be communicated to the clients.

5.2.7. Risk Disclosure: The risks associated with the Model portfolio shall be
          clearly mentioned in model portfolio report.

5.2.8.  Benchmarking: A portfolio may be benchmarked against an index. The
        benchmark index shall be clearly defined and used consistently across all
        reports.

5.2.9. Audit Requirement: Compliance with these guidelines shall be covered
          under the compliance audit set out under the CMI Regulations.

6. This Circular is issued in exercise of powers conferred under sections 12 and 13 of
    the International Financial Services Centres Authority Act, 2019, read with regulation
    45 of the CMI Regulations, and shall come into force with immediate effect.
