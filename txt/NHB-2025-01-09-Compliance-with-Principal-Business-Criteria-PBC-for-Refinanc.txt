     Compliance with Principal Business Criteria (PBC) for Refinance Eligibility

As per the extant policy of the National Housing Bank (NHB), housing finance companies
(HFCs) seeking refinance must adhere to the Principal Business Criteria (PBC) outlined in
Paragraph 4.1.17 of the NBFC-HFC (Reserve Bank) Directions, 2021, dated February 17, 2021,
and as updated from time to time.

2.       In this regard, it is clarified that HFCs who fails to meet the PBC, as per their published

accounts as on March 31st of the latest financial year, but complies with the PBC in subsequent

quarters, shall remain eligible for refinance, provided that such non-compliance or breach of

the PBC must be condoned by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) in writing.

3.       Further, all HFCs must comply with the PBC for both the preceding quarter and the

preceding month in which the refinance sanction and disbursement are being considered.

4.       This relaxation is limited to refinance eligibility from NHB and shall not be construed

as an exemption / relaxation from HFC's obligation to comply with the PBC norms prescribed

by RBI.
