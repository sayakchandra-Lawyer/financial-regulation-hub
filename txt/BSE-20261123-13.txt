Subject: Compliance with the guidelines on ‘Safer participation of Retail investors in Algorithmic
trading issued by Exchange’ – Tech-savvy Retail Investors

This has reference to SEBI Circular No. SEBI/HO/MIRSD/MIRSD-PoD/P/CIR/2025/0000013 dated
February 04, 2025, BSE Notice No. 20250723-41 dated July 23, 2025, and SEBI circular No.
SEBI/HO/MIRSD/MIRSD-PoD/P/CIR/2025/132 dated September 30, 2025, related to ‘Safer
participation of Retail investors in Algorithmic trading issued by the Exchange’. The Retail Algo
framework was implemented with effect from April 01, 2026.

As per Exchange Notice No. 20250723-41 dated July 23, 2025 (Annexure A, Para 14), it is
mandated that "all the strategies shall be run on the brokers servers. The order messages shall
be originated from brokers server." This requirement is central to ensuring the broker's control
over risk management and data confidentiality. However, Tech Savvy client is required to host the
Algo using a static IP at their end where Algo logic resides instead of hosting the Algo on Trading
Member’s cloud server.”

Post April 01, 2026, i.e. the commencement of Retail Algo framework, Exchanges have been
receiving complaints from market participants regarding the misuse of Static IP facilities by
certain Exchange empaneled Algo Providers. The Exchange has also noted that some Algo
Providers are providing Static IPs to retail clients, under the guise of them being ‘Tech-savvy’ retail
clients, which is in violation with SEBI/Exchange guidelines issued for Safer participation of Retail
investors in Algorithmic trading.

Accordingly, the Exchange hereby informs all market participants that:
    1. The Static IP is intended only for ‘Tech-savvy Retail Investors’ who develop their own algos
         using programming knowledge (As per SEBI Circular No. SEBI/HO/MIRSD/MIRSD-
         PoD/P/CIR/2025/0000013 dated February 04, 2025)
    2. Trading Members and Algo Providers must not, under any circumstances, provide Static
         IP access to retail clients who do not fall under the category of ‘Tech-savvy Retail
         Investors’ for Algorithmic Trading. Any such case of misclassification or misuse of ‘Static
         IPs’ will be treated as a violation of SEBI and Exchange guidelines.
    3. Trading Members who are registering such “Tech Savvy Client” shall take an undertaking
         from such clients that “such clients meet the requirements of the SEBI definition of ‘Tech-
         savvy’ Retail clients i.e. they are Retail clients who develop their own algos using
         programming knowledge.”
    4. The undertaking shall further state that “As a “Tech Savvy Client” since we are developing
         our own algos using programming knowledge, we shall have no recourse to the Investor
         Grievance mechanism of the Exchange in the event of losses incurred on account of the
         logic of ALGO used.”
The Exchanges will closely monitor compliance of Trading Members and Algo Providers with the
above guidelines and in the event of any violations shall take following actions:

• Non-Adherence on part of Trading Members - Initiate following penal action on the Trading
    Member (s) concerned.
