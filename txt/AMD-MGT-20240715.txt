         G.S.R. 403.--In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-sections (1) and (2) of section 469 of the Companies
Act, 2013 (18 of 2013), the Central Government hereby makes the following rules further to amend the Companies
(Management and Administration) Rules, 2014, namely:-

1. Short title and commencement. - (1) These rules may be called the Companies (Management and Administration)
Amendment Rules, 2024.

          (2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette.

2. In the Companies (Management and Administration) Rules, 2014, in annexure, for form number MGT-6, the
following form shall be substituted, namely.-
