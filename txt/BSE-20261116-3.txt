             Postponement of revision in Non-Settlement Payment Process from
             Custodians to Members under T+1 Settlement
             No Attachment
All Members & Custodians,

With reference to Notice No. 20261105-1 regarding the Revision in the Non-Settlement
Payment Process from Custodians to Members under the T+1 Settlement Cycle, which
was scheduled for implementation with effect from November 16, 2026.

All Members and Custodians are hereby informed that the implementation has been
postponed until further notice. Accordingly, the existing process shall continue to
remain in force until further communication.

For any further clarifications, Members & Custodians may contact their relationship managers or BSECL
