NHB (ND)/ROD/ Refinance Circular No. 1/2024-25
September 05, 2024

To,

All Eligible Primary Lending Institutions

Madam/Sir,

Requirement of availability of external rating of refinance limit sanction by NHB
                                             in public domain

In continuation to the Refinance Circular No. 5/2023-24 dated: March 21, 2024, and in
furtherance compliance to the requirement under BASEL - III, it has been decided that
the external rating carried out by the PLIs on the refinance exposure (whether availed
or unavailed) of NHB through Rating Agencies must be available in the public domain
i.e. the same shall be available on the website of respective rating agency. In case, the
rating is not available in public domain, the same shall not be considered by the Bank.
All PLIs are advised to take note of the above and ensure compliance.

Yours faithfully,
