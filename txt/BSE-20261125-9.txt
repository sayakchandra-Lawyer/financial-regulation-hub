All Members/Participants,

This is with reference to BSECL circular 20261112-1 dated November 12, 2026, and SEBI circular HO/(561)2026-MRD-
POD2/I/25177/2026 dated November 05, 2026, the threshold for positions without underlying exposure in INR currency
derivatives has been lowered from USD 100 million to a combined limit of USD 5 million.

All Clearing members of the Currency derivative segment are required to report to the Clearing Corporation, the details
of Clients who wish to take positions beyond USD 5 Million (or equivalent) in contracts involving INR in all exchanges put
together, to designate an Authorized Dealer/Custodian. The Clearing member is also required to submit a confirmation
from AD-1 Bank.

The file format of Letter from AD-1 Bank is attached as Annexure 1. Member to send email details pertaining to the AD-1
Bank details as per format provided in Annexure 2. On receipt of the requisite information, BSECL shall provide the
position details to the respective AD-1 Bank. The file format details are attached as Annexure 3

The same needs to be complied with if the Client wishes to take positions beyond USD 5 Million (or equivalent) in
contracts involving INR.

Members are requested to take note of the above
