National Stock Exchange of India

Circular

          DEPARTMENT: INSPECTION

Download Ref. No.: NSE/INSP/77450         Date: November 16, 2026

Circular Ref. No.: 55/2026

To All Trading Members,

Sub: Reminder for submission of Internal Audit Report for the half year ended
September 30, 2026

This is with reference to Exchange Circular Ref. No. NSE/INSP/76701 dated October 1, 2026
and Exchange Circular Ref. No. NSE/INSP/76784 dated October 7, 2026 requiring all Trading
Members to carry out a complete internal audit for the half year ended September 30, 2026.

In view of the above, Members are reminded to submit the Internal Audit Report on or before
November 30, 2026, through the Inspection module in the Member Portal in the prescribed
format and comply with the regulatory requirements to avoid any penal charges/disciplinary
action for the non/late submission of the internal audit report in accordance with Exchange
Circular Ref. No. NSE/INSP/73792 dated April 17, 2026.

Members are advised to take note of the above and comply.

For and on behalf of
National Stock Exchange of India Limited

Shital Lopes
