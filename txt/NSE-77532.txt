National Stock Exchange of India                                        Date: November 20, 2026

Circular

                                                Department: Compliance
  Download Ref No: NSE/COMP/77532
  Circular Ref. No: 101/2026

To All Members,

Sub: Reminder for Submission of Half Yearly Networth Certificate as on September 30, 2026

This has reference to the Exchange Circular ref. no. NSE/COMP/76716 dated October 05, 2026
regarding submission of Half Yearly Networth Certificate as on September 30, 2026. Trading
Members of the Exchange are hereby once again advised to submit the Half Yearly Networth
Certificate as on September 30, 2026 through ENIT portal (https://enit.nseindia.com/MemberPortal/)
by November 30, 2026. Detailed manual for submission of Half Yearly Networth Certificate through
ENIT is available in the above-mentioned Exchange Circular.

Members are advised to take note of the above and submit the Networth certificate on or before
November 30, 2026 to avoid any penal/disciplinary action, as stipulated in Annexure 1.3 of NSE
circular ref no. NSE/INSP/73792 dated April 17, 2026.

For any support, please reach out to the helpdesk on 1800 266 0050 (Select IVR option 3) or email
at memcompliance_support@nse.co.in.

For and on behalf of
