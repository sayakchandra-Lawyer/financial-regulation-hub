Refinance Schemes applicable to Housing Finance Companies

NHB currently has the following refinance schemes in operation for HFCs :

                                                         Scheme    Short
No.                                                               Name

                                             Full Name           RS

    1. Regular Refinance Scheme *

2. Affordable Housing Fund                                       AHF

3. Refinance Scheme for Construction Finance for Affordable RCF
     Housing

* Concession in interest rates is offered for refinance availed in respect of the following loans :

 o Loans upto 10 lakhs
 o Loans in rural areas (as per 2011 Census)
 o Loans to women
 o Loans to persons of 3rd gender, disabled or differently abled persons
 o Loans to persons belonging to Scheduled Castes / Scheduled Tribes (where the beneficiary is owner /

      co-owner of financed property)

 o Loans for green housing
 o Loans for Aspirational Districts as circulated by Niti Aayog
 o Loans for Hilly Areas viz. Himachal Pradesh and Uttarakhand, North-Eastern Sates, J&K and

     Ladakh.

Note to Concession:
    · Only one concession at a time will be available
    · In case of loans to women, concession will be available where the woman is owner / co-owner of financed
         property
    · In case of loans to 3rd gender, disabled or differently abled persons, concession will be available where
         the beneficiary is owner / co-owner of financed property
    · In case of loans for green housing, concession will be available for loans certified by green rating
         agencies viz. GRIHA, IGBC etc. (4 or above star/Platinum or Gold star)
    · This special discount in refinance rates is available till availability of corpus (wherever applicable) or
         31.03.2025 whichever is earlier.
