धारा 23 की उपधारा (3) द्वारा प्रित्त िजिर्ों का प्रर्ोग करते हुए, कंपनी (अनुज्ञेर् अजधकाठरताओं में साधारण िेर्र सूचीबद्ध

1. (1) इन जनर्मों का संजिप्त नाम (अनुज्ञेर् अजधकाठरताओं में साधारण िेर्र सूचीबद्ध करना) संिोधन जनर्म, 2026

G.S.R. 968(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (3) of section 23 read with section 469 of the

Companies Act, 2013 (18 of 2013), the Central Government hereby makes the following rules further to amend the

Companies (Listing of equity shares in permissible jurisdictions) Rules, 2024, namely:̶

6  THE GAZETTE OF INDIA : EXTRAORDINARY                             [PART II—SEC. 3(i)]



1. (1) These rules may be called the Companies (Listing of equity instruments in permissible jurisdictions) Amendment

Rules, 2026.



   (2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette.



2. Throughout the Companies (Listing of equity shares in permissible jurisdictions) Rules, 2024 (hereinafter referred

to as the principal rules), for the word “shares”, wherever it occurs, the word “instruments’ shall be substituted.



3. In the principal rules, in sub-rule (1) of rule 2, after clause (b), the following clause shall be inserted, namely: -



’(ba) “equity instruments” means equity shares, convertible debentures, preference shares issued by an Indian

Company;’



4. In the principal rules, in rule 3,-



(i) in clause (a), after the word, “companies”, the words, “other than public companies in the International Financial

Services Centres jurisdiction”, shall be inserted;



(ii) after clause (b), the following clause shall be inserted, namely:–



“(c) unlisted public companies in the International Financial Services Centres jurisdiction, in so far as they are in

accordance with the provisions of the International Financial Services Centres Authority Act, 2019 (50 of 2019) and

with the regulations framed thereunder.”.



5. In the principal rules, in rule 4, for the words “existing shareholders” occurring at both the places, the words “existing

holders” shall be substituted.



6. In the principal rules, in rule 5,



      (i) in clause (d), after the words “has a negative net worth” the following proviso, shall be inserted, namely: -



                     “Provided that a company having a negative net worth shall be eligible to issue its equity

               instruments for listing, if the issuer undertakes to allot at least seventy-five per cent. of the net offer

               to qualified institutional buyers in accordance with the regulations framed in this regard by the

               respective securities market regulator in permissible jurisdiction for purpose of this rule.”.



(ii) in clause (e), in the proviso, for the words “two years”, the words, “more than six months” shall be substituted;



(iii) in clause (g), after the words “specified period”, the words “and such default has not been made good” shall be

inserted;



7. In the principal rules, in the Second Schedule, for Form LEAP – 1, the following form shall be substituted, namely:̶
