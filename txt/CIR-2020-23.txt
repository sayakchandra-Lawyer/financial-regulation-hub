        2. On account of the pandemic caused by the COVID-19, representations have

       been received in this Ministry, requesting that the timelines related to filing of

       3. In view of the above, the Central Government in exercise of its powers under
       section 460 read with section 4O3 of the Act and the Companies (Registration
        Offices and Fees) Rules, 2014 (Fees Rules) has decided to introduce a Scheme,

       modification of charges under the Companies Act, 2013" for the purpose of

       condoning the delay in filing certain forms related to creation/ modification of

         charges.

(ii) Applicability: The scheme shall be applicable in respect of l-rling of Form No.

(iii) Relaxation of time:
   (a) In case a form is filed in respect of a situation covered under sub-para

   (b) In case a form is filed in respect of a situation covered under sub-para
