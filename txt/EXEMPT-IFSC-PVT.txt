          G.S.R. 9(E).--In exercise of the powers conferred by clauses (a) and (b) of sub-section (1) of section 462 and in
pursuance of sub-section (2) of the said section of the Companies Act, 2013 (18 of 2013), the Central Government, in the
interest of public, hereby directs that certain provisions of the Companies Act, 2013 (18 of 2013), as specified in column
(2) of the Table, shall not apply or shall apply with such exceptions, modifications and adaptations as specified in column
