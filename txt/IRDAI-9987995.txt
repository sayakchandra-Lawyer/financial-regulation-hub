Attention is drawn to the directions of the Hon’ble Supreme Court, in CA No.
14369/2025 National Insurance Company Ltd. Vs. Smt. Thungala Dhana Laxmi &
others); wherein it has directed to bring in a four-layer motor insurance policy structure
offering insurance cover against i) Third Party liability, ii) Legal liability to occupants,
iii) Personal accident, and iv) Own damage of the vehicle; with respect to private
vehicles (4 wheelers and 2 wheelers). This is to bring clarity about availability of
various policy options to the vehicle-owner so as to allow him/her to make an informed
choice of purchasing suitable insurance protection. It has also stated that the IRDAI
in consultation with General Insurance Council and insurance companies, shall
formulate uniform policy wordings for the above optional covers.

3. All General Insurers carrying on motor insurance business, shall provide the cover
vide a policy as per four-layer motor insurance policy structure adopting uniform
wordings along with mandatory option form and customer information sheet for all
private vehicles including new cars and new two wheelers. Obtaining the duly
completed option form from customers is mandatory for all insurance policies issued
to this category of vehicles.

4. For existing policies also, if cover under section II is sought during the currency
of the policy, the same shall be made available upon the payment of an additional
premium. However, the risk shall start from the date and time mentioned in the
endorsement issued thereupon. This option can only be availed from the insurer
who has issued the Third Party insurance policy. No existing Motor Third Party
Insurance need to be cancelled by either the insurer or the insured for opting the
additional covers.
