RBI issues Draft Amendment Directions for ‘Preventive Safeguards for Electronic Banking Transactions (EBTs)’ | 
 The Reserve Bank had issued the Amendment Directions dated June 24, 2026 on ‘Customer Protection in Fraudulent Electronic Banking Transactions’ which, inter alia, strengthened the existing framework for limiting liability of customers in unauthorised Electronic Banking Transactions (EBTs). While the revised framework primarily addresses customer liability in the event of fraudulent EBTs, a need is felt to strengthen the same from a preventive perspective by providing customers with greater control over the manner and extent to which digital payment channels may be used.
 Further, on a review to bring in uniformity in practices adopted by banks and ensure that customers are protected against specific acts of omission or commission on part of the banks, it has been decided to expand the scope of the definition of ‘negligence by a bank’ specified in the aforementioned Amendment Directions.
 2. Accordingly, the Reserve Bank has today issued the following draft Amendment Directions for public comments, which propose to amend Directions on Responsible Business Conduct issued by the Department of Regulation, RBI:
 Reserve Bank of India (Commercial Banks - Responsible Business Conduct) Fifth Amendment Directions, 2026

 Reserve Bank of India (Small Finance Banks - Responsible Business Conduct) Fifth Amendment Directions, 2026

 Reserve Bank of India (Payments Banks - Responsible Business Conduct) Third Amendment Directions, 2026

 Reserve Bank of India (Local Area Banks - Responsible Business Conduct) Fifth Amendment Directions, 2026

 Reserve Bank of India (Regional Rural Banks - Responsible Business Conduct) Fifth Amendment Directions, 2026

 Reserve Bank of India (Urban Co-operative Banks - Responsible Business Conduct) Fifth Amendment Directions, 2026

 Reserve Bank of India (Rural Co-operative Banks - Responsible Business Conduct) Fifth Amendment Directions, 2026

 3. The comments / feedback on the draft Amendment Directions may be submitted by the regulated entities and members of public / other stakeholders on or before December 14, 2026 through the following channels:
 the ‘Connect 2 Regulate’ section on the website by following the corresponding hyperlink provided against each document in the page where they are hosted; or

 by email with the subject line ‘Feedback on (full name of the draft Amendment Directions (including the type of Regulated Entity))’.
