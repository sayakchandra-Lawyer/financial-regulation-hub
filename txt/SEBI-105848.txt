        SEBI hosts IOSCO’s Asia-Pacific Regional Committee Regulatory
                    Roundtable on Market Infrastructure Institutions

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI), on November 16-17, 2026,
hosted the IOSCO’s Asia-Pacific Regional Committee (APRC) Regulatory
Roundtable on Market Infrastructure Institutions (MIIs) in Mumbai. The
programme brought together securities market regulators, market infrastructure
institutions and other stakeholders from across the Asia-Pacific region.

SEBI Symposium on Technology Solutions for the Securities Market

The programme commenced with a SEBI Symposium on Technology Solutions for
the Securities Market that featured demonstrations of technology solutions and
systems developed by SEBI in the areas of Supervisory Technology (SupTech) and
Artificial Intelligence (AI) for applications relating to the securities market. The
demonstrations provided participants with insights into how technology and data-
driven approaches can support regulatory supervision, surveillance and market
oversight. The Symposium also featured a demonstration by Nasdaq on the use of
Artificial Intelligence in Market Surveillance.

Joint APRC-WFE Session

A joint APRC-World Federation of Exchanges (WFE) session provided an
opportunity for Regulators and Exchanges to share perspectives on the evolving
role of exchanges and market infrastructure institutions in modern capital markets,
the regulatory considerations underpinning their functioning, and the importance of
developing robust, efficient and resilient market infrastructure.

APRC Regulatory Roundtable

The programme concluded with a Regulatory Roundtable focused on two key areas
of contemporary relevance to securities markets viz. Accelerating Settlement: T+1
and Tokenization.

The session on T+1 settlement explored the experiences of regulators and markets
in transitioning towards shorter settlement cycles, benefits of accelerated settlement
and the challenges associated with such transitions.

The session on Tokenization examined the potential applications and benefits of
distributed ledger technology and tokenization in securities markets. Discussions
included potential efficiencies in issuance, trading and settlement, as well as the
evolving regulatory, technological, legal and risk considerations related to these
technological developments.

SEBI Chairman Mr. Tuhin Kanta Pandey, Vice-Chair of the APRC, acknowledged
the support of IOSCO, APRC and WFE members in making the Roundtable a
success.
