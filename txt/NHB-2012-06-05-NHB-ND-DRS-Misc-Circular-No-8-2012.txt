NHB (ND)/DRS/Misc. Circular No.8/2012  NATIONAL
June 5, 2012  HOUSING BANK
All Registered Housing Finance Companies (HFCs)
Dear Sirs,
Foreign Exchange Management Act, 1999 -
Providing housing loan in rupees to NRIs/PIOs
Please refer to our Circular NHB /ND/HFC (P&D)/1.33/1474/2003 dated June 2, 2003 on
the above subject. One of the registered housing finance company has sought clarification
as to whether it can provide housing loan to a non-resident Indian (NRI) or a person of
Indian origin (PIO) in view of the term "housing finance institution in India approved by
the National Housing Bank" used in Regulation 8 of Notification No. FEMA 4/2000-RB
dated May 3, 2000.
2. In this connection, we invite your attention to the provisions contained in Foreign
Exchange Management (Borrowing and Lending in rupees) Regulations, 2000 (Notification
No. FEMA 4/2000 – RB dated May 3, 2000) as also A.P(DIR Series) Circular No.95 dated
April 26,2003 and A.P (DIR Series) Circular No. 93 datedMay25, 2004 issued by the
Reserve Bank of India which, inter alia, provide for housing loans in rupees to NRIs /PIOs.
Reserve Bank of India has, vide its letter dated March 19, 2012, further clarified that the
term "housing finance institution in India approved by the National Housing Bank" in
Regulation 8 of Notification No. FEMA 4/2000 – RB dated May 3, 2000 may be interpreted
as a housing finance institution regulated by the National Housing Bank and for the
purpose is registered with the National Housing Bank.
3. We hope this clarifies the position.
