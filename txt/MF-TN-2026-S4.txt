                                      NOTIFICATIONS BY GOVERNMENT

                                                                HOME DEPARTMENT
                                                                        (Police-XIII)

          APPOINTMENT OF DISTRICT COLLECTORS OF RESPECTIVE DISTRICTS AS REGISTERING
                              AUTHORITY UNDER THE TAMIL NADU MONEY LENDING ENTITIES
                                            PREVENTION OF COERCIVE ACTIONS ACT, 2025.

         [G.O. Ms. No.85, Home (Police-XIII), 16th February 2026,  4, ,  -2057.]

No. II(2)/HO/55(e)/2026.

      In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 4 of the Tamil Nadu Money Lending Entities
(Prevention of Coercive Actions) Act, 2025 (Tamil Nadu Act 40 of 2025) the Governor of Tamil Nadu hereby appoints
the District Collectors of respective districts as the Registering Authority of money lending entities and defines the
respective district as their area of Jurisdiction.

                                                                                                      DHEERAJ KUMAR,
                                                                                      Additional Chief Secretary to Government.
