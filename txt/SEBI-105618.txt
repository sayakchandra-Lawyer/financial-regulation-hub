CIRCULAR  November 05, 2026

HO/19/34/11(6)2026-AFD-POD1/I/25244/2026

To,
All Alternative Investment Funds (“AIFs”)
All recognised Accreditation Agencies
All Portfolio Managers
All Investment Advisers
All Research Analysts
All Mutual Funds

Sir/ Madam,

        Sub: Review of the framework for Accredited Investors

1. With an objective to ease and expedite the process of accreditation, the SEBI
        (Alternative Investment Funds) Regulations, 2012 (“AIF Regulations”), have
        been amended and notified on October 15, 2026.

2. The modalities for implementing these provisions are specified below.

3. Manager led accreditation

        3.1. A Manager may determine and record an investor’s accredited status at
                 onboarding or during the tenure of a scheme, after verifying that the
                 investor meets eligibility criteria under the AIF Regulations and this
                 circular. For the purpose of accreditation, “Manager” means the manager
                 of an AIF, the asset management company of a Specialized Investment
                 Fund (SIF) (as defined under SEBI (Mutual Funds) Regulations, 2026)
                 or a portfolio manager (as defined under SEBI (Portfolio Managers)
                 Regulations, 2026), as applicable.

        3.2. A determination made by a Manager shall be recognised across
                 Managers of the same Group for AIFs, SIFs and portfolio management
                 services during the validity period specified in paragraph 4. “Group” has
                 the meaning assigned to “corporate group” under Explanation I to
                 Regulation 19F(1) of the AIF Regulations.
        3.3. An Investment Adviser (IA) or Research Analyst (RA) shall not determine
                 accredited status of an investor independently, but may rely on a valid
                 determination made by a Manager in its Group.

        3.4. The Manager shall record the accreditation status of an investor and
                 shall confirm the same in writing to the investor. The Manager shall
                 inform the investor of the date of accreditation and the validity period.

        3.5. Manager led accreditation is an additional and optional route. The
                 Accreditation Agency route shall continue to be available. When an
                 investor furnishes a valid certificate of accreditation from a recognised
                 Accreditation Agency, the Manager shall rely on it and shall not conduct
                 fresh determination during its validity.

4. Validity of accreditation

        4.1. Accreditation determined by a Manager shall be valid for a period of three
                 years from the date of accreditation.

        4.2. Accreditation shall be determined afresh each time an investor is
                 onboarded by a Manager who is not of the same Group.

        4.3. A certificate of accreditation issued by a recognised Accreditation
                 Agency shall be valid for three years from the date of accreditation.

        4.4. An investor who is an Accredited Investor at the time of onboarding into
                 a scheme shall continue to be reckoned as an Accredited Investor for
                 the remaining life of that scheme.

        4.5. A certificate of accreditation issued before the date of applicability of this
                 circular shall remain valid for its unexpired period of validity.

5. Securities market exposure

        5.1. The minimum qualifying securities market exposure shall be INR 5 crore
                 in the case of an individual, Hindu Undivided Family, family trust or sole
                 proprietorship, and INR 20 crore in the case of a body corporate or a
                 trust other than a family trust.

        5.2. The following shall qualify as securities market exposure:

                 5.2.1. Securities held in dematerialised form;

                 5.2.2. Mutual fund investment held through mutual fund folios;
5.2.3. Unlisted securities held in dematerialised form;

5.2.4. Investment in the overseas securities market; and

5.2.5. Investments under the National Pension System.

5.3. For securities held in dematerialised form, the value reflected in the
         eCAS statement as of the statement date shall be used.

5.4. For unlisted securities and overseas securities market investments not
         valued in the eCAS statement, the applicant shall furnish a certificate
         from a practising Chartered Accountant. The certificate shall identify the
         nature of securities, valuation date, and value.

6. Safeguards for Manager led accreditation

6.1. Accountability: A Manager undertaking accreditation shall be
         responsible for ensuring compliance with the prescribed eligibility criteria
         and with the requirements of this circular.

6.2. Maintenance of records: A Manager shall maintain all records,
         supporting documents and verification evidence relied upon for the
         determining accredited status, for atleast five years from the date of
         accreditation or until five years after the investor ceases to be an investor
         in any scheme managed by the Manager, whichever is later. The records
         shall be made available for inspection as and when sought.

6.3. Audit: Periodic internal audit and compliance review of the accreditation
         process shall be carried out as part of any existing audit undertaken by
         the Manager.

6.4. Accreditation policy: Every Manager undertaking accreditation shall
         adopt an accreditation policy specifying:

6.4.1. conflict of interest controls, including separation of the
          accreditation function from the business development and
          distribution function;

6.4.2.  an escalation and grievance redressal process for resolving
        investor concerns arising from the determination of accredited
        status; and
6.4.3.  record retention infrastructure, covering inter alia data privacy
        and cybersecurity, so that the confidentiality of the information
        submitted by investors is protected.

6.5. The Manager shall document the accreditation decision, the eligibility
         criterion relied upon and the supporting verification in a manner that
         enables the decision to be independently reviewed.

7. Eligibility criteria and documents for verification

7.1. The Manager or Accreditation Agency, as applicable, shall verify that the
         applicant meets the eligibility criteria specified under Regulation 2(1)(ab)
         of the AIF Regulations, based on income, net worth or securities market
         exposure, using the documents specified in the table below:

8. Accreditation of Limited Liability Partnerships

        8.1. A Limited Liability Partnership (LLP) set up under the Limited Liability
                 Partnership Act, 2008, shall be recognised as an Accredited Investor
                 where each partner independently satisfies the eligibility criteria for
                 accreditation, applicable to that partner as on the date of determination.

        8.2. Where a partner is itself a body corporate, including another LLP, a firm
                 or a trust, that partner shall independently satisfy the eligibility criteria
                 applicable to it.
9. Persons Resident Outside India

9.1. A Person Resident Outside India, as defined under the Foreign
         Exchange Management Act, 1999, shall be deemed to be an Accredited
         Investor and shall not be required to obtain a certificate of accreditation
         or undergo Manager led accreditation.

9.2. The Manager shall record the basis on which the investor has been
         identified as a Person Resident Outside India.

9.3. Deemed Accredited Investor status shall not dilute the obligations of an
         AIF and its Manager, as reporting entities under the Prevention of
         Money-laundering Act, 2002 and the rules framed thereunder, to conduct
         client due diligence, identify beneficial owners and comply with
         applicable requirements relating to source of funds.

10. Applicability

10.1. This circular shall come into force with immediate effect.

10.2.  This circular is issued in exercise of the powers conferred under Section
       11(1) of the Securities and Exchange Board of India Act, 1992 read with
       Regulations 2 (1) (ab) and 36 of the AIF Regulations, to protect the
       interests of investors in securities and to promote the development of,
       and to regulate, the securities market.

10.3.  This circular is available on the SEBI website at www.sebi.gov.in under
       the categories “Legal → Circulars” and “Info for → Alternative Investment
       Funds”.
