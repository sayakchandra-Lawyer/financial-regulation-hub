Madam/Sir,
Refinance Circular- Release of Excess Loan Pool
Housing Finance Companies (HFC) having Refinance Outstanding with NHB may
submit a request for approval of release of their excess flagged loan pool if any, by
complying the sanction conditions after a lock-in period of two years from the date of
disbursement of the loan. HFCs can raise such request only once in two years. Approval
on releasing excess flagged loan, if any, will be given post assessment by the NHB, for
releasing the excess flagged loan pool of equivalent amount towards outstanding subject
to terms and conditions of sanction letter and approval of the Competent Authority.
The decision to release the excess flagged loan pool will vest with the Competent
Authority of NHB and the decision will be final and binding to the HFC.
