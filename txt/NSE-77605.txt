NSE Clearing Limited              Date: November 25, 2026

  Department: COMPLIANCE
 Download Ref No: NCL/CMPL/77605
 Circular Ref. No: 38/2026

All Members

Sub: Reminder for Submission of Half Yearly Networth Certificate as on September 30, 2026.

This has reference to our circular ref no. NCL/CMPL/76751 issued on October 06, 2026 Submission of
Half Yearly Networth Certificate as on September 30, 2026.

In view of the above, members are hereby advised to submit the Half Yearly Networth Certificate as
on September 30, 2026 as per prescribed formats on or before November 30, 2026.

Members may note that as per Chapter X of the SEBI (Stockbrokers) Regulations, 2026, Net worth
requirement for members shall be Base Net worth or Variable Net worth, whichever is higher.
Members are further advised to refer to NCL Circular ref no. NCL/CMPL/55460 dated February 01,
2023 and NCL/CMPL/72712 dated February 09, 2026 for clarification on ascertaining Net worth as
per SEBI Gazette Notification No. SEBI/LAD- NRO/GN/2022/73 dated February 23, 2022.

It is reiterated that as per SEBI PR No.58/2026 dated September 17, 2026 and NCL circular no.
NCL/CMPL/76430 dated September 18, 2026 regarding “Samuhik Prativedan Manch- A Technology
based sharing mechanism of common submissions extended to Members of Clearing Corporations”,
Clearing/Self Clearing Members of the Clearing Corporation who are also registered with NSE will be
required to submit their Half Yearly Net worth Certificate to NSE only. NSE in turn will share the Half
Yearly Net worth Certificate (including data/documents) of the Common Clearing/Self Clearing
Members with the Clearing Corporation(s).

Submitting data to NSE alone will be treated as submission to the Clearing Corporation and the same
will be in compliance of the regulatory requirements for such Members.

It may further be noted that, Clearing/Self Clearing Members of NCL who are not registered with NSE
for submissions and Professional Clearing members registered with NCL are required to make
submissions directly to NCL through the NCL portal at link
(https://ims.connect2nsccl.com/NCLMemberPortal/).

Non-submission of Net worth certificates within the due date or in case of any shortfall, failure to
submit a revised Net worth certificate along with the original Net worth certificate as on September
30, 2026, shall result in action as prescribed in NCL Master Circular ref no. NCL/CMPL/74024 dated
April 30, 2026, administrative circular ref no. NCL/CMPL/67927 dated May 8, 2025 and
NCL/CMPL/70806 dated October 14, 2025.

Any issues faced while uploading the networth certificate in the NCL portal may be sent to the email
id “membership@nseclearing.co.in” with the subject line “Half Yearly Networth Certificate as on
September 30, 2026 – Issues in networth Submission – “<Name of the Clearing member and Member
code>”.

All members are advised to take note of the same and comply.
