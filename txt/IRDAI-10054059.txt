भारतीय बीमा विवियामक और विकास प्राविकरण (`आईआरडीएआई’ अथिा `प्राविकरण’) िे मेससस
स्टार हेल्थ एण्ड अलायड इंश्योरंेसं कपिी वलवमटेड (`बीमाकतास’)े क विरूद्ध, 31 जििरी से 11 फरिरी
2022 तक वकये गये एक विरीक्षण तथा अिुिती कारण बताओ िोवटस वििांक 4 विसंबर 2024 और 18
जूि 2025 को आयोवजत एक िैयक्तिक सुििाई से उत्पन्न वकया गया एक आिेश पाररत वकया है।

प्राविकरण िे पाया वक कोविड-19 िािों सवहत, स्वास्थ्य बीमा िािों को संभालिे मंे बीमाकतास िे
असमथसिीय आिारों पर िािों को विराृकत वकया था। प्राविकरण िे यह भी पाया वक बीमाकतास िे ऐसी
कटौवतयाँा की थीं जो पावलसी की शतोंे क द्वारा समवथसत िहीं थीं, तथा कई प्रवतिशीृकत मामलों में, उि
रावश का भुगतािे किल प्राविकरण द्वारा उजागर करिेे क बाि ही वकया गया था। पावलसीिारकों को
विवशष्ट पावलसी की शतें सूवित िहीं की गई थीं वजिेक अंतगसत िािों का विराकरण वकया गया था अथिा
उन्हंे कम वकया गया था, वजससे उन्हें इि विणसयों को िुिौती िेिेे क वलए प्रामावणक आिार से िंवित
वकया गया था।

प्राविकरण िे बीमा अविवियम, 1938 की िारा 102े क अिीि रु. 2 करोड़ काु कल अथसिंड लगाया है

The Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (‘IRDAI’ or ‘Authority’) has
passed an Order against M/s Star Health and Allied Insurance Company Limited (‘Insurer’)
arising from an inspection conducted from 31st January to 11th February 2022, following a
Show Cause Notice dated 4th December 2024 and a personal hearing on 18th June 2025.

The Authority found that, in handling health insurance claims including COVID-19 claims, the
Insurer had repudiated claims on untenable grounds. The Authority further found that the
insurer had made deductions which were not supported by the policy terms, and in many
sampled cases, the amount was paid only after the Authority highlighted them. Policyholders
were not informed of the specific policy conditions under which claims were repudiated or
reduced, depriving them of an informed basis to challenge these decisions.

The Authority has imposed a total penalty of Rs. 2 crore under Section 102 of the Insurance
Act, 1938, comprising Rs. 1 crore for violation of Regulation 27(v) of the IRDAI (Health
Insurance) Regulations, 2016 in respect of repudiation of claims, and Rs. 1 crore for violations
of Regulations 27(v), 33(d)(ii) and 33(d)(iv) of the said Regulations and Regulation
21(3)(c)(i)(c) of the IRDAI (Third Party Administrators – Health Services) Regulations, 2016 in
respect of deductions from claims and communication of claim decisions.

The Insurer has also been warned for not ensuring that discounts agreed with network
hospitals are reflected in hospital bills and made known to policyholders (Regulation 20(9) of
the said TPA Regulations), and directed to ensure that its claim settlement letters itemise all
allowed, disallowed, non-payable and discount amounts and the reduction in Sum Insured. It
was further cautioned for collecting renewal premium more than 90 days in advance
(Regulation 13(iii) of the Health Insurance Regulations) and for not providing Insurance
Ombudsman details to complainants; its corrective steps on these were taken on record. The
Order is to be placed before the Insurer’s Board, with an Action Taken Report to follow.

The detailed Order is available at https://irdai.gov.in/web/guest/document-
detail?documentId=10053459.

IRDAI remains committed to policyholder protection and fair, transparent settlement of claims.
