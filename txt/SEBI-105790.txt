SEBI Constitutes a Sub-Group of PMAC to Review Specified Provisions of the SEBI
                                          (ICDR) Regulations, 2018

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has constituted a Sub-Group of the
Primary Market Advisory Committee (PMAC) to review Chapters II, IIA, IV, V and VI of
the SEBI (Issue of Capital and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2018 (“ICDR
Regulations”), along with the relevant Schedules and related regulatory directions.

The review aims to identify opportunities to facilitate ease of access to the capital market
for eligible issuers, simplify and rationalise regulatory requirements, eliminate duplicative
disclosures and procedures, and improve regulatory clarity and consistency, while
maintaining adequate disclosures, investor protection and market integrity.

The Terms of Reference of the Sub-Group are as follows:

1. Review of Eligibility Conditions: Review of eligibility conditions applicable to issuers
    under the relevant modes of capital raising, including conditions governing IPOs, FPOs,
    preferential issues and QIPs, as applicable.

2. Review of Lock-in Provisions: Review of lock-in requirements applicable to promoters,
    pre-issue capital, securities allotted under preferential issues and other relevant
    categories, as applicable to the respective modes of capital raising.

3. Review of Application, Allocation and Allotment Mechanism: Review of the application
    process, allocation methodology and allotment mechanism applicable to the respective
    modes of capital raising, including related procedural requirements.

4. Review of Disclosure Requirements: Review and rationalisation of disclosure
    requirements applicable to IPOs, FPOs, preferential issues and QIPs, including
    disclosures in offer documents and other relevant documents prescribed under the ICDR
    Regulations and the relevant Schedules.

5. Review of Pricing and Price Discovery Framework: Review of pricing requirements and
    the applicable price discovery framework, including determination and disclosure of the
    price or price band, floor price and other related requirements, as applicable to the
    respective modes of capital raising.

6. Review of Due Diligence Requirements: Review of the format, content and requirements
    relating to due diligence certificates and other due diligence submissions by lead
    manager(s) and other relevant intermediaries, as applicable.

7. Review of Related Regulatory Directions: Review of relevant SEBI circulars, advisories,
    general orders and other directions or instructions issued in relation to the aforesaid
    provisions, with a view to identifying the need for their consolidation, modification or
    alignment with the ICDR Regulations.

8. Review of Other Related Requirements: Examination of other regulatory and procedural
    requirements under the aforesaid Chapters and Schedules, including inconsistencies,
    ambiguities, duplicative requirements and implementation-related issues, and
    recommendation of appropriate changes, wherever necessary.

9. Other Matters: Examination of any other matter incidental or relevant to the above, as
    considered necessary by the Sub-Group.

The Sub-Group may co-opt additional members or invite subject matter experts and

necessary secretarial and technical assistance in its deliberations.

The recommendations of the Sub-Group will be placed before PMAC for consideration.
