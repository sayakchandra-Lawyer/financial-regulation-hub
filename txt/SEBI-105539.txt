Subject: Cooling off period for offer for sale by Private listed InvITs

1. SEBI vide Circular No. SEBI/HO/MRD-PoD2/CIR/P/2024/00181 dated December
    30, 2024 and circular no. SEBI/HO/MRD/MRD-PoD-3/P/CIR/2023/134 dated
    August 03, 2023 specified the comprehensive framework on Offer for Sale (OFS)
    of shares including units of REITs and InvITs through stock exchange mechanism.

2. Based on the feedback received from market participants and in order to facilitate
    ease of doing business, it has been decided to reduce the cooling off period for
    illiquid private listed InvITs. Accordingly, Paragraph 19.2 under Chapter 1 of SEBI
    Master Circular No. SEBI/HO/MRD-PoD2/CIR/P/2024/00181 dated December
    30, 2024, has been modified as under:

   “19.2 OFS Framework for sale of units of Real Estate Investment Trusts (REITs)
   and Infrastructure Investment Trusts (InvITs)

Page 1 of 2
    The OFS framework prescribed at paragraph 19.1 above for equity shares of listed
    companies shall also be applicable for OFS of units of REITs and InvITs subject to
    the following -

    (i) In case of OFS for InvITs, the trading lot shall be same as the trading lot in
             the secondary market as prescribed in SEBI (Infrastructure Investment
             Trusts) Regulations, 2014

    (ii) The provisions related to retail investors specified in paragraph 19.1 above
             shall not be applicable in case of OFS for private listed InvITs and such OFS
             shall remain open only for one day (i.e. T day)

    (iii) The cooling off period in case of private listed InvITs whose units are illiquid
             shall be ±8 weeks”

3. This circular shall come into force with immediate effect.

4. All recognized stock exchanges are advised to:
      a) take necessary steps and put in place necessary systems for implementation
          of the above.
      b) make necessary amendments to the relevant bye-laws, rules and regulations,
          wherever required, for the implementation of the above; and
      c) bring the provisions of this circular to the notice of market participants
          (including investors) and also to disseminate the same on their website;

5. This circular is issued in exercise of the powers conferred under Section 11(1) of
    the Securities and Exchange Board of India Act, 1992 to protect the interest of
    investors in securities and to promote the development of and to regulate the
    securities market.

6. This Circular is available on the website of the Securities and Exchange Board of
    India at www.sebi.gov.in under the category “Legal” and under the drop down
    “Circulars”.
