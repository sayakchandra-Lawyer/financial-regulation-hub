Sir/ Madam,

Extension of due date for submission of various returns to the Department of Supervision (DoS)

With a view to redress the hardship faced by the housing finance companies (HFCs) due the COVID-19
pandemic, the National Housing Bank in exercise of its powers under the provisions of Paragraph 46 of
the Housing Finance Companies (NHB) Directions, 2010, hereby-

(i) extends the due date of all monthly returns, which will become due for submission upto June 30,
     2020, for a further period of 15 days from the prescribed due date of submission of the returns
     respectively; and

(ii) extends the due date of all quarterly, half-yearly and annual returns, which will become due for
     submission upto June 30, 2020, for a further period of 30 days from the prescribed due date of
     submission of the returns respectively.

2.        The extension is being accorded for the specified period and for the specific purpose to grant

extensions of time to the HFCs to comply with the provisions, and shall not be construed as an exemption

from any of the provisions of these Directions either generally or otherwise.

3.        Days mentioned above will include holidays and weekends. The above extension will not be

applicable to returns/ information which are to be reported as-and-when basis. The HFCs capable of

submitting the returns earlier on best effort basis may continue to do so.

4.        Instructions issued by the other regulators/ authorities in this regard shall be followed insofar

as they are not inconsistent with any of these instructions.

This arrangement will continue until further review.

Regards,
