16.11.2026:   We have called the appeal multiple times, however, the

Appellant remained absent all the time and was not represented.

2. We notice that on the last date also the Appellant was not represented
before us. In the interest of justice, we are passing over this appeal to be
taken up in the revised list/ revised call.

Revised List

3. We again called the Appellant in revised list, however, the Appellant
remained unrepresented even after given multiple calls. We have been
informed that there is no adjournment request either physical or through
email.

4. We also notice that earlier in the morning when we have called this
appeal nobody was present for the Appellant and even when this appeal was
last listed, the Appellant was unrepresented, however, we did not pass any
adverse order in the interest of justice and adjourned the appeal to be taken
up today.

5. In view of the fact that the Appellant was unrepresented before us on
the last date, as well as in the morning and even now when the list has been
revised, it appears that the Appellant has lost interest in pursuing this appeal.
Therefore, in view of the above facts and circumstances, this appeal is
dismissed for non-prosecution.
