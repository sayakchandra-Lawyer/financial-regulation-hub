Dear Sirs,
MOST IMPORTANT TERMS AND CONDITIONS
To facilitate quick and good understanding of the major terms and conditions of
housing loan agreed upon between a Housing Finance Company(HFC) and the
individual borrower, it has been decided that HFCs shall henceforth obtain a
document containing the most important terms and conditions of such loan in all cases.
A suggestive format of such document is annexed (Annexure A).
This document will be in addition to the existing loan and security documents being
obtained by the HFCs. HFCs are advised to prepare the said document in duplicate and
in the language understandable by the borrower. Duplicate copy duly executed
between the HFC and the borrower should be handed over to the borrower under
acknowledgement.
Please acknowledge receipt and ensure compliance.
Yours faithfully,
