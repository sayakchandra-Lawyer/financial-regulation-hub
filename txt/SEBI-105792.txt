                                             PART B — DETAILED APPLICATION

1. Brief background of the Applicant and eligibility under the Scheme

1.1 Navigant Corporate Advisors Limited (the “Applicant™) is a company incorporated under the Companies
Act, 1956 and holds a valid Certificate of Registration as a Merchant Banker granted by the Securities and
Exchange Board of India (“SEBI” / “Board”) under the SEBI (Merchant Bankers) Regulations, 1992 (the “MB
Regulations”). The Applicant is duly compliant with all applicable provisions of the MB Regulations.

1.2 Being an intermediary registered with the Board, the Applicant is eligible to seek informal guidance under
Paragraph 6(a) of the Scheme.

1.3 The application relates to the Applicant’s own, existing and continuing business activities. The Applicant
therefore has a direct and proximate interest in the subject matter of the application, and the application does
not involve any hypothetical situation.

2. Purpose of this application

2.1 The Applicant seeks an Interpretive Letter under Paragraph 7(b) of the Scheme on the interpretation and
applicability of Regulation 13A of the MB Regulations, as amended, specifically in relation to valuation
activities carried out by the Applicant under:

       (a) the Income-tax Act, 1961 and/or the Income-tax Act, 2025, read with the Income-tax Rules
       (collectively, the “IT Act”); and

       (b) the Foreign Exchange Management (Non-Debt Instruments) Rules, 2019 framed under the Foreign
       Exchange Management Act, 1999 (the “NDI Rules”).

2.2 The guidance is sought so as to enable the Applicant to determine, with regulatory certainty, whether such
valuation assignments must be concluded within the transitional period prescribed under Regulation 13A(4) of
the MB Regulations, or whether they may lawfully be continued as permissible activities under Regulation
13A(2)(iii).

3. Regulatory framework— Regulation 13A of the MB Regulations

3.1 The SEBI (Merchant Bankers) (Amendment) Regulations, 2025 (the “Amendment Regulations™) were
notified on December 03, 2025 and came into force on January 01, 2026. Regulation 13A of the MB Regulations
was substituted thereby. The relevant sub-regulations are reproduced below for ready reference.

3.2 Regulation 13A(2) — Permissible activities:

       “4 merchant banker may also undertake —

       (i) other activities regulated by the Board in accordance with the respective regulations made by the
      Board by obtaining the relevant certificate of registration; and/or

       (ii) activities which fall under the purview of any other financial sector regulator or an authority specified
       by the Board, in accordance with the regulations or guidelines, if any, issued by such financial sector
       regulator or authority; and/or

       (iii) activities that do not fall under the purview of the Board or any other financial sector regulator or
       authority, which shall be fee-based, non-find based and pertain to the financial services sector;

       on an arms-length basis through separate business units of such merchant banker, in such manner and
       subject to such other conditions as may be specified by the Board.”

3.3 Regulation 13A(4) — Transitional provision for valuation activities:

       “The merchant banker that does not possess the registration for valuation related activities from the
       relevant regulatory authority shall conclude the existing valuation assignments undertaken by it within a
      period of nine months from the date of coming into force of the Securities and Exchange Board of India
       (Merchant Bankers) (Amendment) Regulations, 2025.”
4. Nature of the valuation activities carried out by the Applicant

4.1 The Applicant presently undertakes valuation assignments in the following two distinct regulatory contexts.

4.2 Valuation under the IT Act (IT Act Valuation). The Applicant determines the fair market value (FMV)
of unquoted equity shares and other assets as required under various provisions of the IT Act, including the
provisions corresponding to Section 56(2)(x) and Section S0CA of the Income-tax Act, 1961 read with Rules
11U, 11UA and 11UAA of the Income-tax Rules, 1962 (and the equivalent or successor provisions under the
Income-tax Act, 2025). These valuations are required for fiscal purposes such as computation of income,
determination of deemed consideration and the anti-abuse provisions relating to transfer of property for
inadequate consideration.

4.3 Valuation under the NDI Rules (FEMA Valuation). The Applicant undertakes valuations for the purposes
of pricing of equity instruments in transactions involving foreign direct investment, issue of equity instruments
to persons resident outside India, and transfer of equity instruments between persons resident in India and
persons resident outside India, in compliance with the pricing guidelines prescribed under Rule 21 of the NDI
Rules read with the Foreign Exchange Management Act, 1999.

4.4 In each case the activity is advisory and certification-based in character: it is fee-based, non-fund based, and
pertains to the financial services sector. The Applicant does not assume any principal position, does not deploy
its own funds, and does not undertake any fund-based exposure in connection with such assignments.

5. Position under the IT Act and the NDI Rules regarding eligibility of the valuer

5.1 The Applicant respectfully submits that neither the IT Act nor the NDI Rules prescribes a separate
registration, licence or authorisation as a pre-condition for undertaking the valuation assignments described in
Paragraph 4 above. In particular:

       (i) neither framework requires such valuations to be undertaken only by a person registered as a “registered
       valuer” with the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India under Section 247 of the Companies Act,
       2013 read with the Companies (Registered Valuers and Valuation) Rules, 2017, nor by a person registered
       in any other authority in a valuation-specific capacity; and

       (ii) there is accordingly no “registration for valuation related activities™ issued by any “relevant regulatory
       authority™ that the Applicant could obtain in respect of these particular assignments.

5.2 On the contrary, both frameworks expressly recognise a SEBI-registered Merchant Banker as an
eligible valuer for these purposes. Under the relevant Income-tax Rules, the fair market value of unquoted
equity shares determined under the discounted free cash flow method is required to be determined by amerchant
banker. Similarly, Rule 21 of the NDI Rules requires the price of equity instruments to be worked out in
accordance with any internationally accepted pricing methodology on an arm’s length basis, duly certified by
a Chartered Accountant, a SEBI-registered Merchant Banker or a practising Cost Accountant. The
Applicant’s certificate of registration as a Merchant Banker granted by the Board is therefore itself the
qualification that the applicable frameworks recognise for the performance of these assignments.

6. Legal analysis and submissions

6.1 Regulation 13A(4) is, on its plain language, a transitional provision addressed to a specific class of merchant
bankers, namely those that do not possess “registration for valuation related activities from the relevant
regulatory authority™. The provision presupposes the existence of (a) a relevant regulatory authority, and (b) a
registration granted by that authority for valuation related activities.

6.2 Where the applicable law does not contemplate any such registration for the valuation in question, the
condition that triggers Regulation 13A(4) is not satisfied, and the nine-month wind-down obligation would, on
a purposive and harmonious construction, not be attracted. A contrary reading would produce the anomalous
result that a merchant banker must discontinue an activity which the applicable law expressly permits it to
perform in its capacity as a merchant banker, and for which no registration is available to be obtained.
6.3 The valuation activities described in Paragraph 4 above are not regulated as a distinct or separately registered
financial activity by the Board or by any other financial sector regulator or authority. While the Income-tax Act
and the NDI Rules prescribe the circumstances in which valuations are required and recognise a SEBI-registered
Merchant Banker as an eligible valuer, they do not require the valuer to obtain any separate registration, licence
or authorisation for undertaking such valuation assignments. These activities are advisory and certification-
based in nature, are fee-based and non-fund based, and pertain to the financial services sector. Accordingly, the
Applicant is of the prima facie view that such activities fall within the scope of Regulation 13A(2)(iii) of the
MB Regulations.

6.4 The Applicant is, in the premises, of the prima facie view that such valuation activities may be continued
under Regulation 13A(2)(iii), through a separate business unit and on an arm’s-length basis, subject to
compliance with the conditions prescribed thereunder and with such manner and conditions as may be specified
by the Board, without attracting the transitional obligation under Regulation 13A(4). The Applicant seeks the
Board’s interpretive guidance to confirm this understanding.

6.5 The Applicant confirms that it will, in any event, conduct such activities through a separate business unit on
an arm’s-length basis and in compliance with all conditions that may be specified by the Board in this regard.

7. Query / clarification sought

The Applicant respectfully submits that the interpretation set out above reflects its bona fide understanding of
the applicable legal provisions, and seeks the Board’s guidance in order to obtain regulatory certainty. In view
of the foregoing, the Applicant seeks an Interpretive Letter on the following queries:

Query 1: Whether Navigant Corporate Advisors Limited, a Merchant Banker registered with SEBI, is permitted
to continue carrying out valuation activities under the Income-tax Act, 1961 / Income-tax Act, 2025 (including
FMV-based valuations for fiscal purposes) and under the Foreign Exchange Management (Non-Debt
Instruments) Rules, 2019 (including pricing-related valuations for foreign direct investment and resident-non-
resident transfer transactions), in the absence of any mandatory registration requirement for such valuation
activities under the said laws, without being subject to the nine-month wind-down obligation prescribed under
Regulation 13A(4) of the MB Regulations.
Query 2: Whether such valuation activities, in the absence of any requirement of registration under the
applicable statute or rules, qualify as permissible activities under Regulation 13A(2)(iii) of the MB Regulations
— being fee-based, non-fund-based activities pertaining to the financial services sector which do not fall under
the purview of the Board or of any other financial sector regulator or authority — subject to compliance with
the conditions prescribed thereunder.
