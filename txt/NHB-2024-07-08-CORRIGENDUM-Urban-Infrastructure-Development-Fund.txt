                     Urban Infrastructure Development Fund
                                     CORRIGENDUM

                                                                                         Date - 08th July 2024

         Modifications under Urban Infrastructure Development Fund (UIDF)

It is to inform that the respective States / UTs shall submit the Detailed Project Report
(DPR) of the proposals within 6 months from the date of the issuance of letter of
Provisional Sanction (In-Principle Approval).
In case, the States/UTs is not able to submit the DPR, the Provisional Sanction shall
stand cancelled.
The Regional Offices may communicate the same to the respective States / UTs.
