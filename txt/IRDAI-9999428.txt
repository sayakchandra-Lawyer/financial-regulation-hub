The Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (IRDAI) is observing
Grievance Resolution Month during November 2026, with the objective of facilitating
the expeditious resolution of pending grievances in the insurance sector.

As part of this initiative, 16 Grievance Redressal Camps will be organised across the
country at various locations during November 2026.

The city-wise schedule of the camps is as follows:

S. No.                  City                  Date      Organised By
   1    Guwahati                    13th November 2026     LI Council
   2    Jaipur                      14th November 2026     LI Council
   3    Amaravati (Andhra Pradesh)  16th November 2026    GI Council
   4    Chandigarh                  16th November 2026     LI Council
   5    Chennai                     17th November 2026    GI Council
   6    Bhopal                      18th November 2026    GI Council
   7    Lucknow                     18th November 2026     LI Council
   8    Dehradun                    20th November 2026    GI Council
   9    Kolkata                     20th November 2026    GI Council
  10    Pune                        20th November 2026     LI Council
  11    Ahmedabad                   21st November 2026     LI Council
  12    Bhubaneswar                 21st November 2026    GI Council
  13    Patna                       23rd November 2026    GI Council
  14    Thiruvananthapuram          24th November 2026     LI Council
  15    Ranchi                      25th November 2026    GI Council
  16    Bengaluru                   27th November 2026     LI Council

The details of the respective venues will be communicated by the insurance councils.

The policyholders are encouraged to participate in these camps to seek prompt
resolution of their pending grievances and familiarise themselves with the various
grievance redressal mechanisms available in the insurance sector, including the
Insurance Ombudsman framework.
