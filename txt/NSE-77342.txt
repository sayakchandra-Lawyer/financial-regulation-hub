National Stock Exchange of India

Circular                                  Department: Compliance

 Download Ref No: NSE/COMP/77342                                  Date: November 06, 2026
 Circular Ref. No: 100/2026

To All Members,

Sub: Extension of Display Period for Investor Awareness Messages under Project Jagrook

This is in continuation of Exchange circular ref. no. NSE/COMP/76720 dated October 5, 2026, regarding the display of
investor awareness messages by Trading Members on their trading applications and websites under Project Jagrook.

Pursuant to a communication received from SEBI, all Trading Members are requested to take note of the following:

1. The investor awareness messages under Project Jagrook shall continue to be displayed till December 31, 2026.
     The schedule prescribed in paragraph 2(c) of the aforesaid Exchange circular shall remain unchanged.

2. The effective dates specified under paragraphs 4.1(b) and 4.2(b) of the SEBI Circular dated October 1, 2026, stand
     extended to November 15, 2026.

Trading Members are further advised that the aforesaid investor awareness messages shall be displayed in a bilingual
format (Hindi and English).

All other provisions contained in the aforesaid Exchange circular dated October 5, 2026, and the SEBI Circular dated
October 1, 2026, shall remain unchanged.

All Trading Members are advised to take note of the above and ensure compliance.
