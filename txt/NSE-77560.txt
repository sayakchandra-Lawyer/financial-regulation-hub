National Stock Exchange of India Limited

Circular

                            DEPARTMENT: INSPECTION

Download Ref No: NSE/INSP/77560          Date: November 20, 2026

Circular Ref. No: 59/2026

To All Trading Members,

Subject: Reminder for Submission of Risk Based Supervision Data for the period April 01,
2026 – September 30, 2026

This has reference to Exchange circular reference no. NSE/INSP/76664 dated October 01, 2026
requiring trading members to submit the information / data towards the Risk Based Supervision
for the period April 01, 2026 – September 30, 2026 (Half Year ended September - 2026).

The submission of RBS data is mandatory for all active Members of the Exchange (i.e. those who
have executed even a single trade during April 01, 2026 – September 30, 2026). Submission is
to be made electronically through the Inspection module in the Member Portal on or before due
date i.e., November 30, 2026, to avoid any penal charges/disciplinary action as prescribed in
Annexure 1.2 of Exchange’s Circular No. NSE/INSP/ 73792 dated April 17, 2026.

All members are advised to take note of the above and comply.
