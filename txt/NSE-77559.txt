DEPARTMENT: INSPECTION

Download Ref No: NSE/INSP/77559  Date: November 20, 2026

Circular Ref. No: 58/2026

To All Market Participants,

Sub: Compliance with the guidelines on ‘Safer participation of Retail investors in Algorithmic
       trading’ issued by SEBI and Exchanges – Tech-savvy Retail Investors

This has reference to SEBI Circular Ref. No. SEBI/HO/MIRSD/MIRSD-PoD/P/CIR/2025/0000013
dated February 4, 2025, NSE Circular Ref. No. NSE/INVG/69255 dated July 22, 2025 and SEBI Circular
Ref. No. SEBI/HO/MIRSD/MIRSD-PoD/P/CIR/2025/132 dated September 30, 2025 related to ‘Safer
participation of Retail investors in Algorithmic trading’. The Retail Algo framework was implemented
with effect from April 1, 2026.

As per Exchange Circular Ref. No. NSE/INVG/69255 dated July 22, 2025 (Annexure I, Para 14), it is
mandated that "all the strategies shall be run on the brokers servers. The order messages shall be
originated from brokers server." This requirement is central to ensuring the broker's control over risk
management and data confidentiality. However, Tech Savvy client is required to host the Algo using a
static IP at their end where Algo logic resides instead of hosting the Algo on Trading Member’s cloud
server.”

Post April 1, 2026 i.e. the commencement of Retail Algo framework, Exchanges have been receiving
complaints from market participants regarding the misuse of Static IP facilities by certain Exchange
empaneled Algo Providers. The exchange has also noted that some Algo Providers are providing Static
IPs to retail clients, under the guise of them being ‘Tech-savvy’ retail clients, which is in violation with
SEBI/Exchange guidelines issued for Safer participation of Retail investors in Algorithmic trading.

Accordingly, the Exchange hereby informs all market participants that:

      1. The Static IP is intended only for ‘Tech-savvy Retail Investors’ who develop their own algos
           using programming knowledge (As per SEBI circular SEBI/HO/MIRSD/MIRSD-
           PoD/P/CIR/2025/0000013 dated February 04, 2025)
2. Trading Members and Algo Providers must not, under any circumstances, provide Static IP
    access to retail clients who do not fall under the category of ‘Tech-savvy Retail Investors’ for
    Algorithmic Trading. Any such case of misclassification or misuse of ‘Static IPs’ will be treated
    as a violation of SEBI and Exchange guidelines.

3. Trading Members who are registering such “Tech-Savvy Retail Client” shall take an undertaking
    from such clients that “such clients meet the requirements of the SEBI definition of ‘Tech-savvy’
    Retail clients i.e. they are Retail clients who develop their own algos using programming
    knowledge.”

4. The undertaking shall further state that “As a “Tech Savvy Client” since we are developing our
    own algos using programming knowledge, we shall have no recourse to the Investor Grievance
    mechanism of the Exchange in the event of losses incurred on account of the logic of Algo used.”

The Exchanges will closely monitor compliance of Trading Members and Algo Providers with the
above guidelines and in the event of any violations:

     • Non-Adherence on part of Trading Members - Initiate following penal action on the Trading
         Member (s) concerned.
