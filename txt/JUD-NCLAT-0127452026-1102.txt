02.11.2026:

Company Appeal (AT) (Insolvency) No. 2091 of 2026 :-

This Appeal is filed against an impugned order dated 23.10.2026 passed by
the Ld. Adjudicating Authority (National Company Law Tribunal), New Delhi,
Court V in IA No.5093 of 2026 in CP No. 673 of 2024 wherein IA No.5093 of
2026 for interim protection qua e-auction was dismissed. It is the submission
of the Ld. Senior Counsel for the Appellant on 28.09.2024, an application
                                                             3

under Section 94 IBC was filed by the Appellant and notice to the said
application was issued to the Respondent on 14.10.2024. However, no reply
was filed by the SBI though the SBI appeared on 17.04.2025. On 23.04.2026,
even an application was filed by Canara Bank before the Ld. NCLT for early
hearing of the application under Section 94 IBC and in the meanwhile on
26.05.2026, an amendment came and Section 96(4) of the IBC was introduced
which read as under:-

              “Section 96: Interim-moratorium. (4) The provisions
              of this section shall not apply where an application is
              filed for initiating an insolvency resolution process in
              respect of a personal guarantor to a corporate debtor.”
2. It is on the basis of the above amendment, the application has been dis-
allowed by the Ld. NCLT by the impugned order. It is the submission of the
Ld. Senior Counsel for the Appellant had the timelines been followed per
Section 96(1), the moratorium would have been in place but simply because
of this amendment; the SBI filed the application and went ahead with an e-
auction of the assets of the personal guarantor, the Appellant herein.

3. We have also heard Ld. Counsel for the Respondent. At this stage, it is
submitted by the Ld. Senior Counsel for the Appellant, the Appellant shall be
satisfied in case a direction is given to the Ld. NCLT to dispose of the
proceeding pending before it within 30 days.

4. Considering the submissions, we request Ld. NCLT to dispose of the
application under Section 94 IBC as expeditiously as possible, preferably
within thirty days.
                                                             4

5. With these observations, Appeal is disposed of. Pending applications viz.
IA No. 7864, 7865, 7866, 7867, 7868, 7869, 7870, 7871, 7872, 7873 of 2026
are also disposed of.

6. In view of the similar request, the Company Appeal (AT) (Insolvency) No.
2090 of 2026, Company Appeal (AT) (Insolvency) No. 2092 of 2026, Company
Appeal (AT) (Insolvency) No. 2093 of 2026 and Company Appeal (AT)
(Insolvency) No. 2094 of 2026 are also disposed of.
7. Pending applications in such appeals are closed.
