Circular

Department: Listing

Download Ref No: NSE/CML/77260           Date: November 03, 2026

Circular Ref. No: 1988/2026

To All Members,

Subject: Reversal of action initiated against BKM Industries Limited (Symbol -BKMINDST) on
           account of payment of Annual Listing Fees (“ALF”) for Financial Year ended 2023-24.

This is in continuation to the Exchange Circular No. (Download Ref. No. NSE/CML/62205) dated May
27, 2024, notifying restricted trading in securities of BKM Industries Limited (“the Company”) on
account of non-payment of ALF for the Financial Year ended 2023-24.

Members of the Exchange are hereby informed that the Company has cleared the pending ALF dues.
Accordingly, it is decided to reverse the action initiated against the company.

However, trading in the securities of the Company shall remain suspended as per the Exchange Circular
No. (Download Ref No: NSE/CML/65272) dated November 27, 2024.

For and on behalf of
National Stock Exchange of India Limited

Jeetendra Rangnani
Manager
