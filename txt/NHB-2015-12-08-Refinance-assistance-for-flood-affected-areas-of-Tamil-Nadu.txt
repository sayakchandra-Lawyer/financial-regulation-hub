Refinance assistance for flood affected areas of Tamil Nadu
Refinance Circular No. 02/2015-16
Recent torrential rains in several parts of Tamil Nadu have displaced large segments
of the population and resulted in large scale destruction of property. Re-building of
the ravaged dwelling units and rehabilitation of the affected people will be a
mammoth task requiring multi-pronged action, with involvement of all
stakeholders. The financial sector is expected to play a major role in this massive
effort through innovative financial products customized to optimally serve the needs
of the people.
2.  The Housing Finance Companies (NHB) Directions, 2010 issued by NHB enables
HFCs to re-schedule their loans given to the persons affected by natural calamities
without classification of such loans as sub-standard. We may mention that such re-
scheduled loans would thereafter be governed by the revised terms and conditions.
With the objective of improving the flow of housing finance at concessional rates to
the residents of flood affected districts of Tamil Nadu to help them in reconstruction,
repairs, renovation and up-gradation of the existing dwelling units, NHB has
further decided to extend refinance to HFCs at reduced rates under the Urban
Housing Fund Scheme. NHB has also modified income / area parameters as
applicable under the said Scheme. The modification in the Scheme as applicableto
the affected areas of Tamil Nadu are as follows:
a) Purpose - The refinance assistance will be provided e to Housing Finance
Companies in respect of their direct housing loans extended in the flood affected
areas in the state of Tamil Nadufor the following purposes :
Reconstruction / repairs / renovation / up-gradation of the existing dwelling
units
b) Eligible Loans
Loan size - 10 lakhs
Eligible loans - Housing loans satisfying all of the following criteria would be
eligible for refinance under the Scheme :
Direct housing loans to individuals
