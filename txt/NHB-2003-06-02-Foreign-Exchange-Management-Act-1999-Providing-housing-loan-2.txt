                    Foreign Exchange Management Act, 1999 -
                Providing housing loan in rupees to NRIs/PIOs

As you are aware, in terms of Regulation 8 of Notification No. FEMA 4/2000-
RB dated May 3, 2000 an authorized dealer or a housing finance institution
of India approved by the National Housing Bank can provide housing loan to
a non-resident Indian (NRI) or a person of Indian origin (PIO) resident
outside Indian for acquisition of a residential accommodation in India
subject to condition indicated therein.

2. The Reserve Bank of India vide its Circular No. 95 under the A.P.(DIR
Series) [copy enclosed] dated April 26, 2003 has now clarified that housing
finance institutions referred to above may also grant loans to NRIs / PIOs for
purpose of repairs/renovation/improvement of residential accommodation
owned by them in India.
