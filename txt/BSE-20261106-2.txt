This is in continuation of Exchange Notice No. 20261005-37 dated October 5, 2026, regarding the display
of investor awareness messages by Trading Members on their trading applications and websites under
Project Jagrook.
Pursuant to a communication received from SEBI, all Trading Members are requested to take note of the
following:
1. The investor awareness messages under Project Jagrook shall continue to be displayed till December

    31, 2026. The schedule prescribed in paragraph 2(c) of the aforesaid Exchange Notice shall remain
    unchanged.
2. The effective dates specified under paragraphs 4.1(b) and 4.2(b) of the SEBI Circular dated October 1,
    2026, stand extended to November 15, 2026.
Trading Members are further advised that the aforesaid investor awareness messages shall be displayed
in a bilingual format (Hindi and English).
All other provisions contained in the aforesaid Exchange Notice dated October 5, 2026, and the SEBI
Circular dated October 1, 2026, shall remain unchanged.
Trading Members are advised to take note of the above and ensure compliance.
