                                                                                                 PR No.68/2026

 Memorandum of Understanding between Securities and Exchange Board of India
                        (SEBI) and Digital India BHASHINI Division (DIBD)

Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has entered into a Memorandum of
Understanding (MoU) with Digital India BHASHINI Division (DIBD) on November 3, 2026.
The MoU was signed by Shri Amitabh Nag, Chief Executive Officer, DIBD and
Shri Sandip Pradhan, Whole Time Member, SEBI, in the presence of senior officers from
both the organisations.

DIBD is an independent division functioning under the aegis of the Digital India Corporation,
a Section 8 not-for-profit company established by the Ministry of Electronics and Information
Technology (MeitY), Government of India.

The MoU will enable SEBI to leverage BHASHINI’s language translation APIs, models and
tools, including Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR), Text-to-Speech (TTS), Machine
Translation and Optical Character Recognition (OCR), to facilitate access to SEBI’s services
in regional Indian languages. It will also facilitate the enhancement of BHASHINI’s language
models with securities market related terminology.

This marks the beginning of enhanced cooperation between SEBI and DIBD. The MoU
comes into force from today.
