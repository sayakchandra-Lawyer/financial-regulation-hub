      Clarification on certain media reports regarding Consultation Paper on
    Strengthening the Governance of Market Infrastructure Institutions (MIIs)

SEBI has noticed that some media reports and articles are misinterpreting certain
aspects of the Consultation Paper on “Strengthening the Governance of Market
Infrastructure Institutions (MIIs)” issued on November 20, 2026. These reports mention
that SEBI is proposing a change in age limit of Managing Directors (MDs) of the MIIs.

In this regard, it is clarified SEBI’s consultation papers set out proposals only for those
provisions in respect of which a change is intended. Provisions on which no change is
intended are not included as a proposal in the consultation paper.

It is further clarified that the instant consultation paper does not propose any change
in the maximum age limit of 65 years applicable to the MDs of MIIs. The same was
also clearly stated in SEBI’s post on the consultation paper on its social media handles,
which mentioned that the age limit of MDs remains 65 years.

The proposals relating to MDs in the consultation paper concern only to their term and
tenure in a single MII and across MIIs. The consultation paper also provides that a
PID, MD or ED holding office on the date of commencement of the amendments shall
continue to hold office till the end of the ongoing term.
