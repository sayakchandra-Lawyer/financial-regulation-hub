This is with reference to SEBI Circular No. HO/(79)2026-MIRSD-PODMMC dated February 26, 2026 and
Exchange Notice Nos. 20260721-15 dated July 21, 2026, 20260602-34 dated June 02, 2026, and 20260428-47
dated April 28, 2026, regarding the disclosure of digital platforms used by Research Analysts (RAs) for carrying
out research services activities.

In accordance with the aforesaid circulars and notices, the Exchange has made available a facility on its portal
for submission and modification of details pertaining to websites, mobile applications, and social media
handles used by Research Analysts.

All registered Research Analysts are required to log in to the Exchange portal and access the “Digital Platform”
option available in their existing login.

    • In cases where the details have already been submitted to the Exchange, the existing information will be
         auto-populated. Research Analysts are advised to verify the details, make modifications wherever
         required, and submit the case.

    • In cases where no information has been previously reported, the relevant fields will appear blank.
         Research Analysts shall be required to furnish the requisite details and submit the case.

Accordingly, all registered Research Analysts are advised to report and/or update the details of their websites,
mobile applications, and social media handles through the Exchange portal.

The websites, mobile applications, and social media handles reported to the Exchange shall be considered for
whitelisting with Meta and Google, wherever applicable. Research Analysts are therefore advised to ensure
that all digital platforms being used for research services activities are duly reported to the Exchange.

The aforesaid activity shall be completed on or before November 25, 2026.

Please note that any website, mobile application, or social media handle used for research services activities
and not reported to the Exchange within the stipulated timeline may not be considered for whitelisting and may
be liable for deletion, removal, or such other action as may be applicable under the prevailing regulatory
framework.

Guidance for Submission

    1. Links should be provided only for the main landing page/profile of the respective website, mobile
         application, or social media handle and not for any individual post, reel, story, video, or other specific
         content.

    2. Research Analysts should ensure that the correct links are provided in the corresponding fields. All
         details should be carefully verified and cross-checked before submission.

    3. Ensure that the information furnished is complete, accurate, and updated as on the date of submission.

    4. Refer to the attached User Manual for detailed instructions on the submission process.
Research Analysts are advised to complete the submission within the prescribed timeline to ensure regulatory
compliance.
