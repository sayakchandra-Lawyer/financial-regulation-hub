Reserve Bank issues Foreign Exchange Management (Export and Import of Goods and Services) (Second Amendment) Regulations, 2026 | 
 The Foreign Exchange Management (Export of Goods and Services) Regulations, 2015 were superseded by the Foreign Exchange Management (Export and Import of Goods and Services) Regulations, 2026, which were issued on January 13, 2026, and became effective from October 01, 2026. The new regulations are principle based; intended to promote ease of doing business, especially for small exporters and importers; and empower Authorised Dealers to provide quicker and more efficient service to their customers.
 Based on subsequent feedback received from stakeholders, the Reserve Bank has issued Amendment Regulations to Foreign Exchange Management (Export and Import of Goods and Services) Regulations, 2026 that were notified on November 02, 2026, and came into effect, retrospectively, from October 01, 2026.
 In this regard, to facilitate better understanding of the new trade regulations by the exporters/importers and other stakeholders, the Reserve Bank has placed a Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) on the Foreign Exchange Management (Export and Import of Goods and Services) Regulations, 2026 on its website.
