Advisory regarding Data Submitted to the Central Registry of Securitisation Asset
Reconstruction and Security Interest of India (CERSAI)
Reference is invited to Para 213 of Reserve Bank of India (Housing Finance Companies)
Directions, 2025 and Chapter VII of Reserve Bank of India (Non-Banking Financial
Companies – Credit Risk Management) Directions, 2025. In this regard, it is informed that
under the provisions of Section 23 of the SARFAESI Act, every transaction of creation of
security interest is required to be filed with the Central Registry of Securitisation Asset
Reconstruction and Security Interest of India (CERSAI).
CERSAI has flagged various issues with respect to quality and accuracy of data being
submitted by Housing Finance Companies (HFCs). A summary of the issues is given
below-
A  The registry has received large number of requests for rectification of data related
to security interest registered by HFCs due to incorrect information relating to
borrowers, property details, addresses, and security interests being entered in
CERSAI records;
A  Details such as addresses, names, or property particulars of persons who have not
availed any loan facility are incorrectly recorded;
A  Financial institutions routinely rely upon CERSAI searches before sanctioning
loans. If a secured creditor has entered incorrect borrower or property details,
subsequent lenders may be unable to identify the existing charge;
A  Inaccurate registrations can lead to disputes regarding ownership, priority of
charges, enforcement proceedings, and SARFAESI actions. Such errors also affect
the reliability and credibility of the registry as a public notice mechanism;
In view of the above, all HFCs are advised to ensure meticulous compliance with the
extant regulations issued by Reserve Bank of India, establish appropriate internal controls
and carry out periodic reconciliation to identify and rectify any discrepancies in data
reported to CERSAI.
