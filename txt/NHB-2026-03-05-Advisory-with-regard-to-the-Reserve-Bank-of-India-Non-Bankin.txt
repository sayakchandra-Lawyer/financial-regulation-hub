        Advisory with regard to the Reserve Bank of India (Non-Banking Financial

                         Companies - Know Your Customer) Directions, 2025

With reference to the Reserve Bank of India (Non-Banking Financial Companies - Know
Your Customer) Directions, 2025, the following non-compliances have been observed in
number of Housing Finance Companies (HFCs) during supervisory inspections:

    1) Para 63 - Delay in uploading KYC Records into the Central KYC Records Registry
         (CKYCR).

     2) Para 42 - Delay in periodic Updation of KYC.
     3) Non-adherence to Customer Identification Procedures (CIP).

2. In this regard, all HFCs are hereby advised to strictly ensure meticulous compliance
with the prescribed Reserve Bank of India (Non-Banking Financial Companies - Know
Your Customer) Directions, 2025.

3. HFCs are also required to remain aware of any changes or updates issued by the
Reserve Bank of India (RBI) to the relevant Directions and ensure that all revised
requirements are complied with in a timely manner.
