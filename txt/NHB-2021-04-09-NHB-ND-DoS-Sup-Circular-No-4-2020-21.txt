Public Health Response to COVID-19
Campaign for COVID-19: Appropriate Behaviour Reg.
Please refer to our circular NHB(ND)/DoS/ Sup. Circular No.2/2020-21 dated
October 26, 2020 on the subject.
2.  Hon'ble Prime Minister of India,onApril 04, 2021, reviewed the emerging
situation due to alarming upward trend of COVID-19 cases in the country. It has been
decided to focus on the five-fold strategy to deal with the emerging crisis viz. Testing,
Tracing, Treatment, COVID appropriate behaviour and Vaccination. A low cost
people's campaign emphasisingCOVID-19appropriate behaviour with focussed
messaging namely on 'Dawai bhi, Kadai bhi', encouraging people to wear mask when
in public places, follow physical distancing, washing hands frequently etc. by
displaying banners/posters, messaging through SMS/emails etc. and interpersonal
communication through functionaries etc. is expected to create public awareness.
3.  Accordingly, you are advised to give wide publicity to this message in Regional
languages.
4.  In order to amplify the campaign in a coordinated manner, photographs of
outreach activities and displays of creatives may be tweeted with #Unite2FIghtCorona
and #NHB.
5.  Action initiated with regard to the above may be intimated to the Department
of Supervision, National Housing Bank by April 16, 2021.
