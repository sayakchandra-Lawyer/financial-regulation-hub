Format for reporting of non-compliance(s) under the IFSCA (Bullion Market)
Regulations, 2025

Reference is drawn to Regulation 31 of the International Financial Services Centres
Authority (Bullion Market) Regulations, 2025, which reads as under:

    a) A bullion exchange or bullion clearing corporation shall appoint a Compliance
        Officer who shall be responsible for monitoring the compliance of the
        provisions of the Act or relevant rules, regulations and circulars, guidelines or
        directions issued thereunder by the Authority or any other regulatory Authority,
        and also for the redressal of customers’ grievances.

    b) The Compliance Officer shall immediately and independently report to the
        Authority, any non-compliance as referred to in sub-regulation (1) observed by
        her and shall also submit a report thereon, to the Authority on a quarterly basis
        within 45 days from the end of the quarter in a manner specified by the
        Authority.

2. While it has been the primary responsibility of the Compliance Officer, ever since
    the notification of the IFSCA (Bullion Exchange) Regulations, 2020 [refer sub-

                                                                        Page 1 of 3
    regulation (2) of regulation 24], to monitor compliance with the applicable
    regulatory requirements, maintain a database of instance(s) of any non-
    compliance(s), and immediately and independently report such non-
    compliance(s) to the IFSCA, the IFSCA (Bullion Market) Regulations, 2025
    introduced additional requirement of quarterly reporting in a manner specified by
    the Authority.

3. In this regard, the format in which such non-compliance(s) shall be reported by the
    Compliance Officer on a quarterly basis is provided at Annexure to this Circular.

4. Such report in the specified format shall be submitted to the IFSCA within 45
    calendar days from the end of each quarter. In the absence of any instance of non-
    compliance during the relevant reporting period, a Nil Report shall be submitted to
    the Authority within the prescribed timeline.

5. This Circular is issued in exercise of the powers conferred under Sections 12 and
    13 of the International Financial Services Centres Authority Act, 2019, read with
    regulation 78 of the International Financial Services Centres Authority (Bullion
    Market) Regulations, 2025, and shall come into force with immediate effect.

A copy of this Circular is available on the website of International Financial Services
Centres Authority at www.ifsca.gov.in at “Legal Framework→Circulars”.
