NHB (ND)/ROD/ Refinance Circular No. 3/2024-25
October 08, 2024
All Housing Finance Companies
Madam/Dear Sir,
Sub: Regarding submission of Book Debt to NHB for availing refinance
National Housing Bank (NHB) extends refinance to Housing Finance Companies (HFCs)
against the book debts (flagged loan pool) of the respective HFCs. While processing the
refinance applications, it has been observed by the Bank in certain cases that the pool
flagged to NHB also consists of 'Loan Against Property (LAP)' and 'Plot + Construction,
where progress of construction is zero i.e. plot loan'. As you are aware that the refinance
can be given only against Individual Housing Loan (IHL) i.e. loan for purchase/
construction of houses and for repair / upgradation of existing house, any refinance
availed against the loan pool other than IHL is not admissible.
In view of above, all the HFCs are hereby advised to verify their loan pool submitted to
NHB at the time of taking refinance and if the cases of 'LAP' and 'Plot loan' has been
earmarked under the flagged loan pool, these cases must be carved out from the master
pool and the equivalent amount may be remitted along with adverse balance distinctly.
All HFCs are advised to take a note of the above and address the concern, as also ensure
that no ineligible book debts are assigned to NHB as flagged loan pool.
