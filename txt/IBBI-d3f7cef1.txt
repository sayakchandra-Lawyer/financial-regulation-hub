Press Release

Shri Rahul Madan takes charge as Chairperson of Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of
India

Shri Rahul Madan assumed charge as Chairperson of Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of
India in New Delhi on 25th November, 2026.

Shri Rahul Madan is a Chartered Accountant, Cost and Management Accountant and qualified
Insolvency Professional. Prior to joining the IBBI as Chairperson, he served as the Managing
Director of the Indian Institute of Insolvency Professionals of ICAI (IIIPI) for over 6 years.
Prior to that, he had an extensive career spanning over 24 years in the banking and financial
services sector, having served with institutions including the State Bank of India, Kotak
Mahindra Bank and ICICI Bank. During his professional career, he has worked in diverse areas,
including corporate banking, credit and risk management, investment banking, debt advisory,
structured finance and institutional wealth management.
