1. Executive Summary

The Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (IRDAI) proposes to issue
the IRDAI (Subsidiary Instructions and Consultative Committees) Regulations, 2026
(“herein after referred to as ‘ the proposed Regulations”) in light of the framework
introduced under the Sabka Bima Sabki Raksha (Amendment of Insurance Laws) Act,
2025 (“herein after referred to as SBSR Act”).

Section 114B of the Insurance Act, 1938 as inserted by the SBSR Act has introduced
provisions relating to the manner and conditions for issuance of Subsidiary Instructions
after consulting the Consultative Committees. Further, section 114C of the Insurance Act
empowers the Authority to specify the manner of constitution of Consultative Committees.

IRDAI vide Exposure draft dated 17.06.2026 had issued draft IRDAI (Procedure for
making Regulations and Subsidiary Instructions) Regulations, 2026. It is now proposed
that the framework governing the issuance of subsidiary instructions under the Insurance
Act, 1938 be specified through a separate regulation. A separate regulatory framework
would clearly delineate the scope and limits of the power under section 114B. It would
also enable the Authority to specify specific safeguards governing the procedure for
clarification of ambiguity, the nature of permissible procedural requirements, consultation
with the concerned Consultative Committee, the circumstances in which consultation may
be dispensed with on account of urgency, publication, review and withdrawal of
subsidiary instructions.

The proposed Regulations seek to operationalize these statutory provisions and establish
a structured, and consultative framework for issuance of subsidiary instructions and
constitution of Consultative Committees.

The proposed Regulations, inter alia, provide for:
• Framework governing issuance of subsidiary instructions;
• Constitution, composition and functioning of Consultative Committees;
• Periodic review of subsidiary instructions;
• Procedure for amendment of subsidiary instructions; and
• Framework for urgent subsidiary instructions.

2.  Key Features of the proposed Regulations:

2.1 Framework for Subsidiary Instructions:

The SBSR Act recognises subsidiary instructions as a distinct regulatory instrument.
The proposed Regulations specify the scope and nature of subsidiary instructions that
may be issued for:

    • Clarification of ambiguities in regulations; and
    • Procedural matters ancillary to regulations.

    The proposed regulations provide for the scope of ‘identification of ambiguity’ and
    ‘procedural requirement’ contemplated in section 114B of the Insurance Act, 1938.
2.2 Constitution of Consultative Committees:

For the specific purpose of Sections 114B and 114C of the Insurance Act,1938, the
proposed Regulations provide for constitution of Consultative Committees to provide
advice on matters relating to subsidiary instructions and such other matters as may be
referred by IRDAI.

2.3 Amendment and Review Framework:

The proposed Regulations provide for review of the subsidiary instructions on specified
grounds and require amendments to the subsidiary instructions.

2.4 Urgent Subsidiary Instructions:

The proposed regulations recognise issuance of Urgent Subsidiary Instructions
considering the urgency where prior consultation with the concerned Consultative
Committee is not practicable.

The framework requires recording of reasons for such action, thereby balancing
regulatory responses to certain exigencies with accountability.

2.5 Subject matters for which Consultative Committees shall be constituted:

The proposed regulations list out the subject matter for which Consultative Committees
shall be constituted, including Policyholder Protection, Sectoral development, Market
conduct, Financial Stability and Health of regulated entities, Insurance and insurance
related services, Insurance distribution and allied activities, Reinsurance and such other
matters as deemed necessary.

2.6 Conduct of meeting and terms of office of Consultative Committee

The proposed regulations provide for the procedure and manner of conduct of meetings
of the Consultative Committee including the quorum.

In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (zca) and (zcb) of sub section (2) of
Section 114A read with Section 114B and Section 114C of the Insurance Act, 1938 and
section 14 and section 26 of Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority Act,
1999, the Authority, in consultation with the Insurance Advisory Committee, hereby
makes the following regulations, namely, -

                               CHAPTER I
                             PRELIMINARY

                                       7
1. Short title and commencement
(1) These regulations may be called the Insurance Regulatory and Development
Authority of India (Subsidiary Instructions and Consultative Committees) Regulations,
2026.
(2) These regulations shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official
Gazette.
(3) These Regulations shall be reviewed once every three years from the date of
publication, unless a review, repeal or amendment is warranted earlier.

2. Objective
To lay down the manner for issuing subsidiary instructions and constitution of
consultative committees. The framework seeks to standardize the process of issuing
subsidiary instructions in a transparent and consultative manner.

3. Definitions
(1) In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires, -

     (a) ‘Act’ means the Insurance Act, 1938 (4 of 1938);
     (b) “Authority” means the Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India
     established under sub-section (1) of section 3 of the Insurance Regulatory and
     Development Authority Act, 1999 (41 of 1999);
     (c) ‘Consultative Committee’ means the Committee constituted by the Authority
     under Section 114C of the Act.
     (d) ‘Regulations’ means the regulations framed by the Authority under section 114A
     of the Act or Section 26 of the Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority
     Act, 1999.
     (e) ‘Subsidiary Instructions’ mean the instructions issued under section 114B of the
     Act.
(2) All words and expressions used herein and not defined in these regulations but
defined in the Insurance Act, 1938 (4 of 1938) or the Insurance Regulatory and
Development Authority Act, 1999 (41 of 1999) or any Rules or Regulations made
thereunder shall have the meanings respectively assigned to them in those Acts or
Rules or Regulations.

                                                 CHAPTER II
                                    SUBSIDIARY INSTRUCTIONS

                                                             8
4. Scope and nature of subsidiary instructions
The Authority may issue subsidiary instructions for the purposes specified in sub
section (1) of section 114B of the Act, namely—

   (a) clarifying the ambiguity of any regulation, if any; and
   (b) laying down any procedural requirement ancillary to any regulation.

5. Persons competent to issue subsidiary instructions
(1) Subsidiary instructions may be issued by—

     (a) the Chairperson; or
     (b) one or more whole-time Members authorised by the Chairperson; or
     (c) jointly by the Chairperson and one or more whole-time Members.
(2) Every subsidiary instruction shall expressly mention—
     (a) the regulation to which it relates; and
     (b) the specific purpose under sub section (1) of section 114B.

6. Procedure for issuing subsidiary instructions
(1) Before issuing any subsidiary instruction, the concerned Consultative Committee
constituted under these regulations shall be consulted.
(2) The Authority may consult Insurance Councils, associations or organisations of
insurance intermediaries, agents and other stakeholders before issuing any Subsidiary
Instruction.

7. Identification of ambiguity

(1) For the purposes of clause (a) of sub-section (1) of section 114B of the Act, an
ambiguity may include—

         (a) an expression or provision in a regulation which is reasonably capable of
         more than one interpretation;

         (b) an apparent inconsistency between two provisions of the same regulation; or

         (c) an omission in the provision which is necessary to give effect to the
         regulation;

         (d) any other matter in the regulation for which clarification is required.

                                                             9
(2) A subsidiary instruction issued for clarification of an ambiguity shall not alter the
substantive obligation or substantive right established by the regulation.

8. Procedural requirements

(1) A subsidiary instruction may lay down procedural requirements relating to the
implementation or administration of a regulation, including—

         (a) the manner and form of submission of information, statements, returns or
         applications;

         (b) timelines for procedural steps, where such timelines are ancillary to the
         regulation;

         (c) the electronic or other mode of submission;

         (d) the format, authentication or manner of maintenance of records;

         (e) the manner of communication between the Authority and a regulated entity;
         and

         (f) such other procedural matter as is necessary for giving effect to the
         regulation.

(2) No subsidiary instruction shall be issued under this regulation for specifying a matter
which, by virtue of the Act, is required to be specified by regulations.

9. Consultation with Consultative Committee

(1) The concerned Consultative Committee shall be consulted either at a in person
meeting or virtually or through circulation of agenda and notes thereof, before a
subsidiary instruction is issued.

Provided that where subsidiary instructions are required to be made urgently, it may be
issued without consulting the Consultative Committee but after recording the reasons
thereof.

(2) For the purposes of sub-regulation (1), the draft subsidiary instruction, together with
the reasons for proposing the instruction, shall be placed before the concerned
Consultative Committee.

                                                             10
(3) The Consultative Committee shall ordinarily furnish its advice within such period as
may be specified.

10. Publication and Commencement
(1) All subsidiary instructions shall be issued by notification and published on the
website of the Authority.

(2) Subsidiary instructions shall come into effect from the date specified therein.

11. Review and Amendment

(1) The Authority may, on its own motion or on consideration of any representation,
review a subsidiary instruction.

(2) Where a subsidiary instruction is found to—

(a) be inconsistent with the Act, Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority Act,
1999, rules or regulations;

(b) be no longer necessary; or

(c) have become impracticable or redundant,

the Authority may amend, replace or withdraw such subsidiary instruction.
(3) An amendment or replacement under this regulation shall be issued in the same
manner as the original subsidiary instruction.

                                                CHAPTER III
                                   CONSULTATIVE COMMITTEES
12. Constitution of Consultative Committee(s)
The Authority shall constitute Consultative Committees for the purposes of section
114C of the Act on the following matters:
    1. Policyholder Protection;
    2. Sectoral development;
    3. Market conduct;
    4. Financial Stability and Health of regulated entities;
    5. Insurance and insurance related services;
    6. Insurance distribution and allied activities;
    7. Reinsurance;

                                                             11
    8. Such other matters as deemed necessary.

13. Functions of Consultative Committee
(1) The Consultative Committee shall advise the Authority on—

     (a) matters relating to the making of the subsidiary instructions under section 114B;
     and
     (b) any other issue as may be determined by the Authority.
(2) The advice of the Consultative Committee shall be recommendatory in nature and
shall not be binding on the Authority.

14. Composition of Consultative Committees

(1) The Authority may constitute each Consultative Committee with such number of
members as it may determine, subject to the composition specified in this regulation.

(2) Each Consultative Committee shall ordinarily consist of not more than eleven
members, including—

         (a) Chairperson, being nominated by the Authority from among the members;

         (b) such whole time members or officers of the Authority, not exceeding three,
         as the Authority may nominate;

         (c) five members having domain expertise or experience;

         (d) one person representing policyholder interests or consumer interests;

         (e) one person having non-insurance financial services experience; and

         (f) one person from the field of technology, analytics or data science.

(3) The Authority shall ensure that the composition of a Committee is balanced and
has a fair representation of all relevant stakeholders.

(4) In constituting a Committee, the Authority shall have regard to the need for—

         (a) appropriate representation of relevant areas of expertise;

         (b) independence and avoidance of conflicts of interest;

         (c) diversity of professional and institutional experience; and
                                                             12
         (d) representation of policyholder interests or consumer interests, wherever
         relevant to the subject matter.
(5) The Authority may nominate an officer of the Authority as Member-Secretary of a
Committee.
(6) The Chairperson of the Consultative Committee may invite any person whose
expertise is considered relevant.
15. Term of office
(1) The term of office of a member shall be for a period of two years from the date of
appointment and may be extended by the Authority.
(2) A member may resign by giving written notice to the Authority.
(3) The Authority may remove a member where—
         (a) the member is unable to discharge his functions;
         (b) the member has a conflict of interest which materially affects the discharge
         of functions;
         (c) the member has acted in a manner prejudicial to the objectives of the
         Committee; or
         (d) the Authority considers such removal necessary in the public interest.
16. Meetings
(1) A Consultative Committee shall meet at such times and places and in such manner
as may be determined by the Chairperson of the Committee.
(2) Meetings may be conducted physically, electronically or through any other mode
capable of recording the proceedings.
(3) The notice of a meeting shall ordinarily specify the agenda and relevant documents.
17. Quorum and decision-making

                                                             13
(1) The quorum for a meeting shall be one-half of the total number of members, subject
to a minimum of six members.

(2) The advice of the Consultative Committee shall ordinarily be determined by
consensus.

(3) Where consensus cannot be reached, the views of the members may be recorded
separately.

(4) The advice of the Committee, including dissenting views, shall be recorded in the
minutes of the meeting.

18. Conflict of interest

(1) Every member of a Consultative Committee shall disclose any direct or indirect
interest which may give rise to a conflict in relation to any matter under consideration.

(2) Where a member has a material conflict of interest, such member shall recuse
himself from consideration of the relevant matter.

19. Confidentiality

A member of a Consultative Committee shall maintain confidentiality in respect of
information obtained in the course of the member's functions and shall not disclose
such information except—

         (a) with the Authority; or
         (b) as required by law.

                                                CHAPTER IV
                                            MISCELLANEOUS
20. Delegation of powers
The Authority may delegate its powers and functions in these regulations to
Chairperson or one or more Whole-Time Members or both or any officer of the
Authority.
21. Power to issue clarification and to remove difficulties, if any

In order to remove any doubts or the difficulties that may arise in the application or
interpretation of any of the provisions of these regulations, the Chairperson may issue
appropriate clarifications as and when deemed necessary.
