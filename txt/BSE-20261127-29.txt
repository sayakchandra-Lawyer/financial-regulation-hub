Sub: Discontinuation of weekly trading in securities of companies suspended for non-compliances with
provisions of certain Regulations of SEBI (Depositories and Participants) Regulations,2018

This is in continuation to Exchange Notice No. 20260422-16 dated April 22, 2026, regarding suspension of trading
in securities for non-compliance with Regulation 76 (i.e., Submission of Reconciliation of share capital audit
report) of SEBI (Depositories & Participants) Regulations, 2018 for two consecutive quarters i.e., September
2025 and December 2025.

As per SEBI Master Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 (“Master Circular”) issued on July
11, 2023 and last updated on January 30, 2026, the time period of the mandated six months of trading on first
trading day of every week shall be completed on December 14 ,2026, hence the trading in the securities of the
below mentioned companies on ‘Trade for Trade’ basis in Z group on the first trading day of every week will be
discontinued w.e.f, December 15, 2026.

Sr. No.  Scrip Code  Company Name
1        530151      Vijay Textiles Ltd

2        511357      Kailash Auto Finance Ltd
3        531898      Sanguine Media Ltd
4        542724      Murae Organisor Ltd
