PR No.69/2026

    SEBI Constitutes Working Group to Review the Innovators Growth Platform
                                                  Framework

The start-up and innovation ecosystem has evolved significantly, with the emergence of
enterprises in areas such as deep-tech, biotechnology, semiconductors, space and other
research-intensive sectors. Such enterprises may have distinct financing requirements
and business models. In this context, a review of the existing regulatory framework
governing the Innovators Growth Platform (IGP) is being undertaken to assess its
relevance and effectiveness and to facilitate access to capital markets for innovation-led
enterprises, while maintaining appropriate standards of investor protection.

Against this backdrop, the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has
constituted a Working Group to undertake a comprehensive review of the regulatory
framework governing the IGP under the SEBI (Issue of Capital and Disclosure
Requirements) Regulations, 2018.

The Terms of Reference of the Working Group are as follows:

1. Existing IGP Framework: Review the existing regulatory framework governing IGP,

including eligibility and listing requirements, and assess its relevance and effectiveness.

2. Research-stage and Innovation-led Enterprises: Examine the suitability of the IGP

framework for research-stage, pre-revenue, IP-led, deep-tech and other innovation- and

technology-intensive enterprises.

3. IGP Investor Framework: Review the eligibility, participation and investment
    requirements applicable to IGP Investors, while ensuring appropriate investor protection.

4. Listing and Capital Raising: Review the framework for listing and capital raising by
    IGP entities, including public issue, OFS, pricing, allocation and minimum subscription
    requirements.

5. Post-listing Framework: Review the post-listing regulatory framework applicable to
    IGP entities, including disclosure, governance, trading and continuing obligations.

6. Migration to Main Board: Review the framework for migration from IGP to the Main
    Board, including eligibility and procedural requirements.

7. International Benchmarking: Benchmark the IGP framework against relevant
    international frameworks for innovative, technology-led and growth-stage companies
    and consider suitable adaptations for the Indian securities market.

8. Recommendations and Implementation: Recommend necessary amendments to the
    applicable regulatory framework, including transitional and implementation measures,
    and examine any other matter incidental or consequential to the above.

Members of the Working Group may engage with start-ups, industry associations and
other relevant stakeholders to understand sector-specific needs and identify measures
to improve access to the IGP, while maintaining market integrity and investor protection.
The recommendations of the Working Group will be submitted to SEBI for consideration.
