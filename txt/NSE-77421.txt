DEPARTMENT : FINANCE AND ACCOUNTS

Download Ref No. : NSE/FA/77421             Date : November 13, 2026

Circular Ref No. : 022026

To all the Trading Members
Sub : Transaction Charges for Electronic Gold Receipts (EGRs)

This has reference to Exchange circular regarding Transaction charges on Electronic Gold
Receipts (EGRs) segment, Circular no. NSE/FA/75523 dated July 31, 2026.
In order to encourage active participation in Electronic Gold Receipts (EGRs) segment, it has
been decided that no transaction charges will be levied on the trades in Electronic Gold Receipts
(EGRs) segment of the exchange till March 31, 2027.
