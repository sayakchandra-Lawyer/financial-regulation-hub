Sub: Action against the Defaulting Companies for non-payment of Annual Listing Fees (ALF)– Equity Segment

In continuation to the Exchange’s Notice No. 20210625-43 dated June 25, 2021, 20260811-33 dated August 11, 2026,
the Exchange had identified the companies which have failed to pay outstanding Annual Listing fees as per Regulation
14 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.

In view of the above, Trading Members are requested to note that on account of compliance with aforesaid
requirement, suspension of trading in the equity shares of the below mentioned company shall be revoked w.e.f.
Thursday i.e. November 26, 2026, and trading will be resumed to Normal Segment.
