To All Members,

Sub: Submission of Action taken report (ATR) for observations reported during
inspection of Authorised Persons

This has a reference to Exchange circular ref. no. NSE/INSP/70751 dated October 10, 2025,
and Exchange circular ref. no. NSE/COMP/75878 dated August 21, 2026, regarding the
indicative disciplinary actions to be applied by trading members against their Authorised
Persons (APs) in case of non-compliance identified during AP inspections and requirement of
submission of ATR along with status of closure of observation to Exchange.

Members are hereby informed that the functionality is provided on ENIT (Electronic NSE
Interface for Trading Members) portal for reporting of ATR for observations reported during
inspection of Authorised Persons.

Members are advised to submit ATR for observations reported during inspection of Authorised
Persons to Exchange. Timelines for submission of ATR are mentioned below:

Inspections undertaken during the FY   Due date for submission  Due date for
                                       of AP inspection report  submission of ATR
Inspections done during the Quarter-1
(April-June)                                     July 31st         September 30th
Inspections done during the Quarter-2
(July-September)                               October 31st        December 31st
Inspections done during the Quarter-3
(October-December)                             January 31st           March 31st
Inspections done during the Quarter-4
(January-March)                                 April 30th             June 30th

Accordingly, ATR along with status of closure of observation reported during AP inspections
for quarter ended September 30, 2026, shall be submitted by December 31, 2026, and onwards
as per above timelines.

Details for accessing the ENIT portal and reporting of ATR are enclosed as under:

Annexure A: The user manual for submission of AP inspection report and ATR
Annexure B: List of validations applicable for uploading ATR file on ENIT

Inspections of APs undertaken during each quarter shall be reported within one month from
end of the said quarter. In case no inspection is undertaken during a particular quarter, the
Members may submit a "NIL" declaration for that quarter through the ENIT portal. All reports
shall be submitted through the online system in ENIT.

Part 2: General Guidelines for submission of AP Inspection Action Taken Report (ATR)
1. Submission of ATR is mandatory for the members if any “Not Complied” reported in any

   of the criterion during quarterly AP Inspection reporting.
   (Please note that template will be available only if there is any non-compliance reported in
   respective quarter for any of the Authorised Person inspection reporting)
2. Member shall review non compliances reported/observed during AP inspection and shall
   take necessary action against AP within reasonable time. Also, member shall ensure that non
   compliances are rectified/ complied before submission of ATR to Exchange i.e. within 60
   days of the due date for submission of AP inspection report with the status of compliance.
3. The ATR template will be made available for download at ENIT portal in the following
   path: ENIT → Compliance → Authorized Person → Applications → Action on Non-
   Compliance in AP Inspection Report
4. Below details shall be provided in the downloaded template.
   • Type of Action Taken as prescribed under Exchange circular Ref No: NSE/INSP/70751

       dated October 10, 2025
   • If type of action selected as Others, then please specify
   • If action taken by member is not in line with action prescribed in Exchange circular Ref

       No: NSE/INSP/ 70751, then specify reason
• Date of Action Taken (DD-MM-YYYY)
• Compliance status of the non-compliance observed

5. Under the column “Type of Action Taken” in ATR Template, select indicative actions as
   prescribed in circular NSE/INSP/70751 dated October 10, 2025, from the dropdowns
   available for each criterion where non-compliance was marked against the AP in AP
   Inspection Reporting for the quarter.

These actions may include, but are not limited to:

Violation           Indicative Action
Category
           Termination on Disciplinary grounds, informing all clients of the AP, withhold
    A      money due to the AP, and public notification. (for violations mentioned in point
           no. 2, 4, 5, 6, 8 and 9 of Annexure A of the circular NSE/INSP/70751 dated
    B      October 10, 2025, it is mandatory to file police complaint for termination of
    C      AP on disciplinary grounds).
           Issuance of a warning Letter, with instruction to avoid repeat violations, with
           termination on disciplinary grounds in case of repeated offenses.
           Issuance of an Advisory Letter, with instruction to avoid repeat violations. In
           case of repeat violation, warning letter to be issued.

 6. If “Others” is selected in the “Type of Action” column, it is mandatory to provide the required
     details in the column “If type of action selected as Others, then please specify”.

 7. If the action is not in line with the prescribed Indicative action in the above table, it is
     mandatory to provide the reason under the column, “If action taken by member is not in line
     with action prescribed in Exchange circular Ref No: NSE/INSP/ 70751, then specify reason”

 8. Date of Action Taken should be captured in the DD-MM-YYYY format.
 9. All observations shall be acted upon and complied before submission of ATR to Exchange.

     In the exceptional cases where compliance status is not closed/completed, member shall
     report ATR with Type of action “In Progress” and compliance status as “open” within due
     date for submission of ATR.
10. ATR submission window will be available till due date for submission of ATR. In case of
     any discrepancy in ATR submitted to Exchange, same can be rectify by resubmission of
     entire file again. The ATR submitted for respective quarter will be overwritten with the latest
     submission. The option of resubmission of ATR will be available only till the due date of
     filing of ATR.
Part 3-Steps for Submission of ATR

Step 1: Navigate to the ATR submission page

Log in to the ENIT Member Portal and follow this navigation path:

ENIT → Compliance → Authorized Person → Applications → Action on Non-Compliance
in AP Inspection Report

STEP 2 Download ATR Template
    1. Select the relevant quarter for the AP Inspection from the drop-down menu.
    2. Download the file template from the page.
         (Please note that template will be available only if there is any non-compliance
         reported in respective quarter for any of the Authorised Person inspection reporting)

Step: 3 Fill in the details of action taken and status of closure of non-Compliance in the
template and upload on ENIT
The required information should be provided in file downloaded from ENIT and should be
uploaded back on the ENIT at Path: Compliance -> Authorized Person -> Applications ->
Action on Non-Compliance in AP Inspection Report
Step 4: Verify Upload Status
After uploading, one of the following messages will appear:
• For Successful submission: “File Uploaded Successfully – The file has been accepted”.

   Or
• On identification of Error: “Error File Generated – Check Comments and Rectify”. The

   file contains errors that must be corrected.

STEP 5: Rectify and Re-upload (If Errors Occur)
If an error message is displayed, follow these steps to resolve the issue:

    1. Click the OK button and download the error file that is generated.
    2. Open the error file and review the comments to identify the issues.
    3. Refer general guidelines and validation sheet available on the page to assist in

         correcting the data.
    4. After rectifying all errors, re-upload the corrected file.
