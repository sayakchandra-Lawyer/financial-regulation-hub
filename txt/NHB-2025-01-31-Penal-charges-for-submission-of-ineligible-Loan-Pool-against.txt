    Penal charges for submission of ineligible Loan Pool against Refinance Facility

It has been decided that the National Housing Bank (NHB) will levy a penal charge of 5% on
the aggregate outstanding amount of loans, within the underlying loan pool, that are
subsequently found ineligible post-disbursement of refinance, as per the eligibility criteria of
the refinance scheme. The penal charge shall be applicable with effect from the date of
disbursement.

2.  This provision shall uniformly apply to all underlying loan pools submitted for

disbursement or replenishment, across all refinance schemes, including those with or without

concessions.

3.  Further, this constitutes a refinance arrangement covenant in the nature of a lender-

to-borrower obligation and shall be deemed applicable to all prospective refinance facilities

and to all such refinance facilities outstanding as on January 1, 2025. The necessary

documentation as may be required in this regard will be informed separately.

Please ensure strict adherence to the above provisions.
