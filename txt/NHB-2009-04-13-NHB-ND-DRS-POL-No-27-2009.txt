ALL REGISTERED HOUSING FINANCE COMPANIES
Dear Sir,
RATINGS OF HOUSING FINANCE COMPANIES (HFCS)
It has been decided that all the Housing Finance Companies (HFCs) having
assets of Rs. 100 crores and above and issuing financial products like
Commercial Paper, Debentures etc. to which rating is assigned by rating
agencies  shall,  hereafter,  furnish  the  information  about
downgrading/upgrading of assigned rating of any financial product issued
by them, within fifteen days of such a change in rating to the Department of
Regulation & Supervision, National Housing Bank, Core 5A, India Habitat
Centre, Lodhi Road, New Delhi-110 003.
Yours faithfully
