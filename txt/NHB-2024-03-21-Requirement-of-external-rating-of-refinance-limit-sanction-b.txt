           Requirement of external rating of refinance limit sanction by NHB

In compliance to the requirement of BASEL - II, it has been decided that at the time of

accepting the sanction granted by National Housing Bank (NHB), all PLIs arerequired to
submit External Rating of the refinance exposure (whether availed or unavailed).
Submission of acceptance of sarnction terms and conditions without external rating shall

not be accepted by NHB henceforth.

Further, for the existing exposure, all PLIsare advised to submit their external rating

latest by April 08, 2024.

All eligible PLIsare accordingly advised to take necessary steps to ensure compliance of

the above.
