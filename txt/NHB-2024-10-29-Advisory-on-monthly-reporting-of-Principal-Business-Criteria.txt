                 Advisory on monthly reporting of Principal Business Criteria
In terms of the provisions of paragraph 4.1.17 r/w 4.1.16 of Master Direction - NBFC-HFC

(Reserve Bank) Directions, 2021, every Housing Finance Company (HFC) must comply with

the Principal Business Criteria (PBC).

2. To enhance the monitoring of PBC criteria in alignment with regulatory requirements, it
has been decided that the HFCs to submit their PBC compliance data monthly, instead of
quarterly submissions.

3. Henceforth, HFCs are advised to submit the PBC data on a monthly basis, with each
submission to be made by 15th of the following month.
4. The provisions of this advisory shall be effective from November 01, 2024, onwards.
