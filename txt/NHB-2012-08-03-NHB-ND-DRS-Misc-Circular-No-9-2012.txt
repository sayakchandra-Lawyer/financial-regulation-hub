TO ALL REGISTERED HOUSING FINANCE COMPANIES (HFCs)
Madam/Sir,
Submission of Returns
Please refer to the Bank's Misc Circular No NHB(ND)/DRS/Misc-Cir No.3/
3986/2010-11 dated April 7, 2011 in the captioned matter.
2.  It is observed that, despite above instructions few housing finance
companies (HFCs) are still continuing to submit their returns in the old
formats. The mistake is being repeated even after reminders and notices to
individual HFCs and is affecting compilation of data. All HFCs are required to
exercise due care in submission of returns and ensure that they are only in
formats prescribed under the Housing Finance Companies (NHB) Directions,
2010. Please also note that these returns are further revised from time to time
by amendments to the said Directions and HFCs are expected to update their
return formats accordingly.
3.  Please note that the said Directions, the amendments to them from time
to time and updated formats of returns are also available on our Bank's
website.
4.  Further, it is also observed that many HFCs and their auditors are
altering the format of the returns and/ or certificates appended to such
returns. It is advised that all HFCs may exercise proper caution in this
connection and ensure furnishing of such certificates including auditor's
certificate only in formats specified under the said Directions.
Kindly acknowledge receipt.
Yours faithfully,
