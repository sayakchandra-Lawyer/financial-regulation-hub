Sir/ Madam,

Submission of Supervisory Returns through Centralised Reporting and Management
Information Systems (CRaMIS) Portal

The Bank has implemented the XBRL based reporting platform namely Centralised Reporting
and Management information System (CRaMIS) for ease of data submission, more efficient
data collection and its usage.

2.   In this regard, all HFCs are advised to submit the returns which are due w.r.t. position

as on December 31, 2022 through the XBRL based CRaMIS Portal. No return/ information

submitted through the existing ORMIS portal will be accepted. The existing ORMIS portal will

be deactivated by midnight of December 31, 2022.

3.   All other reporting instructions will be governed by the provisions of the Master

Circular No. NHB(ND)/DOS/Sup.Circular No.7/2021-22 dated December 31, 2021.

Yours faithfully,
