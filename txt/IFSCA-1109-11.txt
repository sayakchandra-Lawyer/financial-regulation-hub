                                           Consultation Paper

          Guidelines for engaging Financial Solicitation Professionals (FSPs)

A. Objective:

1. The objective of this paper is to seek comments and suggestions from the public and
    stakeholders on the proposed guidelines for engaging of Financial Solicitation
    Professionals (“FSPs”) by Financial Institutions (“FIs”) in the International Financial
    Services Centre (“IFSC”).

B. Background:

2. The International Financial Services Centres Authority (“IFSCA”) has been established
    under the International Financial Services Centres Authority Act, 2019 (“IFSCA Act”)
    with the mandate to develop and regulate the financial services market in the IFSC in
    India and for matters connected therewith or incidental thereto.

3. The IFSC ecosystem has witnessed significant growth in banking, capital markets,
    fund management, insurance and other financial services. As the range of products
    and services available in the IFSC continues to expand, there is also a need to facilitate
    greater awareness and access to such products and services to the clients.

4. FIs in the IFSC may expand their outreach by engaging professionals (i.e. individuals),
    other than their employees, in India and overseas jurisdictions to solicit business on
    behalf of such FIs thereby enhancing reach and expanding their business to new
    clients, new geographies etc.

5. Such solicitation activities require appropriate transparency and consumer
    protection measures, so that clients are able to readily ascertain whether an individual
    approaching them is engaged by an FI. It is therefore proposed that the FIs shall
    disclose relevant details of the FSPs engaged by them on their websites or, as the case
    may be, on a dedicated webpage on the website of their parent entity, to ensure
    transparency.

C. Proposed Guidelines:

6. IFSCA had earlier issued a consultation paper titled – “Consultation paper on the draft
    International Financial Services Centres Authority (IFSC Financial Advisers)
    Regulations, 2026” dated February 24, 2026, seeking comments from the public.

7. Upon further consideration and deliberations on the nature of the proposed activities
    and the existing regulatory framework applicable to Financial Institutions set up in
    IFSC, it is considered that a separate regulatory framework for Financial Advisers may
    not be warranted at this stage. Accordingly, it has been decided to adopt a disclosure-
    based approach to ensure transparency in the solicitation activities done by FSPs on
    behalf of already regulated Financial Institutions.

8. Therefore, the proposed guidelines inter alia, cover the following aspects:

    a. The FI will undertake appropriate due diligence before engaging an FSP;

    b. The FSP shall comply with specified requirements while soliciting clients or
        facilitating their access to financial products or financial services, including
        disclosure of the identity of the FI, providing accurate and appropriate
        information, not making any false or misleading representations, complying with
        applicable jurisdictional requirements, not accepting or holding client assets and
        maintaining confidentiality of client information;

    c. The FI will remain responsible for all acts and omissions of the FSPs in the course
        of soliciting clients or facilitating their access to financial products or financial
        services on its behalf;

    d. The FI shall take appropriate action, including termination of engagement, against
        an FSP whose conduct is not in accordance with the proposed Guidelines; and

    e. The FI shall publish and maintain an updated list of all FSPs engaged by it on its
        website or, as the case may be, on a dedicated webpage on the website of its parent
        entity.

D. Public Comments

9. In view of the above, comments and suggestions from the public are invited on the
    proposed guidelines for engaging of FSPs, as set out in the draft Circular at Annexure
    A. The comments may be sent by email to Shri Akash Boddeda, Manager at
    akash.boddeda@ifsca.gov.in and Shri Nikhil, Assistant Manager at
    nikhil.solanki@ifsca.gov.in with a copy to Shri Arjun Prasad, Chief General Manager
    at arjun.pd@ifsca.gov.in with subject line “Comments on the draft Circular on
    Guidelines for engaging of Financial Solicitation Professionals” latest by
    November 30, 2026.

Subject: Guidelines for engaging Financial Solicitation Professionals

1. In order to facilitate wider outreach of, and access to the financial products or
    financial services offered by the Financial Institutions in the International
    Financial Services Centre (“IFSC”), while ensuring transparency and adequate
    customer protection, the International Financial Services Centres Authority
    (“IFSCA” or “Authority”) hereby issues the following guidelines (“Guidelines”) for
    the engagement of Financial Solicitation Professionals by such Financial
    Institutions.

2. These Guidelines shall apply to all Financial Institutions registered, licensed,
    recognised, authorised, permitted or approved by the Authority which engage, or
    propose to engage, individuals, other than their employees, for soliciting or
    facilitating access to financial products or financial services on their behalf. Such
    an individual shall be referred to as a “Financial Solicitation Professional” or “FSP”.

3. The Financial Institution shall undertake appropriate due diligence before
    engaging an FSP and ensure that every FSP engaged by it, while soliciting clients
    or facilitating their access to financial products or financial services:

     a. clearly discloses to the client the name of the Financial Institution whose
         financial product or financial service is being offered;

     b. does not claim to be an employee of such Financial Institution, or to be
         registered with or approved by the Authority;

            Page 4 of 5
     c. provides accurate information on the financial product or financial service,
         and does not make any false, misleading or exaggerated statement or give any
         assurance of returns;

     d. solicits only the financial products or financial services, offered by the FI, and
         in the jurisdiction for which the FSP is engaged, in compliance with the laws
         of that jurisdiction;

     e. does not accept or hold any money, securities or other assets of a client; and

     f. keeps the information of the client confidential.

     The requirements set out above are in addition to any code of conduct applicable
     under the Regulations governing the Financial Institution.

4. The Financial Institution shall remain responsible for all acts and omissions of an
    FSP in the course of soliciting clients or facilitating their access to financial
    products or financial services on its behalf.

5. The Financial Institution shall take appropriate action, including termination of
    the engagement, against an FSP whose conduct is not in accordance with these
    Guidelines.

6. The Financial Institution shall publish a list of all FSPs engaged by it on its website
    or, as the case may be, on a dedicated webpage on the website of its parent entity,
    and shall promptly update the list upon appointment, suspension, termination or
    cessation of engagement of any FSP.

7. This Circular is issued in exercise of the powers conferred under sections 12 and
    13 of the International Financial Services Centres Authority Act, 2019, and shall
    come into force with immediate effect.

8. A copy of this Circular is available on the website of the Authority at
    www.ifsca.gov.in.
