TO ALL REGISTERED HOUSING FINANCE COMPANIES (HFCs)
Madam/ Dear Sir,
The Housing Finance Companies (NHB) Directions, 2010:
Submission of Returns
As you are aware that National Housing Bank has issued the revised Housing Finance
Companies (NHB) Directions, 2010 as published and notified it in the Gazette of India,
dated June 26 - July 2, 2010, after incorporating the amendments affected in the earlier
Directions, 2001 till May, 2010. The updated version of the captioned Directions is
available on our Bank's website. Further, a copy of the same was also sent to all
registered HFCs for their reference. Thereafter, amendments on Loan to Value (LTV)
Ratio, Risk Weight and Provisioning Norms were made in the said Directions vide
Notification No. NHB.HFC.DIR.2/CMD/2010 dated 24th December, 2010 and published.
A copy of said notification was also circulated to all HFCs, for ensuring compliance.
2. However, it is observed that in some cases, HFCs have submitted their returns in the
old format, instead of using the new format prescribed in the said Directions. Further, it
is also observed that information furnished in the return by certain HFCs were
incomplete, either on account of deficiency in data or omission of certain part of the
format/enclosures. It is advised that all HFCs may exercise due care in submission of
returns and provide complete information by strictly adhering to the specified formats
provided as per the captioned Directions.
3. It may please be noted that the updated format of all returns are available on our
Bank's website.
4. Kindly acknowledge receipt.
Yours faithfully,
Ajom
