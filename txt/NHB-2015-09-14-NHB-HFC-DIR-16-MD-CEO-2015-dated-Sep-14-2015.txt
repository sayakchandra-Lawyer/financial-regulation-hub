Notification No. NHB.HFC.DIR.16/MD&CEO/2015 - In exercise of the powers conferred by sections
30A and 31 of the National Housing Bank Act, 1987 (53 of 1987) and all the powers enabling it in this
behalf, the National Housing Bank having considered it necessary in the public interest and on being
staisfie4d that for the purpose of enabling it to regulate the housing finance system of the country to its
advantage, it is necessary so to do, hereby directs that the Housing Finance Companies (NHB)
Directions, 2010 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Directions), shall, with immediate effect, be
amended in the following manner, namely:
