NHB (ND)/ROD/ Refinance Circular No. 2/2024-25
September 05, 2024

To,

All Housing Finance Companies

Madam/Sir,

    NHB's right to carry out valuation of underlying residential units flagged by
                                     HFCs for availing refinance

This is to bring to the notice of all Housing Finance Companies (HFCs) that National
Housing Bank (NHB) in its discretion may carry out valuation of underlying
residential properties upto 10% of the flagged loan pool assigned to NHB against
refinance. There should not be any inconsistency w.r.t. book debt value of the
respective property assigned in the flagged loan pool vis-à-vis valuation carried out
by NHB through its select valuers.

Yours faithfully,
