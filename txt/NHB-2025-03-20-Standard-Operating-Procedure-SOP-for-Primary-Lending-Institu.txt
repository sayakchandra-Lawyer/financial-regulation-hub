Preface

Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana - Urban 2.0 (PMAY-U 2.0) has been launched
with an objective to provide pucca houses to 1 crore people of urban India.
The Scheme emphasizes on empowerment of urban poor and middle-class
families by giving them secured pucca houses to focus on the betterment
of their lives. PMAY-U 2.0 will provide financial assistance to 1 crore
families to construct, purchase or rent a house in urban areas over a
period of 5 years. Government Assistance of 2.30 lakh crore will be
provided under the Scheme.

Families belonging to EWS/LIG/MIG segments, having no pucca house
anywhere in the country, are eligible to purchase or construct a house
under PMAY-U 2.0. The Scheme will be implemented through different
verticals, giving eligible beneficiaries the option avail benefits under any
one of the four verticals as per their choice and eligibility.

A unified web portal has been developed to effectively execute the Scheme.
It also ensures transparency, helps in monitoring and efficient
management of the Scheme and more importantly, to enable beneficiaries
to directly apply under PMAY-U 2.0. The portal simplifies the process for
application, allows beneficiaries to know the status of application,
modalities of data exchange among stakeholders, ensures faster due-
diligence, approval and release of subsidy. The web portal is accessible to
all stakeholders and will help in monitoring and evaluating of PMAY-U 2.0
scheme.

The user manual will help the beneficiaries and all stakeholders about the
web portal and will guide them on how to use it effectively. It will take the
users through the portal's features, sections, tools to ensure smooth
implementation.

Background

PMAY-U, launched in June 2015, is one of the major flagship programmes of
Government of India to provide all-weather pucca houses to all eligible
beneficiaries in the urban areas. Based on the learnings gathered during the
implementation of the PMAY-U Scheme and, also to meet the housing
requirements arising out of the increase in the number of eligible families, the
Union Cabinet on 10th June 2024 resolved to provide assistance to 3 crore
additional rural and urban households for the construction of houses.
Subsequently, on 9th August 2024, the Union Cabinet, chaired by the Hon'ble
Prime Minister Shri Narendra Modi, approved Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana -
Urban 2.0 for the next 5 years.

In pursuance of the Hon'ble Prime Minister's vision, PMAY-U 2.0 will address the
housing needs of 1 crore families, ensuring that every citizen leads a better quality
of life. The Scheme will be implemented through the following four verticals:

· Beneficiary Led Construction (BLC)

· Affordable Housing in Partnership (AHP)

· Affordable Rental Housing (ARH)

· Interest Subsidy Scheme (ISS)

About Interest Subsidy Scheme (ISS)

PMAY-U 2.0 will achieve the vision of `Housing for All' by fulfilling the housing
dreams of EWS/LIG/MIG segments. The Scheme will also ensure equity across
different segments of population by addressing the requirement of slum dwellers,
SC/STs, minorities, widows, Persons with Disabilities and other underprivileged
sections of the society.

Interest Subsidy Scheme (ISS) vertical is implemented as a Central Sector Scheme
to expand institutional credit flow to the affordable housing sector though
implementing agencies such as CNAs viz; NHB/SBI/HUDCO and PLIs. Subsidy
will be provided on home loans sanctioned and disbursed on 01.09.2024 or after
to eligible beneficiaries of EWS/LIG and MIG for purchase/ re-
purchase/construction of houses.

For scheme implementation, Primary Lending Institutions such as Scheduled
Commercial Banks, Housing Finance Companies, Regional Rural Banks (RRBs),
State Cooperative Banks, Urban Cooperative Banks [Small Financial Banks, Non-
Banking Financial Company- Micro Finance Institutions' (NBFC- MFIs)] or any
other financial institutions as may be identified by the RBI are eligible to claim
subsidy on eligible home loans only after signing of MoU with one of the CNAs for
implementation of the Scheme. The system will ensure that the loan is taken from
a financial institution that is a Primary Lending Institution (PLI) registered under
PMAY-U 2.0 for ISS.

Roles and Responsibility of PLI:

    · PLI is responsible for claiming the subsidy of their existing borrower.

    · PLI are also required to take action on applications of interested citizens
         who have shown interest for taking loan.

    · PLI are required to enter the detail information of beneficiary/borrower and
         family details such as co-borrower details and submit applications for
         review and approval by MoHUA/CNAs.

    · PLI is responsible for crediting the subsidy into home loan account of the
         beneficiary upon release of funds from MoHUA through CNAs into the PLI
         account.
Coverage

All Statutory Towns as per Census 2011 and towns notified subsequently and
areas falling within the boundary of Notified Planning Areas, Notified
Planning/Development area under the jurisdiction of Industrial Development
Authority/Special Area Development Authority/Urban Development Authority or
any such Authority under State legislation which is entrusted with the functions
of urban planning and regulations shall be included for the coverage under the
Scheme for all verticals.

The Cities/Towns and areas falling under Notified Planning Areas, Notified
Planning/Development area under the jurisdiction of Industrial Development
Authority/Special Area Development Authority/Urban Development Authority or
any such Authority, where PMAY-U is being implemented will continue to be
covered under PMAY-U 2.0. Cities/towns which were not part of PMAY-U may also
be included in PMAY-U 2.0 with due approval of MoHUA.

List of cities can also be accessed at PMAY-U 2.0 website under publication.

Scheme features1

Eligibility

    1. The beneficiary can find out their eligibility based on the following eligibility
         parameters under ISS at unified web portal:
             a. Undertaking on not owning a house anywhere in India except for the
                  one being acquired/constructed
             b. Declaration on not availing benefit under any housing schemes in last
                  20 years
             c. Household income is not exceeding 9 lakh per annum
             d. Home loan is not more than 25 lakh
             e. House value is not more than 35 lakh
             f. Carpet area of the house is not more than 120sqm

    2. A beneficiary family will comprise of husband, wife, unmarried sons and/or
         unmarried daughters. User must fill the details of all family members with
         Aadhar Details, Date of Birth, Relationship with Beneficiary and
         Occupation.

    3. In case of a married male, details of wife should be included; and in case of
         unmarried children, their details should be included.

    4. In case if the borrower is unmarried and earning, then he/she can take loan
         with parents/siblings.

    5. EWS households are defined as households with an annual income up to 3
         lakh. LIG households are households with an annual income from 3 lakh
         up to 6 lakh. MIG households are households with an annual income from
         6 lakh up to 9 lakh.

    6. As per the scheme guidelines, under ISS vertical, a maximum interest
         subsidy of 1.80 lakh is provided to eligible beneficiaries having annual
         family income up to 9 lakh. Home loan value up to 25 lakh for property
         value up to 35 lakh with carpet area of houses up to 120 sqm. are eligible
         for a subsidy at 4.0% on first 8 lakh for a tenure up to 12 years.

    7. The subsidy will be provided to those beneficiaries who have submitted the
         approved layout plan to the PLIs as per guidelines of States/UTs. However,
         the layout plan may not be insisted by PLIs where States/UTs have provided
         the exemption for construction of houses having provision of deemed

1 These are to be referred with main guidelines of PMAY-U 2.0 only.
         approval or pre-approved building plan.

Timeline

    8. Subsidy will be provided on home loans sanctioned and disbursed on
         01.09.2024 or after to eligible beneficiaries of EWS/LIG and MIG for
         purchase/ re-purchase/construction of houses.

    9. For availing the benefit under ISS vertical, the PLI shall claim the subsidy
         amount through a proper channel within thirty (30) days from the date of
         disbursement of home loan to the beneficiary; otherwise, it shall be
         responsibility of the PLI.

    10. Subsidy will be released in 5 yearly instalments in home loan accounts
         provided loan is standard and live at the time of release of subsidy and more
         than 50% principal is outstanding.

    11. PLI are required to update the credit /non-credit of subsidy in beneficiary
         account on UWP within 7 days from the date of funds released by CNAs to
         the PLI Ac.

    12. Physical progress of the construction of a house is required to be updated
         on the annual basis, for claiming of each instalment against a loan account
         over 5 years. It is to be ensured by PLI that before releasing of 4th
         instalment (subsidy) to the beneficiary the geo-tagging of house at
         completed level is to be captured in MIS/UWB in 4th year.

Points to note

    13. A citizen who wants to take loan and avail interest subsidy is required to
         submit the application through Unified Web Portal of PMAY-U 2.0, Common
         Service Centre (CSC) or by visiting the concerned Urban Local
         Bodies/Municipalities/Primary Lending Institution (PLI).

    14. PLIs will not take any processing charge from the beneficiary for housing
         loans up to the eligible loan amount (upto 8 lakh) under the Scheme.

    15. A 'Pucca' house is defined as an all-weather dwelling unit with roof and
    walls made up of sturdy, modern, and durable materials like burnt bricks,
    stones packed with cement or lime, cement concrete, timber, Galvanized
    Iron (GI) sheets, asbestos sheets, machine-made tiles etc.

16. Present address is the current location where the applicant is residing at
    the time of registration.

17. Permanent address is the official and long-term residence of the applicant
    as per the legal or official documents.

18. Construction site address is the location where the housing construction
    or the proposed site of construction under PMAY-U 2.0.

19. The subsidy under the scheme will be provided only once for a property. If
    it is sold to someone else, the purchaser can't take benefit of ISS on this
    property. PLIs are to ensure that subsidy benefit is not taken by the
    previous owner by checking on the unified web portal, before initiating the
    claim of the current occupant.

20. The houses under PMAY-U 2.0 will be used only for residential purpose
    and shall not use for any other activities and the same will be self-certified
    by the beneficiaries. Otherwise, the Subsidy will be discontinued, and
    recovery of already leased amount shall be recovered.

21. Preference under the scheme will also be given to widows, Persons with
    Disabilities, transgenders, persons belonging to Scheduled
    Castes/Scheduled Tribes, and Minorities.

22. A loan given for land purchase and construction shall be limited to 25
    lakh only and the cost of land plus construction cost shall not exceed 35
    lakh and the size of the house constructed shall not exceed 120 sqm.

23. Geo-tagging through Bhuvan/Bharat HFA/PMAY mobile application, of
    houses along with their geo-spatial location is required to be done by the
    respective PLI/Beneficiary as per the payment plan. Geo-tagging of houses
    to be done after release of 1st instalment, in case of purchase of completed
    house. In case of construction/under construction house, Geo-tagging is to
    be done before release of each instalment.
Exclusions/Ineligibility Criteria under ISS

    24. The subsidy under the scheme will be provided only once per property. If
         it is sold to someone else, the purchaser can't take benefits of ISS on this
         property.

    25. If two or more family members take combined or separate loan on same
         property, they will be treated as same family for calculation of family income
         and benefit.

    26. In case a borrower has taken a housing loan from one PLI and later
         switches to another PLI for balance transfer such beneficiary will not be
         eligible to claim the benefit of interest subsidy again. If a person has not
         claimed interest subsidy from first PLI, he/she shall not be eligible after
         balance transfer.

    27. Only loan up to 25 lakhs with the house value up to 35 lakhs will be eligible
         for subsidy under this vertical.

    28. In case of any false declaration on income level by a beneficiary who has
         already availed the scheme benefits, he/she will be liable for legal
         proceedings under applicable laws.

-Rejected: The applications will be sent back to Sent for verification bin in the
CNA tab.
- Clarify: Will be sent back to the PLI for clarification and re-upload.

- Approved CAF: CNA will select the PLI for Subsidy Released from the list of
    ALL PLIs

- The list will show the CAF that has been generated and approved along with
    the details.
System will generate the release form along with the reference number with the
details of the initial release.

PLI LOGIN- Once the release is initiated by the CNA- it will be visible in Subsidy
Released by CNA/Download payment file for processing on PFMS
User may de-select the applications they wish to reject in this list.
Click on Generate Payment File to download the Excel file for processing on
PFMS.

PLI can also REJECT applications for subsidy release here. Once applications
are approved/rejected, the payment file is generated.
Once the Payment file has been updated on PFMS, PLI can now release subsidy
to beneficiary for which payment file has been generated.

PLI can see the release details, click on the applications to open the list of
beneficiaries.
Upon clicking "Proceed to Release" against each beneficiary, the system will
display the details. PLI needs to fill the following details-

    1. Fund released in PFMS date
    2. Date of credit in Beneficiary account.
    3. PFMS transaction ID
    4. Subsidy amount credited

Click on Proceed

PLI may also reject applications at this point. If the beneficiary details are not
released in PFMS, the PLI must enter the rejection reason for the user and reject
on the portal.
