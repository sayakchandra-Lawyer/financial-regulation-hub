                                              MINISTRY OF CORPORATE AFFAIRS

                                                              NOTIFICATION

                                                     New Delhi, the 21st August, 2018

          G.S.R. 797(E).--In exercise of the powers conferred by sections 396,398,399, 403 and 404 read with
sub-sections (1) and (2) of section 469 of the Companies Act, 2013 (18 of 2013), the Central Government hereby makes
the following rules further to amend the Companies (Registration Offices and Fees) Rules, 2014, namely:--

     1. (1) These rules may be called the Companies (Registration Offices and Fees) Fourth Amendment Rules, 2018.

          (2) They shall come into force from the date of their publication in the Official Gazette.

          2. In the Companies (Registration Offices and Fees) Rules, 2014, in the Annexure, under the head VII, for note
below Fee for filing e-form DIR-3 KYC, the following note shall be substituted, namely:-

          "for the current financial (2018-2019), no fee shall be chargeable till

           the 15th September, 2018 and fee of Rs.5000 shall be payable on or after the 16th September, 2018".

                                                                                                            [F. No. 01/16/2013 CL-V (Pt-I)]
                                                                                                                  K. V. R. MURTY, Jt. Secy.
