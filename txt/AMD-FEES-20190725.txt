                                        MINISTRY OF CORPORATE AFFAIRS

                                                        NOTIFICATION

                                                New Delhi, the 25th July, 2019

         G.S.R. 527(E).--In exercise of the powers conferred by sections 396, 398, 399, 403 and 404 read with
sub-sections (1) and (2) of section 469 of the Companies Act, 2013 (18 of 2013), the Central Government hereby
makes the following rules further to amend the Companies (Registration Offices and Fees) Rules, 2014,
namely:--

1. (1) These rules may be called the Companies (Registration Offices and Fees) Fourth Amendment
              Rules, 2019.

         (2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette.

2. In the Companies (Registration Offices and Fees) Rules, 2014, in the Annexure, for item VII, the
following item shall be substituted, namely:-

      "VII. FEE FOR FILING e- Form DIR-3 KYC or DIR-3 KYC-WEB under rule 12A of the Companies
      (Appointment and Qualification of Directors) Rules, 2014.
