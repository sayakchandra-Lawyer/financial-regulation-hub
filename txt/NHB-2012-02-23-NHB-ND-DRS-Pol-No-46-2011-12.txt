                                    Uniformity in charging interest (floating
                                                rate basis) -Clarification

      This has reference to our Policy Circular No. 44 dated October 19, 2011 on the above
      subject. Bank has received representations from some of the housing finance
      companies as also from certain borrowers seeking clarifications/ review of the
      Circular. The borrowers have desired to know whether the instructions contained in
      the Circular are mandatory and whether the same applies retrospectively or
      prospectively. The housing finance companies have sought time to complete the
      risk/ credit profiling of the customers. They have also represented that bringing
      uniformity may result in the increase of the interest rates to some of their existing
      borrowers and may not be accepted by them in view of the covenants in the
      agreements entered into with such borrowers. Certain other operational issues have
      also been raised.

      2. The position has been reviewed in the above background as also that the
      lending practices adopted by the housing finance companies are simple, non-
      discriminatory, transparent and fair. The position with regard to the Circular is
      hereby again clarified and the housing finance companies are advised as under:

      a) The instructions contained in the Circular are mandatory and applies to all
          the housing loans disbursed on or after the date of the issue of the Circular.
          Consequently, HFCs should apply uniform rates of interest to all the
          individual borrowers having same credit/risk profile and have availed the
          housing loans on floating rate basis after the date of the Circular.

      b) HFCs should complete the credit/risk profiling of its individual housing loan
          borrowers by April 30, 2012 and convey the same to the borrowers.

      c) This will not apply to the special Housing Loan Schemes offered by the HFCs
          on certain occasion for the limited period for which the said special rate is
          applicable.

      3. You are advised to ensure compliance of the above, as non-compliance will
      attract penal provision under National Housing Bank Act, 1987.
