                                  Amendment of HFC (NHB) Directions, 2010

Housing Finance Companies (NHB) Directions, 2010 have been amended with immediate effect,
as under:

    1. After paragraph 27 of the aforesaid Directions, the following has been inserted.

" 27 A. Loan to Value (LTV) Ratio
No housing finance company shall (i) grant housing loans upto Rs. 20 lakh to individuals
with LTV ratio exceeding 90% and (ii) grant all other housing loans above Rs. 20 lakh to
individuals with LTV ratio exceeding 80%."
