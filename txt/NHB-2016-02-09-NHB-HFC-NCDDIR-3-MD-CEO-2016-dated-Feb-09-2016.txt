                          NATIONAL HOUSING BANK

                                 New Delhi, the 9th February, 2016

Notification No. NHB.HFC.NCD-DIR.3/MD&CEO/2016 - In exercise of the powers
conferred by sections 30A and 31 of the National Housing Bank Act, 1987 (53 of 1987)
and of all the powers enabling it in this behalf, the National Housing Bank, having
considered it necessary in the public interest, and on being satisfied that for the
purpose of enabling it to regulate the housing finance system of the country to its
advantage, it is necessary so to do, hereby directs that the Housing Finance Companies
issuance of Non-Convertible Debentures on private placement basis (NHB)
Directions, 2014 shall, with immediate effect, be amended in the following manner,
namely -

1. Amendment to Paragraph 5

In paragraph 5 of the Housing Finance Companies issuance of Non-Convertible
Debentures on private placement basis (NHB) Directions, 2014, sub-paragraph (2)
shall be substituted by the following, namely:-

"(2) The housing finance company should have minimum credit rating of moderate
degree of safety regarding timely servicing of financial obligations."

                                                     Managing Director & Chief Executive Officer
