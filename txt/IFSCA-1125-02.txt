                           CIRCULAR

IFSCA-CSD/MSC/05/2026-DCS            November 25, 2026

To,
All Regulated Entities in the International Financial Services Centres (IFSCs)

Dear Madam/Sir,

Sub: Adherence to advisories and vulnerability notes issued by the Indian
Computer Emergency Response Team (CERT-In)

1. IFSCA, vide circular dated March 10, 2025, issued the 'Guidelines on Cyber Security
     and Cyber Resilience for Regulated Entities in IFSCs', as amended vide circular IFSCA-
     CSD/MSC/1/2026-DCS dated March 10, 2026 (hereinafter referred to as the
     'Guidelines on Cyber Security for all REs'), prescribing a principles-based minimum
     baseline framework applicable to all Regulated Entities (REs). Subsequently, vide
     circular IFSCA-CSD/MSC/2/2026-DCS dated April 20, 2026, IFSCA issued the
     'Guidelines on Cyber Security and Cyber Resilience for Market Infrastructure
     Institutions (MIIs) in IFSC' (hereinafter referred to as the 'Guidelines on Cyber
     Security for MIIs').

2. The Indian Computer Emergency Response Team (CERT-In), the national agency for
     incident response under Section 70B of the Information Technology Act, 2000
     (hereinafter referred to as the 'IT Act'), regularly issues advisories and vulnerability
     notes on cyber threats and vulnerabilities affecting software, hardware and services,
     along with the recommended countermeasures. These are published on the website
     of CERT-In (www.cert-in.org.in).

3. The Guidelines on Cyber Security for MIIs, inter alia, require the MIIs to:

i) align their log retention policy with the guidelines and directions issued by
       CERT-In

ii) align their Vulnerability Assessment and Penetration Testing with the
       applicable guidelines and requirements prescribed by the National Critical
       Information Infrastructure Protection Centre (NCIIPC) and CERT-In

                                                                                Page 1 of 4
     iii) report cyber incidents to CERT-In in accordance with the guidelines and
            directions issued by CERT-In from time to time.

4. Accordingly, with a view to strengthening cyber resilience and ensuring timely
     identification, assessment and remediation of known vulnerabilities across the IFSC
     ecosystem, it has been decided that all REs shall ensure compliance with the
     applicable advisories and vulnerability notes issued by CERT-In from time to time.
     Such compliance shall extend to all relevant IT systems, applications, network
     infrastructure and services deployed, operated or managed by the REs, including
     those hosted, operated or managed on their behalf by parent entities, group entities
     or third-party service providers.

     REs shall be responsible for ensuring compliance with these requirements,
     irrespective of the ownership, hosting or operational management arrangements
     governing such systems and services.

     Towards this effect, all REs, including the MIIs, shall:

     a) Put in place a mechanism for the timely receipt, monitoring and tracking of
            advisories and vulnerability notes issued by CERT-In. This shall include regular
            monitoring of the official CERT-In website, subscription to CERT-In alerts and
            maintenance of an appropriate internal tracking mechanism to ensure that
            relevant advisories are promptly identified and acted upon.

     b) Assess, without undue delay, each advisory and vulnerability note for its
            applicability to the RE’s IT systems, applications, network devices and services,
            including those hosted, operated or managed on its behalf by its parent entity,
            group entity or third-party service provider.

     c) Implement the countermeasures recommended by CERT-In, such as patches,
            configuration changes or compensating controls, within the timelines specified
            in the patch management policy of the RE, which shall be commensurate with
            the severity of the vulnerability. Vulnerabilities rated as critical or high severity
            by CERT-In in the relevant advisory or vulnerability note, those known to be
            actively exploited and those affecting internet-facing systems shall be accorded
            the highest priority for remediation.

     d) Where a recommended countermeasure cannot be implemented within the
            timelines referred to in clause (c) above, the RE shall formally document:

            with the approval of the Designated Officer referred to in para 6 of the
            Guidelines on Cyber Security for all REs, or in the case of MIIs, the Chief
            Information Security Officer (CISO) appointed in terms of para 7 of Annexure A
            to the Guidelines on Cyber Security for MIIs.

     e) Maintain adequate and verifiable records of the assessment of each CERT-In
            advisory and vulnerability note and the action taken thereon. Such records shall
            be made available to IFSCA as and when sought and to the cyber security
            auditor for examination during the course of the cyber security audit.

5. Clauses (b) to (e) of para 4 above shall apply to the advisories and vulnerability notes
     issued by CERT-In on or after the date of this circular.

6. REs operating within group-wide security arrangements may rely on group-level
     arrangements for compliance with para 4 above, provided that such arrangements
     cover the systems supporting their operations in the IFSC and the records referred
     to in para 4(e) above are available with the RE and can be produced to IFSCA as and
     when sought. The responsibility for compliance shall remain with the RE.

7. REs are also reminded that the Directions issued by CERT-In under Section 70B(6) of
     the IT Act, vide No. 20(3)/2022-CERT-In dated April 28, 2022, apply to them
     independently of the guidelines issued by IFSCA. These include the requirements to
     report cyber incidents to CERT-In within six (6) hours of noticing such incidents or
     being brought to notice about such incidents, and to maintain logs of all ICT systems
     securely for a rolling period of 180 days. The REs shall also ensure that logs
     maintained on their behalf by their parent entity, group entity or third-party service
     provider are maintained, and can be produced to CERT-In, in accordance with the
     said Directions.

8. Compliance with this circular shall be covered in the annual cyber security audit of
     the RE under the Guidelines on Cyber Security for all REs, or in the case of MIIs, the
     annual cyber audit under the Guidelines on Cyber Security for MIIs. This para shall

     not apply to the REs exempted from the annual cyber security audit under para 23 of
     the Guidelines on Cyber Security for all REs.
9. REs shall put in place the mechanism referred to in para 4(a) above within thirty (30)
     days from the date of this circular.
10. This Circular is issued in exercise of the powers conferred by Sections 12 and 13 of
     the International Financial Services Centres Authority Act, 2019, to develop and
     regulate the financial services market in the International Financial Services Centre.
This Circular shall come into force with immediate effect. A copy of this circular is
available on the website at www.ifsca.gov.in.
