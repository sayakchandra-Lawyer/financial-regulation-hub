             ANNEXUREI ; ANNEXUREII
All Members & Custodians,

In continuation to the notice no. 20201001-2, dated 01st Oct 2020, Settlement Cycle for non-settlement
related payments from Custodians to Trading Members to be realigned from T+2 to T+1, in line with
the Cash Market segment. Members are advised that the process for settlement of non-settlement
related payments between Custodians and Trading Members has been revised.

Revised Process for Non-Settlement Related Payments

• Custodians shall provide the non-settlement related payment file in the prescribed format
    (Annexure I) on T+1 (settlement) day by 4:00 PM.

• Based on the details provided by the Custodians, the Clearing Corporation shall debit the
    Custodian's clearing bank account and credit the corresponding amount to the Clearing
    Member's (linked to the Trading Member) clearing bank account on the same day.

• A report containing details of such payments shall be made available to Trading Members. The
    report format is provided in Annexure II.

Trading Members and Custodians are requested to take note of the revised process and make the
necessary system and operational changes to ensure smooth implementation.
