October 16, 2024
All Eligible HFCs for availing Refinance from NHB
Madam/Sir,
Addendum to Office Circular No. 04/2024-25
Pool of flagged IHL for availing refinance
In continuation to the office circular no. 04/2024-25 dated: October 09, 2024, it is clarified
that out of total refinance limit availed, HFCs should assign at least 75% of the loan pool
data to NHB which are compliant with the definition of Individual Housing Loan (IHL)
under Priority Sector Lending (PSL) guidelines.
Accordingly, the value of loan pool flagged by HFC, beyond 75% of the refinance amount
and margin may consist of non-priority sector lending individual housing loan accounts.
All the HFCs are advised to take note of the above.
