                                         Further, we also wish to clarify that a dealer is an entity which
                                         buys or sells securities on its own account, assumes the market
                                         risk of the inventory held on its books and seeks to earn profit
                                         from these activities. In financial markets, a dealer typically acts
                                         as a market-maker and trades on its own account. In contrast, a
                                         distributor, is an entity that offers, recommends or sells
                                         financial instruments or services to investors usually for a fee or
                                         commission paid by the client or the entity who engages the
                                         distributor. Therefore, a distributor acts as an agent, whereas, a
                                         dealer acts as a principal.
