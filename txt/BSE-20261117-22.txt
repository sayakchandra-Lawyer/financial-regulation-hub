To All Trading Members,

This has reference to Exchange Circular No. 20260514-27 dated 14 May 2026, regarding the submission of the
VAPT Report for FY 2025-26.

As per the said Circular, in case of any “Open Vulnerabilities” have been identified by the CERT-In empanelled
auditor in the VAPT Report for FY 2025-26, Trading Members are required to submit the Action Taken Report
(ATR), to the Exchange through the BEFS Portal on or before 30 November 2026.

It may be noted that the submission of VAPT Action Taken Report shall be considered complete only upon
submission of the digitally signed report to the Exchange.

With reference to Exchange Notice No. 20250929-81 dated September 29, 2025, regarding technology-based
sharing mechanisms for common submissions among exchanges, Members of the Exchange who are also
registered with NSE shall submit their VAPT Action Taken Report to NSE only. Members of the Exchange who are
not registered with NSE shall continue to make submissions to the Exchange as per existing process.

Submission is to be made electronically through BEFS Portal on or before due date, to avoid any
penalty/disciplinary action, as prescribed in Annexure A of Exchange circular no. 20260417-45 dated 17 April
2026 regarding "Ease of Doing Business - Rationalization and Standardization of penalties levied on Trading
Members”.
