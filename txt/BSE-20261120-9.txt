Subject: Update in State/Union Territory Names in UCC Database

Trading Members are hereby advised that, pursuant to the changes in the nomenclature of certain States/Union
Territories and the merger of specified Union Territories, the following modifications are being implemented in
the Exchange's UCC (Unique Client Code) system:

Current State Name    Future State Name
Dadra & Nagar Haveli
                      Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu
Daman & Diu           To be discontinued (no new client onboarding)
Pondicherry           Merged with Dadra & Nagar Haveli
Kerala                Puducherry
                      Keralam

Trading Members are requested to:

    • Make necessary changes in their back-office, client onboarding, and UCC management systems.
    • Ensure that all fresh client registrations and modifications use the revised State/UT names.
    • Update existing client records, wherever applicable.
    • Ensure successful upload and validation of UCC data using the revised nomenclature.
    • The above- mentioned changes will be made available on UCC module w.e.f. Saturday, November 21,

        2026, from 2.00 pm

Trading Members are requested to take note of the above and ensure compliance.
