    1. All the TechFin and Ancillary Service Providers in International Financial Services
         Centres (“IFSC”);

Madam / Sir,

Subject: Reporting Norms for the TechFin and Ancillary Service Providers, Global In-
House Centres and Book-keeping, Accounting, Taxation and Financial Crime
Compliance Service Providers- regd.

1. In exercise of the powers conferred under regulation 12 of the IFSCA (TechFin and
Ancillary Services) Regulations, 2025, regulation 12 of the IFSCA (Global In-House Centres)
Regulations, 2025, and regulation 14 of the IFSCA (Book-keeping, Accounting, Taxation and
Financial Crime Compliance Services) Regulations, 2024, the International Financial Services
Centres Authority (the ‘Authority’) hereby specifies the following reporting norms for the
regulated entities under the aforementioned regulations as under:

2. These reports/certificates/disclosures shall be submitted to the Authority by way of an
email at fsssd-dot@ifsca.gov.in.

3. The Authority will continue to monitor the industry, in relation to the aforementioned
regulations, in IFSC and may supplement / update the reporting formats, if so required.

4. Accordingly, at the end of each half-year period, the regulated entities shall access and
use the latest version of the reporting formats available to the IFSCA website under the
“Downloads” section (https://ifsca.gov.in/Download).

5. This Circular shall come into force with immediate effect.

A copy of this circular is available on the website of International Financial Services Centres
