Sir/Madam,
Sub: Credit Risk Guarantee Fund Trust Scheme (CRGFS) for Low Income
Housing-Increase in the eligible housing loan amount from Rs. 5 lakh to Rs. 8
lakh
As you are aware that CRGFS has been notified by the Government of India for the
purpose of providing guarantee in respect of Housing loans to EWS/LIG households
in urban areas. At present, the scheme covers housing loans up to Rs. 5 lakh extended
by Member Lending Institutions (MLIs) to EWS/LIG Households in urban areas.
In the aforesaid context, we are pleased to inform that the maximum limit of eligible
housing loans under CRGFS has been enhanced from the present ceiling of Rs. 5 lakh
to Rs. 8 lakh in order to extend the benefit of the Scheme to moe number of intended
beneficiaries.
The enhanced housing loan limit of Rs. 8 lakh is effective from October 1, 2014.
Accordingly, the housing loans upto Rs. 8 lakh extended by MLIs to EWS/LIG
households in urban areas on or after 1st October, 2014 are eligible for guarantee cover
under CRGFS. You are requested to kindly issue necessary instructions at the
Zonal/Regional/Branch level in this regard.
Kindly acknowledge receipt.
