   All Registered Housing Finance Companies

   Madam/Sir,

   Submission of data related to suit-filed accounts and non-suit filed accounts of
        wilful defaulters of ~25 lakh and above to Credit Information Companies

   Please refer to the Circular No. NHB(ND)/DRS/Policy Circular NO.74/2015-16 dated
   December 31, 2015 whereby the National Housing Bank had issued Guidelines on
   Wilful Defaulters, including the mechanism of reporting the information on wilful
   defaults by the Housing Finance Companies (HFCs) to all Credit Information
   Companies (CICs). HFCs were advised to follow the instructions / directions of the
   said Guidelines for meticulous compliance.

   2. Paragraph 2.9 of the above Guidelines stipulates that HFCs should submit the
   list of suit-filed accounts and non-suit filed accounts of wilful defaulters of ~25 lakh
   and above on a monthly or more frequent basis to all Credit Information Companies.
   It has, however, been brought to the notice of the National Housing Bank that barring
   a few, many of the HFCs are not submitting the above information to the CICs.

   3. The above non-submission of information by the HFCs is seen to completely
   undermine the overall policy efforts towards addressing the concerns over
   persistence of wilful default in the financial system and has been viewed seriously by
   the National Housing Bank.

   4. HFCs are, hereby, once again advised to ensure submission of the requisite
   information to all CICs on a monthly or more frequent basis, latest by 15th of the
   subsequent month. Any further instance of non-compliance by a HFC shall render
   the Company liable to regulatory action, including penal action, as per the provisions
   of the National Housing Bank Act, 1987.

   Yours faithfully,

   (A. P. Saxena)
   General Manager
