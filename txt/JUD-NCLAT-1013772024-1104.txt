Oral: Justice Sharad Kumar Sharma, Member (Judicial)

04.11.2026:  These two Company Appeals arise out of the proceedings

which were initiated under Section 95 of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code,

2016, which were numbered as CP(IB) No.137/BB/2022 and CP(IB)

No.138/BB/2022, against Ashok Chattaraj and M.R. Amalnath, respectively, the

personal guarantors to the debt availed by the Corporate Debtor, M/s. Siesta

Hospitality Pvt Ltd. The said applications were allowed and the Appellants were

admitted into Personal Insolvency Resolution Process (PIRP) by the orders of the

Ld. Adjudicating Authority vide its orders dated 28.05.2024, which are being

challenged in the present appeals.

__________________________________________________________________________________

2. After hearing the two Company Appeals, we had reserved our judgement
on 02.07.2025.

3. The Corporate Debtor, M/s. Siesta Hospitality Pvt Ltd. too, was directed to
be admitted into CIRP under Section 7 of the I & B Code, 2016, by an order
passed by the Ld. Adjudicating Authority on 14.11.2014 in CP(IB)
No.13/BB/2024 and one of the suspended director of the Corporate Debtor, Mr.
Ashok Chattaraj had preferred CA(AT) (CH) (Ins) No.469/2024 challenging the
order of admission of the Corporate Debtor to CIRP.

4. During the pendency of these appeals, the parties have arrived at a
settlement and an application under Section 12A of the I & B Code was filed
before the Ld. Adjudicating Authority in the respective proceedings. The
application filed under Section 7 of the Code, was permitted to be dismissed as
withdrawn vide order dated 08.09.2026, consequent upon which the proceedings
under Section 7 of the Code, in the shape of CP (IB) No.13/BB/2024 has been
closed.

5. Subsequent to the order dated 08.09.2026 passed by the Ld. Adjudicating
Authority, the Appellant Ashok Chattaraj sought withdrawal of the Company
Appeal (AT) (CH) (Ins) No. 469/2024, which has been permitted to be withdrawn
by the Coordinate Bench vide its order dated 27.10.2026.

__________________________________________________________________________________

Comp App (AT) (CH) (Ins) No. 247 & 249/2024  Page 3 of 5
6. Now the Ld. counsel for the appellant would submit that owing to the fact,
the settlement has already been arrived at between the parties, the matters may be
listed for passing appropriate orders.

7. The Ld. Counsel for the Appellant after prior service of the memorandum
to the Ld. Counsel for the Respondents, has placed before this Appellate Tribunal,
the fact that consequent to the settlement as arrived at between the parties, the RP
had filed applications, being IA No.536/2026 in CP(IB) No.138/BB/2022 and IA
No. 534/2026 in CP(IB) No.137/BB/2022 seeking to withdraw the Personal
Insolvency Resolution Process, against Mr. M.R. Amalnathan and Mr. Ashok
Chattaraj, i.e., the Appellants herein, in view of the settlement that has been
arrived at. She has further stated that the said company petitions have been
permitted to be dismissed as withdrawn by the Ld. Adjudicating Authority vide
orders dated 08.09.2026. Now the Ld. Counsel for Appellants would submit, that
since the proceedings CP(IB)/138/2022 and CP(IB)/137/2022 have been
permitted to be dismissed as withdrawn, nothing remains to be adjudicated in
these instant Company Appeals.

8. Owing to the aforesaid development that has taken place after reserving of

the orders passed by us and because of the fact that the withdrawal applications

have already been allowed by Ld. NCLT and because the respective company

petitions have been closed, due to the settlement that has been arrived at between

the parties, these two company appeals too would stand closed because of the fact

that once the proceedings of the company petition has been withdrawn, nothing
survives to be adjudicated.

9. Accordingly, both the Company Appeals stand closed in view of the
withdrawal and closure of the underlying proceedings before the Ld.
Adjudicating Authority. Accordingly, these two company appeals too would
stand closed.
