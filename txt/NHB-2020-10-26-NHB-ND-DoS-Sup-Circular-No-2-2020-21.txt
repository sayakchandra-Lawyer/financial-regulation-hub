                   Campaign for COVID-19: Appropriate Behaviour

The COVID-19 pandemic has led to unprecedented and unanticipated challenges
requiring collective action and support from all. While all necessary measures to
fight the spread of virus are being effectively led by the Central Government and.
State Governments, there is a need to reinforce the importance of preventive
measures and practices in a sustained manner to deal with the pandemic.

2. Hon'ble Prime Minister of India has launched 'Jan Andolan' an awareness
campaign to promote COVID appropriate behavior. The three key messages of the
campaign are: Wear Mask; Follow Physical Distancing; and Maintain Hand
Hygiene.

3. In order to reach every citizen of India through 'Jan Andolan' Campaign, the
partnership between various stakeholders including Central Government, State
Governments, Regulators, Public and Private Sector Industry is essential.

4. Accordingly, the Housing Finance Companies (HFCs), being pivotal players
in the finance sector and with their proximity to the public, are hereby advised to
publicize the message far and wide so as to reach both internal and external
audiences.

5. In order to create awareness about COVID as well as to motivate people to
be more responsible, the following may be considered for giving wide publicity:

     · Display of messages/banners/billboards across all offices/branches and
         associate organizations;

     · Posters, banners, billboards etc. at prominent locations;
     · Pop up/flash messages on opening of website/ app to reflect the message;
     · SMS campaign with message containing to the audience with link to sites

         which have COVID behavior guidelines;
     · Rephrasing of awareness/information messages to carry safety precaution

         related message for COVID;
