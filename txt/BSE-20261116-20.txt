This is with reference to Exchange Notice No. 20260821-26 dated August 21, 2026, regarding action taken report
for observations reported in inspection of Authorised Persons.

Members may note that the Action Taken Report (ATR) in respect of non-compliances observed during AP
inspections for a quarter shall be submitted within two months from the closure of the AP Inspection Report
submission period.

Submission Timelines

Inspections Undertaken During the          Due Date of AP    Due Date of ATR
               Financial Year          Inspection Reporting      Reporting

Inspections done during the Quarter-1       July 31, XXXX      Sep 30, XXXX
Inspections done during the Quarter-2       Oct 31, XXXX       Dec 31, XXXX
Inspections done during the Quarter-3        Jan 31, XXXX      Mar 31, XXXX
Inspections done during the Quarter-4       April 30, XXXX     Jun 30, XXXX

Accordingly, Members are requested to submit ATR in respect of non-compliances observed during AP
inspections for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 (Quarter -2), on or before December 31, 2026, through
BEFS (BSE Electronic Filing System).

The BEFS platform is enabled for submission of the Action Taken Report (ATR). The user manual explaining the
reporting process is enclosed as Annexure A.

All members are requested to take note of the above and ensure timely submission of the ATR within the
stipulated timeline.
