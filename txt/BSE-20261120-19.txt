Sub: Discontinuation of weekly trading in securities of companies suspended for non-compliances of
certain Regulation of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015

This is in continuation to Exchange Notice No. 20260416-28 dated April 16, 2026 regarding suspension of trading
in securities for non-compliance with Regulation 31 (i.e., Submission of Shareholding Pattern) of SEBI (Listing
Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 for two consecutive quarters i.e., September 2025
and December 2025.

As per SEBI Master Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 issued on July 11, 2023 and last
updated on January 30, 2026, the time period of the mandated six months of trading on first trading day of every
week shall be completed on December 07,2026, hence the trading in the securities of the below mentioned
companies on ‘Trade for Trade’ basis in Z group on the first trading day of every week will be discontinued w.e.f,
December 08, 2026.

Sr. No.  Scrip Code  Company Name
1        508922      MSR India Ltd
