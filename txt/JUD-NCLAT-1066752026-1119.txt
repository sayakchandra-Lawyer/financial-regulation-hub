                           O R D E R

                          (Hybrid Mode)

19.11.2026 :    The Bank has filed the present appeal impugning the

order dated 18.05.2026, passed by the National Company Law Tribunal,

Jaipur Bench, (for short the “NCLT”) in I.A. (IBC) No. 20/JPR/2026 in C.P.

(IB) No. 86/95/JPR/2022.

2. The prayer made by the Appellant before the NCLT was for
commencement of Insolvency Resolution Process against the Respondent
No. 1, who is the personal guarantor of U.N. Automobiles Private Limited,
the corporate debtor.

3. It is not in dispute that when the Bank Guarantee of the Respondent
was invoked way back on 01.06.2013, in the first round of litigation when
notice under Section 13(2) of the Securitisation and Reconstruction of
Financial Assets and Enforcement of Security Interest Act, 2002 (for short
the “SARFAESI Act, 2002”) was issued, in an Original Application filed by
the Appellant – Bank before the Debt Recovery Tribunal, Jaipur (for short
the “DRT”), the matter was settled.

4. In terms of the settlement amount was paid by the borrower in part.
As there was again default in the process, fresh SA No. 122/2022 was filed
by the Bank before the DRT. Subsequent thereto, the Bank also filed
petition before the NCLT seeking to initiate Insolvency Process against the
Respondent being a personal guarantor. The claim of the appellant having
been rejected by the NCLT, the matter is before this Tribunal.

5. After having heard the learned counsel for the Appellant, we are not
impressed with the arguments raised by him for invocation of Bank
Guarantee. At the initial stage way back in the Year 2013, when the issue
was settled before the DRT and time for payment of the defaulted loan
amount was extended, the OA was disposed of. Once the proceedings
initiated for recovery of the loan against the borrower as well as the
guarantor in 2013 ended with settlement of the dispute with extension of
time for payment of the dues, the invocation of guarantee by the Bank at
that time will not still survive. In case there was a default by the borrower
in payment of the amount as per the settlement arrived at before the DRT,
fresh invocation was required.
