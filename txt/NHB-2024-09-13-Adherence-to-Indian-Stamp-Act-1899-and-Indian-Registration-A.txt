Adherence to Indian Stamp Act, 1899 and Indian Registration Act, 1908- Advisory

           Bank has received communication from State Governments that certain

financial institutions are not doing registration of the equitable mortgages or deposit
of title deeds as required under various State laws. In some cases, it has also been
observed that there is under-stamping of various legal documents.

2.  In response to these concerns, it is advised that HFCs may ensure necessary

compliance/adherence with the applicable provisions & procedures of the Indian

Stamp Act, 1899, relevant State Stamp Acts & their Schedules and amendments, the

Registration Act, 1908 and various Rules/Notifications/Orders/Circulars/

Guidelines issued by State Authorities from time to time. The compliance will be

verified during the Inspections.

3.  All HFCs are advised to take note of this advisory and ensure compliance.
