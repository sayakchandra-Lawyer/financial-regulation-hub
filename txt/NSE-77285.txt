All Participants/Custodians/Members,

Sub: Settlement of Request for Quote (RFQ) Transactions on Muhurat Trading

This has reference to the circular reference number NSE/DS/77072 dated October 22, 2026
issued by National Stock Exchange of India Limited.

As per the said circular the Exchange shall have a special live trading session in RFQ which
shall be held on Sunday, November 08, 2026, on account of Muhurat trading on Diwali. All
trades executed during this session shall result in settlement obligations and shall be settled as
per below schedule.

Settlement Cycle             Settlement Date           Settlement Number
T+1                          November 09, 2026         2611009
