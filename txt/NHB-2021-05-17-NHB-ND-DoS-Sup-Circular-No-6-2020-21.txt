NHB(ND)/DOS/Sup. Circular No. 6/2020-21  NATIONAL
HOUSING BANK
May 17, 2021
To,
All registered Housing Finance Companies
Madam/Sir,
Submission of Supervisory Returns - Extension of Timelines
In view of the situation prevailing due to the second wave of COVID-19 Pandemic,
the timelines for submission of various supervisory returns to the Department of
Supervision (DoS) are being extended as given below:
(i) the due dates of all monthly returns, which will become due for submission
upto June 30, 2021, have been extended for a further period of 15 days from
the prescribed due dates of submission of the respective returns; and
(ii) the due dates of all quarterly, half-yearly and annual returns, which will
become due for submission upto June 30, 2021, have been extended for a
further period of 30 days from the prescribed due dates of submission of the
respective returns.
2.  The extension is being accorded for the specified period and for the specific
purpose to grant extension of time to the HFCs to comply with the provisions, and
shall not be construed as an exemption from any of the provisions with regard to
submission of returns, either generally or otherwise.
3.  Days mentioned above will include holidays and weekends. The above
extension will not be applicable to returns/ information which are to be reported as-
and-when basis. The HFCs capable of submitting the returns earlier on best effort basis
may continue to do so.
4.  Instructions issued by the other regulators/ authorities in this regard shall be
followed insofar as they are not inconsistent with any of these instructions.
