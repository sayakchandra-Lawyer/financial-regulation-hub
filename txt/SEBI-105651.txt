   Consultation paper on Introduction of Anti-Money Laundering and Countering the
    Financing of Terrorism (AML/CFT) Certification requirement for Principal Officer

1. Objective
    1.1. The objective of this consultation paper is to seek comments/suggestions from the
          public and stakeholders on the proposal relating to introduction of Anti-Money
          Laundering and Countering the Financing of Terrorism (AML/CFT) certification
          requirement for Principal Officer of all intermediaries registered with SEBI under
          Section 12 of the SEBI Act, 1992.
    1.2. The proposed certification is intended to strengthen the knowledge, competence and
          professional capacity of Principal Officers responsible for implementation of AML/CFT
          compliances in the securities market.

2. Background
    2.1. The Prevention of Money Laundering Act, 2002 (“PMLA”) and the Prevention of
          Money-Laundering (Maintenance of Records) Rules, 2005 (“PML Rules”), inter-alia
          prescribe obligations for reporting entities, including intermediaries registered with
          SEBI.
    2.2. As per Rule 2(f) of the PML Rules, the ‘Principal Officer’ has been defined as ‘an
          officer designated by a registered intermediary; provided that such officer shall be an
          officer at the management level’.
    2.3. SEBI has issued the Master Circular dated June 06, 2024 on ‘Guidelines on Anti-
          Money Laundering Standards and Combating the Financing of Terrorism / Obligations
          of Securities Market Intermediaries under the PMLA and Rules framed thereunder’.
          The Master Circular inter-alia provides for appointment of a ‘Principal Officer’ by
          registered intermediaries, including the following responsibilities of a Principal Officer:
         2.3.1. To timely report suspicious transactions to the authorities (FIU-IND);
         2.3.2. To record reasons for treating any transaction or a series of transactions as
                   suspicious
         2.3.3. To act as a central reference point in facilitating onward reporting of suspicious
                   transactions;
         2.3.4. To play an active role in the identification and assessment of potentially
                   suspicious transactions.
    2.4. Further, the Master Circular provides that registered intermediaries should give due
          regard to risk of money laundering and terrorist financing while hiring and ensuring
          existing personnel taking up key positions are fully suitable and competent to perform
          their duties. Additionally, registered intermediaries should conduct ongoing employee

          about regulatory requirements under AML/CFT framework.

3. Proposals for consideration
    3.1. Proposal 1: Certification for Principal Officer: The role and responsibilities of the
          Principal Officer include overseeing and ensuring overall compliance with the
          regulatory guidelines on AML/CFT framework. The Principal Officer is expected to
          have adequate understanding of :
           3.1.1. the provisions of the PMLA, 2002 and PML Rules, 2005;
           3.1.2. SEBI's AML/CFT framework;
           3.1.3. Customer due diligence and Beneficial ownership requirements;
           3.1.4. Risk-based classification of clients;
           3.1.5. Enhanced due diligence;
           3.1.6. Identification and assessment of suspicious transactions;
           3.1.7. Reporting requirements to FIU-IND;
           3.1.8. Confidentiality and prohibition against tipping-off;
           3.1.9. Targeted financial sanctions under UAPA;
           3.1.10. Requirements relating to proliferation financing under the WMD Act;
           3.1.11. FATF high-risk jurisdictions;
           3.1.12. Transaction monitoring systems;
           3.1.13. Maintenance and retrieval of records; and
           3.1.14. Emerging ML/TF risks, including risks arising from new products, technologies
                     and delivery mechanisms.
    3.2. The effective implementation of the AML/CFT framework depends on Principal Officer
          possessing adequate knowledge and competence to discharge their responsibilities.
    3.3. At present, the Master Circular issued by SEBI provides for mandatory appointment
          of the Principal Officer at the management level. However, the said Circular does not
          prescribe any AML/CFT related certification requirement for the Principal Officer.
    3.4. So, the level of AML/CFT knowledge/expertise of Principal Officers may vary across
          intermediaries.
    3.5. SEBI prescribes various NISM certifications for persons engaged or employed by
          securities market intermediaries under Regulation 3(1) of the SEBI (Certification of
          Associated Persons in the Securities Markets) Regulations, 2007. Currently, there are
          a total of 28 NISM certifications mandated for different categories of persons engaged
          or employed by intermediaries. A few of these certifications are listed below:

3.6. Considering the central role performed by Principal Officers in ensuring compliance

with AML/CFT framework, it is proposed that AML/CFT related certification may be

introduced for the Principal Officers.

Proposal 1
Whether AML/CFT certification be made mandatory for every Principal Officer
of a SEBI-registered intermediary?

3.7. Proposal 2: Certification Institute: As part of SEBI’s ongoing efforts to strengthen
     the capital market, SEBI has been organising outreach programs on Anti-Money
     Laundering (AML) and Combating the Financing of Terrorism (CFT) framework for the
     benefit of securities market intermediaries. Towards this objective, the certification
     course on AML/CFT Provisions in Securities Market Certification Examination, has
     been conceptualized and developed by SEBI in collaboration with NISM.

3.8. Vide communique dated February 07, 2025, NISM announced the launch of NISM-
     Series-XXIV: Anti-Money Laundering and Combating the Financing of Terrorism
     Provisions (AML and CFT) in Securities Market Certification Examination w.e.f. Feb
     28, 2025. The Certification examination covers various aspects related to Anti Money

3|Page
          Laundering (AML), Combating the Financing of Terrorism (CFT) and Proliferation
          Financing (PF), requirements under Prevention of Money Laundering Act, 2002
          (PMLA) and PML Rules, 2005, Scheduled Offences, AML and CFT Guidelines issued
          by SEBI, KYC Guidelines, PMLA related cases etc.
    3.9. This certification examination seeks to create a common minimum knowledge
          benchmark for the employees of securities market intermediaries about AML/CFT
          Provisions in Securities markets.
   3.10.In view of the above, it is proposed that the above NISM certification conducted by
          National Institute of Securities Markets (NISM) may be mandated for Principal
          Officers.

            Proposal 2
            Whether NISM-Series-XXIV: Anti-Money Laundering and Combating the
            Financing of Terrorism Provisions (AML and CFT) in Securities Markets
            Certification Examination be mandated for Principal Officers?

 3.11. Proposal 3: Timeline for acquiring the certification: To ensure that Principal
         Officers possess an adequate understanding of their statutory obligations under Anti-
         Money Laundering and Countering the Financing of Terrorism (AML/CFT) framework
         through formal certification, it is proposed that all Principal Officers shall successfully
         complete the prescribed certification course within the following specified timelines.
         3.11.1. For existing Principal Officers: Within a maximum period of six (6) months
                     from the date of issuance of this requirement.
         3.11.2. For newly appointed Principal Officers: Within a maximum period of six (6)
                     months from the date of their official appointment.

 3.12. A Principal Officer shall continuously hold and maintain the validity of the said
         certification at all times throughout their tenure while discharging their official duties
         and responsibilities under the SEBI Guidelines on Anti-Money Laundering (AML)
         Standards and Combating the Financing of Terrorism (CFT) / Obligations.
            Proposal 3
            Whether the timeline of six (6) months for compliance with the certification
            requirement should be mandated for both existing and newly appointed
            Principal Officers?

Dear Sir/Madam,

Subject: NISM Certification for Principal Officer under AML/CFT Guidelines

1. In terms of Paragraph 76 of SEBI Master Circular no.
    SEBI/HO/MIRSD/MIRSDSECFATF/P/CIR/2024/78 dated June 06, 2024,
    registered Intermediaries are required to appoint a Principal Officer to fulfill
    compliance obligations under the Prevention of Money-Laundering (Maintenance
    of Records) Rules, 2005 (PML Rules) and SEBI Guidelines on Anti-Money
    Laundering and Countering the Financing of Terrorism (AML/CFT).

2. To ensure that Principal Officers possess an adequate understanding of their
    statutory obligations under Anti-Money Laundering and Countering the Financing
    of Terrorism (AML/CFT) framework and effectively discharge their obligations, it
    has been decided that all Principal Officers of registered Intermediaries shall hold
    a valid certification of "NISM-Series-XXIV: Anti-Money Laundering and Combating
    the Financing of Terrorism Provisions (AML and CFT) in Securities Markets
    Certification Examination" conducted by the National Institute of Securities Markets
    (NISM).

3. A Principal Officer shall continuously hold and maintain the validity of the aforesaid
    certification at all times throughout their tenure while discharging their official duties
    and responsibilities.

4. The Principal Officer shall successfully complete the course within six (6) months
    from the date of issuance of this circular, or from the date of their appointment as
    such, as the case may be.

5. All registered intermediaries shall ensure strict compliance with these provisions.
    Further, apart from the Principal Officer, other employees handling AML/CFT-

    related activities for the registered intermediaries may also be encouraged to
    undergo the aforementioned certification examination.
6. This circular is issued in exercise of the powers conferred under Section 11(1) of

    7(3) of the Prevention of Money-Laundering (Maintenance of Records) Rules, 2005
    and Regulation 3(1) of the SEBI (Certification of Associated Persons in the
    Securities Markets) Regulations, 2007, to protect the interests of investors in
    securities and to promote the development of, and to regulate the securities
    market.
