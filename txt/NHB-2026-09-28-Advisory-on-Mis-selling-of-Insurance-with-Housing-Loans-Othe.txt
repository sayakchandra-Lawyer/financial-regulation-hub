Advisory on Mis-selling of Insurance with Housing Loans/Other Loans
Reference is invited to Para 49 (1) of Reserve Bank of India (Non-Banking Financial
Companies – Undertaking of Financial Services) Directions, 2025 which states that,
"The Housing Finance Companies (HFCs) should not follow any restrictive practices of forcing
a customer to either opt for products of a specific insurance company or link sale of such
products to any banking product. It should be prominently stated in all publicity material
distributed by the HFC that the purchase by an HFC's customer of any insurance products is
purely voluntary and is not linked to availment of any other facility from the HFC."
2.  In this regard, it is informed that during the course of supervisory inspections
carried out by National Housing Bank (NHB) several instances have been observed
with respect to mis-selling of Property Insurance and Life Insurance along with
Housing Loan/Non-Housing Loan including making Property Insurance and Life
Insurance mandatory for availing loans from the HFC. NHB has also received
complaints from borrowers on the mis-selling of insurance with housing loans/other
loans.
3.  In order to promote transparency in the operations of HFCs, the HFCs are
advised to provide disclosure on the notice board of all the branches (in English and
Hindi or vernacular language of the state) of their company stating that the purchase of
any insurance products by a customer of the company is purely voluntary and is not
linked to availment of any other loan/facility from the HFC. Suggestive disclosure to
be provided on the notice board of the HFC is enclosed as Annexure-I. It is also
advised to ensure that the minimum size of the notice board may be 2 feet by 2 feet as
the board of such a size would facilitate comfortable viewing from a distance of 3 to 5
meters. The HFCs are required to ensure compliance with above advisory and submit
action taken report to NHB within 30 days from the date of advisory.
