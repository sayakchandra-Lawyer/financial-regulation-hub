NHB(ND)/ROD/Refinance Circular no. 15/2024-25
Dated : 02-05-2025

To All Housing Finance Companies

Madam / Sir,

Requirement of Prior Consent for Appointment or Continuation of MD/CEO/Head
    of the Institution in cases of Promoter Shareholding Dilution by HFCs under
                                        Refinance Arrangements

Please refer to NHB(ND)/ROD/Refinance Circular No. 11/2024-25 dated 08-01-2025
on the captioned subject.

In this context, it may be noted that the request for prior written consent for
appointment or continuation of MD/CEO/Head of the Institution, as mentioned in the
above circular must invariably be accompanied by a resolution duly passed by the
Board of the Housing Finance Company on or after the date of the said circular no.
11/2024-25.

Further, in case of existing appointment / continuation, a new resolution may be
passed.

All other terms and conditions of NHB refinance as communicated from time to time
remain unchanged.
