Madam/Sir,
Revision in format of Refinance Returns prescribed for Housing Finance Companies
(HFCs)
It is to be brought into the notice of all HFCs availing refinance facility from National
Housing Bank that the extant return formats prescribed under Annexure – I of HFC – 5
and HFC - 10 have been revised in view of internal requirements and all HFCs are
henceforth advised to fill the information in the revised return format for the mentioned
two returns.
The returns can be accesses using the extant URL on NHB's website as under:
https://www.nhb.org.in/refinance-returns-prescribed-for-housing-finance-companies-
hfcs/
Further, all HFCs are advised to fill the information on NHB's ARRS Portal
(https://arrs.nhbonline.org.in/)
