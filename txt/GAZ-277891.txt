                                                            NOTIFICATION
                                                 New Delhi, the 13th November, 2026
          S.O. 6196(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-clause (vi) of clause (d) of sub-section (1) of
section 3 of the International Financial Services Centres Authority Act, 2019 (50 of 2019), the Central Government
hereby notifies ‘commodity trading contract’ as financial product.
Explanation. – For the purpose of this notification, –
(a) “commodity” shall mean goods of every description, which are capable of being delivered and traded, but excludes:-
       (i) actionable claims, money, currency, securities;
       (ii) any species or specimens listed under the Convention on International Trade in Endangered Species of Wild
              Fauna and Flora; and
       (iii) any items specified under Special Chemicals, Organisms, Materials, Equipment and Technologies by the
              Central Government;
(b) “commodity trading contract” shall mean a contract under which a unit of an International Financial Services Centre
trades a commodity with another party.
2. This notification shall come into force on the date of its publication in the Official Gazette.
