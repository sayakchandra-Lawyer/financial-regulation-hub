Revision in the Refinance Scheme under Affordable Housing Fund (AHF)
National Housing Bank (NHB) aims to promote a sound, healthy, viable and cost effective
housing finance system to cater to all segments of the population.
2.  Pursuant to the announcement in the Union Budget 2018-19, a dedicated Affordable
Housing Fund (AHF) in NHB was established to provide refinance assistance to PLIs in
respect of their individual housing loans to target segmentsin rural andurban areas. A
corpus of 10,000 crore has been allocated for the FY 2019-20 under the AHF scheme.
3.  NHB shall provide refinance for lending to the rural housing sector undertaken by
people falling under the Weaker section category as defined in the guidelines on lending to
priority sector and the rural population with the annual income not exceeding  3 lakh.
Further, NHB shall provide refinance for lending to the urban housing sector falling under
the lower income segments.
4.  In line with the Government initiatives towards addressing the housing needs of
Economically Weaker Sections (EWS), NHB shall provide refinance up to 100% of the claim
amount in rural areas with annual household income not exceeding 3 lakh. It has been
further decided to extend refinance up to 100% of the claim amount in respect of individual
borrowers with annual household income not exceeding 3 lakh (EWS category) in urban
areas also.
5.  The claims will be processed on first-come first-serve basis and subject to
availability of funds. The terms and conditions of Affordable Housing Fund scheme may be
referred from the Bank's website.
