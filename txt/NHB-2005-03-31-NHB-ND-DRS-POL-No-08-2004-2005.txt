Please refer to our Circular No. NHB(ND)/DRS/POL-No.02/2004-05 dated
August 24, 200-wherein the “KYC” guidelines were introduced by NHB for
Housing Finance Companies (HFCs), with effect from October 1, 2004. As
per clause 2.1 of these guidelines, a customer should be introduced by a
person known to the HFC in addition to establishing identity through PAN,
passport, ration-card, driving license, election commission identity card etc.,
in case of individuals and registration certificate, partnership
deed/agreement etc and other reliable documents in respect of companies,
firms and other bodies.
