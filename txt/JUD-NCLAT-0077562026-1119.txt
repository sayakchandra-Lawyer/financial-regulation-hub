19.11.2026 :   Aggrieved against the order dated 15.05.2026, passed by

the National Company Law Tribunal, Court Room No 1, Mumbai Bench (for

short the “NCLT”), the Appellant is before this Tribunal.

2. The NCLT had rejected the claim of the Appellant finding the same
could not have been filed after the resolution plan was already approved. To
put the record straight, we may add that the claim was filed by the
Appellant on 08.10.2025 much beyond the date of approval of the resolution
plan by the Committee of Creditors (“CoC”) on 18.08.2024.

3. Considering the aforesaid fact, we do not find any error in the order

impugned. The appeal is dismissed. Pending applications, if any, are also

disposed of.
