National Stock Exchange of India Limited
Circular

                            DEPARTMENT: INSPECTION

Download Ref No.: NSE/INSP/77476  Date: November 17, 2026
Circular Ref No.: 56/2026

To All Trading Members,

Sub: Reminder for Submission of System Audit Report for the period ending September 30, 2026

This is with reference to Exchange circular no. NSE/INSP/76769 dated October 07, 2026, requiring all
eligible trading members (Type – III) to submit the System Audit Report for the half year ending
September 30, 2026, to the Exchange on or before November 30, 2026.

The submission of System Audit Report is mandatory for all Trading Members (Type – III) who have
traded using algorithm software for all NNF trading software across the segments during the period April
01, 2026, to September 30, 2026. Submission is to be made electronically through ENIT Member Portal
on or before due date, to avoid any penalty/disciplinary action, as prescribed in the Annexure 1.2 of
Exchange circular no. NSE/INSP/73792 dated April 17, 2026, regarding “Ease of Doing Business -
Rationalization and Standardization of penalties levied on Trading Members”.

Trading Members are advised to take note of the above and comply.

For and on behalf of
National Stock Exchange of India Limited

Prashant Aier
