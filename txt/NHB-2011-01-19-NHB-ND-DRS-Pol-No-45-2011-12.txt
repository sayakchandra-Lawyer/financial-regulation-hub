The Housing Finance Companies (NHB) Directions, 2010 have been amended with immediate
effect, as under:

1. Amendment of paragraph 2

In paragraph 2 of the principal Directions, in sub-paragraph (1), in clause (y), -

    (a) in sub-clause (ii), for the words "the Overseas Economic Cooperation
         Fund(OECF), the words " Japan Bank for International Cooperation (JBIC)", shall
         be substituted.

    (b) after sub-clause (x), the following sub-clause shall be inserted, namely:-
         "(xi) any amount received by issuance of commercial paper, in accordance with
         the guidelines issued by the Reserve Bank of India, vide Circular No.
         IECD.3/08.15.01/2000-2001 dated October 10, 2000".
