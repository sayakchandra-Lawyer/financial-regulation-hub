     Introduction / Enhancements in Concessions under the Liberalized Refinance Scheme

The National Housing Bank (NHB), provides concessions under the Liberalized Refinance Scheme
(LRS), extended to Primary Lending Institutions (PLIs) to promote credit flow to various target
segments.

2.   In order to further strengthen credit flow to the priority sector lending and to woman

beneficiaries, NHB has introduced / enhanced the concessions under LRS in the following categories:

     (a) a concession of 25 bps under LRS will now be applicable for refinance against the underlying
          loan pool of individual housing loans that fully comply with the PSL criteria as defined in
          Paragraph 12.1 (i) of the RBI Master Directions on Priority Sector Lending - Targets and
          Classification, and as amended from time to time.

     (b) the existing concession of 10 bps for refinance against the underlying loan pool of individual
          housing loans extended to women borrowers has been increased to 25 bps to further support
          and encourage credit flow under this segment.

     Note: The clubbing of multiple concessions is not allowed.

3.   Further, it is reiterated that refinance availed under the LRS - Fixed Rate Refinance, as outlined

in NHB (ND)/ROD/Refinance Circular No. 07/2024-25 dated January 2, 2025, will not be eligible for

any concessional rates.

4.   All other concessions rates and conditions under existing LRS will remain unchanged and the

above modifications shall be applicable for all future disbursements.
