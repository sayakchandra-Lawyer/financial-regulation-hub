Pre-payment penalty on pre-closure of housing loans
Please refer to our circular No. NHB (ND)/DRS/POL-No. 36/2010 datedOctober
18, 2010 on the above subject. The issue of levying pre-payment penalty or pre-
closure charges by housing finance companies on pre-closure of housing loans by
the borrowers has been considered further by the National Housing Bank in the
light of subsequent developments and it has been decided that hereafter housing
finance companies should not charge prepayment levy or penalty on pre-closure
of housing loans under the following situations:
a) Where the housing loan is on floating interest rate basis ( pre-closed
through any source).
b) Where the housing loan is on fixed interest rate basis and the loan is pre-
closed by the borrowers out of their own sources.
