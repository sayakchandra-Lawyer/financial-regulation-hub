National Stock Exchange of India Limited

Circular

  Department: SURVEILLANCE

Download Ref No: NSE/SURV/77439  Date: November 14, 2026

Circular Ref. No: 877/2026

To All NSE Members,

Sub: Market making compliance file dissemination - Update

This is further to the Exchange Circular no. NSE/SURV/77153 dated October 29, 2026
and NSE/SURV/77202 dated October 30, 2026.

Members are requested to note that the release date w.r.t dissemination of a separate
market making compliance file containing the data for SME scrips traded in ‘Periodic Call
Auction Session (PCAS) Market’ on the Extranet path (/cmftp/Onlinebackup) on an hourly
basis has been revised to November 23, 2026.

Further, all other provisions of the Exchange Circular no. NSE/SURV/77153 dated
October 29, 2026 shall remain unchanged.

In case of any further queries, you may write to us at surveillance@nse.co.in

For and on behalf of
National Stock Exchange of India Limited

Amit Shinde
Chief Manager
Surveillance
