Earmarking of Loan Pool Flagged to NHB for Refinance in the Internal System of
                                Primary Lending Institutions (PLIs)

It has been decided that all Primary Lending Institutions (PLIs) availing refinance
from the National Housing Bank (NHB) shall ensure that the loan pools flagged to
NHB for refinance are distinctly earmarked in their respective internal systems. The
relevant system data shall be made available to NHB, at its discretion, as and when
required.
