Dear Madam/Sir,

  Loans under Rural Housing Funds Schemel Urban Housing Fund Scheme - Clarification

Clarifications have been sought regarding the categorization of loans granted by the Housing
Finance companies under the Rural I'lousing Fund Schemel Urban Housing Fund Scheme of the
National Housing Bank for the purposes of paragraph 28 of the Housing Finance Companies
(NHB) Directions, 20 IO.

The issue has been examined and it is clarified that such loans are not to be regarded as loans
given at teaser/special rates within the meaning of item (iv) of sub-paragraph (I) of paragraph 28
of the Housing Finance Companies (NHB) Directions, 2010.
