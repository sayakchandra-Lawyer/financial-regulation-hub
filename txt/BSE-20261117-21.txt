To All Trading Members,

This is with reference to Exchange Notice No. 20261008-50 dated October 8, 2026, regarding System Audit
Report of Trading Members.

Members are required to submit the System Audit Report for the period ended September 30, 2026, to the
Exchange on or before November 30, 2026. Submission is to be made electronically through BEFS Portal on or
before due date, to avoid any penalty/disciplinary action, as prescribed in Annexure A of Exchange circular no.
20260417-45 dated 17 April 2026, titled "Ease of Doing Business - Rationalization and Standardization of
penalties levied on Trading Members”.

To ensure ease of use and better understanding of the system audit module, a user manual for both Members
and Auditors was previously shared through earlier notices. For your convenience, the same is enclosed herewith
(Annexure A for Member and Annexure B for Auditor).

All trading members are advised to take note of the above and ensure compliance.
