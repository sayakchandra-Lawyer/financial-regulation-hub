                                ORDER

                       (Hybrid Mode)

20.11.2026:        One of the shareholder and director of the Corporate Debtor

has filed the present Appeal impugning the order dated 03.08.2026 passed by

the Adjudicating Authority (National Company Law Tribunal) New Delhi Bench

(Court-II) in IA- 648/ND/2026.

2. The Appellant herein is also a guarantor of the loan raised by the
Corporate Debtor. The issue sought to be raised is that opportunity to file
objections was not afforded by him. From the order dated 11.02.2026 passed
by the NCLT, Delhi Bench, it is evident that the notice of the petition filed by
the Bank was given to the Appellant. It is not in dispute that the same was
duly served. The impugned order was passed on 03.08.2026 and during the
intervening period, the Appellant failed to avail of the opportunity to file any
objection though he has direct relation with the Corporate Debtor as he is the
shareholder and director of the same.

                                       Contd/-……………
                                                             2
3. Considering the aforesaid facts, we find no merit in the present Appeal
and the same is accordingly dismissed.
