                              Preface

Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana - Urban 2.0 (PMAY-U 2.0) has been
launched with an objective to provide pucca houses to 1 crore
people of urban India. The Scheme emphasizes empowerment of
urban poor and middle-class families by giving them secured pucca
houses to focus on the betterment of their lives. PMAY-U 2.0 will
provide financial assistance to 1 crore families to construct,
purchase or rent a house in urban areas over a period of 5 years.
Government Assistance of 2.30 lakh crore will be provided under
the Scheme.
Families belonging to EWS/LIG/MIG segments, having no pucca
house anywhere in the country, are eligible to purchase or construct
a house under PMAY-U 2.0. The Scheme will be implemented
through different verticals, giving eligible beneficiaries the option
avail benefits under any one of the four verticals as per their choice
and eligibility.
A unified web portal has been developed to effectively execute the
Scheme. It also ensures transparency, helps in monitoring and
efficient management of the Scheme and more importantly, to
enable beneficiaries to directly apply under PMAY-U 2.0. The portal
simplifies the process for application, allows beneficiaries to know
the status of application, modalities of data exchange among
stakeholders, ensures faster due diligence, approval and release of
subsidy. The web portal is accessible to all stakeholders and will
help in monitoring and evaluating of PMAY-U 2.0 scheme.
The user manual will help the beneficiaries and all stakeholders
with the web portal and will guide them on how to use it effectively.
It will take the users through the portal's features, sections, and
tools to ensure smooth implementation.

 Background

Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana-Urban 2.0 - `Housing for All' Mission will be
implemented for 5 years to provide financial assistance to 1 crore urban poor
and middle-class families through States/Union Territories (UTs)/Primary
Lending Institutions (PLIs) to construct, purchase or rent a house at an
affordable cost in urban areas.

PMAY-U, launched in June 2015, is one of the major flagship programmes of
Government of India to provide all-weather pucca houses to all eligible
beneficiaries in the urban areas. Based on the learnings gathered during the
implementation of the PMAY-U Scheme and, also to meet the housing
requirements arising out of the increase in the number of eligible families, the
Union Cabinet on 10th June 2024 resolved to provide assistance to 3 crore
additional rural and urban households for the construction of houses.
Subsequently, on 9th August 2024, the Union Cabinet, chaired by the Hon'ble
Prime Minister Shri Narendra Modi, approved Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana -
Urban and Rural for the next 5 years.

In pursuance of the Hon'ble Prime Minister's vision, PMAY-U 2.0, will address
the housing needs of 1 crore families, ensuring that every citizen leads a better
quality of life. The Scheme will be implemented through following four
verticals:

 · Beneficiary Led Construction (BLC)
 · Affordable Housing in Partnership (AHP)
 · Affordable Rental Housing (ARH)
 · Interest Subsidy Scheme (ISS)

PMAY-U 2.0 will achieve the vision of `Housing for All' by fulfilling the housing
dreams of EWS/LIG/MIG segments. The Scheme will also ensure equity across
different segments of population by addressing the requirement of slum
dwellers, SC/STs, minorities, widows, Persons with Disabilities and other
underprivileged sections of the society.

  Coverage

 All Statutory Towns as per Census 2011 and towns notified subsequently
and areas falling within the boundary of Notified Planning Areas, Notified
Planning/Development area under the jurisdiction of Industrial
Development Authority/Special Area Development Authority/Urban
Development Authority or any such Authority under State legislation which
is entrusted with the functions of urban planning and regulations shall be
included for the coverage under the Scheme for all verticals.

The Cities/Towns and areas falling under Notified Planning Areas, Notified
Planning/Development area under the jurisdiction of Industrial
Development Authority/Special Area Development Authority/Urban
Development Authority or any such Authority, where PMAY-U is being
implemented will continue to be covered under PMAY-U 2.0. Cities/towns
which were not part of PMAY-U may also be included in PMAY-U 2.0 with
due approval of MoHUA.

  Eligibility Criteria

Families belonging to EWS/LIG/MIG category, living in urban areas, having no
pucca house anywhere in the country, are eligible to purchase or construct a
house under PMAY-U 2.0. A beneficiary will be eligible for availing benefit
under any one vertical of the scheme.

The beneficiary who has been allotted house under any of the housing scheme
of Central Government, State/UT Government and Local Self Government in
last 20 years in urban or rural areas, shall not be eligible to seek benefit under
PMAY-U 2.0. An undertaking may also be submitted by the beneficiary while
validating the demand by ULB in this regard.

EWS households are defined as households with an annual income of up to 3
lakh; LIG as households with an annual income from 3 lakh up to 6 lakh;
and MIG as households with an annual income from 6 lakh up to 9 lakh.
States/UTs shall have the flexibility to redefine the annual income criteria of
EWS as per prevailing conditions with concurrence of MoHUA.

Preference under the Scheme will be given to Widows, single women, Persons
with Disabilities, Senior Citizens, Transgenders, persons belonging to
Scheduled Castes/ Scheduled Tribes, Minorities and other weaker and
vulnerable sections of the society. Special focus will be given to Safai Karmi,
Street Vendors identified under PMSVANidhi Scheme and different artisans
under Pradhan Mantri- Vishwakarma Scheme, Anganwadi workers, building
and other construction workers, residents of slums/chawls and other groups
identified during operation of PMAY-U 2.0.

It would also be ensured that if a pucca house has been provided to the
parents of any eligible beneficiary under the previous housing schemes then,
he/she will be considered only after other eligible families/beneficiaries whose
parents have not availed any benefit under the previous housing schemes are
included in the list of beneficiaries.
                                                         9
The houses sanctioned under PMAY-U which got curtailed by Central
Sanctioning and Monitoring Committee (CSMC) after 31.12.2023 due to any
reason on the recommendation of States/UTs, shall not be sanctioned in
PMAY-U 2.0 to those beneficiaries.

The beneficiaries may avail benefit in PMAY-Gramin or PMAY-U 2.0 as per
their jurisdiction. The MIS linkage between Department of Rural Development
for PMAY-G and MoHUA for PMAY-U 2.0 would be done to avoid duplication of
beneficiaries. The final list of beneficiaries may be checked and verified by
States/UTs/ULBs before sending to the CSMC for sanctioning.

All eligible beneficiaries (including family members) should have an Aadhaar/
Aadhaar Virtual ID integrated with the details of beneficiaries. In case, any
eligible beneficiary does not have an Aadhaar card/Aadhaar Virtual ID,
States/UTs should ensure that Aadhaar/Aadhaar Virtual ID enrolment of such
beneficiary is done on priority.

PMAY-U 2.0 MIS

The PMAY-U 2.0 website is designed and developed for filing of

applications, record keeping, tracking of applications, monitoring,

documentation and knowledge sharing among the stakeholders at the

National, State/UT/ULB level. The web portal can also be accessed by

parastatals, implementing agencies, and other public/private entities. The

PMAY-U  2.0  website                                    can  be  accessed  at

https://pmaymis.gov.in/pmaymis2_2024/pmaydefault.aspx .

Users can find a wide range of information on the website, including details
about the PMAY-U 2.0 mission, operational guidelines, progress of the
scheme, details regarding the four verticals, FAQs, latest updates and
media coverage, as well as highlights from different events, including all
photos and videos.

The PMAY-U 2.0 website features a dedicated Management Information
System (MIS) that facilitates user logins for stakeholders, including
MoHUA, States/UTs/ULBs, and other implementing agencies.

Additionally, the website provides facilities for public and private entities to
register and submit applications for various components of the scheme.
