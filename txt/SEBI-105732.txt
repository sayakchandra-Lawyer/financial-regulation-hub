                              PUBLIC NOTICE IN THE MATTER OF
 CITRUC CHECK INNS LIMITED AND ROYAL TWINKLE STAR CLUB PVT. LTD.

                                          REFUND – PHASE II
                  FINAL EXTENSION OF CLAIM SUBMISSION WINDOW

As investors are aware that the Committee vide Public Notice dated July 29, 2026
SEBI | PUBLIC NOTICE IN THE MATTER OF CITRUS CHECK INNS LIMITED AND
ROYAL TWINKLE STAR CLUB PVT. LTD FOR REFUND (PHASE-II). had opened
the online refund portal for Phase II refund from 1 August 2026 for a period of 45 days,
with the original claim submission window scheduled to close on 14th September 2026.
Subsequently, the Committee extended the claim submission window by a further
period of 30 days, i.e. up to and including 14th October 2026. Thereafter, considering
the need to provide an additional opportunity to eligible investors who had not
submitted their claim, the Committee granted further extension of claim submission
window by 30 days i.e. up to and including 13th November, 2026

(https://www.sebi.gov.in/media-and-notifications/public-notices/oct-
2026/public-notice-in-the-matter-of-citrus-check-inns-limited-and-royal-
twinkle-star-club-pvt-ltd-for-refund-phase-ii-_105204.html)

The Committee has now decided to grant a final extension of 30 days to the claim
submission window i.e. up to and including 13th December, 2026, to provide an
opportunity to eligible investors who have not yet submitted their claim.

Accordingly, the Phase-II refund portal shall remain open under the domain name
https://www.citrusroyalrefund.com for submission of claims up to and including 13th
December 2026.

It is hereby informed that no further extension of the claim submission window shall
be granted beyond 13th December 2026.

Eligible Investors who have not yet submitted their claims are, therefore, advised to
submit their claims on the portal within the extended period.
