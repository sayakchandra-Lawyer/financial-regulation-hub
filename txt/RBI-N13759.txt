 Section 42(1) of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 - Change in Daily Minimum Cash Reserve Maintenance Requirement
 Please refer to our circular DoR.RET.REC.247/12.01.001/2026-27 dated October 09, 2026 on the captioned subject.
 2. On the basis of an assessment of the current and evolving macroeconomic situation and liquidity conditions, it has been decided to reduce the minimum daily maintenance of the cash reserve ratio (CRR) from 99 per cent of the requirement to 90 per cent effective from the fortnight beginning December 1, 2026.
