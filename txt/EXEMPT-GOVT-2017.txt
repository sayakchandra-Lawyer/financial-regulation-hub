          G.S.R. 582 (E).--In exercise of the powers conferred by clauses (a) and (b) of sub-section (1) of section 462

India, Extraordinary, Part-II, Section 3, Sub-section (i), dated the 5th June 2015 (hereinafter referred to as the principal

notification), namely:-
