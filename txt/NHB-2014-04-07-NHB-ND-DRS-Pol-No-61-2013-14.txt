                   I Reserve Fund under Section 29C of the NHB Act, 1987

     As you are aware that Section 29C (1) of the National Housing Bank Act, 1987 requires
every housing firiance company to create a Reserve Fund and transfer therein a sum not less
than twenty perdent of its net profit every year, as disclosed in the profit and loss account
before any dividend is declared. The provision also provides that a housing finance
company creating and maintaining any special reserve in terms of Section 36(1) (viii) of the
Income Tax Act, 1961 may take into account any sum transferred by it for the year to such
special reserve for the purposes of Section 29C. Further, Section 29C(2) of the NHB Act
provides that no~appropriation of any sum from the reserve fund including any sum in the
special reserve which has been taken into account for the purposes of reserve fund in terms
of sub-section (1), shall be made by such company except for the purpose as may be
specified by the National Housing Bank from time to time and every such appropriation
shall be reported to the National Housing Bank within twenty-one days from the date of
such withdrawal.
