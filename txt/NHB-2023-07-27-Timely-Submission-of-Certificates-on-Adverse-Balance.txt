Refinance Circular No. 01/2023-24-
Timely Submission of Certificates on Adverse Balance
As per the existing guidelines, the HFCs having refinance outstanding with NHB shall
furnish a Quarterly Certificate of Adverse Balance (NHB-HFC-10) and Half Yearly
Certificate of Adverse Balance (NHB-HFC-05) stating that the total outstanding of the
loans flagged as security to NHB is in conformity with the sanctioned terms. On
occurrence of any adverse balance, the adverse balance amount shall be repaid by the
respective HFC to the Bank. The formats of these certificates are available at our
website.
1.  HFC-10 is to be submitted within fifteen calendar days of the reporting quarter
and the adverse balance amount, if any, be remitted by the end of succeeding month
of the reporting quarter.
2.  HFC-05 is to be submitted within sixty calendar days of the reporting half year
and the adverse balance amount, if any, be remitted by the end of succeeding two
months of the reporting half year. The same should be duly signed by the Statutory
Auditors of the HFC and the UDIN number should be mentioned in it.
3.  HFCs are advised to submit these returns in a timely manner and strictly
adhere to the stipulated timelines.
