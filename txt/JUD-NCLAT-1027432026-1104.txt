[Per: Justice N. Seshasayee, Member (Judicial)]

04.11.2026:

These twin appeals are preferred by the financial creditor in a
proceeding initiated under Section 94 of the IBC by the personal
guarantors.

2. The appellant challenges the common order of the
Adjudicating Authority in IA (IBC) No. 406/2026 & IA (IBC) No.
411/2026, which the personal guarantors have taken out, and the
Adjudicating Authority has allowed these applications.

3. Vide the applications now under reference, the personal
guarantors have sought the appointment of a valuer for assessing
the value of their immovable properties and other assets and also
to consider the repayment plan.

4. The Tribunal, vide the impugned order, has appointed a
valuer as required and also directed consideration of the
repayment plan. This order is now under challenge.

5. The only concern expressed by the appellant herein is that,
in their applications, the personal guarantors had mentioned that
pursuant to the consent of both sides made before the Tribunal on
22.08.2025, they had preferred the applications also for

Comp App (AT) (CH) (Ins) No. 582 & 583/2026         Page 4 of 5
considering their repayment plan. The learned counsel brought to
the notice of the Tribunal that on 22.08.2025, the Tribunal has
not recorded any such consent as having been given by the Bank
for considering any such repayment plan. The learned counsel for
the appellant also brought to the notice of this Tribunal on how
the personal guarantors adopt every conceivable tactic to delay the
proceedings.

6. Heard Mr. Vaibhav Gaggar, for the respondent, and the
learned senior counsel defended the order.

7. The short question is, whether the appellant herein has
consented to consider any repayment plan. While keeping other
things constant, we only require that the repayment plan be
considered by the financial creditors afresh. It is underscored that
the personal guarantors might not be entitled to repeat the same
exercise at their convenience often. In other words, this perhaps
will be the last opportunity we want to give the personal guarantors
to submit their repayment plan.

8. We accordingly dispose of these appeals. No costs.
