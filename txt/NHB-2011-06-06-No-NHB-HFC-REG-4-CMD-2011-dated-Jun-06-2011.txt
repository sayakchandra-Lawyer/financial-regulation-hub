NATIONAL HOUSINGBANK
New Delhi, the 6th June, 2011
No. NHB.HFC.REG-4/CMD/2011. In exercise of the powers conferred
by clause (b) of sub-section (1) of section 29A of the National Housing
Bank Act, 1987 (53 of 1987) the National Housing Bank, hereby specifies
the minimum net owned fund to be ten crore of rupees for a housing
finance institution which is a company which commences the business of
a housing finance institution on or after the date of publication of this
notification in the Official Gazette:
Provided that the specification of higher net owned fund shall not be
applicable to a company whose application for certificate of registration
under section 29A of the said Act is submitted to the National Housing
Bank before the date of publication of this notification in the Official
Gazette.
R. V. VERMA
Chairman and Managing Director
