How to add a feed

Outlook (Windows)

In the folder list, right-click RSS Subscriptions (or RSS Feeds), choose Add a New RSS Feed and paste the feed address.

Slack

Add Slack's RSS app to your workspace, then in a channel type /feed subscribe followed by the feed address.

A feed reader

In Feedly, Inoreader, NetNewsWire or any reader, choose Add or Follow and paste the feed address.

Want to keep an eye on particular rules instead? Use the Watch button on any instrument or judgment and see what changed on your watchlist.
