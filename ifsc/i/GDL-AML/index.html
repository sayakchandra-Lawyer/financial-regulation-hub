Key concepts

  • Designated Director and Principal Officer
  • Customer due diligence and KYC
  • CKYCR and KRA
  • Reports to FIU-IND on FINGate 2.0
  • Record keeping

Key obligations

  • Appoint a Designated Director and a Principal Officer and give their details to FIU-IND and IFSCA
  • Carry out customer due diligence and update documents; use the CKYC Records Registry and KYC registration agencies as specified
  • File reports under PML Rules rules 3, 7 and 8 on FINGate 2.0
  • Filing a suspicious transaction report must not by itself lead to restrictions on the customer's accounts

Deadlines and cycles

  • clause 9.2: keep CDD, transaction, STR and related records for at least six years (or longer if the law requires) from the end of the business relationship or the completion of the transaction
  • Customers submit updated documents within 30 days of a change

If you breach it

  • In reporting to FIU-IND, each day of delay in reporting a transaction, or in correcting a misreported one, beyond the time limit is a separate violation

General consequences under the statute. International Financial Services Centres Authority Act, 2019: in an IFSC, IFSCA exercises all the powers of RBI, SEBI, IRDAI and PFRDA under the Acts listed in the First Schedule (s. 13(1)). The provisions of those Acts on inspection, investigation, prosecution, settlement, compounding, adjudication, appeals, and the fees, fines and penalties for contravention apply to IFSC products, services and institutions as they apply under those Acts (s. 13(4)). Penalties, fines, fees and settlement amounts are collected in the foreign currency equivalent, at the RBI rate on the date of the order (s. 13(5)). Most IFSCA regulations also let the Authority suspend, withdraw or cancel a registration, licence or recognition after giving the entity an opportunity to make its submissions, as the regulation concerned provides.
