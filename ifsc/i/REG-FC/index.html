Key concepts

  • Certificate of registration
  • Core activities (lending, factoring and forfaiting, investments, derivatives, treasury centres)
  • Non-core activities (merchant banking, advisory, operating leases, distribution and others)
  • Owned fund (Schedule)
  • Capital ratio, LCR and exposure ceiling
  • Change in control
  • Reporting in USD

Key obligations

  • reg 3: no Finance Company or Finance Unit carries on business without a certificate of registration; a regulated parent needs a home regulator no-objection; the applicant comes from a FATF-compliant jurisdiction
  • reg 3: a Finance Unit may undertake core activities only if it is a regulated financial institution with a no-objection, except a treasury centre; an LLP or trust may undertake non-core activities only
  • reg 4: an entity doing core activities keeps a capital ratio of 8% of risk-weighted assets, keeps an LCR (a Finance Unit may hold it at the parent with approval), and does not exceed 25% of its eligible capital base to a single counterparty or group without approval
  • reg 5 (as amended in 2026): leasing or financing by a Special Purpose Vehicle (a finance company incorporated or administered by a Trust and Company Service Provider) is a non-core activity, as permitted by the Authority
  • reg 5: core activities are lending (loans, guarantees, credit enhancement, securitisation, financial lease, portfolio buys and sales), factoring and forfaiting, investments, derivatives and global or regional corporate treasury centres; non-core activities include merchant banking, investment advisory, portfolio management, operating leases of aircraft, ships and equipment, distribution and similar services
  • reg 5: non-core activities sit in a separately identifiable department with firewalls; entities doing only non-core activities use derivatives only to hedge; no speculative transactions; dealings with residents are subject to FEMA
  • reg 6: deal in freely convertible foreign currency, keep a rupee account only for administrative expenses, and keep the balance sheet in USD
  • reg 7: follow the KYC and AML requirements that apply to Banking Units
  • reg 8: follow the governance guidelines; prior approval for any change in control (20% or more)
  • reg 9: report to the Authority in USD

Deadlines and cycles

  • reg 3: an applicant has 30 days to rectify deficiencies the Authority points out
  • reg 8: a Finance Unit intimates a change in control of its parent within 15 days

If you breach it

  • reg 11: on failure to meet a registration condition, the Authority may suspend, withdraw or cancel the registration after giving the entity an opportunity to make its submissions

General consequences under the statute. International Financial Services Centres Authority Act, 2019: in an IFSC, IFSCA exercises all the powers of RBI, SEBI, IRDAI and PFRDA under the Acts listed in the First Schedule (s. 13(1)). The provisions of those Acts on inspection, investigation, prosecution, settlement, compounding, adjudication, appeals, and the fees, fines and penalties for contravention apply to IFSC products, services and institutions as they apply under those Acts (s. 13(4)). Penalties, fines, fees and settlement amounts are collected in the foreign currency equivalent, at the RBI rate on the date of the order (s. 13(5)). Most IFSCA regulations also let the Authority suspend, withdraw or cancel a registration, licence or recognition after giving the entity an opportunity to make its submissions, as the regulation concerned provides.
