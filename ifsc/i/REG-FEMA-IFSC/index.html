Key concepts

  • IFSC unit treated as a person resident outside India
  • Business in foreign currency with the persons the regulator determines
  • Other FEMA regulations do not apply to IFSC units except as provided

Key obligations

  • reg 3: a financial institution or a branch of a financial institution set up in an IFSC and permitted or recognised as such by the Government of India or a regulator is treated as a person resident outside India
  • reg 4: it conducts business in the foreign currency and with the persons, resident or non-resident, that the concerned regulator determines; the 2020 proviso lets RBI allow business in rupees
  • reg 5: subject to s. 1(3) of FEMA, and save as these or any other FEMA regulations provide or RBI directs, no other FEMA regulations apply to such an institution or branch; this does not exempt residents dealing with it from their own FEMA obligations

General consequences under the statute. International Financial Services Centres Authority Act, 2019: in an IFSC, IFSCA exercises all the powers of RBI, SEBI, IRDAI and PFRDA under the Acts listed in the First Schedule (s. 13(1)). The provisions of those Acts on inspection, investigation, prosecution, settlement, compounding, adjudication, appeals, and the fees, fines and penalties for contravention apply to IFSC products, services and institutions as they apply under those Acts (s. 13(4)). Penalties, fines, fees and settlement amounts are collected in the foreign currency equivalent, at the RBI rate on the date of the order (s. 13(5)). Most IFSCA regulations also let the Authority suspend, withdraw or cancel a registration, licence or recognition after giving the entity an opportunity to make its submissions, as the regulation concerned provides.
