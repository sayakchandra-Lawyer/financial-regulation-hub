Key concepts

  • Registration and unified registration
  • Net worth (Schedule I)
  • Principal officer and compliance officer in the IFSC
  • Grievances, business continuity, cyber security and risk management
  • Change in control
  • Annual compliance audit

Key obligations

  • reg 4: register with IFSCA for each capital market activity; unified registration for several activities is allowed; a Banking Unit may act as banker to an issue or investment banker
  • reg 7 and Schedule I net worth: credit rating agency USD 200,000; custodian USD 1 million; debenture trustee USD 1.5 million; distributor USD 50,000; ESG ratings provider, investment adviser and research entity USD 25,000 each; investment banker USD 100,000; broker dealer, clearing member and depository participant as the exchange, clearing corporation or depository specifies
  • reg 7: a branch may keep the net worth at parent level, earmarked for the IFSC branch; an intermediary in several categories keeps the highest applicable figure
  • reg 9: appoint an IFSC-based principal officer (3 years' experience) and a separate compliance officer (2 years' experience)
  • regs 18, 20, 21 and 22: redress grievances, and keep business continuity, cyber security and risk management frameworks
  • reg 23: an incorporated intermediary needs prior approval for a change in control; a branch intimates it

Deadlines and cycles

  • reg 17(2): intimate a change of principal officer, compliance officer or KMP within 15 days
  • reg 23: a branch intimates a change in control within 15 days
  • reg 25: an annual compliance audit by a member of ICAI, ICSI or ICMAI (or a foreign auditor) is submitted by 30 September
  • Custodians already registered meet the revised USD 1 million net worth by 30-06-2026

If you breach it

  • reg 41: the Authority may suspend or cancel the registration

General consequences under the statute. International Financial Services Centres Authority Act, 2019: in an IFSC, IFSCA exercises all the powers of RBI, SEBI, IRDAI and PFRDA under the Acts listed in the First Schedule (s. 13(1)). The provisions of those Acts on inspection, investigation, prosecution, settlement, compounding, adjudication, appeals, and the fees, fines and penalties for contravention apply to IFSC products, services and institutions as they apply under those Acts (s. 13(4)). Penalties, fines, fees and settlement amounts are collected in the foreign currency equivalent, at the RBI rate on the date of the order (s. 13(5)). Most IFSCA regulations also let the Authority suspend, withdraw or cancel a registration, licence or recognition after giving the entity an opportunity to make its submissions, as the regulation concerned provides.
