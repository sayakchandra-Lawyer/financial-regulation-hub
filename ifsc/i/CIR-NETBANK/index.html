Key concepts

  • Unified dashboard for all linked accounts (deposit and loan)
  • Explicit customer consent
  • Dual-layer authentication, maker-checker and beneficiary whitelisting
  • Bar on onboarding new customers if non-compliant

Key obligations

  • Offer internet banking with explicit customer consent, and display the specified currencies and cut-off times
  • Provide a unified dashboard with real-time balances of all linked accounts (deposit and loan), machine-readable statements, inward remittance instructions, premature withdrawal and SMS or email alerts (live rates are not mandatory)
  • Transactional features include transfers and fixed deposits, with dual-layer authentication, maker-checker controls and beneficiary whitelisting
  • Applies to IBUs that began operating both before and after the circular

Deadlines and cycles

  • Original deadline 31-07-2026, extended once to 30-09-2026

If you breach it

  • From 01-10-2026, an IBU that has not complied stops onboarding new customers for the liability product concerned

General consequences under the statute. International Financial Services Centres Authority Act, 2019: in an IFSC, IFSCA exercises all the powers of RBI, SEBI, IRDAI and PFRDA under the Acts listed in the First Schedule (s. 13(1)). The provisions of those Acts on inspection, investigation, prosecution, settlement, compounding, adjudication, appeals, and the fees, fines and penalties for contravention apply to IFSC products, services and institutions as they apply under those Acts (s. 13(4)). Penalties, fines, fees and settlement amounts are collected in the foreign currency equivalent, at the RBI rate on the date of the order (s. 13(5)). Most IFSCA regulations also let the Authority suspend, withdraw or cancel a registration, licence or recognition after giving the entity an opportunity to make its submissions, as the regulation concerned provides.
