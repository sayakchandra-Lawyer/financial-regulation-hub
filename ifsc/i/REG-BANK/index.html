Applies to
IFSC Banking Units: an IFSC Banking Unit set up as a branch (IBU) or an IFSC Banking Company set up as a subsidiary (IBC); Representative offices and Global Administrative Offices of banks in the IFSC

Key concepts

  • Banking Unit as IBU (branch) or IBC (subsidiary)
  • Minimum capital
  • LCR, NSFR, leverage and exposure norms
  • Specified foreign currencies (First Schedule)
  • Permissible activities and referral services
  • No cash; savings accounts only for individuals
  • No lender of last resort
  • Reporting in USD

Key obligations

  • reg 3(3): an IBU keeps minimum capital of USD 20 million (maintained at the parent bank), with a no-objection from the home regulator and a liquidity support undertaking
  • reg 3(3A): an IBC keeps minimum capital of USD 50 million
  • reg 4: an IBU also follows its home regulator's directions
  • reg 5: a Banking Unit maintains the LCR (an IBU may, with approval, hold it at the parent) and the NSFR as the Authority specifies
  • regs 6 and 7: leverage ratio and exposure norms as specified
  • reg 8: no CRR on deposits generally, but deposits from individuals attract reserve ratios as specified; an IBC keeps reserves as the Banking Regulation Act and RBI Act require
  • reg 9: no lender-of-last-resort support is available to a Banking Unit
  • reg 10: business is conducted in the specified foreign currencies (First Schedule: USD, EUR, JPY, GBP, CAD, AUD, CHF, HKD, SGD, AED, RUB, and since 2024 SEK, NOK, NZD and DKK); rupee transactions are settled in a specified currency
  • reg 11: Banking Units may open accounts in the specified foreign currencies for individuals and corporate or institutional entities, resident or non-resident; resident individuals use them for transactions permitted under the Liberalised Remittance Scheme
  • reg 12: no cash transactions; savings accounts only for individuals
  • reg 13: activities permitted under IFSCA Act s. 3(1)(e) and Banking Regulation Act s. 6 unless prohibited; referral services allowed
  • reg 14: follow the IFSCA AML, CFT and KYC guidelines
  • regs 15 to 17: report in USD, keep books in the specified currencies, and keep a rupee account only for administrative expenses
  • reg 19: netting of margins as specified

If you breach it

  • reg 21: the Authority may withdraw the licence or permission after giving the Banking Unit 30 days to make its submissions

General consequences under the statute. International Financial Services Centres Authority Act, 2019: in an IFSC, IFSCA exercises all the powers of RBI, SEBI, IRDAI and PFRDA under the Acts listed in the First Schedule (s. 13(1)). The provisions of those Acts on inspection, investigation, prosecution, settlement, compounding, adjudication, appeals, and the fees, fines and penalties for contravention apply to IFSC products, services and institutions as they apply under those Acts (s. 13(4)). Penalties, fines, fees and settlement amounts are collected in the foreign currency equivalent, at the RBI rate on the date of the order (s. 13(5)). Most IFSCA regulations also let the Authority suspend, withdraw or cancel a registration, licence or recognition after giving the entity an opportunity to make its submissions, as the regulation concerned provides.
