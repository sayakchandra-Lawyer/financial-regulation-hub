Applies to
Fund Management Entities (FMEs) in the IFSC and the schemes they manage: venture capital, restricted and retail schemes, portfolio management, investment trusts and family investment funds

Key concepts

  • Authorised FME, Registered FME (Non-Retail) and Registered FME (Retail)
  • Principal officer, compliance officer and KMPs based in the IFSC
  • Net worth (Second Schedule)
  • Scheme corpus, minimum investment and manager contribution
  • Code of conduct
  • Independent custodian

Key obligations

  • reg 3: register as an Authorised FME (venture capital schemes, family investment funds), a Registered FME (Non-Retail) (restricted schemes, portfolio management, private investment trusts) or a Registered FME (Retail) (retail schemes, public investment trusts, ETFs)
  • reg 5: the FME is a company, LLP or branch
  • reg 7: appoint a principal officer; a Registered FME also appoints a compliance officer; KMPs are based in the IFSC and complete the specified certification
  • reg 8 and Second Schedule: net worth of USD 75,000 (Authorised), USD 500,000 (Non-Retail) or USD 1,000,000 (Retail); a branch may hold it at the parent
  • Venture capital scheme: corpus USD 3 million to USD 200 million; minimum investment USD 250,000 (unless accredited); manager contribution at least 2.5% of corpus or USD 750,000, whichever is lower
  • Restricted scheme: minimum investment USD 150,000 (unless accredited); minimum corpus USD 3 million; manager contribution 2.5% or USD 750,000 (close-ended) and 5% or USD 1.5 million (open-ended), whichever is lower
  • Retail scheme: at least 20 investors, no single investor above 25%; corpus USD 3 million; a close-ended retail scheme investing more than 15% in unlisted securities has a minimum investment of USD 10,000 and may not invest more than 50% in them
  • Portfolio management minimum USD 75,000 per client; family investment fund minimum corpus USD 10 million
  • reg 118: follow the Code of Conduct in the Third Schedule; reg 124: no guaranteed returns
  • regs 121 and 122: keep a business continuity plan reviewed every year and a cyber security framework
  • reg 132: appoint an independent custodian (based in the IFSC, with limited exceptions) for retail schemes, open-ended restricted schemes and every other scheme with AUM above USD 70 million; not mandatory for a fund of funds whose underlying schemes have one

Deadlines and cycles

  • reg 7: a Retail FME appoints a fund-management KMP before its first retail scheme; an FME whose AUM reaches USD 1 billion appoints an additional KMP within 6 months
  • An open-ended restricted scheme may start investing after raising USD 1 million and must reach USD 3 million within 12 months of the placement memorandum being taken on record
  • Retail scheme: the 20-investor and 25% conditions are met within 6 months of the offer closing
  • reg 119: keep records for 8 years, and scheme records for 5 years after winding up

If you breach it

  • reg 143: the Authority may suspend or cancel the registration

General consequences under the statute. International Financial Services Centres Authority Act, 2019: in an IFSC, IFSCA exercises all the powers of RBI, SEBI, IRDAI and PFRDA under the Acts listed in the First Schedule (s. 13(1)). The provisions of those Acts on inspection, investigation, prosecution, settlement, compounding, adjudication, appeals, and the fees, fines and penalties for contravention apply to IFSC products, services and institutions as they apply under those Acts (s. 13(4)). Penalties, fines, fees and settlement amounts are collected in the foreign currency equivalent, at the RBI rate on the date of the order (s. 13(5)). Most IFSCA regulations also let the Authority suspend, withdraw or cancel a registration, licence or recognition after giving the entity an opportunity to make its submissions, as the regulation concerned provides.
