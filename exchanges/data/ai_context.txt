GLOSSARY (plain-language meanings, not statutory text):
NSE (National Stock Exchange of India): A recognised stock exchange. It lists equity, NCDs and commercial paper, runs an EBP for private placements and an RFQ platform for bond trades; NSE Clearing settles them.
BSE (BSE Limited): A recognised stock exchange. It lists equity, NCDs and commercial paper and runs the BSEBOND EBP platform and an RFQ platform; Indian Clearing Corporation settles its trades.
Recognised stock exchange: An exchange recognised by the Central Government (now SEBI) under s. 4 of the SCRA. Its ownership and governance follow the SECC Regulations.
Listing agreement: The agreement a listed entity signs with each exchange when it lists. It binds the entity to the LODR Regulations and the exchange's rules; breach can lead to fines, suspension or delisting under the SCRA.
NCD (Non-convertible debenture): A debenture that cannot be converted into shares. Privately placed NCDs of Rs 20 crore or more are issued through an EBP and listed within T+3 days of the issue closing.
Non-convertible securities (NCS): NCDs, non-convertible redeemable preference shares and similar debt-like instruments issued and listed under the SEBI NCS Regulations, 2021.
Commercial paper (CP): A short-term unsecured money market instrument issued under RBI's directions. Listed CP follows SEBI's framework: listing on T+1 from receipt of funds, minimum lot of Rs 5 lakh, and no in-principle approval.
In-principle approval: The exchange's approval of the draft offer or placement document before an issue opens. Needed for NCDs (not for CP) before the issue is put on an EBP.
EBP (Electronic book provider platform): The exchange platform on which private placements of debt of Rs 20 crore or more are bid and allotted. NSE's EBP and BSEBOND are the two exchange platforms.
Anchor investor: An investor who commits in advance to part of a private placement on an EBP. The anchor portion is capped at 30%, 40% or 50% of the base issue depending on the rating.
Arranger: A SEBI-registered intermediary (such as a merchant banker or broker) who bids on an EBP for its clients. Repeated client pay-in defaults bar it from bidding.
Green shoe: The option to keep subscriptions above the base issue size. On an EBP it may not exceed five times the base issue.
ISIN (International Securities Identification Number): The code that identifies a security in the depositories. SEBI caps how many debt ISINs maturing in a financial year an issuer may have, to push re-issues into existing ISINs.
REF (Recovery Expense Fund): A fund each debt issuer deposits with the designated exchange, at 0.01% of the issue up to Rs 25 lakh. Debenture trustees draw on it to enforce security after a default.
Debenture trustee (DT): The SEBI-registered trustee that holds security and acts for debenture holders. The issuer must send it periodic security cover and other reports on set timelines.
Security cover: The value of the assets charged for secured NCDs compared with the amount outstanding. Certified quarterly to the trustee and the exchange.
Credit Risk-o-Meter: A coloured meter showing the credit risk of a debt security, based on its rating. Required in offer documents, advertisements and online bond platforms from 21-11-2026.
Integrated filing: The single quarterly filing (governance and financial parts) that replaced several separate LODR filings. Governance within 30 days of the quarter end; financials with the results.
XBRL: The machine-readable format used for exchange filings. Regulation 50 prior intimations for listed debt are accepted only in XBRL since 01-12-2025.
Record date: The date that fixes who receives interest or redemption on listed debt. It is notified to the exchange in advance through the prescribed filing.
Default disclosure: Disclosure to the exchanges of a default on a loan from a bank or financial institution that continues beyond 30 days, made within 24 hours of the 30th day.
Encumbrance: A pledge, lien, non-disposal undertaking or similar arrangement over shares. Promoters disclose encumbrances, and reasons when they cross 50% of their holding or 20% of the company's share capital.
SOP fines: Fines the exchanges levy under SEBI's standard operating procedure for late or missed filings, per day or per instance, with escalation to freezing of promoter holdings and suspension.
Suspension: Stopping trading in a company's securities for continuing non-compliance. Revocation needs payment of fines and compliance.
RFQ (Request for quote platform): The exchange platform for negotiated secondary trades in corporate bonds and CP. Since October 2026 it settles on T+0 for trades up to 5 p.m.
Liquidity Window Facility (LWF): A put option an issuer may offer retail investors in its debt, exercised through an exchange window at a price set from a rating agency's valuation.
OBPP (Online bond platform provider): A SEBI-registered broker that offers listed debt to investors online. Must display the Credit Risk-o-Meter from 21-11-2026.
Tri-party repo: A repo in corporate bonds in which the exchange's clearing corporation acts as the third party, managing collateral and settlement.
Merchant banker: A SEBI-registered intermediary who manages an issue. Since October 2026 certain regulated, listed issuers need not appoint one for privately placed debt.

STOCK EXCHANGE CHANGES (2025-11-21 to 2026-11-26):
- 2026-10-21 Exchange circular: Further issues: one lead exchange handles queries for both NSE and BSE (NSE/CML/2026/22). Applies: Listed companies applying for in-principle approval of a further issue of shares.. Changes: Applications are still filed with every exchange, but the exchanges assign a lead exchange in turn. The other exchange routes its queries through the lead exchange; the company answers only the lead exchange. Applies to applications filed on or after 01-11-2026. Actions: Route further issue correspondence to the lead exchange named for the application.
- 2026-10-16 Exchange circular: RFQ trades in corporate bonds settle on T+0 until 5 p.m. (NSE/DS/76973; BSE notice 20261019-21). Applies: Participants on the NSE and BSE RFQ platforms.. Changes: RFQ trading hours for T+0 settlement extended to 5 p.m. (NSE from 19-10-2026; BSE from 21-10-2026). Participants must confirm their entity type every half-year to keep platform access. Actions: Update treasury settlement cut-offs and complete the half-yearly entity confirmation.
- 2026-10-12 SEBI regulation: Entities with listed debt must list all later NCDs (SEBI/LAD-DOP/2026/329 (LODR Third Amendment)). Applies: Entities whose non-convertible debt securities are listed.. Changes: Reg 62A(1) substituted: an entity with listed NCDs must list all non-convertible debt securities it issues afterwards. Reg 62A(2) and (3) omitted. Actions: Build listing into every future NCD issue plan and timeline.
- 2026-10-08 Exchange circular: New caps on ISINs for privately placed debt (NSE/CML/D/2026/05; BSE notice 20261008-21). Applies: Issuers of privately placed debt securities and their arrangers.. Changes: Up to 17 ISINs may mature in a financial year: 12 for plain vanilla debt and 5 for structured, market-linked, floating rate, zero coupon and Tier II bonds, plus 6 for s. 54EC bonds. One more plain vanilla ISIN for each Rs 3,000 crore once the amount maturing in the year reaches Rs 15,000 crore. Government-serviced (EBR) bonds and ESG debt securities are outside the count. Actions: Map existing ISINs by maturity year against the new caps.
- 2026-10-08 Exchange circular: Some listed, regulated issuers can place debt without a merchant banker (NSE/CML/D/2026/06; BSE notice 20261008-22). Applies: Issuers regulated by SEBI, RBI, IRDAI or PFRDA, including NBFCs and HFCs.. Changes: No merchant banker is needed for a private placement if the issuer is regulated, has been listed for a year with no pending fines, has no default in three years, and issues senior secured debt rated AA- or above. Actions: Check each condition before dropping the merchant banker.
- 2026-10-08 Exchange circular: Credit Risk-o-Meter on debt offer documents and bond platforms (NSE circular of 08-10-2026; BSE notice 20261008-23). Applies: Debt issuers, arrangers and online bond platform providers.. Changes: Offer documents, placement memoranda, advertisements and online bond platforms must show a Credit Risk-o-Meter. The rating agency, the rating and, for unsecured debt, the word 'unsecured' in bold red appear below it. In force 45 days after 07-10-2026. Actions: Add the meter to placement memorandum and term sheet templates.
- 2026-09-17 Exchange circular: Revised window for tri-party repo auctions in corporate bonds (NSE/DS/76400). Applies: Participants in tri-party repo in corporate bonds.. Changes: Repo auctions run between 3.30 p.m. and 4 p.m. from 18-09-2026. Actions: Update desk procedures.
- 2026-09-02 Exchange notice: BSE's list of companies with high promoter pledge (BSE notice 20260902-39). Applies: Listed companies on the list, their promoters, and lenders holding pledges.. Changes: Listed securities attract a minimum 35% margin on all open and new positions in the equity and derivatives segments. Actions: Check whether pledged collateral is on the list and review margins.
- 2026-08-17 Exchange circular: Changes to the framework for online bond platforms (NSE/COMP/75793; BSE notice 20260817-28). Applies: Online bond platform providers and stock brokers.. Changes: SEBI modified the OBPP framework for ease of doing business; OBPPs also show the Credit Risk-o-Meter from 21-11-2026. Actions: Review OBPP arrangements against the modified framework.
- 2026-06-19 Exchange circular: One filing reaches both NSE and BSE (NSE/CML/2026/17; BSE notice 20260619-11). Applies: Listed entities filing Regulation 30 disclosures.. Actions: Align disclosure workflows so XBRL follows within 24 hours.
- 2026-06-12 Exchange circular: FAQs on the quarterly Integrated Filing (Governance) (NSE/CML/2026/16; BSE notice 20260612-13). Applies: Equity-listed entities.. Changes: Clarifies scope and timing: file within 30 days of each quarter end; newly listed entities start with the first quarter ending after listing. Actions: Check filing calendars against the FAQs.
- 2026-04-10 Exchange circular: Lock-in of pledged shares (NSE/CML/2026/07). Applies: Issuers with locked-in pledged shares, and lenders or pledgees.. Changes: Issuers follow the depositories' framework for locked-in pledged shares: suitable provisions in the articles, intimation to lenders and disclosure in the offer document. Actions: Lenders: check lock-in status of pledged shares.
