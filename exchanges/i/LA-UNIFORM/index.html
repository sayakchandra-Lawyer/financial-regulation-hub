Every issuer listing securities on the exchange for the first time, including first-time NCD and CP issuers

Key concepts

  • A short agreement that binds the issuer to LODR, SEBI circulars and the exchange's bye-laws, regulations and circulars
  • An issuer already listed on the exchange that lists a new kind of security (NCDs, CPs, perpetual debt) does not sign afresh; it ticks the new security in 'Information about the Company and Securities' and files it with a covering letter

Key obligations

  • cl. 1 and 2: comply with all statutes on issue, listing and continued listing, LODR and other SEBI regulations and circulars, and the exchange's bye-laws, regulations, circulars and directions
  • cl. 3: pay listing and other fees and fines levied by the exchange within the prescribed period
  • cl. 4: keep the exchange informed of any change in the issuer's information
  • cl. 5: admission and continued admission to dealings is at the exchange's discretion and subject to its power to prohibit, suspend or withdraw listing
  • cl. 6: a board (or authorised committee) resolution for initial listing must be in place

General consequences under the statute. Securities Contracts (Regulation) Act, 1956: a listed entity must comply with the conditions of its listing agreement (s. 21); a recognised stock exchange may delist securities on the prescribed grounds after recording reasons (s. 21A); failure to comply with listing or delisting conditions attracts a penalty of Rs 5 lakh to Rs 25 crore (s. 23E), and breach of the Act, rules, the exchange's bye-laws or regulations or SEBI directions with no separate penalty attracts a penalty under s. 23H. SEBI Act, 1992: failure to furnish documents, returns or reports, or furnishing false or incomplete ones, attracts Rs 1 lakh to Rs 1 lakh a day up to Rs 1 crore (s. 15A(a)). Exchanges also act under SEBI's standard operating procedures: fines per day or per instance, freezing of promoter holdings, moving the scrip to the Z category, suspension of trading and compulsory delisting; and, for debt and CP issuers, a bar on new issues through any EBP and on further listing until fines are paid.
