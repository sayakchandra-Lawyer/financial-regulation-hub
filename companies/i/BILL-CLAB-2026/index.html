Key concepts

  • Introduced in Lok Sabha on 23-03-2026 and referred to a Joint Committee the same day; the Committee's report was presented in Lok Sabha and laid in Rajya Sabha on 03-08-2026

Key obligations

  • Clause 18: raise the small company ceilings in s. 2(85) to Rs 20 crore paid-up capital and Rs 200 crore turnover
  • Clause 30: for prescribed classes of companies, the further 60-day window under s. 77(1) second proviso (b) for registering a charge would be read as 120 days; the Joint Committee recommends limiting this to prescribed classes of small companies
  • Clause 60: s. 185(1)(b) would also cover a limited liability partnership in which a director or relative is a partner (accepted by the Joint Committee)
  • Clause 61: defaults in keeping the s. 186(9) and (10) register would move from fine to civil penalty: company Rs 1 lakh plus Rs 500 a day, up to Rs 5 lakh; officer Rs 25,000 plus Rs 200 a day, up to Rs 1 lakh (accepted without change)
  • Clause 97: the Regional Director could compound offences where the maximum fine does not exceed Rs 1 crore (now Rs 25 lakh)
  • Clause 98: s. 446B would allow the lesser penalty to be one-half, or such lower percentage as may be prescribed
  • Clause 101: in s. 454, Assistant Registrars would become adjudicating officers, and a new s. 454(1A) would let a company or officer in default apply for adjudication of a penalty

General consequences under the statute. Companies Act, 2013: where no specific penalty is provided, the company and every officer in default are liable to a penalty of Rs 10,000 and Rs 1,000 a day for a continuing contravention, up to Rs 2 lakh for the company and Rs 50,000 for an officer (s. 450). Penalties are imposed by an adjudicating officer (the Registrar) after a hearing, with an appeal to the Regional Director within 60 days; failing to comply with the order within 90 days is an offence (s. 454). One Person Companies, small companies, start-ups and producer companies pay at most half the specified penalty, up to Rs 2 lakh for the company and Rs 1 lakh for an officer (s. 446B). Offences not punishable with imprisonment only, or with imprisonment and fine, may be compounded by the NCLT or, where the maximum fine does not exceed Rs 25 lakh, by the Regional Director (s. 441). A false statement or knowing omission of a material fact in a filing is punishable under s. 447 (s. 448).
