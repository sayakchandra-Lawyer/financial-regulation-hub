Applies to
All NBFCs (non-banking financial institutions) and their auditors (s.45-IA; s.45MA)

Key concepts

  • s.45-I definitions: business of an NBFI (a), deposit (bb, with its exclusions), financial institution (c), NBFC (f); the Act uses but does not define 'principal business'
  • s.45-IA(1): Certificate of Registration and NOF of ₹25 lakh or such other amount, not exceeding ₹100 crore, as RBI notifies (different amounts for different categories allowed)
  • s.45-IB(1): invest in unencumbered approved securities at least 5% (or a higher rate up to 25% that RBI specifies) of deposits outstanding at the close of the second preceding quarter
  • s.45-IC: reserve fund; at least 20% of net profit each year before any dividend; appropriations only for RBI-specified purposes, reported to RBI within 21 days
  • s.45-IE: supersession of the Board of an NBFC (other than a Government company) for up to 5 years in all, with an Administrator
  • s.45JA power to issue directions
  • s.45K/45L power to collect information and give directions
  • s.45MA: auditor's duty to inquire into the deposit statements; s.45MA(1A): RBI directions to NBFCs or their auditors on the balance sheet, P&L, disclosures and audit
  • s.45MB: RBI may prohibit acceptance of deposits for breach of Chapter IIIB or of a direction or order, and may bar the sale, transfer or charge of assets without its permission for up to 6 months
  • s.45MC: RBI may petition for winding up if the NBFC cannot pay its debts, is disqualified under s.45-IA, has been barred from deposits by an order in force for 3 months or more, or its continuance harms the public or depositors; deemed unable to pay debts if it fails to meet a lawful demand within 5 working days and RBI certifies this
  • s.45N inspection
  • s.45NC exemption power
  • s.45QA NCLT order to repay deposits
  • s.58B / s.58G penalties
  • s.45-ID: removal of a director of an NBFC (other than a Government-owned NBFC) by a reasoned RBI order after a chance to make a representation

Key obligations

  • s.45-IA(1): obtain a Certificate of Registration and hold the minimum NOF before commencing or carrying on NBFI business
  • Maintain liquid assets on public deposits (s.45-IB)
  • s.45-IC(1): transfer at least 20% of net profit, as shown in the P&L, to a reserve fund every year before any dividend is declared
  • ss.45JA, 45K, 45L, 45M: comply with RBI directions and furnish the statements and information RBI calls for
  • s.45QA: repay every deposit on its terms unless renewed; the Tribunal (formerly the Company Law Board) may order repayment on its own motion or on a depositor's application

Deadlines and cycles

  • Reserve fund transfer every year before dividend
  • Returns/information as RBI directs
  • s.45-IC(2): report each appropriation from the reserve fund to RBI within 21 days of the withdrawal
  • s.45-IA(7): appeal against rejection or cancellation of the CoR to the Central Government within 30 days of the order being communicated
  • s.58G(3): pay an RBI penalty within 30 days of the demand notice, failing which it is recovered through the civil court

If you breach it

  • s.45-IA(6): cancellation of the CoR if the NBFC stops NBFI business, breaches a CoR condition, fails the s.45-IA(4)(d)-(g) conditions, fails to comply with directions, keep accounts or produce books, or has been barred from deposits by an order in force for 3 months or more; chance to comply and a hearing first
  • s.45MB: prohibition on accepting deposits; bar on selling, transferring or charging assets for up to 6 months
  • s.45-IE: supersession of the Board for up to 5 years; s.45-ID: removal of a director
  • Winding-up petition (s.45MC)
  • s.58B (headed 'Offences' since 23-06-2026; amounts as substituted w.e.f. 09-08-2019): false statement up to 3 years and fine (58B(1)); not producing books or information, fine up to ₹1 lakh per offence plus ₹5,000 a day (58B(2)); NBFI business without a CoR, 1-5 years and ₹1 lakh-₹25 lakh (58B(4A)); accepting deposits in breach, or not complying with a Chapter IIIB direction or order, up to 3 years and fine (58B(5)); any other contravention, fine up to ₹1 lakh plus ₹10,000 a day (58B(6)). The auditor's offence in s.58B(4AA) was omitted from 23-06-2026 (Jan Vishwas (Amendment of Provisions) Act 2026, Schedule serial 7(A))
  • s.58G(1) (headed 'penalties' since 23-06-2026; amounts as substituted w.e.f. 09-08-2019): RBI penalty up to ₹25,000; for a s.58B(4A) or s.58B(5)(a)/(aa) default, up to ₹10 lakh or twice the amount involved where quantifiable, whichever is more; up to ₹1 lakh a day while the default continues; show-cause notice and hearing first (58G(2)); penalty and prosecution are alternatives (58G(5)-(6))
  • Jan Vishwas (Amendment of Provisions) Act 2026 (8 of 2026; assent 07-04-2026), in force for the RBI Act from 23-06-2026 (S.O. 3301(E) of 22-06-2026): s.58B(4AA) omitted; s.58B heading changed to 'Offences' and s.58G heading to 'penalties' (Schedule serial 7). Its s.3 raises the fines and penalties in the Acts it amends by 10% of the minimum amount after every three years from commencement
