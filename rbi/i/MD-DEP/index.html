  • Liquid assets (s.45-IB)
  • Unencumbered approved securities
  • Minimum investment-grade credit rating
  • Floating charge / asset cover
  • Problem NBFC

Key obligations

  • para 10: keep at least 15% of public deposits in unencumbered approved securities
  • para 12: obtain investment-grade rating for deposits at least annually
  • para 13: inform RBI of rating change within 15 working days
  • para 18: on downgrade below investment grade stop fresh deposits and regularise excess
  • para 21: brokerage max 2%, expense reimbursement max 0.5%
  • para 22: notify depositor of maturity at least 14 days in advance
  • para 30: proper introduction for new depositors
  • para 43: maintain deposit registers
  • para 53: hold approved securities in SGL/Gilt/demat account with designated bank
  • para 61: full asset cover and floating charge via trust deed for depositors

Deadlines and cycles

  • para 16: tenor 12-60 months
  • para 13/18(3): 15 working days for rating-change reporting
  • para 22: 14 days pre-maturity intimation
  • para 35: 3-month lock-in for premature withdrawal
  • para 44: preserve records 8 years
  • para 45: quarterly return before 10th of next quarter
  • para 60: within 15 days of AGM

If you breach it

  • Penal action under RBI Act 1934 (ss.58B, 58C, 45QA powers of NCLT to order repayment)

General consequences under the statute. Statutory consequences under the RBI Act 1934: RBI may impose a monetary penalty under s.58G: up to ₹25,000, or, for NBFI business without a CoR (s.58B(4A)) or for accepting deposits or not complying with a Chapter IIIB direction or order (s.58B(5)(a), (aa)), up to ₹10 lakh or twice the amount involved, whichever is more, plus up to ₹1 lakh a day while the default continues; a show-cause notice and hearing come first, and penalty and prosecution are alternatives (s.58G(2), (5)-(6)). As offences, s.58B(5) carries up to 3 years and fine, and s.58B(6) a fine up to ₹1 lakh plus ₹10,000 a day. RBI may also cancel the CoR (s.45-IA(6)), prohibit deposits or bar asset transfers (s.45MB), remove a director (s.45-ID), supersede the Board (s.45-IE) and petition for winding up (s.45MC). For HFCs the parallel provisions are NHB Act ss.29A(6), 49 and 52A (NHB or RBI may impose penalties, e.g. up to ₹10 lakh or twice the amount involved plus ₹1 lakh a day for not complying with a direction or order under Chapter V, s.52A(1C)).
