Key obligations

  • para 15: loan agreements to state due dates and SMA/NPA examples
  • para 18-19: classify overdue/SMA/NPA in the day-end process
  • para 23: borrower-wise classification across facilities
  • para 24-25: upgrade NPA only when all arrears cleared
  • para 28: standard asset provisions shown separately
  • para 30: project finance general provisions (e.g. CRE 1.25% construction)
  • para 32: sub-standard 10%; doubtful 100% unsecured + 20/30/50% secured
  • para 33: HP/lease additional provisions by months overdue
  • para 34-35: Ind AS impairment shortfall to Impairment Reserve; withdrawal needs RBI DoS permission
  • para 43-44: BL: NPA test moved from >180 days overdue to >90 days by glide path (>150 days by 31-03-2024, >120 days by 31-03-2025, >90 days by 31-03-2026); doubtful = sub-standard for more than 18 months (para 46)
  • para 51, 53, 56: ML and UL: NPA when overdue for more than 90 days; doubtful = sub-standard for more than 12 months
  • para 48, 55, 58: standard-asset provisions: BL 0.25%; ML 0.40%; UL differential (individual housing and SME 0.25%, CRE-RH 0.75%, CRE 1.00%, teaser-rate housing 2.00%, others incl. medium enterprises 0.40%)
  • para 36D-36F: additional specific provision of 5% of outstanding debt for each resolution plan under Chapter VI-A of the Stressed Assets Directions (cap 100%); written back once the borrower pays at least 20% without slipping into NPA or being restructured again

Deadlines and cycles

  • para 18-19: daily classification
  • para 31: liquidity facility >90 days outstanding fully provided
  • para 44: BL 90-day NPA norm applies from 31-03-2026 (end of glide path)

If you breach it

  • not stated in the Direction

General consequences under the statute. Statutory consequences under the RBI Act 1934: RBI may impose a monetary penalty under s.58G: up to ₹25,000, or, for NBFI business without a CoR (s.58B(4A)) or for accepting deposits or not complying with a Chapter IIIB direction or order (s.58B(5)(a), (aa)), up to ₹10 lakh or twice the amount involved, whichever is more, plus up to ₹1 lakh a day while the default continues; a show-cause notice and hearing come first, and penalty and prosecution are alternatives (s.58G(2), (5)-(6)). As offences, s.58B(5) carries up to 3 years and fine, and s.58B(6) a fine up to ₹1 lakh plus ₹10,000 a day. RBI may also cancel the CoR (s.45-IA(6)), prohibit deposits or bar asset transfers (s.45MB), remove a director (s.45-ID), supersede the Board (s.45-IE) and petition for winding up (s.45MC). For HFCs the parallel provisions are NHB Act ss.29A(6), 49 and 52A (NHB or RBI may impose penalties, e.g. up to ₹10 lakh or twice the amount involved plus ₹1 lakh a day for not complying with a direction or order under Chapter V, s.52A(1C)).
