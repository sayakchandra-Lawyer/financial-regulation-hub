Key obligations

  • s.4(3)-(4): authentication or offline verification is voluntary and needs informed consent; an entity may authenticate only if it meets UIDAI's privacy and security standards and is permitted by a law of Parliament (e.g. PMLA s.11A) or for a prescribed purpose; s.8: obtain consent and use identity information only for authentication with the CIDR

If you breach it

  • s.33A (Chapter VIA, civil penalties): an entity in the Aadhaar ecosystem that fails to comply with the Act, rules, regulations or UIDAI directions is liable to a civil penalty up to ₹1 crore per contravention plus up to ₹10 lakh a day; offences under Chapter VII
