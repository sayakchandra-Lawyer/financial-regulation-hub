Key concepts

  • Fit and proper criteria
  • Board committees (Audit, NRC, RMC, IT Strategy)
  • Chief Risk Officer
  • Chief Compliance Officer
  • Compensation: malus and clawback
  • Deed of covenants
  • Mandatory listing of NBFC-UL

Key obligations

  • para 8: at least one director with bank/NBFC experience
  • para 9: constitute a Risk Management Committee (Board or executive level)
  • para 10: prior RBI permission for management change affecting >30% of directors (excl. independent directors on rotation)
  • para 12: Board-approved fit and proper policy for directors
  • para 14: quarterly statement on change of directors with certificate
  • para 17: Audit Committee of at least three members
  • para 19: appoint CRO where asset size exceeds ₹5,000 crore
  • para 25: appoint CCO and adopt Board-approved compliance policy
  • para 38: whistle-blower mechanism for directors and employees
  • para 43: NBFC-UL to list within three years of identification

Deadlines and cycles

  • para 13(3): annual fit-and-proper declaration as on 31 March
  • para 14(1): change-of-director statement within 15 days of quarter end
  • para 21(2): CRO meets Board/RMC at least quarterly
  • para 43: listing within 3 years of UL identification

If you breach it

  • not stated in the Direction (see RBI Act s.45-IE supersession of Board, s.58B/58G)

General consequences under the statute. Statutory consequences under the RBI Act 1934: RBI may impose a monetary penalty under s.58G: up to ₹25,000, or, for NBFI business without a CoR (s.58B(4A)) or for accepting deposits or not complying with a Chapter IIIB direction or order (s.58B(5)(a), (aa)), up to ₹10 lakh or twice the amount involved, whichever is more, plus up to ₹1 lakh a day while the default continues; a show-cause notice and hearing come first, and penalty and prosecution are alternatives (s.58G(2), (5)-(6)). As offences, s.58B(5) carries up to 3 years and fine, and s.58B(6) a fine up to ₹1 lakh plus ₹10,000 a day. RBI may also cancel the CoR (s.45-IA(6)), prohibit deposits or bar asset transfers (s.45MB), remove a director (s.45-ID), supersede the Board (s.45-IE) and petition for winding up (s.45MC). For HFCs the parallel provisions are NHB Act ss.29A(6), 49 and 52A (NHB or RBI may impose penalties, e.g. up to ₹10 lakh or twice the amount involved plus ₹1 lakh a day for not complying with a direction or order under Chapter V, s.52A(1C)).
