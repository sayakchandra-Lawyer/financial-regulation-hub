Key obligations

  • s.11A(1): verify the identity of clients and beneficial owners by Aadhaar authentication (banking companies), Aadhaar offline verification, passport or other notified documents or modes; a non-bank reporting entity may use Aadhaar authentication only if notified under the s.11A proviso
  • s.12(1), (3)-(4): keep transaction records for 5 years from the transaction, and identity and account records for 5 years after the business relationship ends or the account is closed, whichever is later
  • s.12(1)(b): furnish to the Director, FIU-IND information on prescribed transactions (STRs, CTRs and other reports under the PML Rules)

Deadlines and cycles

  • UNVERIFIED: STR within 7 working days of the Principal Officer being satisfied that a transaction is suspicious, and CTR by the 15th of the following month (PML Rules r.8; the Rules text was not available in the statute sources used)
  • PML Rules r.9(1A): upload KYC records to CKYCR within 10 days of starting an account-based relationship (as recited in the KYC Directions para 63(1))

If you breach it

  • s.13(2): the Director, FIU-IND may issue a warning, direct compliance or periodic reports, or impose a monetary penalty of ₹10,000 to ₹1 lakh for each failure on the reporting entity, its designated director or employees
