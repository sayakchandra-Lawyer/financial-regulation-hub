Key obligations

  • para 8: Board-approved card policy published on website
  • para 11: Audit Committee reviews card operations half-yearly
  • para 12(4): no unsolicited cards or upgrades
  • para 12(6): OTP consent to activate card unused >30 days, else close free of cost
  • para 19: honour closure request within 7 working days
  • para 23(5): report past due to CICs / levy late fee only after >3 days past due
  • para 23(7): changes in charges prospective with one month's notice
  • para 24: at least a fortnight between bill and interest accrual
  • para 25: answer bill disputes with evidence within 30 days
  • para 27: no charges on transactions disputed as fraud until resolved
  • para 7: credit card business only with RBI's prior approval; a CoR and minimum NOF of ₹100 crore are prerequisites
  • para 48-57: co-branded cards need no prior RBI approval; the card-issuer is liable for the co-branding partner, whose role is limited to marketing and distribution, with no access to transaction data
  • para 62-64: block a lost card immediately on being told and send confirmation; multiple reporting channels

Deadlines and cycles

  • para 11: half-yearly review
  • para 12(6): 30 days to activation
  • para 19: 7 working days for closure
  • para 20: inactive 1 year: notify; close if no reply in 30 days
  • para 23(7): 1 month notice
  • para 25: 30 days for dispute response
  • para 73: 30 days' notice of any change in card terms

If you breach it

  • para 12(4): unsolicited card: reverse charges and pay penalty of twice the charges reversed
  • para 19: ₹500 per day of delay in closure, payable to cardholder
  • para 79: card-issuer compensates the complainant for loss of time, expenses, financial loss and harassment where it is at fault or the grievance is not redressed in time; customer may go to the RBI Ombudsman if there is no reply within 30 days

General consequences under the statute. Statutory consequences under the RBI Act 1934: RBI may impose a monetary penalty under s.58G: up to ₹25,000, or, for NBFI business without a CoR (s.58B(4A)) or for accepting deposits or not complying with a Chapter IIIB direction or order (s.58B(5)(a), (aa)), up to ₹10 lakh or twice the amount involved, whichever is more, plus up to ₹1 lakh a day while the default continues; a show-cause notice and hearing come first, and penalty and prosecution are alternatives (s.58G(2), (5)-(6)). As offences, s.58B(5) carries up to 3 years and fine, and s.58B(6) a fine up to ₹1 lakh plus ₹10,000 a day. RBI may also cancel the CoR (s.45-IA(6)), prohibit deposits or bar asset transfers (s.45MB), remove a director (s.45-ID), supersede the Board (s.45-IE) and petition for winding up (s.45MC). For HFCs the parallel provisions are NHB Act ss.29A(6), 49 and 52A (NHB or RBI may impose penalties, e.g. up to ₹10 lakh or twice the amount involved plus ₹1 lakh a day for not complying with a direction or order under Chapter V, s.52A(1C)).
