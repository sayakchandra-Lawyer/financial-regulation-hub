Applies to
Housing Finance Companies (regulated by RBI since 2019; supervised by NHB)

Key concepts

  • s.29A(1): no housing finance institution that is a company may carry on housing finance as its principal business without a CoR (issued by RBI since the Finance (No.2) Act 2019) and NOF of ₹10 crore or such higher amount as RBI notifies
  • s.29C: reserve fund of at least 20% of net profit before any dividend (the special reserve under IT Act s.36(1)(viii) may count); appropriations reported to NHB and RBI within 21 days
  • s.30A: RBI power to determine policy and give directions to HFCs

Key obligations

  • s.29A(1)-(2): apply to RBI for registration and hold the minimum NOF
  • s.29C(1): transfer at least 20% of net profit to a reserve fund before any dividend

Deadlines and cycles

  • Annual reserve transfer
  • s.29C(2): report any appropriation from the reserve fund to NHB and RBI within 21 days
  • s.29A(7): appeal against rejection or cancellation of the CoR to the Central Government within 30 days

If you breach it

  • s.29A(6): RBI may cancel the CoR (stopping housing finance business, breach of CoR conditions, failing directions, accounts or inspection, or a deposit ban in force for 3 months or more); s.49(2A): housing finance without a CoR, 1-5 years and ₹1 lakh-₹5 lakh; s.52A (penalties imposed by NHB or RBI, as the case may be): up to ₹25,000 (52A(1)(a)); for s.49(2A) or s.49(3)(a) defaults, up to ₹10 lakh or twice the amount involved, whichever is more, plus up to ₹1 lakh a day (52A(1)(b)); not producing books or information, up to ₹1.5 lakh per default plus ₹7,500 a day (52A(1A)); an auditor not complying with a s.33 direction or order, up to ₹10 lakh (52A(1B)); not complying with a direction or order under Chapter V, up to ₹10 lakh or twice the amount involved, whichever is more, plus up to ₹1 lakh a day (52A(1C)); any other contravention, up to ₹1 lakh plus ₹10,000 a day (52A(1D)); show-cause notice and hearing first (52A(2)); payable within 30 days of the demand notice (52A(3))
