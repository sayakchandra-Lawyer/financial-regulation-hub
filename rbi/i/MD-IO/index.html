Key concepts

  • Internal Ombudsman (IO) and Deputy IO
  • Auto-escalation of partly resolved / rejected complaints
  • Three-category complaint classification
  • Competent Authority overrule
  • Reasoned decision

Key obligations

  • para 5: IO of GM rank with 7+ years' experience; never employed by the NBFC or its group
  • para 6: Deputy IO of DGM rank with 5+ years' experience
  • para 7: appoint at least one IO; Board fixes numbers annually
  • para 8(4): fixed IO term of at least 3 years, max 5 years
  • para 8(6): early removal needs Board approval; report vacancies to RBI
  • para 12(1): IO handles only complaints the NBFC partly resolved or rejected
  • para 13(3): IO decision overruled only by Competent Authority, reviewed by Board
  • para 14(1): auto-escalate partly resolved / rejected complaints to IO, generally within 20 days
  • para 14(2): only three CMS categories: fully resolved, partly resolved, wholly rejected
  • para 14(9): final decision to complainant within 30 days of receipt

Deadlines and cycles

  • para 1(2): clauses 7(2), 14(2), 14(4) by 30-06-2026
  • para 8(5): start fresh appointment 3 months before tenure ends
  • para 8(6): inform RBI within 10 working days of unplanned vacancy; fill within 3 months
  • para 11(1): annual internal audit of IO compliance
  • para 13(2): IO reports to Board quarterly (not less than half-yearly)
  • para 16(1): IO details to CEPD within 5 working days of appointment
  • para 16(2): quarterly report to CEPD by 15th of following month

If you breach it

  • not stated in the Direction

General consequences under the statute. Statutory consequences under the RBI Act 1934: RBI may impose a monetary penalty under s.58G: up to ₹25,000, or, for NBFI business without a CoR (s.58B(4A)) or for accepting deposits or not complying with a Chapter IIIB direction or order (s.58B(5)(a), (aa)), up to ₹10 lakh or twice the amount involved, whichever is more, plus up to ₹1 lakh a day while the default continues; a show-cause notice and hearing come first, and penalty and prosecution are alternatives (s.58G(2), (5)-(6)). As offences, s.58B(5) carries up to 3 years and fine, and s.58B(6) a fine up to ₹1 lakh plus ₹10,000 a day. RBI may also cancel the CoR (s.45-IA(6)), prohibit deposits or bar asset transfers (s.45MB), remove a director (s.45-ID), supersede the Board (s.45-IE) and petition for winding up (s.45MC). For HFCs the parallel provisions are NHB Act ss.29A(6), 49 and 52A (NHB or RBI may impose penalties, e.g. up to ₹10 lakh or twice the amount involved plus ₹1 lakh a day for not complying with a direction or order under Chapter V, s.52A(1C)).
