Key concepts

  • s.73(1) proviso: the Chapter V deposit rules do not apply to banking companies and NBFCs as defined in the RBI Act (RBI's deposit Directions apply instead)
  • s.177(1): Audit Committee for listed public companies and prescribed classes of companies
  • s.180(1)(c): borrowing beyond paid-up capital, free reserves and securities premium (excluding temporary loans from bankers in the ordinary course) needs a special resolution
  • s.185(3)(b): the bar on loans to directors does not apply to a company that lends in the ordinary course at interest not below the yield on a Government security of comparable tenor; s.186(11): s.186 (except sub-s.(1)) does not apply to (a) ordinary-course loans, guarantees, securities and investments of banking, insurance and housing finance companies and of companies set up to finance industrial enterprises or provide infrastructure, or (b)(iii) investments, for investment or lending activities, by an NBFC registered under RBI Act Chapter IIIB whose principal business is acquisition of securities
  • s.135(1), (5): CSR applies at net worth ₹500 crore, turnover ₹1,000 crore or net profit ₹5 crore in the preceding year; spend at least 2% of average net profit of the 3 preceding years
  • ss.230-234: compromise, arrangement and amalgamation through the NCLT; s.230(2)(c)(iv) refers to RBI's corporate debt restructuring guidelines

Key obligations

  • Board and shareholder approvals for borrowing and investment
  • Audit Committee and auditor appointment
  • CSR spend if thresholds met

If you breach it

  • Penalties under the respective sections; NCLT proceedings
  • s.186(13): company fine ₹25,000-₹5 lakh; officers in default up to 2 years and ₹25,000-₹1 lakh
