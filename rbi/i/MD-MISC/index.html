Key concepts

  • Reporting of changes (address, directors, auditors)
  • Private placement of NCDs
  • NACH mandate / phase-out of post-dated cheques
  • Ban on partnership / LLP / AOP investments
  • NBFC-Factor principal business test
  • IDF-MF sponsorship

Key obligations

  • para 6: report change of registered/corporate office address to RBI within one month
  • para 7: report change of directors, principal officers, auditors, authorised signatories within one month
  • para 11(1)-(3): private-placement NCDs: min ₹20,000 per investor; issues below ₹1 crore capped at 200 secured subscribers per FY
  • para 11(5)-(6): NCD proceeds only for own balance sheet; no loans against own debentures
  • para 16-19: no capital contribution or partnership in firms, LLPs or AOPs; exit existing stakes
  • para 20: NBFCs ≥ ₹100 crore to report rating changes within 15 days
  • para 23: no fresh PDCs / EMI cheques where NACH is available
  • para 24: cash disbursal to comply with Income Tax Act ss.269SS, 269T
  • para 42: NBFC-Factor: ≥50% of assets and income from factoring
  • para 62: IDF-NBFC sponsoring IDF-MF needs prior approval and NOF ₹300 crore, CRAR 15%, NNPA <3%

Deadlines and cycles

  • para 6-7: within one month of change
  • para 20: within 15 days of rating change
  • para 41: NBFC-Factor to begin factoring within 6 months of CoR
  • para 13A(7): NUCFDC quarterly statement within 15 days
  • para 13B: para 13A in force till 31-03-2029

If you breach it

  • not stated in the Direction

General consequences under the statute. Statutory consequences under the RBI Act 1934: RBI may impose a monetary penalty under s.58G: up to ₹25,000, or, for NBFI business without a CoR (s.58B(4A)) or for accepting deposits or not complying with a Chapter IIIB direction or order (s.58B(5)(a), (aa)), up to ₹10 lakh or twice the amount involved, whichever is more, plus up to ₹1 lakh a day while the default continues; a show-cause notice and hearing come first, and penalty and prosecution are alternatives (s.58G(2), (5)-(6)). As offences, s.58B(5) carries up to 3 years and fine, and s.58B(6) a fine up to ₹1 lakh plus ₹10,000 a day. RBI may also cancel the CoR (s.45-IA(6)), prohibit deposits or bar asset transfers (s.45MB), remove a director (s.45-ID), supersede the Board (s.45-IE) and petition for winding up (s.45MC). For HFCs the parallel provisions are NHB Act ss.29A(6), 49 and 52A (NHB or RBI may impose penalties, e.g. up to ₹10 lakh or twice the amount involved plus ₹1 lakh a day for not complying with a direction or order under Chapter V, s.52A(1C)).
