Commercial Paper and Non-Convertible Debentures of original or initial maturity up to one year, 2024

Issued under
RBI Act 1934 ss.45J, 45K, 45L, 45W r/w s.45U
Applies to
NBFCs, incl. HFCs, issuing CP or NCDs of up to one year, if all their fund-based facilities from banks, AIFIs and NBFCs are Standard at issue (para 3(a))

Key concepts

  • Eligible issuers
  • Minimum credit rating A3 (para 5(e))
  • CP tenor 7 days to 1 year; NCD 90 days to 1 year; no call / put options; no underwriting (para 5(a))
  • para 12: the NBFC public-deposit Directions do not apply to funds raised by CP under these Directions (the text still names the 2016 deposit Master Direction)

Key obligations

  • para 5(a), (d): demat form only; minimum ₹5 lakh denomination; primary settlement within T+4 working days; individuals together ≤ 25% of an issue; end-use disclosed in the offer document
