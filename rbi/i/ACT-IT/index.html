Key concepts

  • s.43A: compensation where a body corporate handling sensitive personal data is negligent in reasonable security practices and causes wrongful loss or gain (omitted by DPDP s.44(2)(a) from 13-05-2027)
  • s.70B(6): CERT-In may call for information and give directions to service providers, intermediaries, data centres, body corporates and any other person

Key obligations

  • CERT-In Directions No. 20(3)/2022-CERT-In of 28-04-2022 under s.70B(6), direction (ii): service providers, intermediaries, data centres, body corporates and Government organisations must report the cyber incidents listed in Annexure I to CERT-In within 6 hours of noticing them or being told of them
  • CERT-In Directions (iv): keep ICT system logs for a rolling 180 days within Indian jurisdiction; the Directions took effect 60 days after issue
  • Cybersecurity Directions para 141: report cyber incidents to RBI on DAKSH within six hours of detection, and notify CERT-In proactively (para 28 sets the same six hours for the Base Layer chapter); HFCs report to NHB, not RBI (para 141 Note)

Deadlines and cycles

  • CERT-In: within 6 hours of noticing an incident or being told of it (CERT-In Directions (ii))
  • RBI: within six hours of detection, on DAKSH (Cybersecurity Directions para 141); HFCs report to NHB instead (para 141 Note)

If you breach it

  • s.70B(7): not providing information or not complying with a CERT-In direction, up to 1 year, or fine up to ₹1 crore, or both; s.70B(8): cognizance only on a complaint by an authorised CERT-In officer; s.43A compensation (until omitted)
