Key concepts

  • Data fiduciary
  • Consent
  • Data principal rights
  • s.44(2)(a): omits IT Act s.43A once that provision commences (13-05-2027)

Key obligations

  • Process personal data with valid consent and purpose limitation
  • s.8: a Data Fiduciary is accountable for its processors, must take reasonable security safeguards (8(5)), tell the Board and each affected Data Principal of a personal data breach (8(6)), and erase data once the purpose is served (8(7))

Deadlines and cycles

  • Commencement (G.S.R. 843(E) of 13-11-2025): definitions, the Board and procedural provisions from 13-11-2025; Consent Manager provisions (s.6(9), s.27(1)(d)) from 13-11-2026; the main obligations (ss.3-17 incl. s.8), penalties (s.33) and the omission of IT Act s.43A from 13-05-2027
  • DPDP Rules 2025 (G.S.R. 846(E) of 13-11-2025): r.4 (Consent Managers) after one year; rr.3, 5-16, 22, 23 after eighteen months; r.7(2): tell the Board of a breach without delay and send a detailed report within 72 hours

If you breach it

  • s.33 and Schedule (from 13-05-2027), imposed by the Data Protection Board after inquiry into a significant breach: security safeguards (s.8(5)) up to ₹250 crore; breach intimation (s.8(6)) up to ₹200 crore; children's data (s.9) up to ₹200 crore; Significant Data Fiduciary duties (s.10) up to ₹150 crore; any other provision up to ₹50 crore
