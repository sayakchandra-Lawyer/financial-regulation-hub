Applies to
Any resident non-individual incorporated, established or registered under a Central or State Act and permitted to raise ECB under the applicable Act, incl. NBFCs (Schedule I para 1(1)); Borrowing limit (higher of USD 1 billion of ECB or 300% of net worth) does not apply to borrowers regulated by a financial-sector regulator, which includes RBI (Schedule I para 5(1), (3); reg 2(1)(m))

Key concepts

  • Cost of borrowing in line with prevailing market conditions; for average maturity under 3 years the trade-credit cost ceiling applies (Schedule I para 7; reg 2(1)(g))
  • Minimum average maturity of 3 years; manufacturing borrowers 1-3 years up to USD 150 million outstanding (Schedule I para 6(1)-(2))
  • End-use negative list (reg 3A(1)): chit funds, Nidhi companies, real estate business, farm houses, agriculture and plantations (with exceptions), TDR trading, securities (except strategic corporate actions), repaying restricted or NPA rupee loans, and on-lending for any of these purposes (reg 3A(1)(i))

Key obligations

  • Draw down only after obtaining a Loan Registration Number through the designated AD Category-I bank (Schedule I para 10(1))
  • Entities regulated by RBI shall not issue any type of guarantee for ECB (Schedule I para 11(3))
  • Report through the designated AD bank: Form ECB 1 (LRN), Revised Form ECB 1 for changes, and Form ECB 2 for proceeds and debt servicing (Schedule I para 16(1))

Deadlines and cycles

  • Form ECB 2 and Revised Form ECB 1 within seven calendar days from the end of the month concerned (Schedule I para 16(1))
  • ECBs with an LRN obtained before the 2026 amendment continue under the earlier rules, except that reporting follows the amended Regulations (reg 1(3))

If you breach it

  • Late submission fee for late reporting (Schedule I para 16(2)); a borrower that files nothing for four consecutive quarters may be treated as untraceable (para 16(6))

General consequences under the statute. Contraventions of FEMA regulations attract a penalty under FEMA s.13 and may be compounded under s.15.
