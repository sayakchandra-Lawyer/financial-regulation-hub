Applies to
NBFCs receiving foreign investment: investing companies registered as NBFCs are on the 100% automatic route (para 5.2.6.3); foreign investment in CICs and in unregistered investing companies needs Government approval (para 5.2.6.1)

Key concepts

  • para 5.2.4: foreign investment in financial services other than those at serial F of the table in para 3(b) of Schedule I of the NDI Rules needs Government approval; para 5.2.6.2: CICs must also follow RBI's CIC framework
  • Reporting forms (FC-GPR, FC-TRS) are prescribed in the separate Master Direction on Reporting under FEMA; this Direction only refers to them (e.g. para 6.15.1) and does not mention FIRMS

Key obligations

  • para 7.11.2(c): a non-resident may pledge equity instruments of an Indian company to an RBI-registered NBFC to secure credit to that company; on invocation the NBFC must stay within credit concentration norms (the text still cites the 2016 NBFC Master Directions, para 22)

Deadlines and cycles

  • para 7.11.2(c): if invoking the pledge breaches the NBFC's concentration norms, the shares must be sold and the breach rectified within 30 days of invocation
