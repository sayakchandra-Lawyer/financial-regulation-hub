Key concepts

  • Independent credit appraisal
  • Related-party lending and materiality thresholds
  • Specified employees
  • No lending against own shares
  • CDS as user only
  • Legal Entity Identifier (LEI)

Key obligations

  • para 5: Board-approved credit policies for activities undertaken
  • para 6: Notified NBFCs: independent appraisal, sensitivity tests, verify promoter equity source
  • para 7: check directors against defaulter lists using DIN/PAN
  • para 13G: related-party loan materiality: UL/TL ₹10 crore, ML ₹5 crore, BL ₹1 crore
  • para 13I: related-party loans above threshold sanctioned by Board
  • para 13J: interested directors, KMP and specified employees to recuse
  • para 14-15: no loans against own shares or own debentures
  • para 16-17: CDS only to buy protection on bonds held
  • para 19-20, 24: non-individual borrowers with aggregate exposure of ₹5 crore or more from banks and financial institutions (incl. NBFCs and HFCs) must obtain an LEI; without it, no new exposure, renewal or enhancement (Central / State Government departments exempt); LEI renewals tracked
  • para 28: file charges for all current transactions with CERSAI on an ongoing basis (security interests in immovable, movable and intangible assets, per the 22-01-2016 notification)

Deadlines and cycles

  • para 13M: internal audit reviews related-party lending quarterly or more often
  • para 13L: loans to specified employees reported to Board annually

If you breach it

  • para 13Q: supervisory/enforcement action incl. monetary penalty, full provisioning, staff accountability, forensic audit and restrictions

General consequences under the statute. Statutory consequences under the RBI Act 1934: RBI may impose a monetary penalty under s.58G: up to ₹25,000, or, for NBFI business without a CoR (s.58B(4A)) or for accepting deposits or not complying with a Chapter IIIB direction or order (s.58B(5)(a), (aa)), up to ₹10 lakh or twice the amount involved, whichever is more, plus up to ₹1 lakh a day while the default continues; a show-cause notice and hearing come first, and penalty and prosecution are alternatives (s.58G(2), (5)-(6)). As offences, s.58B(5) carries up to 3 years and fine, and s.58B(6) a fine up to ₹1 lakh plus ₹10,000 a day. RBI may also cancel the CoR (s.45-IA(6)), prohibit deposits or bar asset transfers (s.45MB), remove a director (s.45-ID), supersede the Board (s.45-IE) and petition for winding up (s.45MC). For HFCs the parallel provisions are NHB Act ss.29A(6), 49 and 52A (NHB or RBI may impose penalties, e.g. up to ₹10 lakh or twice the amount involved plus ₹1 lakh a day for not complying with a direction or order under Chapter V, s.52A(1C)).
