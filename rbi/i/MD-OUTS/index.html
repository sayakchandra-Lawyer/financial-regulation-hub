Key obligations

  • para 9: responsibility stays with the NBFC and its Board
  • para 10: Board-approved outsourcing policy
  • para 15: no outsourcing of core management, internal audit, compliance, strategy or loan sanction
  • para 18: non-group providers not owned by directors or relatives
  • para 19: NBFC files CTR/STR for outsourced activity
  • para 28: notify RBI immediately of data breaches
  • para 33-34: written contracts with audit and RBI inspection rights
  • para 36: central record of material outsourcing, reviewed half-yearly
  • para 61: IT outsourcing: service providers report cyber incidents to the NBFC without undue delay so that the NBFC reports to RBI within six hours of detection by the service provider
  • para 82, 84-88: inventory of outsourced IT services and dependency mapping; exit strategy in the IT outsourcing policy; safe removal or destruction of data at exit; service provider must cooperate in transition

Deadlines and cycles

  • para 2 proviso: existing IT outsourcing agreements to comply on renewal or by 10-04-2026, whichever is earlier; new agreements from the start
  • para 12, 36: half-yearly review
  • para 28: immediate breach notification

If you breach it

  • para 28: NBFC liable to customers for breach losses

General consequences under the statute. Statutory consequences under the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934: RBI may impose a monetary penalty under s.58G: up to ₹25,000, or, for NBFI business without a CoR (s.58B(4A)) or for accepting deposits or not complying with a Chapter IIIB direction or order (s.58B(5)(a), (aa)), up to ₹10 lakh or twice the amount involved, whichever is more, plus up to ₹1 lakh a day while the default continues; a show-cause notice and hearing come first, and penalty and prosecution are alternatives (s.58G(2), (5)-(6)). As offences, s.58B(5) carries up to 3 years and fine, and s.58B(6) a fine up to ₹1 lakh plus ₹10,000 a day. RBI may also cancel the CoR (s.45-IA(6)), prohibit deposits or bar asset transfers (s.45MB), remove a director (s.45-ID), supersede the Board (s.45-IE) and petition for winding up (s.45MC). For HFCs the parallel provisions are National Housing Bank Act, 1987, ss.29A(6), 49 and 52A (NHB or RBI may impose penalties, e.g. up to ₹10 lakh or twice the amount involved plus ₹1 lakh a day for not complying with a direction or order under Chapter V, s.52A(1C)).
