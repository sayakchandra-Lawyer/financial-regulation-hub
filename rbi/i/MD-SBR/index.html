Key concepts

  • Scale Based Regulation layers (BL/ML/UL/TL)
  • Principal business criteria
  • Public funds
  • Customer interface
  • Type I NBFC
  • Net Owned Fund (NOF)
  • Group asset consolidation
  • Layer test (paras 10-15, 24): BL = non-deposit NBFCs below ₹1,000 crore, plus NBFC-P2P, NBFC-AA, NOFHC and Type I NBFCs (always BL); ML = all NBFC-D, non-deposit NBFCs of ₹1,000 crore or more, SPD and IDF-NBFC (always ML); NBFC-D, CIC, NBFC-IFC and HFC sit in ML or UL, never BL; NBFC-ICC, MFI, Factor and MGC can sit in any layer by size; UL = NBFCs with assets of ₹1,00,000 crore or more on the latest audited balance sheet, identified annually by RBI; TL ideally empty
  • Amendments in the current text: SBR Amendment Directions 2026 (29-04-2026, in force 01-07-2026: Type I / Type II registration, exemption for NBFCs with no public funds and no customer interface) and Second Amendment Directions 2026 (24-06-2026: UL asset-size test, guidelines for NBFC group entities of banks)

Key obligations

  • para 17: aggregate assets of all NBFCs in a group / with common promoters to determine Middle Layer classification
  • para 20: statutory auditor to certify group-wide asset size as on 31 March every year and furnish to DoS
  • para 27(1): NBFC-UL to adopt Board-approved policy and implementation plan for the enhanced framework
  • para 27(3): submit Board-approved UL implementation roadmap to RBI
  • para 38A(2): Type I NBFCs seeking deregistration to apply through PRAVAAH with prescribed documents
  • para 38A(6): Type I NBFCs to disclose in Notes to Accounts that no public funds / customer interface were used; auditor exception report
  • para 43: NBFC not achieving prescribed NOF in time ceases to be eligible to hold CoR
  • para 47: no new investor from FATF non-compliant jurisdictions may acquire significant influence (20%+ voting power)
  • para 68: comply with reporting requirements prescribed by DoS

Deadlines and cycles

  • para 16: from 01-10-2022, references in earlier instructions to NBFC-ND are read as NBFC-BL, and to NBFC-D / NBFC-ND-SI as NBFC-ML or NBFC-UL (a reference-mapping rule, not the layer test)
  • para 20: annual group asset-size certificate (as on 31 March)
  • para 27(1): within 3 months of RBI advice of UL classification
  • para 27(2): up to 24 months from advice to comply with UL norms
  • para 28: stay in UL at least 5 years; exit only after 5 consecutive years below criteria
  • para 31: UL asset threshold reviewed every 3 years
  • para 38A(1): from 01-07-2026, NBFCs with no public funds and no customer interface and assets below ₹1,000 crore are exempt from s.45-IA and s.45-IC (para 65A); existing ones, incl. Type I NBFCs, may apply for deregistration by 31-12-2026
  • para 42-43: NOF glide path for existing NBFC-ICC (₹5 crore by 31-03-2025), NBFC-MFI and NBFC-Factor (₹7 crore by 31-03-2025) to ₹10 crore by 31-03-2027; NBFCs that miss it lose eligibility for the CoR
  • para 24: UL test is assets of ₹1,00,000 crore or more (substituted by the SBR Second Amendment Directions of 24-06-2026); para 22: ML rules apply as soon as a non-deposit NBFC reaches ₹1,000 crore

If you breach it

  • para 38A(10): violation by unregistered Type I NBFC invites penal action under RBI Act 1934
  • para 43: failure to reach prescribed NOF makes the NBFC ineligible to hold CoR

General consequences under the statute. Statutory consequences under the RBI Act 1934: RBI may impose a monetary penalty under s.58G: up to ₹25,000, or, for NBFI business without a CoR (s.58B(4A)) or for accepting deposits or not complying with a Chapter IIIB direction or order (s.58B(5)(a), (aa)), up to ₹10 lakh or twice the amount involved, whichever is more, plus up to ₹1 lakh a day while the default continues; a show-cause notice and hearing come first, and penalty and prosecution are alternatives (s.58G(2), (5)-(6)). As offences, s.58B(5) carries up to 3 years and fine, and s.58B(6) a fine up to ₹1 lakh plus ₹10,000 a day. RBI may also cancel the CoR (s.45-IA(6)), prohibit deposits or bar asset transfers (s.45MB), remove a director (s.45-ID), supersede the Board (s.45-IE) and petition for winding up (s.45MC). For HFCs the parallel provisions are NHB Act ss.29A(6), 49 and 52A (NHB or RBI may impose penalties, e.g. up to ₹10 lakh or twice the amount involved plus ₹1 lakh a day for not complying with a direction or order under Chapter V, s.52A(1C)).
