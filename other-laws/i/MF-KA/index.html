Karnataka micro loan coercive-recovery law (2025)

Karnataka Micro Loan and Small Loan (Prevention of Coercive Actions) Act, 2025 (replacing the Ordinance of 12-02-2025)

Status
In force
Reference
Karnataka Act 15 of 2025
Issued
24 Mar 2025
Effective
12 Feb 2025
Issued by
Government of Karnataka
Applies to
Micro finance institutions, money lending agencies, organisations and lenders operating in Karnataka, including firms, persons, groups and digital lending platforms whose principal or incidental activity is lending at interest (s. 2(1)(e))
Does not apply to
Banks (including small finance banks, local area banks, regional rural banks and their business correspondents) regulated by RBI, co-operative banks and societies, souharda societies, and NBFCs and housing finance companies registered with RBI (s. 1(3))

Key concepts

  • Lenders within the Act must register with the district Registering Authority (the Deputy Commissioner) before granting or recovering loans (s. 3(2)); registration is for one year and renewal is sought sixty days before expiry (s. 3(3)-(4))
  • Coercive action includes pressure, violence, insults or intimidation of the borrower or family, persistent following, frequenting the home or workplace, using outside agencies or persons with a criminal background, and forcibly taking documents for Government benefits (s. 8, Explanation)
  • If an unregistered lender resorts to coercive action against a borrower from a vulnerable section of society, loans made before the commencement are deemed discharged and civil suits for recovery are barred (s. 15)

Key obligations

  • s. 6: take no security by pawn, pledge or any collateral; security taken before the Act stands released
  • s. 7: price loans only through interest, processing charge, insurance premium and delayed penal payment; give a loan card in Kannada with the effective rate of interest; display the rate in offices, literature and website; give receipts and copies of documents
  • s. 7(11): file quarterly and annual statements with the Registering Authority before the 10th day of the following quarter or year
  • s. 8: no coercive action, directly or through agents, to recover a loan

If you breach it

  • s. 13: coercive recovery, imprisonment up to ten years and fine up to Rs 5 lakh; offences are cognizable and non-bailable
  • s. 7(12): failure to file the quarterly or annual statement, imprisonment for six months, fine up to Rs 10,000, or both
  • s. 4: registration may be suspended or cancelled; a conviction under the Act is sufficient cause
