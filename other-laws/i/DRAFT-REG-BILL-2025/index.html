Applies to
Lenders taking mortgages by deposit of title-deeds and other security over immovable property; Property owners and borrowers

Key concepts

  • Would repeal and replace the Registration Act, 1908
  • Online registration: electronic presentation and admission, electronic certificates and digital records; Aadhaar-based authentication with consent, with alternatives
  • Wider compulsory registration: agreements to sell, powers of attorney to transfer property, sale certificates and documents setting out the terms of a mortgage by deposit of title-deeds
  • Banks, financial institutions and other creditors lending on a mortgage by deposit of title-deeds must file a copy of the title deed with the registering officer and notify the mortgage; a filed mortgage would take effect against any unfiled one over the same property

Key obligations

  • No obligation until enacted; lenders' title-deed mortgage processes would need a filing step if it passes in this form
