Security Interest (Enforcement) Rules 2002

Security Interest (Enforcement) Rules, 2002 under the SARFAESI Act: demand notice, possession, valuation and sale of secured assets

Applies to
Secured creditors and their authorised officers enforcing security under SARFAESI s. 13; Borrowers and guarantors whose assets are sold

Key concepts

  • The authorised officer is an officer of the secured creditor designated by its Board to exercise the secured creditor's rights (rule 2)
  • Movable assets: possession, valuation and fixing of a reserve price, and sale by quotations, public tender, public auction or private treaty (rules 4 to 6)
  • Immovable assets: possession notice, valuation by an approved valuer and fixing of a reserve price before sale (rule 8)

Key obligations

  • Rules 6(2) and 8(6): serve the borrower a notice of thirty days for sale of the secured asset; for sale by public tender or auction, publish a public notice in two leading newspapers, one in the vernacular, setting out the terms of sale including the reserve price
  • Rule 9(1): no sale of immovable property before thirty days from the public notice of sale or service of the sale notice on the borrower
  • Rule 9(3)-(4): the purchaser pays 25% of the sale price immediately and the balance on or before the fifteenth day of confirmation of sale, unless extended as the rule allows; a sale certificate issues on confirmation and payment (rule 9(6))

If you breach it

  • Rule 9(5): if the purchaser defaults on the balance, the deposit is forfeited and the property is resold
