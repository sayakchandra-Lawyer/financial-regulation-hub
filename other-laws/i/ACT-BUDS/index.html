BUDS Act: ban on unregulated deposit schemes

Banning of Unregulated Deposit Schemes Act, 2019: ban on unregulated deposit schemes, fraudulent default in regulated schemes, and information to the central database

Applies to
Deposit takers: individuals, firms, LLPs, companies, associations, trusts, co-operative societies and any other arrangement receiving or soliciting deposits (s. 2(6)); Promoters and persons inducing investment in unregulated schemes
Does not apply to
Statutory corporations, banking companies, SBI, regional rural banks and co-operative banks are not deposit takers (s. 2(6)); Loans received from banks, public financial institutions, RBI-registered NBFCs, regional financial institutions and insurers, Government and statutory authorities, and partners' capital, are not deposits (s. 2(4))

Key concepts

  • Deposit: money received as an advance, loan or in any other form with a promise to return it, with or without interest or other benefit, subject to the exclusions in s. 2(4)
  • Regulated Deposit Schemes are those listed in the First Schedule, including deposits accepted by RBI-registered NBFCs, and schemes regulated under the RBI Act or by SEBI, IRDAI and the State Governments; every other deposit scheme is an Unregulated Deposit Scheme and is banned (s. 3)
  • Prize chits and money circulation schemes banned under the 1978 Act are deemed Unregulated Deposit Schemes (s. 6)

Key obligations

  • s. 3: do not promote, operate, advertise or accept deposits under an Unregulated Deposit Scheme
  • s. 4: a deposit taker under a Regulated Deposit Scheme must not commit fraudulent default in repayment or in rendering a promised service
  • s. 10(1): a deposit taker must intimate the authority maintaining the central database about its business, in the prescribed form and time; this applies to companies accepting deposits under Chapter V of the Companies Act, 2013

If you breach it

  • s. 21: soliciting deposits under a banned scheme, imprisonment of one to five years and fine of Rs 2 lakh to Rs 10 lakh; accepting them, two to seven years and Rs 3 lakh to Rs 10 lakh; accepting and fraudulently defaulting, three to ten years and fine of Rs 5 lakh up to twice the funds collected
  • s. 22: fraudulent default in a Regulated Deposit Scheme, imprisonment up to seven years, or fine of Rs 5 lakh up to Rs 25 crore or three times the profits made, whichever is higher, or both
  • s. 23: false or misleading inducement to invest in a banned scheme, imprisonment of one to five years and fine up to Rs 10 lakh
