Tamil Nadu coercive-recovery law (2025)

Tamil Nadu Money Lending Entities (Prevention of Coercive Actions) Act, 2025, with the 2025 Rules and notifications under ss. 4 and 31

Applies to
All money lending entities in Tamil Nadu (microfinance institutions, money lending agencies, firms, persons and digital lending platforms), except banks, RBI-registered NBFCs, co-operative banks and co-operative societies (s. 2); RBI-registered NBFCs, co-operative banks and co-operative societies for the coercive-action provisions only (s. 2 proviso)

Key concepts

  • Micro loan: a loan to a household with annual income up to Rs 3 lakh, or a notified limit (s. 3(g))
  • Coercive action includes: violence, insult or intimidation; persistently following the borrower or family or interfering with their property; frequenting their home or workplace; using private or outsourced agencies to press for payment by coercion or undue influence; and forcibly taking government-benefit or other vital documents or household articles (s. 20(2))
  • District Collectors are the Registering Authorities (notification under s. 4(1), 16-02-2026)

Key obligations

  • Entities fully covered (not banks, RBI-registered NBFCs or co-operatives): s. 5: obtain a Certificate of Registration before granting or recovering any loan; the period for entities already operating was extended from 90 to 270 days from 19-11-2025 by the Removal of Difficulties Order of 04-08-2026
  • Entities fully covered: s. 9: display the effective interest rate; price loans with only four components (interest, processing charge, insurance premium and delayed penal payment); give a statement of terms one day before the loan, a standard loan agreement, a loan card and signed receipts; communicate in Tamil
  • Entities fully covered: s. 11: do not take security for loans up to the prescribed amount
  • All lenders in the State except banks, including RBI-registered NBFCs, co-operative banks and co-operative societies: s. 20: no coercive action against the borrower or family while recovering a loan

If you breach it

  • s. 18: lending without registration, imprisonment up to three years and fine up to Rs 1 lakh
  • s. 21: coercive action, imprisonment up to three years or fine up to Rs 5 lakh, or both (clauses (a)-(c)); up to five years or fine up to Rs 5 lakh, or both (clauses (d)-(e))
  • s. 22: where a borrower or family member dies by suicide and coercive action immediately before it is proved, the lender is deemed to have abetted the suicide
