CERSAI (Central Registry) Rules 2011

SARFAESI (Central Registry) Rules, 2011, as amended in 2013 and 2016: filing security interests and assignments with CERSAI

Applies to
Secured creditors registering the creation, modification and satisfaction of security interests; Factors and assignees filing assignments of receivables

Key concepts

  • Since the 2016 amendment, filings cover mortgages other than by deposit of title-deeds, hypothecation of plant and machinery, stocks, book debts and receivables, intangible assets such as know-how, patents, trade marks and licences, and under-construction buildings secured otherwise than by mortgage (rule 4(2A)-(2D))
  • Filings are made in the prescribed forms and authenticated with a valid digital signature (rule 4)

Key obligations

  • Rule 5(1): file the particulars of each transaction with the Central Registrar within thirty days of the transaction
  • Pay the filing fee in the Table under rule 7; delays up to a further thirty days may be condoned on application, with a higher fee

If you breach it

  • SARFAESI s. 26D: a secured creditor cannot exercise its enforcement rights under Chapter III of SARFAESI unless the security interest is registered with the Central Registry
  • SARFAESI s. 26E: debts due to a secured creditor with a registered security interest get priority over other debts and government dues, subject to the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016 where proceedings under that Code are pending
