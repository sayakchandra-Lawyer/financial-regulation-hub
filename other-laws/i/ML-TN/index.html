Tamil Nadu Money-lenders Act 1957

Tamil Nadu Money-lenders Act, 1957: licensing of money-lenders, permitted interest and charges, accounts and receipts, and penalties for molesting debtors

At a glance

Type

Status

Reference

Issued
20 Dec 1957
Issued by
Government of Tamil Nadu
Applies to
Money-lenders carrying on business in Tamil Nadu, as defined in the Act

Key concepts

  • Money-lenders must obtain a licence (s. 3), exhibit their names (s. 6) and charge only the interest and charges allowed (s. 7)
  • Molestation of a debtor is an offence (s. 14); the court may limit interest recoverable and allow instalments (ss. 20-A, 20-B)

Key obligations

  • s. 9: keep books and give receipts and statements of account to debtors
