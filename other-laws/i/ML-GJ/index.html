Applies to
Money-lenders carrying on business in Gujarat, registered with the district Registrar

Key concepts

  • No money-lender may carry on business except in the area and on the terms of its registration (s. 5(1))
  • NBFCs registered with RBI are deemed registered under the Act, but must intimate their RBI registration to the concerned Registrar in the prescribed proforma (s. 5(2))
  • The Supreme Court has held that the Gujarat Act, like the Kerala Money Lenders Act, does not apply to NBFCs registered with and regulated by RBI, because Chapter III-B of the RBI Act is a complete code for them (Nedumpilli Finance Co. v. State of Kerala, 2022 INSC 545)
  • The State Government may fix maximum rates of interest for any local area or class of money-lending business, for secured and unsecured loans; an agreement for interest above those rates is invalid (s. 33)

Key obligations

  • s. 5(2): on its face, an RBI-registered NBFC lending in Gujarat must intimate its RBI registration to the Registrar (see the Supreme Court ruling above on whether the Act reaches RBI-regulated NBFCs)
  • s. 33: charge no interest above the maximum rates fixed by the State Government

If you breach it

  • s. 40: molesting a debtor to recover a loan is punishable with imprisonment and fine, with minimum terms for first and repeat offences
