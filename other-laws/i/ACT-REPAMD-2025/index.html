Repealing and Amending Act 2025: notices by speed post

Repealing and Amending Act, 2025: repeal of spent amending Acts and replacement of 'registered post' with 'speed post with registration' in the General Clauses Act and the CPC

Key concepts

  • General Clauses Act, 1897 s. 27: service by post is now deemed effected by properly addressing, pre-paying and posting by 'speed post with registration' instead of 'registered post'
  • Corresponding changes to the Code of Civil Procedure, 1908
  • Repeals spent amending Acts, including the Arbitration amendments of 2015 and 2021, the NI amendment of 2018 and the Factoring amendment of 2021; the changes they made to the principal Acts remain

Key obligations

  • Update notice templates and dispatch processes to use speed post with registration where a law requires service by post
