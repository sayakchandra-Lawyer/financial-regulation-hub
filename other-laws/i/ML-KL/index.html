Kerala Money Lenders Act 1958

Kerala Money Lenders Act, 1958

At a glance

Type
State Act
Status
In force (as amended)
Reference
Kerala Act 35 of 1958
Issued
1958
Issued by
Government of Kerala
Applies to
Money lenders in Kerala: persons whose main or subsidiary occupation is advancing and realising loans, including branch and liaison offices and pawn brokers (s. 2(7))
Does not apply to
Banks and co-operative societies, LIC and certain other named statutory institutions (s. 2(7)); NBFCs registered with and regulated by RBI: the Supreme Court held that the Act does not apply to them (Nedumpilli Finance Co. v. State of Kerala, 2022 INSC 545)

Key concepts

  • No person, firm, joint family or unincorporated association may carry on business as a money lender without a licence (s. 3)
  • Molestation includes obstructing, using violence to or intimidating the debtor, interfering with their property, or annoying or intimidating family members (s. 2(6))

Key obligations

  • s. 7(1): charge no interest above 18% a year plus a 2% processing charge, or any rate the Government notifies
  • s. 6: display the name and the words 'money lender' or 'pawn-broker' over the place of business

If you breach it

  • s. 13: molesting a debtor to recover a loan, imprisonment up to six months, fine up to Rs 10,000, or both
  • s. 17: lending without a licence or in breach of its conditions, imprisonment of three months to three years and fine up to Rs 5 lakh
