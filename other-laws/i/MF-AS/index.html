Assam microfinance money-lending law (2020)

Assam Micro Finance Institutions (Regulation of Money Lending) Act, 2020

Open the full interactive summaryOfficial text ↗Official PDF ↗

At a glance

Type
State Act
Status
In force
Reference
Assam Act V of 2021
Issued
27 Jan 2021
Issued by
Government of Assam
Applies to
Micro finance institutions and money lending agencies or organisations in Assam, including companies, NBFCs as defined in the Act and societies, whose principal or incidental activity is lending to poor or below-poverty-line borrowers

Key concepts

  • Aims to protect economically vulnerable borrowers from usurious interest rates and coercive recovery by microfinance institutions and money lending agencies (Preamble)
  • Borrower includes individuals, self-help groups and joint liability groups borrowing for a productive purpose

Key obligations

  • Registration with the State authority before lending, with renewal, and powers to suspend or cancel registration
