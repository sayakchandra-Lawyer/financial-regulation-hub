Tamil Nadu exorbitant-interest law (2003)

Tamil Nadu Prohibition of Charging Exorbitant Interest Act, 2003

At a glance

Type
State Act
Status
In force
Reference
Tamil Nadu Act 38 of 2003
Issued
14 Nov 2003
Issued by
Government of Tamil Nadu
Applies to
Any person lending money in Tamil Nadu

Key concepts

  • Exorbitant interest includes daily, hourly, kandhu and meter vatti and thandal, each defined by reference to a rate above that fixed under s. 7 of the Tamil Nadu Money-lenders Act (s. 2)

Key obligations

  • s. 3: charge no exorbitant interest on any loan

If you breach it

  • s. 4: charging exorbitant interest or molesting a debtor to recover a loan, imprisonment up to three years and fine up to Rs 30,000
  • s. 5: a debtor may deposit the amount due with interest at the Government rate in court and seek an order recording satisfaction; the court may order restoration of property forcibly taken (s. 6)
