Tribunals Reforms Act 2026: DRT, DRAT and consumer commissions

Tribunals Reforms Act, 2026: s. 6 substitutes ss. 6A and 15A of the RDB Act; s. 12 substitutes s. 55(1A) of the Consumer Protection Act, 2019

Key concepts

  • Qualifications, terms and service conditions of the DRT Presiding Officer (RDB s. 6A) and the DRAT Chairperson (RDB s. 15A) are now governed by the Tribunals Reforms Act, 2026
  • The same applies to members of consumer commissions through the new Consumer Protection Act s. 55(1A)

Key obligations

  • No change to lenders' filing or recovery procedure; the amendments concern tribunal appointments and service conditions
